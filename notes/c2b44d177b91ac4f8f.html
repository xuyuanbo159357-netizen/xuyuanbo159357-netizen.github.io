关系摘要

3.2.2 力矩 / Moments

力矩与力矩原理、力偶与力偶矩、质心与稳定性。

知识节点 / Knowledge nodes

  • D04 力矩与力矩原理 - Moments and the principle of moments：力矩取决于力和到作用线的垂直距离。Moment uses perpendicular distance to the line of action.
  • D05 力偶与力偶矩 - Couples and the moment of a couple：力偶合力为零但有转动效应。A couple has zero resultant force and a nonzero moment.
  • D06 质心与稳定性 - Centre of mass and stability：质心让重力力矩可以用集中重力表示。Treat weight as acting through the centre of mass in a uniform field.
