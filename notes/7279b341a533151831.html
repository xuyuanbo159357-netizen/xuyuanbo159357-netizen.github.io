3.13.3 太阳辐射、太阳能电池与阵列

方法、分析与误差 / Method, analysis and uncertainty

1

小点光源和光敏探测器保持朝向，减少附近反射。

Use a small point light source and LDR detector, with fixed orientation and no nearby reflecting surfaces.

2

距离从光源中心量至敏感面，在适当范围改变。

Measure from the source centre to the active detector surface; vary distance over a suitable range.

3

按给定或实测响应标定，不假设阻值正比光强。

Calibrate LDR resistance/voltage to intensity using a supplied or measured response; do not assume raw resistance is proportional to light intensity.

4

控制或校正背景光，稳定读数并重复。

Control or correct background light, wait for stable readings and repeat.

5

作标定强度与距离倒数平方图或合理对数图，并分析源尺寸、距离零点和朝向误差。

Plot calibrated intensity against 1/r² or a suitable log graph; evaluate source size, distance-zero and orientation errors.

图示：LDR 实验距离与标定 / Distance and calibration in the LDR experiment

读图 / Reading： r 从光源中心量到敏感面，保持朝向与背景条件；电阻必须经响应标定后才可换算光强，不能直接令 R∝I。

Measure r from source centre to active surface with fixed orientation and background; calibrate resistance to intensity rather than assuming direct proportionality.

定量分析与条件核对 / Quantitative analysis and controls

↗ 3.13.3 太阳辐射、太阳能电池与阵列 / Solar energy

必做实验10与LDR标定 / Required practical 10

点光源到LDR的距离从发光中心量起，固定朝向并抑制环境光；改变距离，记录与光强有关的信号，保持光源输出稳定。若用已标定强度或与强度成正比信号，可检验 \(I\)–\(1/r^2\) 直线或 \(\ln I\)–\(\ln r\) 斜率 \(-2\)。

Measure distance from the emitting centre, fix alignment, reduce background light and keep source output stable. A calibrated intensity or proportional detector signal permits inverse-square and log–log tests.

LDR电阻随照度下降但通常并非 \(R\propto1/I\)；不能直接把 \(R\)–\(r^2\) 直线作为无需标定的必然结论。应使用题给或实验建立的响应关系，扣除适用的背景信号并考虑近距离点源近似失效。

LDR resistance is not automatically inversely proportional to intensity. Use a calibrated response or the supplied relationship, with appropriate background treatment and adequate source distance.
