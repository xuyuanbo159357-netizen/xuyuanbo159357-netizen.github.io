3.13.2 风能与风电机组 / Wind energy

考纲 / Specification： 3.13.2　｜　本单元知识谱系

物理量与符号 / Quantities and symbols

符号沿用本节公式；若题目另有定义，以题设为准。 / These symbols follow this section; use any notation defined in the question.

物理量 / Quantity 符号 / Symbol SI单位 / SI unit 说明 / Notes

Definition / 定义

Definition / 定义

English: The region behind a turbine where the wind has lost speed and energy.

中文： 风经过风机后速率和能量降低的区域。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

定义按考纲与指定教材概括；本页未列出可直接对应的定义题评分条目。 Definitions are summarised from the specification and textbook; no directly matched definition-item citation is claimed here.

Formula / 公式与单位

这是可用功率，非总能量；叶片扫掠面积为半径平方乘π。

\(\dot m=\rho Av\); \(P_{\mathrm{available}}=\frac12\rho Av^3=\frac12\rho\pi r^2v^3\); \(P_{\mathrm{output}}=\eta P_{\mathrm{available}}\).

Conditions / 条件与范围

假设入射风近似均匀；使用题给效率，不自行添加需记忆的贝茨极限定量值。

Assume uniform incoming wind, air density and swept area. Use the stated conversion efficiency; a numerical Betz limit is not required unless supplied.

Explanation / 解释

风功率的流量推导 / Available wind power

风速 \(v\)、空气密度 \(\rho\)、叶片扫掠面积 \(A=\pi r^2\)，每秒通过质量 \(\dot m=\rho Av\)，故风的可用动能率 \(P_{\rm available}=\tfrac12\dot m v^2=\tfrac12\rho Av^3\)。这是功率而非能量；题给时间内能量 \(E=Pt\)。

Mass flow through the swept area gives available kinetic power, proportional to swept area and the cube of wind speed. The expression is a power, not an energy.

半径加倍使可用功率变为四倍；风速加倍使其变为八倍。电输出 \(P_{\rm out}=\eta P_{\rm available}\)。空气必须保留速度才能继续离开风轮，不可能把全部动能取走；还存在机械、电气损耗。

Power scales with radius squared and speed cubed. The downstream air must keep moving, preventing total extraction; mechanical and electrical losses further reduce output.

风影、布置与环境 / Wind shadows and evaluation

风轮后方风速降低且湍流增强，形成风影；后排风机功率下降，因此按主导风向留间距、错列布置。由于 \(v^3\) 关系，小幅风速下降也可能显著降低功率。

A turbine leaves slower turbulent air behind it. Space and stagger turbines relative to prevailing winds; the cubic dependence magnifies the effect of reduced wind speed.

运行阶段无需燃料燃烧、直接排放少，但供电随风变化；评价时结合景观、噪声、生态、用地、并网和备用需求，不写成全生命周期“零环境影响”。

Weigh low operational combustion emissions against variable supply, visual and acoustic effects, ecology, land use and backup or grid requirements.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.13.2

U4 Wind power

面积和密度固定时可用风功率随风速三次方增长；非全部可提取。 Available wind power scales as speed cubed.

图示：风穿过叶轮的能量变化 / Airflow through a rotor

读图 / Reading： 叶轮取走部分动能后下游气流变慢；ρ、A、v 分别影响质量流率与每千克动能，解释了可用功率中的 v³。

Removing kinetic energy slows downstream air; density, area and speed affect mass flow and kinetic energy per mass, explaining v³ in available power.

图示：风影与布置 / Wind shadow and layout

读图 / Reading： 下游风速降低并更不均匀，后方机组可用功率受影响；布置时需考虑间距和主风向。

Downstream flow is slower and less uniform, reducing power available to another turbine; account for spacing and prevailing wind.

Applications / 应用与实验

选址综合风资源、可靠性、景观、噪声、生态、陆海施工与输电条件。

Evaluate siting using wind resource, reliability, visual impact, noise, habitat, land/offshore access and transmission needs.

Common errors / 易错点

风速倍增使可用功率八倍；叶轮直径不能直接作为半径。

Doubling wind speed increases available power eightfold, not twofold. Diameter must be halved before using r.

3.13.2 方法 1 · 风功率、尾流与设计论述 / Wind power, wakes and design

  1. 每秒气体质量 \(\dot m=\rho Av\)，来流动能通量 \(P_{wind}=\frac12\rho Av^3\)，\(A=\pi r^2\)；叶长是半径，转子直径需除二。电输出还乘题给效率。 / Distinguish available wind power from extracted and electrical power.
  2. 比例用 \(P_2/P_1=(\eta_2/\eta_1)(\rho_2/\rho_1)(r_2/r_1)^2(v_2/v_1)^3\)；速度的立方通常比线性变化影响大。 / Apply the power to the entire ratio.
  3. 空气要继续流出，不能取尽全部动能；另有轴承摩擦、发电机电阻等具体损失。 / Separate extraction limits from machine inefficiency.
  4. 风场布局考虑主导风向、间距和尾流：前方机组使后方来流动能减少。环境论述给噪声、栖息地、鸟类、视觉和材料等具体影响，并说明运行无燃烧排放的范围。 / Explain mechanisms rather than listing vague advantages.
  5. 转子达到恒角速率时驱动、阻力矩平衡，功率 \(P=\tau\omega\)；加速阶段要扣去阻力矩，不是把全部驱动力矩用于发电。 / Separate acceleration from steady generation.
