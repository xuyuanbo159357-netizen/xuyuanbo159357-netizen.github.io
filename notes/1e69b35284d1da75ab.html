1. 风轮中的质量流率与可用功率 / Mass flow and available wind power

1.1 过程、现象与问题 / Process, observations and questions

风功率的流量推导 / Available wind power

风速 \(v\)、空气密度 \(\rho\)、叶片扫掠面积 \(A=\pi r^2\)，每秒通过质量 \(\dot m=\rho Av\)，故风的可用动能率 \(P_{\rm available}=\frac12\dot m v^2=\frac12\rho Av^3\)。这是功率而非能量；题给时间内能量 \(E=Pt\)。

Mass flow through the swept area gives available kinetic power, proportional to swept area and the cube of wind speed. The expression is a power, not an energy.

半径加倍使可用功率变为四倍；风速加倍使其变为八倍。电输出 \(P_{\rm out}=\eta P_{\rm available}\)。空气必须保留速度才能继续离开风轮，不可能把全部动能取走；还存在机械、电气损耗。

Power scales with radius squared and speed cubed. The downstream air must keep moving, preventing total extraction; mechanical and electrical losses further reduce output.

U4 Wind power

面积和密度固定时可用风功率随风速三次方增长；非全部可提取。 Available wind power scales as speed cubed.

1.3 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

适用范围与条件 / Scope and conditions

Conditions / 适用条件： 风能式给出可用功率，非能量；A=πr² 是叶片扫掠面积。输出功率还须乘效率。 / The wind expression gives available power, not energy; swept area is πr² and output power includes efficiency.

风穿过叶轮的能量变化 / Airflow through a rotor

读图 / Reading： 叶轮取走部分动能后下游气流变慢；ρ、A、v 分别影响质量流率与每千克动能，解释了可用功率中的 v³。

Removing kinetic energy slows downstream air; density, area and speed affect mass flow and kinetic energy per mass, explaining v³ in available power.

Conditions / 条件与范围

假设入射风近似均匀；使用题给效率，不自行添加需记忆的贝茨极限定量值。

Assume uniform incoming wind, air density and swept area. Use the stated conversion efficiency; a numerical Betz limit is not required unless supplied.

Applications / 应用与实验

选址综合风资源、可靠性、景观、噪声、生态、陆海施工与输电条件。

Evaluate siting using wind resource, reliability, visual impact, noise, habitat, land/offshore access and transmission needs.

Common errors / 易错点

风速倍增使可用功率八倍；叶轮直径不能直接作为半径。

Doubling wind speed increases available power eightfold, not twofold. Diameter must be halved before using r.

2. 风影、机组布置与环境 / Wind shadows, turbine layout and environment

2.1 过程、现象与问题 / Process, observations and questions

风影、布置与环境 / Wind shadows and evaluation

风轮后方风速降低且湍流增强，形成风影；后排风机功率下降，因此按主导风向留间距、错列布置。由于 \(v^3\) 关系，小幅风速下降也可能显著降低功率。

A turbine leaves slower turbulent air behind it. Space and stagger turbines relative to prevailing winds; the cubic dependence magnifies the effect of reduced wind speed.

运行阶段无需燃料燃烧、直接排放少，但供电随风变化；评价时结合景观、噪声、生态、用地、并网和备用需求，不写成全生命周期“零环境影响”。

Weigh low operational combustion emissions against variable supply, visual and acoustic effects, ecology, land use and backup or grid requirements.

风影与布置 / Wind shadow and layout

读图 / Reading： 下游风速降低并更不均匀，后方机组可用功率受影响；布置时需考虑间距和主风向。

Downstream flow is slower and less uniform, reducing power available to another turbine; account for spacing and prevailing wind.

考点精讲：风电场设计的三个评分板块 / Three marking areas for wind-farm design

按 PH04 2025 年 6 月 Q06.1 的设问，依次说明单台风机可获得多少功率、场地能布置多少台风机，以及选址会带来哪些环境影响。每一点都把设计因素与它造成的结果联系起来。

For PH04 June 2025 Q06.1, explain what determines the power available to one turbine, how many turbines the site can accommodate and how the location affects people and the environment. Link each factor to its consequence.

A. 最大输出功率 / Maximum power output

  1. 风速 / wind speed。其他条件相同时，来流可用功率随风速的立方增大。风速越大，每秒穿过叶轮扫掠面的空气质量越大，每千克空气携带的动能也越多。 / At fixed density and swept area, available wind power rises with the cube of wind speed. Faster wind brings more air through the swept area each second, and each kilogram carries more kinetic energy.
  2. 叶片半径与扫掠面积 / blade radius and swept area。叶片越长，叶轮扫掠面积越大。同样风速下，叶轮每秒可从更多空气中提取能量。扫掠面积随叶片半径的平方增大。 / Longer blades sweep a larger area. At the same wind speed, the turbine can extract energy from more air each second. Swept area increases with the square of blade radius.
  3. 选址与风况可靠性 / location and wind reliability。优先考虑开阔、少遮挡、风较强且经常达到运行条件的地点。评估一段时间内的风况，判断风场能否经常发电。 / Choose an exposed site with few obstructions and strong winds that regularly fall within the operating range. Assess wind conditions over time to judge how often the farm can generate power.
  4. 下游仍有风速 / air retains speed downstream、轴承摩擦 / friction in bearings。空气必须继续流过叶轮，因此下游仍带有动能。轴承摩擦和发电机电阻发热还会使部分能量转为内能，降低实际电输出。 / Air must continue to flow through the rotor, so it retains kinetic energy downstream. Bearing friction and resistive heating in the generator transfer some energy to internal energy and reduce electrical output.

来流功率、半径与面积、可用功率到电输出的关系见 公式与符号表 / Formulae and symbols。

B. 风机数量与布局 / Number and arrangement of turbines

  1. 可用场地、机组尺寸与间距 / available space, turbine size and spacing。先看场地能提供多少可用空间，再按机组尺寸和所需间距确定能装多少台。固定面积内，机组越大或要求的间距越宽，通常可装台数越少。 / Determine the number of turbines from the available space, turbine size and required spacing. On a fixed site, larger turbines or wider spacing usually mean fewer units can fit.
  2. 风影（尾流） / wind shadow (wake)。上游风机从空气中提取动能，后方风速下降，湍流增强。若另一台风机处在这片区域，其可用风功率会下降。沿主导风向 / prevailing wind direction留出足够的前后间距，并考虑错列布置以减小尾流重叠。 / An upstream turbine extracts kinetic energy, leaving slower, more turbulent air behind it. A turbine in that wake receives less available wind power. Allow sufficient downwind spacing and consider staggering the rows to reduce wake overlap.
  3. 权衡单机功率与整场输出 / balance power per turbine against total farm output。更大的叶轮可提高单机可用功率，同时往往需要更大的间距来控制尾流影响。结合场地大小、风向和各台风机的实际受风条件，比较“大机组、较少台数”和“小机组、较多台数”的整场输出。 / Larger rotors can increase power available to each turbine and often need wider spacing to limit wake effects. Compare the total output of fewer large turbines and more small turbines using the site area, wind direction and the wind reaching each turbine.

结合 风影视图 / Wind shadow diagram，沿主导风向找出上游风机后方可能受影响的机组。

C. 环境因素 / Environmental factors

  1. 噪声 / noise。叶片转动和机械运转产生的噪声可能影响附近居民，选址应考虑与住宅的距离。 / Blade and machinery noise may disturb nearby residents, so consider the distance to homes.
  2. 鸟类与栖息地 / birds and habitats。鸟类可能与叶片碰撞。修建基础和道路也可能破坏栖息地，因此要考虑鸟类活动区和敏感生境的位置。 / Birds may collide with blades. Building foundations and roads can damage habitats, so consider where birds occur and where habitats are sensitive.
  3. 土地用途受限 / restrictions on land use。风机基础、道路和需要预留的区域会占用场地，从而限制其他土地用途，例如建筑开发。 / Foundations, roads and reserved areas take up space and may restrict other land uses such as building development.
  4. 景观影响 / visual impact。例如，把高大的风机设在山脊上，会改变从附近村镇看到的地平线。 / For example, tall turbines on a ridge can change the skyline seen from a nearby village.
  5. 阴影闪烁 / shadow flicker。旋转叶片周期性遮挡阳光。当移动的影子扫过附近住宅的窗户时，室内光线会反复明暗变化。选址时考虑住宅的位置和太阳方向。 / Rotating blades periodically block sunlight. When the moving shadows pass across nearby windows, the light indoors repeatedly brightens and dims. Consider the positions of homes and the direction of the Sun.

3.13.2 方法 1 · 风功率、尾流与设计论述 / Wind power, wakes and design

  1. 每秒气体质量 \(\dot m=\rho Av\)，来流动能通量 \(P_{wind}=\frac12\rho Av^3\)，\(A=\pi r^2\)；叶长是半径，转子直径需除二。电输出还乘题给效率。 / Distinguish available wind power from extracted and electrical power.
  2. 比例用 \(P_2/P_1=(\eta_2/\eta_1)(\rho_2/\rho_1)(r_2/r_1)^2(v_2/v_1)^3\)；速度的立方通常比线性变化影响大。 / Apply the power to the entire ratio.
  3. 空气要继续流出，不能取尽全部动能；另有轴承摩擦、发电机电阻等具体损失。 / Separate extraction limits from machine inefficiency.
  4. 风场布局考虑主导风向、间距和尾流：前方机组使后方来流动能减少。环境论述给噪声、栖息地、鸟类、视觉和材料等具体影响，并说明运行无燃烧排放的范围。 / Explain mechanisms rather than listing vague advantages.
  5. 转子达到恒角速率时驱动、阻力矩平衡，功率 \(P=\tau\omega\)；加速阶段要扣去阻力矩，不是把全部驱动力矩用于发电。 / Separate acceleration from steady generation.
