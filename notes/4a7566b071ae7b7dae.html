3.4.2 伏安特性与欧姆定律 / Current–voltage characteristics

考纲 / Specification： 3.4.2　｜　本单元知识谱系

知识逐项讲解 / Concept-by-concept guide

ohmic conductor — 欧姆导体

Definition / 定义

↗ ohmic conductor — 欧姆导体

Definition / 定义

English: A conductor whose current is proportional to the potential difference across it when physical conditions are constant.

中文： 物理条件不变时，电流与其两端电势差成正比的导体。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

semiconductor diode — 半导体二极管

Definition / 定义

↗ semiconductor diode — 半导体二极管

Definition / 定义

English: A semiconductor component that conducts readily in one direction and has a much higher resistance in the reverse direction.

中文： 正向易于导电、反向电阻大得多的半导体元件。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式与单位

Conditions / 适用条件： R = V/I 适用于工作点。直线且过原点时，V–I 图斜率为 R，I–V 图斜率为 1/R。 / At an operating point use V/I; for a straight graph through the origin, V–I gradient is R and I–V gradient is 1/R.

Ohm's law / 欧姆定律

Law statement / 定律表述

English: The current through a conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference across it, provided its temperature and other physical conditions remain constant.

中文： 在温度和其他物理条件保持不变时，通过导体的电流与其两端的电势差成正比。

\[\Large\displaystyle I\propto V\]

评分依据 / Mark-scheme basis： PH02 · 202306 · Q08.1

Resistance / 电阻定义

区别 / Distinction： 下面的比值是电阻定义；能写出

\[\Large\displaystyle R=\frac{V}{I}\]

本身不等于证明元件遵守欧姆定律。 / The following ratio defines resistance; it does not by itself establish that the component obeys Ohm's law.

\[\Large\displaystyle R=\frac{V}{I}\]
\[\Large\displaystyle m_{V-I}=R\]
\[\Large\displaystyle m_{I-V}=\frac1R\]

Conditions / 条件与范围

欧姆定律要求温度等物理条件不变。未另作说明时，电流表电阻为零，电压表电阻无限大。

Ohm’s law: current is directly proportional to potential difference provided temperature and other physical conditions remain constant. Ideal ammeters have zero resistance; ideal voltmeters have infinite resistance unless stated otherwise.

Explanation / 解释

三类特性曲线 / Three characteristic curves

欧姆导体：温度等物理条件恒定时，\(I\propto V\)，图线为过原点直线。灯丝灯泡：电流增大使温度升高，晶格振动增强、电子散射增多，电阻上升，\(I\)–\(V\) 曲线逐渐变平。

An ohmic conductor has a straight characteristic through the origin at constant physical conditions. In a filament lamp, heating increases lattice vibrations and electron scattering, increasing resistance and reducing the gradient on an \(I\)–\(V\) graph.

二极管：正向电流在一定电压范围后迅速增加，反向电流通常很小；不把某个固定导通电压当作所有二极管的通用常量。灯泡和欧姆导体的正、负方向特性通常关于原点对称，二极管不对称。

A diode conducts readily forwards and very little in reverse. Its forward operating voltage depends on the device; it is not a universal constant. Lamp and ohmic characteristics are normally symmetric about the origin; a diode's is not.

坐标轴与电表 / Axes and meters

在指定点一律用 \(R=\frac{V}{I}\) 求电阻。只有过原点的直线，\(V\)–\(I\) 斜率才等于 \(R\)，\(I\)–\(V\) 斜率才等于 \(1/R\)；非线性图线的切线斜率不能直接作为 \(V/I\)。

At an operating point use \(R=\frac{V}{I}\). For a straight line through the origin, the gradient is \(R\) on a \(V\)–\(I\) graph and \(1/R\) on an \(I\)–\(V\) graph. A tangent gradient on a nonlinear curve is not generally the resistance \(V/I\).

电流表串联，理想电阻为零；电压表并联，理想电阻无穷大。测特性时改变电压并反接极性，逐点记录；测金属导线时使用小电流、间歇接通以控制温度。

Connect an ammeter in series and a voltmeter in parallel. Ideal resistances are zero and infinite respectively. Vary and reverse the supply to record both polarities; limit heating when testing a metal wire.

Forward and reverse responses differ; the illustrated 0.6 V is an example silicon-diode value, not a universal diode constant.

Applications / 应用与实验

能读出两种坐标轴安排，并用指定点的电压与电流之比比较电阻。

Read either axis orientation and compare the resistance at specified points using \(V/I\).

Common errors / 易错点

曲线的切线斜率一般不等于工作点的电压电流之比；理想电流表不能直接并接电源。

For a curved characteristic, a tangent gradient is not generally equal to \(V/I\). An ideal ammeter must not be connected directly across a supply.

3.4.2 方法 1 · 非线性元件与工作点 / Nonlinear components and operating points

补充知识 / Knowledge. 工作点的电阻是 \(R=\frac{V}{I}\)；非线性曲线的切线斜率一般不等于这个电阻。先看横纵坐标。 / Resistance at an operating point is the voltage-to-current ratio, not generally the tangent gradient.

  1. 串联：选同一个 \(I\)，从各元件曲线读取 \(V\)，使各电压之和等于电源端电压；线性电阻加上 \(IR\)。 / In series, use a common current and add component voltages.
  2. 并联：选共同 \(V\)，读取各支路 \(I\) 后相加；灯泡铭牌不能代表所有工作点的恒定电阻。 / In parallel, use a common voltage and add branch currents.
  3. 作图标出原点、额定点和题给点；欧姆区要求 \(V\propto I\)，直线应通过原点。 / Mark specified points; an ohmic range requires proportionality through the origin.
  4. 判断电表误差时分别追踪“表测哪段电压、哪条电流”；电表改变工作点不必然使所测 \(V/I\) 错误。 / Trace exactly what each meter measures.
