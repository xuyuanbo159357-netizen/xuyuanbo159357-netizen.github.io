3.11 热学（仅International A-level） / THERMAL PHYSICS (INTERNATIONAL A-LEVEL ONLY)

考纲章节原文 / Chapter text

考纲依据 / Source： 物理大纲.pdf · OxfordAQA 9630 · Version 4.4；PDF第26页／印刷第27页。

English: In Sections 3.11 – 3.13, students will study alternative energy sources. They must be able to use their knowledge of the advantages and disadvantages of each to make a judgment of appropriate sources to be used in different situations. The latter parts of this section brings together ideas from other parts of the specification.

中文： 3.11—3.13学习替代能源；应能根据各种能源的优缺点，判断不同情境下适合使用的能源。本节后续部分综合考纲其他部分的知识。
