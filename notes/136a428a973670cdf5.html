Concept / 概念

binding energy per nucleon — 平均核子结合能

Definition / 定义

English: The total binding energy of a nucleus divided by its nucleon number.

中文： 核总结合能除以核子数。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式与单位

Formula / 公式

\[\Large\displaystyle \overline E_b=\frac{E_b}{A}\]

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

平均每核子结合能比较不同大小原子核的结合程度。

Binding energy per nucleon compares the degree of binding in nuclei of different sizes.

Conditions / 条件与说明： 真题也常用 MeV per nucleon；注意与总结合能区分。 / Mark schemes also use MeV per nucleon; distinguish it from total binding energy.

图示讲解 / Diagram explained

图示：平均核子结合能曲线 / Binding energy per nucleon

读图 / Reading： 纵轴是每个核子的结合能，不是总结合能。反应后平均结合更强时可释放能量；求释放量仍要按反应中各核的总质量或总结合能计算。

The vertical axis is per nucleon, not total binding energy; energy calculations require complete mass or binding-energy totals for the reaction.

重要考点：特殊点与整体趋势 / Key exam points: landmarks and overall trend

重点记忆：左侧陡升 → \(A\approx60\) 附近达到峰值（8–9 MeV/核子）→ 右侧缓降 → \(A=240\) 时为 7–8 MeV/核子。

Memorise: a steep rise on the left → a peak near \(A\approx60\) (8–9 MeV per nucleon) → a gentle fall on the right → 7–8 MeV per nucleon at \(A=240\).

位置或特征 / Landmark or feature 中文说明 English
横轴与纵轴 / Axes 横轴为核子数（质量数）\(A\)，不是质子数；纵轴为平均核子结合能，常用单位为 MeV/核子。 The horizontal axis is nucleon number (mass number) \(A\), not proton number. The vertical axis is binding energy per nucleon, usually in MeV per nucleon.
峰值位置 / Peak position 峰值约在 \(A=60\)；平均核子结合能在 8–9 MeV/核子之间。 峰值附近的原子核结合较紧。 The peak is at about \(A=60\), with a binding energy per nucleon between 8 and 9 MeV. Nuclei near the peak are tightly bound.
重核区参考点 / Heavy-nucleus landmark 曲线应经过 \(A=240\)、平均核子结合能为 7–8 MeV/核子的位置。 The curve should pass through \(A=240\) at a binding energy per nucleon between 7 and 8 MeV.
左侧趋势 / Left-hand trend 随 \(A\) 增大，平均核子结合能迅速增大；曲线具有较陡的正斜率，通向峰值。 Binding energy per nucleon rises rapidly as \(A\) increases. The curve has a steep positive gradient leading to the peak.
右侧趋势 / Right-hand trend 过峰值后，随 \(A\) 增大，平均核子结合能缓慢减小；曲线具有较缓的负斜率。整体形状为左陡右缓。 Beyond the peak, binding energy per nucleon decreases slowly as \(A\) increases. The curve has a less steep negative gradient. The rise is steeper than the fall.

画图顺序 / Sketching route： 先标出峰值和 \(A=240\) 的参考点，再连接左侧陡升段与右侧缓降段；同时检查坐标轴名称、单位和横轴覆盖范围。 / Mark the peak and the landmark at \(A=240\) first. Then draw the steep rising section and the gently falling section. Check axis labels, units and the horizontal range.

本题评分提醒 / Marking guidance for this question： PH04 202601 Q04.2 共 2 分：左侧陡升并达到正确峰值占 1 分；右侧缓降并经过正确重核区位置占 1 分。若曲线未延伸到至少 \(A=240\)，本题最多得 1 分。 检查核子数位置时，本题评分标准允许左右各一格的误差。 / This 2-mark question awards one mark for the steep rise and correct peak, and one for the gentler fall through the heavy-nucleus landmark. A graph that does not extend to at least \(A=240\) can receive at most one mark. The scheme allows one grid square either side when checking nucleon-number positions.
