简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

During the first 5 ms, the wire heats up and its resistance increases. The supply voltage is approximately constant, so P = V²/R decreases. After the wire reaches a steady temperature, its resistance and power remain constant for the rest of the 10 ms.
