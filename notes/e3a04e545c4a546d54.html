简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

The first law is ΔU = Q + W, where Q is heat supplied to the gas and W is work done on it. The gas does work against atmospheric pressure and the balloon as it expands, so W is negative. Little heat enters during inflation, so Q ≈ 0. Its internal energy and mean molecular kinetic energy decrease, so its temperature falls.
