1. 裂变反应与链式反应 / Fission and the chain reaction

1.1 过程、现象与问题 / Process, observations and questions

热中子裂变与临界条件 / Thermal fission and criticality

U-235等可裂变核吸收热中子后形成激发复合核，分裂成较轻碎片并放出中子和能量；放出的中子再引发裂变，形成链式反应。受控稳态平均每次裂变有效引发一次后续裂变。

A fissile nucleus captures a thermal neutron, forming an excited nucleus that splits and releases neutrons and energy. A controlled steady chain has on average one effective successor fission per fission.

临界质量是在给定形状、纯度和环境下维持链式反应所需的最小质量。中子泄漏受表面积影响，产生数随体积或质量增加；不能把学习资料某个千克值当作各种装置的通用常量。

Critical mass depends on geometry, composition and surroundings. Neutron production scales with volume while escape is linked to surface area; there is no universal kilogram value for all assemblies.

nuclear fission — 核裂变

Definition / 定义

English: The splitting of a heavy nucleus into two smaller nuclei, usually with neutron emission and energy release.

中文： 重核分裂成两个较小核，通常同时放出中子和能量。

Definition / 定义

English: A sequence in which neutrons released by fission cause further fissions.

Released neutrons may trigger later fissions; sustained operation depends on effective next-generation fissions, not every neutron reacting.

1.3 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

Conditions / 条件与范围

采用热中子反应堆模型；临界性与中子损失、材料、形状和环境有关。

Use a thermal-neutron reactor model. Criticality depends on neutron losses, material, geometry and surrounding conditions, not a universal mass value.

2. 核反应堆的工作过程与部件 / Reactor operation and components

2.1 过程、现象与问题 / Process, observations and questions

热中子反应堆中的慢化剂 / Moderator in a thermal nuclear reactor

先跟踪一次循环：裂变释放快中子 → 慢化剂使中子减速 → 热中子更容易被 U-235 吸收 → 形成激发的 U-236 核并可能再次裂变。控制棒则通过吸收一部分中子控制后续裂变率。 / Follow one cycle: fission releases fast neutrons → the moderator slows them → thermal neutrons are more likely to be absorbed by U-235 → excited U-236 forms and may undergo further fission. Control rods regulate subsequent fission by absorbing some neutrons.

A. 为什么需要慢化剂？ / Why is a moderator required?
  1. 裂变产生的中子运动很快，具有较高动能。 / Neutrons released in fission move very fast and have high kinetic energy.
  2. 在本节讨论的 U-235 热中子反应堆中，快中子引发诱发裂变的概率较低。 / In the U-235 thermal reactor considered here, fast neutrons have a lower probability of inducing fission.
  3. 中子减速到热运动速率后，更可能被 U-235 核吸收。U-235 吸收中子形成激发的 U-236 核，继而更容易发生裂变，帮助维持链式反应。 / At thermal speeds, neutrons are more likely to be absorbed by U-235 nuclei. Neutron absorption forms excited U-236, which can then undergo fission and sustain the chain reaction.
B. 中子怎样被慢化？ / How are neutrons moderated?

慢化剂 / Moderator 是通过中子与原子核的碰撞来降低快中子动能的材料，选材时还要求尽量少吸收中子。 / A moderator is a material that reduces the kinetic energy of fast neutrons through collisions with its nuclei; it should also absorb few neutrons.

  1. 快中子进入慢化剂，与其原子核碰撞并发生散射。 / Fast neutrons enter the moderator and scatter from its nuclei.
  2. 在许多次碰撞中，中子将动能传给反冲原子核，自身的动能和速率逐渐降低；慢化通常按弹性碰撞模型解释。 / In many collisions, neutrons transfer kinetic energy to recoiling nuclei and gradually lose speed. Moderation is usually explained using elastic collisions.
  3. 最终，中子的能量降至与慢化剂的热运动相应的范围，即中子与慢化剂达到热平衡；中子仍在运动，并未停止。 / Eventually, neutron energies correspond to thermal motion in the moderator: the neutrons are in thermal equilibrium with it. They are still moving.

读图 / Reading： 蓝色路径表示中子不断被散射，粉色原子核获得反冲动能。图中箭头长度只定性表示速率逐渐降低。 / The blue path shows repeated neutron scattering; the pink nuclei receive recoil energy. Arrow lengths indicate the qualitative reduction in speed.

C. 合适材料需要什么性质？ / What properties should a moderator have?
性质 / Property 物理解释 / Physical explanation
原子核质量较小，与中子质量较接近 / Low-mass nuclei, with mass relatively close to neutron mass. 碰撞中能够转移较大比例的中子动能，减速更有效。比较的是原子核质量，不能写成“水原子核的质量”或只说“原子质量相同”。 / Such collisions can transfer a substantial fraction of neutron kinetic energy. Compare nuclear masses; do not refer to a “water nucleus” or merely to the mass of an atom.
中子吸收率低 / Low neutron absorption. 尽量保留中子，使其减速后可继续诱发裂变。 / Preserve neutrons so that they can cause further fission after being slowed.
化学稳定或热稳定 / Chemical or thermal stability. 能够在反应堆的工作条件下持续发挥作用。 / Remain suitable under reactor operating conditions.
能给出合适实例 / Give a suitable example. 常见实例为水（水中的氢核）、重水（氘核）或石墨（碳核）。 / Common examples are water (hydrogen nuclei), heavy water (deuterium nuclei), or graphite (carbon nuclei).
与控制棒、冷却剂区分 / Distinguish moderator, control rods and coolant
部件 / Component 主要作用 / Main function 性质与实例 / Properties and examples
慢化剂 / Moderator 通过碰撞降低中子动能 / Reduce neutron kinetic energy through collisions. 轻原子核、低中子吸收；水、重水、石墨 / Light nuclei, low neutron absorption; water, heavy water, graphite.
控制棒 / Control rods 吸收中子以调节裂变率 / Absorb neutrons to regulate fission rate. 中子吸收能力强；硼或镉 / Strong neutron absorption; boron or cadmium.
冷却剂 / Coolant 将堆芯释放的热量带走 / Carry thermal energy away from the core. 合适的传热、热容与稳定性；水或合适气体 / Suitable heat transfer, heat capacity and stability; water or a suitable gas.

同一种材料可以承担不止一种作用，例如水可同时用于慢化和冷却，但解释时必须分别说明“降低中子动能”和“传递热能”。 / One material may have more than one role: water may moderate and cool, but distinguish slowing neutrons from transferring thermal energy.

补充理解 / Further explanation： 核质量越接近中子质量，单次弹性碰撞通常能转移越大比例的动能。石墨中的碳核仍属较轻核，但比氢核重，通常需要更多次碰撞来达到热运动速率；无需声称所有慢化剂原子核都与中子等质量。 / A closer nuclear-mass match allows a larger fractional energy transfer in a collision. Carbon nuclei in graphite are light compared with heavy nuclei but heavier than hydrogen nuclei, so more collisions are generally needed to thermalise neutrons.

六分论述的简单英文样例 / Simple English model answer for six marks

Fission releases fast neutrons with high kinetic energy. Fast neutrons are less likely to cause further fission in U-235. Thermal neutrons are more likely to be absorbed by U-235. This forms excited U-236, which can undergo fission.

Fast neutrons collide with and are scattered by nuclei in the moderator. They transfer kinetic energy to these nuclei in many collisions. The neutrons slow to thermal speeds and reach thermal equilibrium with the moderator.

The moderator should have low-mass nuclei so that much kinetic energy can be transferred in collisions. It should absorb few neutrons and be chemically or thermally stable. Graphite is a suitable moderator.

反应堆部件的不同作用 / Reactor component functions

读图 / Reading： 减速剂使快中子减速，控制棒吸收中子，冷却剂带走热量；三者作用不能互换。

The moderator slows neutrons, control rods absorb them, and coolant removes heat; these are distinct functions.

2.3 公式与物理规律 / Formulae and physical laws

\[\Large\displaystyle v_n=\frac{m-M}{m+M}u\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(v_{n}\) 中子碰撞后的有符号速度 / signed final neutron velocity \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)
\(m\) 中子质量 / neutron mass \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(M\) 靶核质量 / target-nucleus mass \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(u\) 中子初速度 / initial neutron velocity \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

中子与静止靶核发生一维弹性碰撞时，由动量和动能守恒得到中子的末速度。

Momentum and kinetic-energy conservation give the neutron’s final velocity in a one-dimensional elastic collision with a stationary nucleus.

2.4 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

弹性碰撞慢化模型 / Elastic-collision moderation

中子质量 \(m\) 以速率 \(u\) 正碰初始静止、质量 \(M\) 的核，若弹性碰撞，动量和动能同时守恒，\(v_n=(m-M)u/(m+M)\)。单次最大能量转移比例为 \(4mM/(m+M)^2\)。

For a head-on elastic collision with a stationary nucleus, conserve both momentum and kinetic energy. Energy transfer is greatest when target and neutron masses are similar.

质量接近中子的轻核可有效减速；真实碰撞方向随机，通常需多次碰撞。这个模型来自U1弹性碰撞的应用，不把所有碰撞都假定成正碰。

Light nuclei close to neutron mass are effective moderators. Real collisions have varying angles and normally require repeated scattering; the head-on formula is a limiting model.

Applications / 应用与实验

分别解释各部件，并按慢化、吸收、移热作用说明材料选择；慢化结合 U1 弹性碰撞模型。

Explain reactor components separately and justify material choices using moderation, absorption or heat removal. Apply the U1 elastic-collision model to moderation.

Common errors / 易错点

慢化剂减慢中子，控制棒吸收中子，两作用不能混同。

A moderator slows neutrons; a control rod absorbs them. These are different functions even when one material can perform several roles.

3. 核反应的能量与功率 / Nuclear energy and power

3.1 公式与物理规律 / Formulae and physical laws

\[\Large\displaystyle E_{\mathrm{released}}=\Delta mc^2\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(E_{\mathrm{released}}\) 能量 / energy \(\mathrm{J}\)
\(\Delta m\) 质量亏损 / mass defect \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(c\) 真空光速 / speed of light in vacuum \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

核反应放出的能量对应反应前总静质量减反应后总静质量。

Energy released in a nuclear reaction corresponds to initial total rest mass minus final total rest mass.

适用条件 / Conditions

比较同一系统，统一核质量或原子质量的使用方式。

Compare the same system and use nuclear or atomic masses consistently.

\[\Large\displaystyle P=R_fE_f\]

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

裂变功率由每秒裂变次数乘每次裂变释放能量得到。

考点精讲：慢化剂论述的三个评分板块 / Three marking areas for a moderator response

① 为什么要慢化：裂变中子动能高，引发后继铀-235裂变的概率较低，热中子更易被吸收形成不稳定的铀-236。② 如何慢化：快中子与慢化剂原子核多次散射碰撞，转移动能，降至热运动速率并达到热平衡。③ 材料条件及实例：核质量较小、低中子吸收、化学及热稳定；水中的氢核质量接近中子，利于能量转移。不要把整个水分子的质量说成接近中子。

Explain why moderation is needed, how it occurs, and suitable properties with an example. Fission neutrons are fast; thermal neutrons are more likely to be absorbed by U-235 to form unstable U-236. Repeated scattering transfers kinetic energy until thermal equilibrium. A suitable moderator has low-mass nuclei, low neutron absorption and chemical and thermal stability. Hydrogen nuclei in water, not whole water molecules, have masses close to a neutron.

考点精讲：控制棒、冷却剂与慢化剂分工 / Distinguish reactor component functions

控制棒通过吸收中子改变后继裂变率；慢化剂通过碰撞降低中子动能；冷却剂把热从堆芯带走。稳定链式反应要求平均每次裂变产生的中子中有一个继续引发裂变，不是每次裂变只产生一个中子。部件材料的性质应与其具体任务对应。

Control rods absorb neutrons to control subsequent fission. A moderator slows neutrons by collisions. Coolant transfers heat out of the core. A steady chain reaction requires one neutron per fission, on average, to cause a further fission; it does not mean only one neutron is emitted. Link material properties to component functions.

3.12.3 方法 1 · 慢化、控制与临界条件 / Moderation, control and criticality

  1. 中子被燃料核吸收 → 形成激发复合核 → 裂变产出能量和多个快中子 → 部分中子再引发裂变。稳定功率要求平均每次裂变只有一个中子引发下一次裂变；不是每次只产生一个中子。 / Distinguish neutrons produced from neutrons causing further fission.
  2. 慢化剂通过多次碰撞降低中子动能，使热中子更容易被U-235吸收并引发裂变。材料要低吸收、核质量较小；例：水、重水、石墨。 / Link the material properties to the collision and absorption roles.
  3. 控制棒吸收中子调节反应率，如硼或镉；插深减少反应率，紧急停堆插到底。冷却剂负责传热，不等于慢化或控制棒作用。 / Keep the functions separate.
  4. 每碰撞剩余比例 \(f\) 时，\(K_n=K_0f^n\)，\(n=\ln(K_f/K_0)/\ln f\)；“再三次”应从首次碰撞后的能量继续算。 / Count the stated number of additional collisions.
  5. 临界质量随形状、富集度、反射及吸收条件改变；球形表面积/体积较小，可减少泄漏。更多中子逸出会减少后继裂变，不改变单次裂变产生数。 / State why a geometric change affects the chain reaction.
