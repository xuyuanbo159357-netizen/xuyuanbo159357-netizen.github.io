3.12.3 诱发裂变、链式反应与反应堆 / Induced fission

Concept / 概念

Definition / 定义

↗ nuclear fission — 核裂变

Definition / 定义

English: The splitting of a heavy nucleus into two smaller nuclei, usually with neutron emission and energy release.

中文： 重核分裂成两个较小核，通常同时放出中子和能量。

↗ chain reaction — 链式反应

Definition / 定义

English: A sequence in which neutrons released by fission cause further fissions.

中文： 裂变释放的中子引起更多裂变的连续过程。

Definition / 定义

English: The minimum amount of fissile material needed to sustain a chain reaction under the specified conditions.

中文： 给定条件下维持链式反应所需的可裂变物质最小质量。

Definition / 定义

English: A neutron with kinetic energy comparable to the random thermal energies of its surroundings.

Formula / 公式与单位

裂变方程满足核子数与电荷守恒；总功率由每次裂变能量与裂变率决定。

Balance total nucleon number and charge in fission equations; reaction energy \(=\Delta mc^2\); thermal power \(=(\text{fissions per second})(\text{energy per fission})\).

Conditions / 条件与范围

采用热中子反应堆模型；临界性与中子损失、材料、形状和环境有关。

Use a thermal-neutron reactor model. Criticality depends on neutron losses, material, geometry and surrounding conditions, not a universal mass value.

Explanation / 解释

热中子裂变与临界条件 / Thermal fission and criticality

U-235等可裂变核吸收热中子后形成激发复合核，分裂成较轻碎片并放出中子和能量；放出的中子再引发裂变，形成链式反应。受控稳态平均每次裂变有效引发一次后续裂变。

A fissile nucleus captures a thermal neutron, forming an excited nucleus that splits and releases neutrons and energy. A controlled steady chain has on average one effective successor fission per fission.

临界质量是在给定形状、纯度和环境下维持链式反应所需的最小质量。中子泄漏受表面积影响，产生数随体积或质量增加；不能把课件某个千克值当作各种装置的通用常量。

Critical mass depends on geometry, composition and surroundings. Neutron production scales with volume while escape is linked to surface area; there is no universal kilogram value for all assemblies.

三个部件与选材 / Components and material choice

慢化剂通过碰撞减慢快中子，同时应少吸收中子；可用石墨或水等适当材料。控制棒吸收中子调节反应率，可用硼或镉；插入更深通常降低裂变率。冷却剂带走堆芯热，需良好传热、合适热容、稳定且与结构相容，可用水或气体。

A moderator slows neutrons with low unwanted absorption. Control rods absorb neutrons to regulate fission. A coolant removes heat and needs suitable thermal, chemical and nuclear properties. Examples are graphite or water, boron or cadmium, and water or gas respectively.

比较部件时按“作用→所需性质→材料”作答；慢化剂不是以吸收中子来慢化，控制棒不是冷却装置。无需背具体反应堆类型和结构细节。

Explain each component through function, desired property and material. Moderation is not neutron absorption, and control rods do not provide cooling. Particular reactor designs are excluded.

弹性碰撞慢化模型 / Elastic-collision moderation

中子质量 \(m\) 以速率 \(u\) 正碰初始静止、质量 \(M\) 的核，若弹性碰撞，动量和动能同时守恒，\(v_n=(m-M)u/(m+M)\)。单次最大能量转移比例为 \(4mM/(m+M)^2\)。

For a head-on elastic collision with a stationary nucleus, conserve both momentum and kinetic energy. Energy transfer is greatest when target and neutron masses are similar.

质量接近中子的轻核可有效减速；真实碰撞方向随机，通常需多次碰撞。这个模型来自U1弹性碰撞的应用，不把所有碰撞都假定成正碰。

Light nuclei close to neutron mass are effective moderators. Real collisions have varying angles and normally require repeated scattering; the head-on formula is a limiting model.

Applications / 应用与实验

分别解释各部件，并按慢化、吸收、移热作用说明材料选择；慢化结合 U1 弹性碰撞模型。

Explain reactor components separately and justify material choices using moderation, absorption or heat removal. Apply the U1 elastic-collision model to moderation.

Common errors / 易错点

慢化剂减慢中子，控制棒吸收中子，两作用不能混同。

A moderator slows neutrons; a control rod absorbs them. These are different functions even when one material can perform several roles.

3.12.3 方法 1 · 慢化、控制与临界条件 / Moderation, control and criticality

  1. 中子被燃料核吸收 → 形成激发复合核 → 裂变产出能量和多个快中子 → 部分中子再引发裂变。稳定功率要求平均每次裂变只有一个中子引发下一次裂变；不是每次只产生一个中子。 / Distinguish neutrons produced from neutrons causing further fission.
  2. 慢化剂通过多次碰撞降低中子动能，使热中子更容易被U-235吸收并引发裂变。材料要低吸收、核质量较小；例：水、重水、石墨。 / Link the material properties to the collision and absorption roles.
  3. 控制棒吸收中子调节反应率，如硼或镉；插深减少反应率，紧急停堆插到底。冷却剂负责传热，不等于慢化或控制棒作用。 / Keep the functions separate.
  4. 每碰撞剩余比例 \(f\) 时，\(K_n=K_0f^n\)，\(n=\ln(K_f/K_0)/\ln f\)；“再三次”应从首次碰撞后的能量继续算。 / Count the stated number of additional collisions.
  5. 临界质量随形状、富集度、反射及吸收条件改变；球形表面积/体积较小，可减少泄漏。更多中子逸出会减少后继裂变，不改变单次裂变产生数。 / State why a geometric change affects the chain reaction.
