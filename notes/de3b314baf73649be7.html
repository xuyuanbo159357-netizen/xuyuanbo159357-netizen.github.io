3.10.4 电磁感应、楞次定律与发电 / Electromagnetic induction

考纲 / Specification： 3.10.4　｜　本单元知识谱系

Concept / 概念

  • electromagnetic induction — 电磁感应

Definition / 定义

↗ electromagnetic induction — 电磁感应

Definition / 定义

English: The production of an emf when the magnetic flux linkage of a circuit changes.

中文： 电路磁通链变化时产生电动势的现象。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

↗ eddy current — 涡流

Definition / 定义

English: A circulating current induced within the bulk of a conductor by changing magnetic flux.

中文： 磁通变化在导体内部诱导出的环形电流。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

定义按考纲与指定教材概括；本页未列出可直接对应的定义题评分条目。 Definitions are summarised from the specification and textbook; no directly matched definition-item citation is claimed here.

Formula / 公式与单位

Faraday's law / 法拉第电磁感应定律

Law statement / 定律表述

English: The magnitude of the induced emf is proportional to the rate of change of flux linkage.

中文： 感应电动势的大小与磁通链的变化率成正比。

Formula / 公式：平均感应电动势的大小 / Magnitude of the average induced emf

\[\Large\displaystyle |\bar\varepsilon|=\left|\frac{\Delta(N\Phi)}{\Delta t}\right|\]
符号 / Symbol 物理量 / Quantity 单位 / Unit
\(\bar\varepsilon\) 平均感应电动势 / average induced emf \(\mathrm{V}\)
\(N\Phi\) 磁通链 / flux linkage \(\mathrm{Wb\,turn}\)（匝数无量纲 / turns are dimensionless）
\(\Delta t\) 时间间隔 / time interval \(\mathrm{s}\)

条件与读图 / Conditions and graphs： 差分式给出一段时间内平均电动势的大小；瞬时电动势的大小取磁通链—时间图在该时刻的斜率绝对值。 / The finite-difference expression gives the magnitude of the average emf; the instantaneous magnitude is the magnitude of the gradient of a flux-linkage–time graph.

评分依据 / Mark-scheme basis： PH03 · 202406 · Q06.2

Lenz's law / 楞次定律

Law statement / 定律表述

English: The direction of the induced emf is such as to oppose the change in flux linkage that produces it.

中文： 感应电动势的方向总是反抗引起它的磁通链变化。

Formula / 公式：含方向约定的瞬时感应电动势 / Instantaneous induced emf with a consistent orientation

\[\Large\displaystyle \varepsilon=-\frac{d(N\Phi)}{dt}\]
符号 / Symbol 物理量 / Quantity 单位 / Unit
\(\varepsilon\) 瞬时感应电动势 / instantaneous induced emf \(\mathrm{V}\)
\(N\Phi\) 磁通链 / flux linkage \(\mathrm{Wb\,turn}\)
\(t\) 时间 / time \(\mathrm{s}\)

解释 / Explanation： 在闭合回路中，感应电流产生的磁场反抗原磁通链的变化。反抗的是变化，不是始终与原磁场反向。负号表示这一判向规则，不能理解为电动势的数值始终小于零。 / In a closed circuit, the induced current produces a magnetic field that opposes the change in flux linkage. The minus sign expresses this directional rule; it does not mean that the numerical emf is always negative.

评分依据 / Mark-scheme basis： PH03 · 202201 · Q06.2

符号统一 / Notation： 本节 \(\Phi\) 表示每匝磁通量，\(N\Phi\)（也可记作 \(\Psi\)）表示磁通链。若题目直接用 \(\Phi\) 表示磁通链，应按题设使用，避免重复乘以匝数 \(N\)。 / Here \(\Phi\) is flux per turn and \(N\Phi=\Psi\) is flux linkage. If a question uses \(\Phi\) for linkage itself, do not multiply by \(N\) again.

运动导体与转动线圈 / Moving conductor and rotating coil

直导体 / Straight conductor

\[\Large\displaystyle \varepsilon=BLv\]

转动线圈 / Rotating coil

\[\Large\displaystyle \varepsilon=NBA\omega\sin\omega t\]

Conditions / 适用条件： \(B\)、\(L\)、\(v\) 互相垂直时使用 \(BLv\)；转动线圈取匀强磁场、恒定角速率，并令 \(t=0\) 时磁通链最大。 / The \(BLv\) expression requires mutually perpendicular field, length and velocity. The rotating coil is in a uniform field at constant angular speed, with maximum linkage at \(t=0\).

Conditions / 条件与范围

法拉第定律给出感应电动势的大小；楞次定律给出反抗磁通链变化的方向。导体棒公式需满足相应的垂直几何关系。

Faraday's law gives the magnitude of the induced emf; Lenz's law gives the direction that opposes the change in flux linkage. The rod formula requires the stated perpendicular geometry.

Explanation / 解释

弗莱明右手定则 / Fleming's right-hand rule

适用情境 / Use： 直导体相对磁场运动并切割磁场时，判断感应电动势及闭合回路中感应传统电流的方向：机械输入 → 电能。 / For a conductor moving across a magnetic field, find the induced emf polarity and, in a closed circuit, the conventional induced-current direction: mechanical input → electrical output.

右手三指互相垂直。 / Hold the three fingers of the right hand mutually perpendicular.

右手手指 / Right-hand finger 对应方向 / Direction
食指 / First finger 磁场 \(B\) / magnetic field
拇指 / Thumb 导体相对磁场的运动 \(v\)，不是导体所受的磁力 / conductor motion relative to the field, not magnetic force
中指 / Second finger 感应传统电流 \(I\)，即正电荷在导体中被推动的方向 / conventional induced current, the direction positive charges are driven along the conductor

开路 / Open circuit： 正、负电荷分离，建立电势差；导体内持续电流不一定存在。中指所指的一端积累正电荷，可据此判断电动势极性。 / Charge separation establishes a potential difference. An open circuit need not carry a sustained current; the end towards which positive charges are driven becomes positive.

选用定则 / Choosing a rule： 导线切割磁场 → 右手发电机定则；线圈磁通改变 → 楞次定律判感应场，再用右手握拳定则判线圈电流；已知电流求磁力 → 左手定则。 / Moving conductor → generator rule; changing flux in a coil → Lenz's law then the grip rule; given current to find force → left-hand rule.

考纲定位 / Scope： 3.10.1 明列左手定则；右手发电机定则作为 3.10.4 运动导体电磁感应的判向方法，见指定教材印刷 p.373（PDF p.389）。 / Section 3.10.1 names the left-hand rule; the generator rule supports the moving-conductor application in 3.10.4, as explained on textbook printed p.373 (PDF p.389).

左手定则：磁场 + 电流 → 受力 / Motor rule: field + current → force

图示：右手：运动、磁场、感应电流 / Right-hand generator rule

读图 / Reading： 右手拇指指导体运动 v，食指指 B，中指指感应传统电流 I。开路时用正电荷受推方向判电动势极性，不意味着一定有持续电流。

Right thumb: conductor motion v; first finger: B; second finger: induced conventional I. In an open circuit this gives polarity, not necessarily sustained current.

感应条件、法拉第与楞次 / Induction and laws

磁铁移近或移远线圈、线圈转动或面积改变、附近电流变化均可能改变磁通链并产生电动势；相对运动本身并非充分条件。开路线圈可有感应电动势，有闭合导电路径才有持续感应电流。

Changing field, area or orientation can change linkage and induce emf. Relative motion alone is insufficient. An open circuit can have an induced emf; current requires a conducting closed path.

法拉第定律说明电动势大小取决于磁通链的变化率；平均值使用一段时间内的变化量，瞬时值使用该时刻的斜率。楞次定律说明感应电动势反抗引起它的磁通链变化，按一致方向写作 \(\varepsilon=-d(N\Phi)/dt\)。

Faraday's law relates the magnitude of the induced emf to the rate of change of flux linkage. Lenz's law gives the direction opposing the change that produces the emf.

判断方向：先说外磁通在增加还是减少→感应场要反抗该变化→用右手握拳定则求电流方向。反抗的是“变化”，不能笼统写成总与原磁场反向；这与能量守恒一致。

Determine the change in external flux, choose the opposing induced field, then use the grip rule for current. The induced field opposes the change, not necessarily the original field.

图示：磁铁与线圈的相对运动 / Relative motion of magnet and coil

读图 / Reading： 只有线圈的磁通链变化时才有感应电动势；相对运动停止、磁通保持恒定时，感应效应不持续。

Induced emf requires changing flux linkage; stationary constant flux does not sustain it.

直导体与涡流 / Moving conductor and eddy currents

导体有效长度、速度和磁场相互垂直时 \(\varepsilon=BLv\)。闭合回路中 \(I=\varepsilon/R\)，磁阻力 \(F=BIL\)，外力维持匀速时 \(Fv=I\varepsilon=I^2R\)；机械能转为电能及内能。

For mutually perpendicular length, velocity and field, motional emf is \(BLv\). In a closed loop the magnetic drag and electrical dissipation account for the required mechanical input.

大片导体中变化磁通可产生闭合涡流，造成发热和反抗运动的磁作用。用于电磁阻尼、制动或加热；铁芯叠片用绝缘层截断大回路、提高回路电阻，从而减小涡流损耗。

Changing flux in bulk conductors produces eddy currents, heating and opposing magnetic effects. Insulated laminations interrupt large loops and reduce losses.

转动线圈的相位和幅值 / Rotating coil phase and amplitude

若 \(\Psi=NBA\cos\omega t\)，则按一致方向 \(\varepsilon=NBA\omega\sin\omega t\)，峰值 \(\varepsilon_0=NBA\omega\)。磁通链最大或最小时斜率为零、电动势为零；磁通链过零时变化率最大、电动势大小最大。

For cosine linkage the induced emf is sinusoidal. Emf is zero at linkage extrema and has maximum magnitude at linkage zero crossings.

增加匝数、面积、磁场或角速率可提高峰值；提高角速率也提高频率。导线运动方向、受力方向和感应电流方向分别判断，不把左手与右手定则混用。

More turns, larger area, stronger field or greater angular speed increases peak emf. Greater angular speed also increases frequency.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.10.4

图示：线圈位置与电动势相位 / Coil position and emf phase

读图 / Reading： 线圈磁通链最大时变化率为零，电动势为零；磁通链过零时变化最快，电动势大小最大。

Maximum linkage has zero rate of change and zero emf; linkage passing through zero has the greatest rate and maximum emf magnitude.

U3 Flux linkage emf

匀速转动线圈的磁通链和电动势；电动势为磁通链的负变化率。 Uniformly rotating coil.

图示：涡流的闭合路径 / Closed eddy-current paths

读图 / Reading： 变化的磁场在金属内部产生闭合电流，造成发热；感应场反抗磁通变化，不是始终反向于原磁场。

Changing magnetic flux induces closed currents in the metal and heating; the induced field opposes the change, not always the original field.

Applications / 应用与实验

用磁通链图负斜率求电动势，按楞次定律判断极性、电流并解释能量守恒。

Use the negative gradient of a flux-linkage–time graph; use Lenz’s law to identify the induced pole/current and explain energy conservation.

Common errors / 易错点

恒定非零磁通不产生感应电动势；感应场反抗的是变化，不一定与原磁场反向。

A non-zero constant flux does not induce emf. The induced field opposes the change, not necessarily the original field itself.

3.10.4 方法 1 · 感应的解释链与波形 / Explaining induction and its waveform

完整解释链 / Causal chain: 题中变化 → 磁通链变化 → 法拉第定律给大小 → 楞次定律给方向 → 若回路闭合才有感应电流。 / State the change, linkage, rate, opposing effect and whether a current can flow.

  • 楞次定律反抗的是磁通变化，不总是反抗原磁场；恒定磁通链没有持续感应。 / The induced field may support a decreasing applied field.
  • 磁体加速穿过线圈：后峰通常更高、更窄；进入、离开时磁通变化方向相反，电动势变号。峰之间零电动势不代表零磁通。 / Connect height to rate and width to transit time.
  • 整线圈平移且始终处于均匀磁场，净磁通不变，不能把每条边的 \(Blv\) 简单相加。单棒开路仍可有端电动势，但无持续回路电流。 / Distinguish a rod emf from a net loop emf.
  • 涡流可在非磁性导体中产生；割缝破坏主要闭合电流路径，使制动显著减弱。阻尼能量转为焦耳热，需由物体机械能或外界驱动补给。 / Magnetism of the material is not required for eddy currents.
  • 突然断电产生大电动势的理由是 \(\Delta(N\Phi)/\Delta t\) 大；分别说明大匝数、大初始磁通、短切换时间。 / Link each design feature to the rate equation.
