角动量与转动能量 / Angular momentum and torque

moment of inertia → angular momentum → torque → flywheel → 角动量与转动能量

推理顺序 / Reasoning

先选转轴，再按条件用转动动力学或角动量守恒；转动惯量变化时考虑内做功。

Choose the rotation axis; use constant-acceleration dynamics or zero-external-torque conservation as appropriate; account for internal work when I changes.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.13.1

3.13.1 转动运动学、转动惯量与角动量

运动学式要求恒角加速度；简单转矩方程要求固定轴且转动惯量恒定；功公式要求恒转矩；扩展物体转动惯量公式需要时会给出。

Uniform angular-acceleration equations require constant \(\alpha\). The simple \(\tau=I\alpha\) equation assumes constant I about the fixed axis; work expression requires constant torque. Moment-of-inertia formulae for extended objects will be given when needed.

恒角加速度运动学与直线匀加速对应。\n\(I=\sum m_ir_i^2\); \(\tau=Fr_\perp=I\alpha\); \(L=I\omega\); \(E_{k,\mathrm{rot}}=\tfrac12I\omega^2\); \(W=\tau\theta\); \(P=\tau\omega\).||单位分别为 kg m\(^2\)、N m、kg m\(^2\) s\(^{-1}\)；角度须用弧度。

\(\omega=\omega_0+\alpha t\); \(\theta=\tfrac12(\omega_0+\omega)t=\omega_0t+\tfrac12\alpha t^2\); \(\omega^2=\omega_0^2+2\alpha\theta\).
