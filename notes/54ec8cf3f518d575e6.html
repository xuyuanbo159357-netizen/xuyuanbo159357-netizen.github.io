3.5.4 横波、纵波、偏振与超声 / Longitudinal and transverse waves

Definition / 定义

↗ transverse wave — 横波

Definition / 定义

English: A wave in which oscillations are perpendicular to the direction of energy propagation.

中文： 振动方向垂直于能量传播方向的波。

Formula / 公式与单位

往返超声时间对应的深度须除以二。

Echo distance \(d=ct/2\) for a pulse travelling to an interface and back.

Conditions / 条件与范围

不要求马吕斯定律；所有电磁波在真空中速率相同，电场和磁场的振动为横向。

Malus’s law is not required. All electromagnetic waves have the same speed in vacuum; their electric and magnetic fields oscillate transversely.

Explanation / 解释

横波、纵波与电磁波 / Transverse, longitudinal and EM waves

横波：粒子位移或场振动垂直于能量传播方向，如弦波、电磁波；纵波：粒子位移平行于能量传播方向，如空气中的声波，表现为疏部和密部。

Transverse oscillations are perpendicular to energy propagation, as on strings and in EM waves. Longitudinal oscillations are parallel, as in sound in air, with compressions and rarefactions.

电磁波的电场、磁场振动互相垂直，并都垂直于传播方向；各种电磁波在真空中速率相同，\(c\approx3.00\times10^8\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)，在介质中可不同。

Electric and magnetic fields in an EM wave oscillate mutually perpendicular and perpendicular to propagation. All EM waves have the same speed in vacuum; speeds in materials can differ.

偏振证据与天线 / Polarisation and aerials

平面偏振把横波的振动限制在一个方向（相应振动平面包含传播方向）。非偏振光通过偏振片后成为平面偏振光；第二片与第一片透振方向平行时最亮、垂直时最暗。

Plane polarisation restricts transverse oscillations to one direction. Unpolarised light becomes polarised after a polariser; a second aligned polariser gives maximum transmission and a crossed one gives minimum transmission.

纵波只有沿传播方向的振动，不能通过这种方式偏振，因此偏振现象是横波证据。发射、接收天线应方向匹配，以有效发射和接收同一偏振的无线电波。只要求定性分析，不要求马吕斯定律。

Longitudinal waves cannot be polarised in this way. Polarisation therefore demonstrates transverse behaviour. Transmitting and receiving aerials should be aligned. Malus's law is not required.

超声脉冲回波 / Ultrasound pulse echoes

超声频率高于人耳听觉上限（约20 kHz）。探头发出短脉冲，组织界面反射部分声能，探头接收回波；深度 \(d=ct/2\)，\(t\) 是往返时间。扫描探头可确定组织边界位置。

Ultrasound is above the human hearing range, about 20 kHz. Short pulses reflect partly at tissue boundaries. Depth is \(ct/2\) because the measured delay is a round trip; scanning locates boundaries.

脉冲需足够短以分辨相近界面，间隔需足够长以等回波返回；凝胶排除皮肤与探头间的空气、改善声能耦合。医学超声使用非电离声波。

Short pulses resolve nearby boundaries; adequate intervals allow echoes to return. Gel removes air gaps and improves coupling. Medical ultrasound uses non-ionising sound waves.

图示：横波的两种方向 / Transverse-wave directions

读图 / Reading： 粒子上下振动，波形和能量向右传播；粒子没有随波峰不断向右走。

Particles oscillate vertically while the wave and energy propagate rightwards; particles do not travel continuously with a crest.

图示：纵波的两种方向 / Longitudinal-wave directions

读图 / Reading： 粒子沿传播方向前后振动；相邻疏部或相邻密部间距为一个波长。

Particles oscillate parallel to propagation; adjacent compressions or adjacent rarefactions are one wavelength apart.

图示：偏振证明横波性质 / Polarisation and transverse waves

读图 / Reading： 第一片筛选振动方向，第二片与第一片成直角时理想透射为零；纵波没有可这样筛选的横向振动方向。

The first filter selects a transverse vibration direction; an ideal crossed second filter blocks it. Longitudinal waves cannot be filtered this way.

Applications / 应用与实验

偏振片降低反射眩光，发射与接收天线需要匹配方向；用反射与脉冲计时解释超声成像。

Polaroid filters reduce reflected glare. Transmitting and receiving aerials need matching orientations. Explain ultrasound imaging using reflection and pulse timing.

Common errors / 易错点

声波振动的是介质粒子，电磁波振动的是场；电磁波不需要介质。

Describe the oscillating quantity: medium particles for sound, fields for electromagnetic waves. Electromagnetic waves do not require a material medium.

3.5.4 方法 1 · 偏振、金属栅与天线 / Polarisation, grilles and aerials

补充知识 / Knowledge. 电磁波的电场、磁场彼此垂直且均垂直传播方向，二者同相。 / Electric and magnetic fields are mutually perpendicular, transverse and in phase.

  • 旋转检偏器：线偏振光的强度连续变化，一周两次极大、两次极小；非偏振光通过理想检偏器时强度不随角度改变。当前考纲不要求额外记忆马吕斯定律。 / Describe the observed variation before inferring polarisation.
  • 金属栅中电场平行金属杆会驱动电子运动，波被衰减；透过的电场分量垂直金属杆。接收天线应沿接收电场方向。 / Distinguish the transmitting axis of a grille from the axis of an aerial.
  • 移动电话方向会变化，说明为何不能只考虑单一固定接收方向。 / Connect polarisation to the use case.

对应真题 / Examples： PH02 · 201906 · Q03；PH02 · 202301 · Q04；PH02 · 202501 · Q03；PH02 · 202506 · Q05。

3.5.4 方法 2 · 超声脉冲与多次回声 / Ultrasound pulses and multiple echoes

  1. 标明声波的完整路径；单次往返回声距离 \(d=c\Delta t/2\)。 / Draw the full outward and return path.
  2. 涂层与基底问题优先用相邻回声的时间差，抵消共有路径的传播时间；多次间隔可平均。 / Use echo differences to remove a common delay.
  3. 解释成像时写“界面反射 → 测往返时间 → 由波速定位”；比较X射线时可指出超声不电离。 / Link reflection, timing and location.
  4. 应用题按题境给出具体用途，例如碎石或清洗；不要把所有应用都写成成像。 / Give a relevant concrete application.

对应真题 / Examples： PH02 · 201901 · Q11；PH02 · 202401 · Q07；PH02 · 202306 · Q05；PH02 · 201801 · Q02。
