简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

Atomic energy levels are discrete, so only particular energy gaps are possible. Electrons de-excite and emit photons with energies equal to these gaps. Each element has its own energy levels, giving its characteristic wavelengths. Inner-shell gaps are large enough to produce X-rays.
