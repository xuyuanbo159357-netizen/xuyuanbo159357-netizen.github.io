简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

The resultant field is the vector sum of the fields of A and B. The fields point in opposite directions between them. At X they are equal and cancel; crossing X changes the sign of the resultant field. X is closer to B because B has the smaller mass and its weaker field must be balanced at a smaller distance.
