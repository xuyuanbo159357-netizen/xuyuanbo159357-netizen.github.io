简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

SHM requires the resultant force to be proportional to displacement and opposite to it. The cloth adds resistance while the needle passes through it, so the resultant force no longer follows this relationship. If that resistance is significant, the motion is not exactly SHM.
