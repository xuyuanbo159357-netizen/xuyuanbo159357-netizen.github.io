RC指数变化与对数图 / Charge and discharge

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

charge → discharge → time constant → log graph → RC指数变化与对数图

推理顺序 / Reasoning

选充电或放电式，用有效串联电阻；取自然对数线性化，并解释斜率与电流面积。

Choose charging or discharging equations; use effective series resistance; linearise discharge with natural logarithms; interpret gradient and current area.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.9.1

3.9.1 电容充放电与时间常量

电阻应恒定，电容近似理想；有效串联电阻包含相关内阻；有符号电流需先定正方向。

Use a constant resistor and ideal capacitor; include any relevant source/internal resistance in the effective series resistance. State the reference direction before giving a signed current.

充电电荷、电压上升，电流衰减；放电三者大小均衰减。\n\(t_{1/2}=RC\ln2\); \(\ln V=\ln V_0-t/(RC)\) for discharge.||半值时间不同于时间常量；对数直线斜率为负时间常量倒数。

\(\tau=RC\). Discharge: \(Q=Q_0e^{-t/RC}\), \(V=V_0e^{-t/RC}\), current magnitude \(|I|=(V_0/R)e^{-t/RC}\). Charge: \(Q=C\varepsilon(1-e^{-t/RC})\), \(V=\varepsilon(1-e^{-t/RC})\), \(I=(\varepsilon/R)e^{-t/RC}\).
