U4/07 常用物理模型

质量亏损与核能释放 / Mass defect and binding energy

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

mass defect → binding energy → fission → fusion → 质量亏损与核能释放

推理顺序 / Reasoning

配平粒子，用一致质量收支求能量，区分总结合能和平均结合能。

Balance particles; sum consistent initial and final masses; convert the mass decrease to energy; distinguish total binding energy from binding energy per nucleon.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.12.2

3.12.2 质能关系、质量亏损与结合能

核质量与核子质量配套；使用原子质量时须保证电子质量抵消；反应质量收支包括所有粒子。

Use nuclear masses with nucleon masses, or use consistent atomic masses so electron masses cancel. Include all reactants/products and free emitted particles.

原子质量单位乘光速平方才对应能量；MeV 转焦耳时还需乘百万。

\(\Delta E=\Delta mc^2\); \(E_b=\Delta mc^2\); \(\Delta m=Zm_p+(A-Z)m_n-m_{\mathrm{nucleus}}\); \(1\,u\,c^2=931.5\,\mathrm{MeV}\); reaction energy \((m_{\mathrm{initial}}-m_{\mathrm{final}})c^2\).

3.12.5

3.12.5 核聚变、等离子体与太阳氢循环

库仑势垒计算用给定接近距离估算，不是精确反应温度预测；太阳聚变限氢循环。

A classical Coulomb-barrier calculation is an estimate using a specified approach distance, not an exact operating-temperature prediction. The solar fusion cycle is limited to the hydrogen cycle.

常见燃料为氘与氚；太阳氢循环净结果含两个正电子和两个中微子。

Example: \({}^{2}_{1}\mathrm H+{}^{3}_{1}\mathrm H\rightarrow{}^{4}_{2}\mathrm{He}+{}^{1}_{0}\mathrm n\). Net solar hydrogen cycle: \(4p\rightarrow{}^{4}_{2}\mathrm{He}+2e^++2\nu_e+\text{energy}\). Estimate thermal kinetic energy from \(\overline E_k=\tfrac32k_BT\) and a given electrostatic barrier.
