Concept / 概念

refractive index — 折射率

Definition / 定义

English: The ratio of the speed of light in vacuum to its speed in the medium.

中文： 真空光速与介质中光速的比值。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式与单位

Formula / 公式

\[\Large\displaystyle n=\frac{c}{v}\]
