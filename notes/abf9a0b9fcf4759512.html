Concept / 概念

nuclear fission — 核裂变

Definition / 定义

English: The splitting of a heavy nucleus into two smaller nuclei, usually with neutron emission and energy release.

中文： 重核分裂成两个较小核，通常同时放出中子和能量。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

图示讲解 / Diagram explained

图示：裂变链式反应 / Fission chain reaction

读图 / Reading： 一次裂变释放的中子可能引发后续裂变；持续反应取决于下一代有效引发的裂变数，而不是每个中子都一定继续反应。

Released neutrons may trigger later fissions; sustained operation depends on effective next-generation fissions, not every neutron reacting.
