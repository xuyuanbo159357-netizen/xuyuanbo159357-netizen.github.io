1. 质量亏损与原子质量单位 / Mass defect and atomic mass unit

1.1 关键概念与定义 / Key concepts and definitions

atomic mass unit — 原子质量单位

Definition / 定义

English: One twelfth of the mass of a neutral carbon-12 atom.

中文： 一个中性碳十二原子质量的十二分之一。

定义评分依据 / Definition marking points： PH04 202606-Q13。原答案在题目下展开；此处按评分要点概括。 Summarised from the marking points; originals are folded below each question.

mass defect — 质量亏损

Definition / 定义

English: The difference between the total mass of the separate constituent nucleons and the mass of the nucleus they form.

中文： 组成核的独立核子总质量与该原子核质量的差。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式

\[\Large\displaystyle \Delta m=Zm_p+(A-Z)m_n-m_{\mathrm{nucleus}}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(\Delta m\) 质量亏损 / mass defect \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(Z\) 质子数、原子序数 / proton number, atomic number 无量纲 / dimensionless
\(m_{p}\) 质子质量 / proton mass \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(A\) 核子数、质量数 / nucleon number, mass number 无量纲 / dimensionless
\(m_{n}\) 中子质量 / neutron mass \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(m_{\mathrm{nucleus}}\) 原子核质量，不含电子 / nuclear mass excluding electrons \(\mathrm{kg}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

核质量亏损是分离核子的总质量减结合后原子核质量。

Nuclear mass defect is the total mass of separated nucleons minus the bound nucleus mass.

适用条件 / Conditions

统一使用核质量；若用原子质量须相容处理电子质量。

Use nuclear masses consistently, or account consistently for electrons when using atomic masses.

1.2 公式与物理规律 / Formulae and physical laws

\[\Large\displaystyle \Delta E=\Delta mc^2\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(\Delta E\) 能量的变化量 / change in energy \(\mathrm{J}\)
\(\Delta m\) 质量亏损 / mass defect \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(c\) 真空光速 / speed of light in vacuum \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

质能关系把能量变化与相应质量变化联系起来。

Mass–energy equivalence relates an energy change to the corresponding mass change.

\[\Large\displaystyle 1\,u\,c^2=931.5\,\mathrm{MeV}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(u\) 统一原子质量单位 / unified atomic mass unit \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(c\) 真空光速 / speed of light in vacuum \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

一个原子质量单位对应的静能约为 931.5 MeV，可用于核反应质量与能量换算。

One atomic mass unit corresponds to about 931.5 MeV of rest energy for nuclear mass–energy conversion.

1.3 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

Conditions / 条件与范围

核质量与核子质量配套；使用原子质量时须保证电子质量抵消；反应质量收支包括所有粒子。

Use nuclear masses with nucleon masses, or use consistent atomic masses so electron masses cancel. Include all reactants/products and free emitted particles.

质量亏损、结合能和单位 / Defect, binding and units

核质量小于组成它的自由核子质量和：\(\Delta m=Zm_p+(A-Z)m_n-m_{\rm nucleus}\)，结合能 \(E_b=\Delta mc^2\) 是将核完全分离为自由核子所需能量。平均每核子结合能为 \(E_b/A\)，不是移走某一个指定核子的分离能。

Binding energy is the energy needed to separate a nucleus completely into free nucleons. Mean binding energy per nucleon is total binding energy divided by nucleon number, not an individual nucleon's separation energy.

\(1\,u\) 为一个碳-12原子质量的十二分之一，\(1\,u\,c^2\approx931.5\,\mathrm{MeV}\)，等价 \(1\,u\approx931.5\,\mathrm{MeV}/c^2\)。\(u\) 是质量单位，不与能量单位直接等同。

The atomic mass unit is defined from a carbon-12 atom. Write the mass–energy conversion with \(c^2\) to maintain dimensional consistency.

若题给原子质量，须用一致电子数处理，必要时用氢原子质量代替质子质量；不要把原子质量和裸核质量任意混用。\(E=mc^2\) 适用于所有能量变化，不仅核反应。

Keep atomic and nuclear mass conventions consistent, accounting for electron masses where necessary. Mass–energy equivalence applies to all energy changes.

2. 结合能与平均核子结合能 / Binding energy and binding energy per nucleon

2.1 关键概念与定义 / Key concepts and definitions

binding energy — 结合能

Definition / 定义

English: The energy required to separate a nucleus completely into its constituent nucleons.

中文： 把原子核完全分离为各组成核子所需的能量。

定义评分依据 / Definition marking points： PH04 202601-Q04、PH04 202206-Q06。原答案在题目下展开；此处按评分要点概括。 Summarised from the marking points; originals are folded below each question.

Formula / 公式

\[\Large\displaystyle E_b=\Delta mc^2\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(E_{b}\) 结合能 / binding energy \(\mathrm{J}\)
\(\Delta m\) 质量亏损 / mass defect \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(c\) 真空光速 / speed of light in vacuum \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

核结合能是把原子核完全拆成分离核子所需的能量，与质量亏损等价。

Binding energy is the energy required to separate a nucleus into nucleons and is equivalent to its mass defect.

binding energy per nucleon — 平均核子结合能

Definition / 定义

English: The total binding energy of a nucleus divided by its nucleon number.

中文： 核总结合能除以核子数。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式

Conditions / 条件与说明： 真题也常用 MeV per nucleon；注意与总结合能区分。 / Mark schemes also use MeV per nucleon; distinguish it from total binding energy.

真题定义依据 / Mark-scheme checks： PH04 202201-Q07、PH04 202206-Q06、PH04 202601-Q04、PH04 202606-Q13。定义按评分要点概括；原答案可在对应题目下展开。 Definitions are summarised from the scoring points, with original answers below the linked questions.

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

平均每核子结合能比较不同大小原子核的结合程度。

Binding energy per nucleon compares the degree of binding in nuclei of different sizes.

读图 / Reading： 纵轴是每个核子的结合能，不是总结合能。反应后平均结合更强时可释放能量；求释放量仍要按反应中各核的总质量或总结合能计算。

2.3 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

Common errors / 易错点

平均结合能高表示结合更紧；产物结合更紧时放能；比较总能量前须乘核子数。

A high binding energy per nucleon means a more tightly bound nucleus; energy is released when products are more tightly bound. Multiply mean binding energy by nucleon number before comparing totals.

3. 核反应的能量收支 / Energy accounting in nuclear reactions

3.1 公式与物理规律 / Formulae and physical laws

\[\Large\displaystyle E_{\mathrm{released}}=(m_{\mathrm{initial}}-m_{\mathrm{final}})c^2\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(E_{\mathrm{released}}\) 能量 / energy \(\mathrm{J}\)
\(m_{\mathrm{initial}}\) 初始质量 / initial mass \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(m_{\mathrm{final}}\) 最终质量 / final mass \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(c\) 真空光速 / speed of light in vacuum \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

核反应放出的能量对应反应前总静质量减反应后总静质量。

Energy released in a nuclear reaction corresponds to initial total rest mass minus final total rest mass.

适用条件 / Conditions

比较同一系统，统一核质量或原子质量的使用方式。

Compare the same system and use nuclear or atomic masses consistently.

\[\Large\displaystyle Q=\left(\sum m_{\mathrm{initial}}-\sum m_{\mathrm{final}}\right)c^2\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(Q\) 核反应释放能量 / reaction energy release \(\mathrm{J}\)
\(m_{\mathrm{initial}}\) 各初态粒子静止质量 / initial-particle rest masses \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(m_{\mathrm{final}}\) 各末态粒子静止质量 / final-particle rest masses \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(c\) 真空光速 / speed of light in vacuum \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

核反应前后总静止质量的减少对应释放能量；若该差为负，则需要输入能量。

A decrease in total rest mass releases energy. A negative mass difference means energy must be supplied.

3.2 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

适用范围与条件 / Scope and conditions

Conditions / 适用条件： 质量式使用核质量，不把原子电子重复计入；释放能量由反应前后总质量差计算。MeV 转 J 时须乘 10⁶ 再乘元电荷数值。 / Use nuclear masses consistently and release energy from the total mass decrease; convert MeV to J with the 10⁶ factor and elementary-charge conversion.

裂变聚变能量与图像 / Reaction energy and binding curve

反应释放能量 \(Q=(\sum m_{\rm initial}-\sum m_{\rm final})c^2\)，质量差取正才表示释放；质量亏损求和需包括中子等全部反应粒子。核反应方程两边质量数和电荷数守恒。

Reaction energy comes from the total initial-minus-final mass, including all particles. Balance nucleon number and charge number.

每核子结合能曲线在中等质量核附近较高：轻核聚变、重核裂变可使产物核子结合更紧并释放能量。由图算总能量时，必须以各核核子数乘其平均结合能，再作总差，不能只减两个“每核子”读数。

Fusion of light nuclei and fission of heavy nuclei can increase total binding energy. Multiply each plotted mean binding energy by its nucleon number before finding the reaction's total energy release.

Calculate energy per reaction, then multiply by the number of reactions from sample mass or power/time. Identify fusion/fission regions on the graph.

考点精讲：单质子核的结合能为何为零 / Why a single-proton nucleus has zero binding energy

氢-1的原子核只含一个质子，没有需要彼此分开的多个核子，因此其核结合能及平均核子结合能均为零。这里讨论的是核结合能，不是氢原子中电子的结合能。核结合能定义中的“完全分离成自由核子”不可替换成任意裂变过程。

A hydrogen-1 nucleus contains only one proton, so there are no separate nucleons to pull apart. Its nuclear binding energy and binding energy per nucleon are zero. This is distinct from the electron’s binding energy in a hydrogen atom. Complete separation into free nucleons is not the same as an arbitrary fission process.

考点精讲：裂变、聚变与结合能曲线的三项比较 / Three comparisons for fission and fusion

分别说明反应的核变化、起始条件和对应的曲线区域。放能聚变使轻核结合为更重的核，发生在峰值左侧的合适反应；放能裂变使重核分裂为较轻的核，发生在峰值右侧。两者都要检查产物总结合能增加、总静质量减小。每核子变化较小的裂变仍可因参与核子数多而释放较多总能量。

Compare the nuclear change, initiation and graph region. Energy-releasing fusion combines light nuclei on the appropriate left-hand side; energy-releasing fission splits a heavy nucleus on the right. Check increased total binding energy and reduced total rest mass in both. Fission can release substantial total energy despite a smaller change per nucleon because many nucleons are involved.

考点精讲：燃料消耗率不等于质量亏损率 / Fuel consumption differs from mass conversion

先由核功率和每次反应释放能量求每秒反应次数，再根据每次反应消耗的燃料核数和单核质量求燃料消耗率。若给的是电功率，还需先考虑转换效率。用功率除以光速平方得到的是转化为能量的净质量率，不能直接当作全部燃料的消耗率。

Use nuclear power and energy per reaction to find reactions per second, then use fuel nuclei consumed and mass per nucleus to find fuel consumption. Account for efficiency if the stated power is electrical. Dividing power by the square of light speed gives net mass converted to energy, not total fuel consumed.

简明英文表述 / Simple English response

In fusion, two light nuclei combine into a heavier nucleus. High temperature gives them enough kinetic energy to overcome electrostatic repulsion and get close enough to fuse. Energy-releasing fusion occurs on the left of the binding-energy-per-nucleon peak, where combining light nuclei increases binding energy per nucleon. In fission, a heavy nucleus splits into two smaller nuclei, often after absorbing a thermal neutron and becoming unstable. Energy-releasing fission occurs on the right of the peak, where the products have greater binding energy per nucleon. In both cases the products have lower total rest mass and energy is released. Fission involves many nucleons, so it can release substantial energy even with a smaller increase per nucleon.

3.12.2 方法 1 · 结合能、质量亏损与反应能 / Binding energy and reaction energy

  1. 定义：把原子核完全分离为各自由质子和中子所需能量。\(\Delta m=Zm_p+(A-Z)m_n-m_{nucleus}\)，\(B=\Delta mc^2\)。质量亏损不是核的全部质量。 / Distinguish total rest energy from binding energy.
  2. 给出每核子结合能时，先分别乘各核的 \(A\)，再作“产物总结合能−反应物总结合能”；自由中子和质子无核结合能。 / Convert per-nucleon values before taking a difference.
  3. 给质量时算“反应物总静质量−产物总静质量”，计全入射中子、所有逸出中子和带质量的轻子；不要混用原子质量与裸核质量而漏电子。 / Use one consistent mass convention.
  4. \(1\,u\,c^2\simeq931.5\,MeV\)；先保留差值有效位再换能量。若有入射动能，反应可用能量还需加它；光子能量问题检查是否忽略反冲。 / Include initial kinetic energy where specified.
  5. 总结合能增加对应释放能量；裂变每核子增量可较小但涉及核子多，单次能量仍可较大。判断反应自发可能性先检查核子数、电荷守恒再看能量。 / Do not infer energy release from the curve region alone.
  6. 反推未知产物结合能时由 \(Q=\sum B_f-\sum B_i\) 列账目，再用 \(B/c^2\) 求其质量亏损。 / Write a signed energy equation before rearranging.

3.12.2 方法 2 · 反冲分配与功率链 / Recoil sharing and power chains

  • 初始静止核二体衰变：两产物动量等大反向；非相对论下 \(K=p^2/(2m)\)，动能与质量成反比，不是等动能。 / Use momentum conservation before sharing the energy.
  • 放电功率或电站电功率先除相应转换效率得核反应热功率；反应率 \(\dot N=P_{nuclear}/Q\)，燃料质量率 \(\dot m=\dot NM/N_A\)。 / Separate thermal power from electrical output.
  • 由 \(P/c^2\) 得的是转化为能量的净质量率；由反应率乘每个燃料核质量得的是燃料消耗率，两者不同。 / Do not equate fuel consumption with mass-energy conversion.
  • 特定裂变产物有特定 \(Q\)，大量裂变产生不同产物组合，因此平均释放能量可能不同。 / Distinguish a single reaction from the average.
