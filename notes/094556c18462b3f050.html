按物理关系整理公式 / Formulae organised by relationship

关系 1 / Relation 1

\[\Large\displaystyle E=\frac{|\Delta V|}{d}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量 / Quantity SI 单位 / SI unit
\(E\) 电场强度大小 / electric field magnitude \(\mathrm{N\,C^{-1}}\)
\(\Delta V\) 电势差或电压大小，按模型 / potential difference or voltage magnitude \(\mathrm{V}\)
\(d\) 间距或厚度 / spacing or thickness \(\mathrm{m}\)

物理关系 / Physical relationship： 同一电势差跨越更小板距时，匀强场更强。 / At fixed voltage difference, a smaller gap gives a stronger uniform field.

适用条件 / Conditions： 忽略边缘效应的理想平行板；E=ΔV/d 为大小关系 / ideal parallel plates away from edges; use magnitudes in E=ΔV/d

关系 2 / Relation 2

\[\Large\displaystyle \vec F=q\vec E\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量 / Quantity SI 单位 / SI unit
\(F\) 力的大小 / force magnitude \(\mathrm{N}\)
\(q\) 带符号粒子电荷 / signed particle charge \(\mathrm{C}\)
\(E\) 电场强度大小 / electric field magnitude \(\mathrm{N\,C^{-1}}\)

物理关系 / Physical relationship： 电荷为正时受力沿电场，负电荷相反。 / Positive charge feels force along the field and negative charge oppositely.

适用条件 / Conditions： 忽略边缘效应的理想平行板；E=ΔV/d 为大小关系 / ideal parallel plates away from edges; use magnitudes in E=ΔV/d

关系 3 / Relation 3

\[\Large\displaystyle \Delta U=q\Delta V\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量 / Quantity SI 单位 / SI unit
\(\Delta U\) 电势能变化 / change in electric potential energy \(\mathrm{J}\)
\(q\) 带符号粒子电荷 / signed particle charge \(\mathrm{C}\)
\(\Delta V\) 电势差或电压大小，按模型 / potential difference or voltage magnitude \(\mathrm{V}\)

物理关系 / Physical relationship： 电势能变化为电荷乘带符号电势差。 / Potential-energy change is charge times signed potential difference.

适用条件 / Conditions： 忽略边缘效应的理想平行板；E=ΔV/d 为大小关系 / ideal parallel plates away from edges; use magnitudes in E=ΔV/d
