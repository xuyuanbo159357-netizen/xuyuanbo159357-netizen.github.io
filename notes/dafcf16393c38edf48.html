Concept / 概念

binding energy — 结合能

Definition / 定义

English: The energy required to separate a nucleus completely into its constituent nucleons.

中文： 把原子核完全分离为各组成核子所需的能量。

定义评分依据 / Definition marking points： PH04 202601-Q04、PH04 202206-Q06。原答案在题目下展开；此处按评分要点概括。 Summarised from the marking points; originals are folded below each question.
