简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

A circular orbit at the listed speed and radius needs F = mv²/r ≈ 7.62 × 10¹² N. The gravitational force is only about 3.88 × 10¹² N. These are not equal, so the stated data do not describe a circular orbit.
