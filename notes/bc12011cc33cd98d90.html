简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

As the sheet enters and leaves the field, its magnetic flux changes, inducing an emf and eddy currents. By Lenz's law their magnetic effect opposes the motion. Energy from the oscillating sheet is dissipated as resistive heating, increasing damping.
