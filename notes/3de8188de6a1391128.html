建模、方程与适用条件 / Model, equations and conditions

先看接触和约束 / Identify the contact constraint

课件原图在顶端把接触力 \(N\) 画为朝内、向下，属于圆环内侧约束情境：\(N+mg=mv^2/r\)，保持非负接触力需 \(v^2\ge gr\)。不能把这个受力图直接套给所有摩天轮座舱。

The original diagram shows inward downward contact at the top, so it uses an inside-loop constraint. This is not the force diagram for every wheel or gondola.

若座舱保持直立、座面只向上托人，顶端 \(mg-N=mv^2/r\)，底端 \(N-mg=mv^2/r\)；匀速时顶端较轻、底端较重。取 \(N=0\) 的限制方向与环内侧模型不同。

For an upright gondola with an upward seat force, reaction is smaller at the top and larger at the bottom. Its zero-reaction limit differs from the inside-loop case.

稳定速率与能量 / Constant speed and energy

驱动装置若维持恒速，顶底速率相同，但重力势能变化由装置交换能量；不能同时强行假定“全程恒速且机械能守恒”。若安全带或座椅还能施加其他方向力，要把这些真实力列入。

A motor maintaining constant speed exchanges energy as height changes. Do not impose both constant speed and isolated mechanical-energy conservation. Include restraints when they exert additional forces.
