简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

Work is done on the gas, so W is positive. The compression is rapid, leaving negligible time for heat transfer, so Q ≈ 0. From ΔU = Q + W, internal energy increases. The mean molecular kinetic energy and therefore temperature increase.
