Conditions / 条件与范围

考纲核心为匀速圆周运动。\(a_r=v^2/r\) 和 \(\sum F_r=mv^2/r\) 也适用于固定半径变速圆周运动的瞬时径向分量；此时另有可能的切向加速度。整周 \(2\pi/T\) 与 \(2\pi r/T\) 作为任一点的角速率、线速率需匀速。不考向心加速度公式的推导。

The core assessed case is uniform circular motion. The radial acceleration and force equations also apply instantaneously at varying speed, when tangential acceleration may exist. Circumference or full angle divided by period gives instantaneous speed only for uniform motion. The centripetal-acceleration derivation is excluded.

Explanation / 解释

角量、线量与向心加速度 / Angular and linear quantities

匀速圆周运动的速率不变，但速度方向不断改变，故存在加速度。任一点的速度沿切线，向心加速度和径向合力指向圆心；“向心力”是实际力的径向合力，不是在重力、张力之外另加的力。

Uniform circular motion has constant speed but changing velocity. Velocity is tangential; centripetal acceleration and the radial resultant point towards the centre. Centripetal force is supplied by real forces, not an extra force.

\(s=r\theta\)，\(v=r\omega\)，匀速时 \(\omega=2\pi/T=2\pi f\)，\(a_r=v^2/r=\omega^2r\)，\(\sum F_{\rm inward}=mv^2/r\)。角量使用弧度，转每分钟先除以60得到 Hz。

Use radians in angular equations. Convert revolutions per minute to hertz before calculating angular speed.

径向关系 \(a_r=v^2/r\) 在变速圆周运动的每一位置也成立，但此时还可有切向加速度，合加速度不一定纯向心；\(v=2\pi r/T\) 给出一周平均速率，仅匀速时也是任一点速率。本考纲不考 \(a_r=v^2/r\) 的推导。

The radial relation also applies instantaneously when speed varies, but tangential acceleration may then exist. Circumference divided by period is the mean speed for a full revolution; it equals instantaneous speed only in uniform motion. The centripetal-acceleration derivation is excluded.

受力情境分类与作答流程 / Force scenarios and method

先定位圆心、画真实受力，再把向心方向定为正并投影列 \(\sum F_r=mv^2/r\)；垂直于圆周平面的方向若无加速度，应另列平衡。最后检查绳是否拉紧、物体是否接触、摩擦是否足够。

Locate the centre, draw real forces and resolve towards the centre. Apply equilibrium separately in directions with zero acceleration. Check tension, contact and friction constraints.

水平绳、圆锥摆、平路转弯、倾斜弯道由不同实际力提供径向合力；拱桥与凹路、竖直绳和游乐装置需根据所在位置重新判定重力方向。下方的模型分类给出独立受力方程。

Different forces supply radial resultants in horizontal strings, conical pendulums, level turns and banked bends. At road crests, dips and vertical rides, re-evaluate weight's radial direction at each position.

变速竖直运动先用机械能关系求局部速率，再列径向方程；临界时可令张力或支持力为零。各模型是考纲向心力公式与已有力学知识的应用，不新增一套无条件背诵公式。

For varying-speed vertical motion, use energy to obtain local speed, then apply the radial force equation. Critical cases may have zero tension or normal reaction. Model results are conditional applications of the specification equations.

读图 / Reading： 圆心在下方，向下为径向正方向，所以 mg−S=mv²/r；不能一律把支持力写成大于重力。

The centre is below, so the inward equation is mg−S=mv²/r; support force need not exceed weight.

Resolve support force vertically and radially; in the ideal frictionless model vertical forces balance and the horizontal component is centripetal.

Here weight and seat reaction both act inward, giving mg+R=mv²/r; redraw forces if the contact constraint changes.

Applications / 应用与实验

画真实受力并沿向心方向列式；竖直圆周情况需考虑重力和该处速率，速率变化时结合能量守恒。

Draw a force diagram and sum real forces towards the centre. For vertical circular situations, include weight and use the speed at the stated position; use energy conservation when speed changes.

Common errors / 易错点

真实受力已列全后，不再额外添加向心力；角速率恒定不等于速度恒定。

Do not add an extra “centripetal force” to a diagram already containing all real forces. Constant angular speed is not constant velocity.

真题检验与答题思路 / Exam coverage and approaches

依据本库现有真题与评分标准核对。下列步骤按题型使用，先核对题设条件；综合题需结合其他关联知识点。 / Checked against the papers and mark schemes held in this vault; select the approach that fits the stated conditions.

3.6.1 方法 1 · 真实受力、约束与临界值 / Forces, constraints and limiting motion

  1. 确定圆心与实际轨道半径，画重力、张力、支持力、摩擦力；向心力是这些力的径向合力，不另画一个附加力。 / Identify the centre and actual radius; centripetal force is the radial resultant.
  2. 竖直方向平衡与径向方程分开。圆锥摆若角度从竖直量，\(T\cos\theta=mg\)、\(T\sin\theta=m\omega^2r\)；若从水平量则互换正余弦。支点偏离轴时 \(r=D+l\sin\theta\)。 / Resolve using the angle actually defined in the diagram.
  3. 水平转弯、转盘由静摩擦提供径向力；临界打滑才令 \(f=f_{max}\)。倾斜弯道由支持力和摩擦的水平分量共同提供径向合力。 / Use maximum friction only at the stated limit.
  4. 竖直圆周顶端临界接触时支持力为零，底部通常 \(T-mg=mv^2/r\)；若比较最大张力，必须保留重力项。 / State which contact or tension becomes zero at the limit.
  5. 固定速度而增大飞机倾角，不可擅自令升力同步增大；根据题设的升力大小，判断竖直分量与重力、水平分量与转弯半径。 / Apply the stated lift model rather than assuming level flight continues.
  6. 侧翻临界绕外轮接地点取矩，内轮支持力为零；水平路面可由 \(mg(w/2)=(mv^2/r)h\) 得到速度限值。 / Use moments as well as translational dynamics at tipping.

3.6.1 方法 2 · 角速率、连接轮与释放运动 / Angular speed, coupled wheels and release

  • \(\omega=\Delta\theta/\Delta t=2\pi f\)，转每分钟先除60；钟针用其完成一周的时间。\(v=r\omega\) 中 \(r\) 是到转轴的垂距。 / Convert revolutions to radians and use the correct radius.
  • 同轴刚体共享 \(\omega\)；无滑动皮带连接两轮共享轮缘线速，故 \(r_X\omega_X=r_Y\omega_Y\)。 / Distinguish common angular speed from common rim speed.
  • 释放后若无合力，物体沿释放瞬间的切线作匀速直线运动；不是继续绕圈，也不是向径向飞出。 / State the observer and use Newton's first law.
  • 旋转空间站由舱壁支持力产生向心加速度，模拟的是接触力感受；深空不能凭图补上地球重力。 / Identify the real source of the apparent weight.
