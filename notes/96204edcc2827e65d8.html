方法、分析与误差 / Method, analysis and uncertainty

图示：弹簧振子的平衡位置 / Mass–spring equilibrium

读图 / Reading： 位移 x 从受重力后形成的平衡位置量起，而不是从弹簧原长量起；计时跨多个完整周期并取平均。

Measure x from the loaded equilibrium position, not the spring’s natural length; time several complete cycles.

图示：单摆的回复作用 / Pendulum restoring effect

读图 / Reading： 摆长从悬点量到摆球质心；沿切线的重力分量指向平衡位置，小角度时才可使用简谐近似。

Measure length from pivot to bob centre; tangential gravity restores equilibrium, with the SHM approximation requiring a small angle.

1

多个完整周期计时再平均并重复。

Measure the period by timing many complete cycles and divide by the cycle count; repeat.

2

弹簧实验改变质量，保持弹簧与小振幅，作周期平方—质量图。

For a spring, vary the attached mass while keeping spring and small amplitude fixed; plot T² against m.

3

单摆测支点到球心长度，保持小角度，改变摆长并作周期平方图。

For a pendulum, measure pivot-to-bob-centre length, keep the angle small, vary length and plot T² against l.

4

两斜率分别为四π平方除劲度系数或重力加速度；数据不支持时不能强迫过原点。

Spring gradient is 4π²/k; pendulum gradient is 4π²/g. Include intercepts rather than forcing a fit through zero if data disagree.

5

控制释放方法，避免半周期计数，并讨论反应时间与长度、质量不确定度。

Control release method and avoid timing half cycles; discuss reaction time and length/mass uncertainties.

定量分析与条件核对 / Quantitative analysis and controls

↗ 3.5.1 振动系统、能量与阻尼 / Oscillating systems

必做实验4 / Required practical 4

弹簧实验作 \(T^2\)–\(m\) 图，理想斜率为 \(4\pi^2/k\)；单摆作 \(T^2\)–\(l\) 图，斜率为 \(4\pi^2/g\)。一次测多次完整振动再除以次数，重复计时。

Plot \(T^2\) against \(m\) for a spring and \(T^2\) against \(l\) for a pendulum. Their gradients are \(4\pi^2/k\) and \(4\pi^2/g\). Time several complete oscillations and repeat.

控制小振幅，释放时不额外推球，使用平衡位置标记；检查支架、长度定义及弹簧自身质量影响。

Use small amplitudes, release without a push and use a reference marker. Consider support stability, effective pendulum length and spring mass.
