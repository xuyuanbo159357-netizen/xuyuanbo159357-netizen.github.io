简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

The blade changes the water's momentum. By Newton's second law, that rate of momentum change requires a force on the water. By Newton's third law, the water exerts an opposite force on the blade. This force acts at a perpendicular distance from the axle and produces a torque.
