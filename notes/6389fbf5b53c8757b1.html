3.4.3 电阻率、热敏电阻与超导 / Resistivity

考纲 / Specification： 3.4.3　｜　本单元知识谱系

Concept / 概念

  • resistivity — 电阻率
  • NTC thermistor — 负温度系数热敏电阻
  • superconductivity — 超导性
  • critical temperature — 临界温度

Definition / 定义

↗ resistivity — 电阻率

Definition / 定义

English: The resistance between opposite faces of a cube of the material with a side length of one metre.

中文： 该材料制成的边长为一米的立方体，相对两面之间的电阻。

评分依据 / Mark-scheme basis： PH02 · 202101 · Q01.1

↗ NTC thermistor — 负温度系数热敏电阻

Definition / 定义

English: A resistor whose resistance decreases as its temperature increases.

中文： 温度升高时电阻减小的电阻元件。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

↗ superconductivity — 超导性

Definition / 定义

English: The property of a material having zero resistivity at and below its critical temperature.

中文： 材料在临界温度及以下电阻率为零的性质。

定义评分依据 / Definition marking points： PH02 201801-Q01、PH02 202301-Q02。原答案在题目下展开；此处按评分要点概括。 Summarised from the marking points; originals are folded below each question.

↗ critical temperature — 临界温度

Definition / 定义

English: The temperature at and below which a material has zero resistivity.

中文： 材料电阻率达到零及保持为零的温度界限。

Formula / 公式与单位

注意使用横截面积，电阻率单位为欧姆米。

\(\rho=RA/L\), \(R=\rho L/A\), \(A=\pi d^2/4\). Resistivity unit: \(\Omega\,\mathrm{m}\).

Conditions / 条件与范围

导线应均匀且温度确定。热敏电阻限负温度系数；不考查临界磁场。

Use a uniform specimen at a stated temperature. Only negative temperature coefficient thermistors are required; critical magnetic field is not assessed.

Explanation / 解释

尺寸与电阻率 / Dimensions and resistivity

\(R=\rho L/A\)，\(A=\pi d^2/4\)，\([\rho]=\Omega\,\mathrm m\)。同种材料、相同温度下 \(R\propto L\)、\(R\propto1/A\)；直径加倍使横截面积变为四倍，电阻变为四分之一。电阻率由材料和温度决定，不由试样尺寸决定。

At fixed material and temperature, resistance is proportional to length and inversely proportional to cross-sectional area. Doubling diameter quadruples area and quarters resistance. Resistivity is a material property at a specified temperature.

实验测多个长度的 \(R=V/I\)，作 \(R\)–\(L\) 图，斜率为 \(\rho/A\)。千分尺在多处、相互垂直方向测直径；考虑零点误差、接触电阻和自热。

Measure resistance for several lengths. The gradient of \(R\) against \(L\) is \(\rho/A\). Repeat diameter readings at different positions and orientations; consider zero error, contact resistance and heating.

温度传感与图像 / Temperature sensing and graphs

金属升温：载流子数近似不变，晶格振动增强，电阻增大。NTC 热敏电阻升温：可参与导电的载流子数增多，电阻非线性减小。解释必须同时说清微观原因和电阻变化。

Heating a metal increases lattice vibrations and resistance, with approximately unchanged carrier density. Heating an NTC thermistor increases available charge carriers and decreases resistance nonlinearly.

读 \(R\)–\(T\) 图时指出变化方向及非线性；将热敏电阻接入分压器后，输出随温度变化的方向还取决于输出取自哪一端。

Describe the trend and nonlinearity on an \(R\)–\(T\) graph. In a potential divider, the output trend also depends on which component is connected across the output.

超导转变与应用 / Superconducting transition

某些材料在各自临界温度及以下电阻率为零；\(\rho\)–\(T\) 图在临界温度处发生超导转变。不能把所有普通金属简单外推成超导体。

Some materials have zero resistivity at and below their own critical temperature. Their resistivity–temperature graph shows a superconducting transition; not every ordinary metal becomes superconducting by extrapolation.

零电阻使持续大电流的电阻发热损耗减小，可用于强电磁铁（MRI、加速器）及输电；维持低温仍需设备和能量。考纲不考临界磁场。

Zero resistance reduces resistive heating and supports large persistent currents in strong electromagnets and power transmission. Cooling still requires equipment and energy. Critical magnetic field is excluded.

Applications / 应用与实验

热敏电阻用于温度传感器；超导体用于强磁场和降低输电电阻损耗；导线直径应在不同位置和方向测量。

Thermistors are used in temperature sensors. Superconductors support strong magnetic fields and reduce resistive losses in electrical transmission. Measure wire diameter at several positions and perpendicular directions.

Common errors / 易错点

区分电阻与电阻率、直径与面积；面积相对不确定度为直径的两倍。

Distinguish resistance from resistivity and diameter from area; the fractional uncertainty of area is twice that of diameter.

依据本库现有真题与评分标准核对。下列步骤按题型使用，先核对题设条件；综合题需结合其他关联知识点。 / Checked against the papers and mark schemes held in this vault; select the approach that fits the stated conditions.

3.4.3 方法 1 · 导体几何与比例 / Geometry and resistance ratios

  1. 画出电流方向，再取垂直于电流的截面积：圆线 \(A=\pi d^2/4\)，薄膜 \(A=wt\)；线圈导线长约为匝数乘每匝周长，不是线圈轴向长度。 / Use the cross-section normal to current; coil wire length follows the turns.
  2. 用 \(R=\rho L/A\) 比例式：\(R_2/R_1=(\rho_2/\rho_1)(L_2/L_1)(d_1/d_2)^2\)。同材料、体积不变时 \(R\propto L^2\)。 / State which quantities remain constant.
  3. 均匀导线等分为 \(n\) 段，每段 \(R/n\)；再将这 \(n\) 段并联得到 \(R/n^2\)。 / Separate cutting from reconnecting.
  4. 熔断功率限制题先求熔丝 \(R\)，再用 \(I_{\rm limit}=\sqrt{P_{\rm melt}/R}\)，结合正常工作电流选额定值。 / Compare the limiting current with the operating current.

对应真题 / Examples： PH02 · 202601 · Q06；PH02 · 202206 · Q10；PH02 · 202301 · Q17；PH02 · 201706 · Q09。

3.4.3 方法 2 · 电阻率与温度实验 / Resistivity and temperature experiments

实验答题链 / Experimental chain. 自变量 → 可操作的改变方法 → 因变量及仪器 → 控制量 → 多组读数 → 图像处理 → 不确定度。 / Name variables, instruments, controls, repeated measurements and analysis.

  • \(R\)–\(L\) 图的斜率为 \(\rho/A\)；固定引线或接触电阻表现为截距，不能强迫拟合过原点。直径在 \(A\) 中平方，因此其百分不确定度贡献乘二。 / Allow a contact-resistance intercept and the squared-diameter contribution.
  • 热敏电阻置于搅拌水浴中，与温度计靠近；每个温度等热平衡后测 \(V,I\)，减小自热，重复并控制其他量。 / Allow thermal equilibrium and minimise self-heating.
  • 超导应用写出“低于临界温度 → 电阻为零 → 大电流下避免 \(I^2R\) 发热”等具体收益；冷却成本、机械强度需另行讨论。 / Link the property to a concrete benefit and practical limitation.

对应真题 / Examples： PH02 · 202201 · Q11；PH02 · 202506 · Q10；PH02 · 202206 · Q07；PH02 · 202206 · Q01。
