可用于备课的观察 / Teaching observations

  • 3.13.1 转动运动 / Rotational Motion：历年合计199分（16.58%），15/15卷有主考点分值；单卷9–19分。 In this collection, the primary allocation ranges from 9 to 19 marks per paper.

  • 3.11.3 理想气体 / Ideal Gases：历年合计141分（11.75%），15/15卷有主考点分值；单卷1–17分。 In this collection, the primary allocation ranges from 1 to 17 marks per paper.

  • 3.11.1 通过加热和做功转移能量 / Energy Transfer By Heating And Doing Work：历年合计138分（11.50%），15/15卷有主考点分值；单卷2–27分。 In this collection, the primary allocation ranges from 2 to 27 marks per paper.

  • 3.12.2 质量和能量 / Mass And Energy：历年合计132分（11.00%），15/15卷有主考点分值；单卷5–14分。 In this collection, the primary allocation ranges from 5 to 14 marks per paper.

  • 按出现频率安排复习次数，但仍完整覆盖考纲；低频、零分小节不删。Use frequency to plan repetition while retaining the complete specification.

  • 将同一小节的定义题、计算题、解释题与实验题交叉练习，避免只会一种题面。Vary the response demand within each topic.

  • 分值多只说明考查量，不能据此判断该内容难、得分率低或未来必考。A large mark share measures emphasis, not difficulty or future certainty.
