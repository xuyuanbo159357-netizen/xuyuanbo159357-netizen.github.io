Conditions / 条件与范围

库仑势垒计算用给定接近距离估算，不是精确反应温度预测；太阳聚变限氢循环。

A classical Coulomb-barrier calculation is an estimate using a specified approach distance, not an exact operating-temperature prediction. The solar fusion cycle is limited to the hydrogen cycle.

Explanation / 解释

反应能量与库仑势垒 / Fusion energy and barrier

适合的轻核例子是氘、氚：\({}^2_1\mathrm H+{}^3_1\mathrm H\rightarrow{}^4_2\mathrm{He}+{}^1_0n\)。能量由总反应质量差乘 \(c^2\) 求得；产物具有更大的总结合能。

Deuterium and tritium are suitable example fuels. Fusion energy is the initial-minus-final total mass converted by \(c^2\), corresponding to increased total binding energy.

两正核要接近核力作用距离，需克服静电斥力。简单估算 \(E_{\rm barrier}\sim Z_1Z_2e^2/(4\pi\varepsilon_0r)\)，再按题设与平均动能 \(3kT/2\) 比较求温度数量级；要明确这是经典估算，不把所算温度当作精确反应阈值。

Estimate the Coulomb barrier at a stated separation and compare with thermal kinetic energy to estimate temperature. This is a classical order-of-magnitude model, not an exact fusion threshold.

太阳氢循环与实际困难 / Solar hydrogen cycle and challenges

氢循环主要步骤：\(p+p\rightarrow{}^2\mathrm H+e^++\nu_e\)；\({}^2\mathrm H+p\rightarrow{}^3\mathrm{He}+\gamma\)；\({}^3\mathrm{He}+{}^3\mathrm{He}\rightarrow{}^4\mathrm{He}+2p\)。前两步各发生两次后净消耗四个质子形成一个氦-4核并释放能量；考纲限氢循环。

In the proton–proton hydrogen cycle, two deuterium-forming steps and two helium-3-forming steps precede helium-4 production. Overall, four protons form one helium-4 nucleus with energy release. Only the hydrogen cycle is required.

实用聚变需把等离子体加热到高温，维持足够密度和约束时间，限制能量损失并保护容器材料；可用磁约束避免热等离子体直接接触器壁。还需处理高能中子损伤、燃料供应和净能量输出。

Practical fusion requires high temperature, sufficient density and confinement time, controlled losses and material protection. Magnetic confinement keeps hot plasma away from walls; neutron damage, fuel supply and net output remain engineering concerns.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.12.5

图示：太阳氢循环的净结果 / Net result of the solar hydrogen cycle

读图 / Reading： 逐步反应中出现的中间核最后可消去；净结果含氦核、两个正电子和两个电子中微子。正电子后续湮灭的能量另按题意处理。

Cancel intermediate nuclei to obtain the net cycle: helium, two positrons and two electron neutrinos; handle later positron annihilation as stated.

Applications / 应用与实验

由质量求反应能量，估算温度，并解释轻核燃料和约束需求。

Estimate kinetic energy/temperature and energy released from masses. Explain why suitable light nuclei and confinement are needed.

Common errors / 易错点

聚变不是把核分开；高温为克服静电斥力，不是为了使核带正电。

Fusion is not splitting a nucleus. High temperature is needed to overcome electrostatic repulsion, not to make nuclei positively charged.

3.12.5 方法 1 · 聚变势垒、太阳循环与工程约束 / Fusion barriers, cycles and engineering

  1. 正核间斥力形成势垒；需足够相对动能使其接近至强相互作用范围。两核均运动时用可转化为相互作用势能的能量，不能无条件把每核平均动能全部当可用能量。 / Account for the relative motion and any remaining centre-of-mass kinetic energy.
  2. 能量有分布，平均动能未达到某估计势垒不排除部分高能碰撞。高电荷核聚变需要更高温度以克服更大斥力。 / Explain the high-energy tail qualitatively.
  3. 太阳质子链：\(p+p\to{}^2H+e^++\nu_e\)；\({}^2H+p\to{}^3He+\gamma\)；\({}^3He+{}^3He\to{}^4He+2p\)。前两步各发生两次，第三步一次；共投入6个质子、返还2个，净消耗4个。 / Distinguish gross inputs from net consumption.
  4. 由太阳功率估燃料寿命：总质子数除4得循环数，乘每循环能量，再除功率；不能假设全部氢静质量都转为辐射。 / Use the stated energy per complete cycle.
  5. 高温等离子体不能接触容器壁，带电粒子由强磁场约束；超导线圈减小大电流电阻损耗。中子不受直接磁约束，可损伤材料并使其活化，因此不等于“聚变无放射性风险”。 / Separate confinement of charges from neutron shielding.
