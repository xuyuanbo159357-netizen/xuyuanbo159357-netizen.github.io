3.5.2 受迫振动与共振 / Forced vibrations and resonance

考纲 / Specification： 3.5.2　｜　本单元知识谱系

知识逐项讲解 / Concept-by-concept guide

free vibration — 自由振动

Definition / 定义

↗ free vibration — 自由振动

Definition / 定义

English: Oscillation at a system’s natural frequency after an initial disturbance, without a continuing periodic driving force.

中文： 受到初始扰动后，无持续周期外力、按固有频率进行的振动。

resonance — 共振

Definition / 定义

↗ resonance — 共振

Definition / 定义

English: Resonance occurs when a system is driven at its natural frequency, producing a maximum amplitude of oscillation.

中文： 当周期驱动力的频率等于系统固有频率时，振动振幅达到最大，这种现象称为共振。

Conditions / 条件与范围

U2 对共振采用定性处理；稳定受迫振动的频率由驱动源决定。

Treat resonance qualitatively in U2. In steady forced vibration, oscillation frequency is set by the driver.

Explanation / 解释

自由、受迫与共振 / Free, forced and resonant motion

自由振动是在初始扰动后没有持续周期性驱动的振动；它仍可有阻尼。稳态受迫振动的频率等于驱动频率；振幅取决于驱动频率、驱动力和阻尼。不能将所有受迫或阻尼运动一概称为理想简谐运动。

Free oscillation occurs after an initial disturbance without continuing periodic driving; it may still be damped. Steady forced oscillation follows the driving frequency, with amplitude depending on frequency, driving force and damping.

驱动频率接近系统固有频率时，能量传递效率大，出现共振峰。阻尼越大，共振峰越低、越宽，响应选择性越弱；轻阻尼时峰值频率接近固有频率。

Resonance gives a large response when the driving frequency is close to the natural frequency. Increasing damping lowers and broadens the peak; with light damping the peak is close to the natural frequency.

实例与图像判断 / Examples and graph interpretation

巴顿摆：与驱动摆长度相近的摆固有频率接近，振幅最大。桥梁、机器或悬架需控制不利共振；乐器弦和空气柱可利用共振增强特定频率。

In Barton's pendulums, the pendulum with a matching natural frequency has the largest response. Resonance can be controlled in bridges and machines or used to reinforce selected frequencies in instruments.

驻波只有在边界允许的频率出现稳定波节、波腹；扫描驱动频率时，合适频率处振幅显著增大。共振图横轴是驱动频率、纵轴是稳态振幅，不是位移随时间图。

Standing-wave resonances occur at frequencies allowed by the boundaries. A resonance curve plots steady amplitude against driving frequency, not displacement against time.

U2 Resonance damping

共振定性图：阻尼增大，峰值降低并展宽；轻阻尼峰接近固有频率。仅读图，不要求此曲线方程。 Qualitative resonance curves.

图示：阻尼的时间图像 / Damping in time

读图 / Reading： 轻阻尼仍有振荡但振幅逐渐衰减；临界阻尼使系统不振荡并尽快返回平衡。

Light damping retains oscillations with declining amplitude; critical damping returns to equilibrium without oscillating as quickly as possible.

图示：共振曲线 / Resonance curves

读图 / Reading： 比较峰高和峰宽：阻尼较小时峰更高、更尖；峰值频率接近固有频率，不能把纵轴振幅当作能量本身。

Lower damping gives a higher, sharper peak near natural frequency; the vertical axis is amplitude, not energy.

Applications / 应用与实验

例子包括秋千、弦、耦合摆以及结构的不希望振动；说明共振利用与阻尼防护。

Examples include a swing, vibrating strings, coupled pendulums and unwanted vibration in structures. Explain both useful resonance and the need for damping.

Common errors / 易错点

自由振动本身不能称为共振。

Resonance is a response to periodic driving, not simply a system oscillating freely.

3.5.2 方法 1 · 共振解释的得分链 / Explaining resonance

解释顺序 / Explanation chain: 周期驱动力 → 驱动频率接近固有频率 → 每周期能量传递较有效 → 稳态振幅较大。 / Periodic driving → driving frequency near natural frequency → efficient energy transfer → a large steady amplitude.

路面凸起间距 \(d\)、速度 \(v\) 时，驱动频率 \(f=v/d\)。增加阻尼使共振峰降低、变宽；受迫稳态振动频率仍是驱动频率。阻尼器应靠近待抑制振型的波腹。 / Calculate the driving frequency and distinguish it from the natural frequency; place damping where motion is large.
