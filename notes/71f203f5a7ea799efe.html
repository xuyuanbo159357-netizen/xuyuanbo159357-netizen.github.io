简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

Fast electrons collide with the metal target and remove inner-shell electrons. Electrons in higher energy levels then fall into the inner-shell vacancies. They emit photons whose energies equal the energy-level differences; these are the characteristic X-rays.
