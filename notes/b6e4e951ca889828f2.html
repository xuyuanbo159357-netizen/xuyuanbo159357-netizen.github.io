原文符号说明 / Notation clarification

原文件以 \(E\) 标记“最大功率”，右侧量纲是功率。教学笔记统一写 \(P_{\max}=\tfrac12\rho\pi r^2v^3\)。 The printed symbol E denotes power in this equation; teaching notes use P.
