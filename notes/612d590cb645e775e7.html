简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

From A to B, gravitational potential energy decreases and kinetic energy increases. From B to C, gravitational potential energy continues to decrease and elastic potential energy in the rope increases. Kinetic energy may first increase, but then decreases to zero at C. Both the lost gravitational potential energy and kinetic energy are transferred to elastic potential energy.
