3.2.10 杨氏模量 / The Young modulus

知识节点 / Knowledge nodes

  • D28 杨氏模量与材料刚度 - Young modulus and material stiffness：杨氏模量是线性范围的应力应变比。Young modulus is a material property in the linear range.
  • P02 必做实验2：线材杨氏模量 - Required practical 2 - Young modulus of a wire：用载荷–伸长梯度、原长和截面积求E。Find E from the load-extension gradient and specimen geometry.
