3.4.3 电阻率、热敏电阻与超导

导线应均匀且温度确定。热敏电阻限负温度系数；不考查临界磁场。

Use a uniform specimen at a stated temperature. Only negative temperature coefficient thermistors are required; critical magnetic field is not assessed.

注意使用横截面积，电阻率单位为欧姆米。
