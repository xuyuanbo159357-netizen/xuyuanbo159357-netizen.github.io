导线几何与电阻率 / Resistivity

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

resistivity → wire → diameter → length → 导线几何与电阻率

推理顺序 / Reasoning

先求截面积，再用电阻率关系；注意直径单位和面积不确定度。

Calculate \(A=\pi d^2/4\); use \(R=\rho L/A\); check diameter units; double the diameter’s percentage uncertainty for area.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.4.3

3.4.3 电阻率、热敏电阻与超导

导线应均匀且温度确定。热敏电阻限负温度系数；不考查临界磁场。

Use a uniform specimen at a stated temperature. Only negative temperature coefficient thermistors are required; critical magnetic field is not assessed.

注意使用横截面积，电阻率单位为欧姆米。

\(\rho=RA/L\), \(R=\rho L/A\), \(A=\pi d^2/4\). Resistivity unit: \(\Omega\,\mathrm{m}\).
