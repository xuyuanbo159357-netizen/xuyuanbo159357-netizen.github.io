3.2 力学和材料 / MECHANICS AND MATERIALS

考纲章节原文 / Chapter text

考纲依据 / Source： 物理大纲.pdf · OxfordAQA 9630 · Version 4.4；PDF第11页／印刷第12页。

English: Vectors and their treatment are introduced followed by development of the student’s knowledge and understanding of forces, energy and momentum. A study of materials is considered in terms of their bulk properties including elastic and plastic behaviour, the Young modulus and tensile strength.

中文： 先介绍矢量及其处理，再发展对力、能量和动量的理解；材料从宏观性质展开，包括弹性和塑性行为、杨氏模量及抗拉强度。

关系摘要

力学与材料 / Mechanics and materials

矢量描述运动，力决定变化，动量与能量组织相互作用，材料把受力连接形变。Motion, forces, conservation and deformation form one connected model.
