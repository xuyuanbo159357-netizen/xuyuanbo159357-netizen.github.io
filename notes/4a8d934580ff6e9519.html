简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

The floor area is π × 21² ≈ 1385 m². The heat-transfer rate is kAΔT/L = 0.24 × 1385 × (570 − 15)/0.45 ≈ 4.1 × 10⁵ W. This is about 410 kW, greater than the allowed 100 kW, so the floor is unsuitable.
