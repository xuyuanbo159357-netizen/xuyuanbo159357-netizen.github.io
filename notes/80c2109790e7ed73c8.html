建模、方程与适用条件 / Model, equations and conditions

受力与临界条件 / Forces and limit

水平路面上 \(N=mg\)；静摩擦力指向弯道中心，\(f=mv^2/r\)。摩擦不是总取最大值，而是按所需径向力调整。若题目给摩擦上限 \(f_{\max}\)，\(v_{\max}=\sqrt{f_{\max}r/m}\)。

Normal reaction balances weight and static friction supplies the inward force. Friction equals the required value until its limit is reached.

只有当题目给出 \(f_{\max}=\mu_sN\) 时，才进一步得到 \(v_{\max}=\sqrt{\mu_sgr}\)；不把摩擦系数模型当作本小节额外必背考纲。

If the limiting-friction model is supplied, substitute it to obtain the speed limit. The coefficient model is conditional input, not an extra requirement of this subsection.

条件变化 / Changing conditions

同一半径下速率加倍需四倍径向力；摩擦不足时车无法保持原半径，初始运动趋势更接近原切线，而不是“存在一个向外的实际力”。

Doubling speed quadruples required force. Insufficient friction prevents the original turn; no extra outward real force is required in an inertial-frame diagram.
