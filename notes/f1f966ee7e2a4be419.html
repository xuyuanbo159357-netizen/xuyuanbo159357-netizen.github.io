简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

Place the source opposite a radiation detector and put absorbers between them. Subtract the background count rate. Radiation stopped by paper or a few centimetres of air is alpha. Radiation that passes through paper but is stopped by a few millimetres of aluminium is beta. Radiation that still passes through the aluminium is gamma.
