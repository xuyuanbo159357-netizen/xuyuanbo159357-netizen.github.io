U3/07 常用物理模型

径向合力与圆周运动 / Centripetal force

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

circle → centripetal → string → radius → 径向合力与圆周运动

推理顺序 / Reasoning

画真实受力，以向心为正列合力；速率变化时结合能量。

Draw real forces; choose the direction towards the centre; set their radial resultant to \(mv^2/r\); use energy if speed changes.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.6.1

3.6.1 圆周运动与向心力

匀速圆周是考纲核心；径向关系也可用于变速圆周的瞬时径向分量，切向运动另行分析；不考向心加速度公式推导。

Uniform circular motion is the core case. The radial equations also apply instantaneously at varying speed; analyse tangential motion separately. The derivation of \(a_c=v^2/r\) is not examined.

角速率使用弧度每秒；向心力为合力的径向分量。

\(\omega=v/r=2\pi f=2\pi/T\); \(a_c=v^2/r=r\omega^2\); \(F_c=mv^2/r=mr\omega^2\). Units: rad s\(^{-1}\), m s\(^{-2}\), N.
