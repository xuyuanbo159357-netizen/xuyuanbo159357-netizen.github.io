U3PM17 拱桥与凹路支持力 / Crest and dip

所属知识 / Knowledge： 3.6.1 Circular motion / 圆周运动 · 模型目录 / Model index

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

crest / hump / dip / bridge / lose contact / apparent weight → 拱桥与凹路支持力 / Crest and dip

关键词只是识别线索；必须同时核对受力方向、几何和约束。 Keywords are cues, not sufficient conditions; check the actual forces and constraints.

建模、方程与适用条件 / Model, equations and conditions

拱顶：圆心在下 / Crest: centre below

重力向下、支持力向上，取向下为径向正：\(mg-N=mv^2/r\)，故 \(N=mg-mv^2/r\)。速率增大时表观重量减小；刚失去接触时 \(N=0\)，\(v=\sqrt{gr}\)。超过此速率，原接触圆轨迹不再成立。

At a crest, inward is downward and the normal reaction is reduced. Zero reaction marks loss of contact; the constrained circular path then ceases to apply.

凹底：圆心在上 / Dip: centre above

\(N-mg=mv^2/r\)，\(N=mg+mv^2/r\)，人或车感到更重。\(r\) 为当地曲率半径，不一定是整座桥的尺寸。

At a dip, inward is upward and normal reaction exceeds weight. Use the local radius of curvature.

变化条件 / Changing conditions

两处必须分别画圆心和箭头；不能把 \(N=mg\) 当成有竖直加速度时的通式。秤或座椅给出的表观重量是接触力，不是改变了地球引力。

Locate the centre separately at each point. Apparent weight is the contact force; gravity itself has not changed.
