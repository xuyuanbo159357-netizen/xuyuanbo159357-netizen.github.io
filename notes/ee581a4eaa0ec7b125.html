U3/07 常用物理模型

交叉电磁场速度选择 / Undeflected

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

electric field → magnetic field → undeflected → zero resultant → 交叉电磁场速度选择

推理顺序 / Reasoning

电磁力相反且不偏转时令两力相等，得到速率为电场除磁场；先核对方向。

For mutually opposing electric and magnetic forces use \(|q|E=|q|vB\), hence \(v=E/B\); verify force directions before cancelling magnitudes.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.8.2

3.8.2 电场强度与带电粒子偏转

平行板匀强近似忽略边缘效应；粒子电荷、质量及水平速度近似恒定。

Uniform parallel-plate approximation neglects edge effects. Treat the deflected particle’s charge, mass and horizontal speed as constant.

单位为 N C\(^{-1}\) 或 V m\(^{-1}\)；负电荷受力与场方向相反。

\(E=F/Q\); uniform \(E=\Delta V/d\) in magnitude; radial magnitude \(E=|Q|/(4\pi\varepsilon_0r^2)\); \(a=qE/m\).

3.10.2

3.10.2 磁场中的带电粒子与回旋加速器

匀强磁场、垂直入射、非相对论速率；平行磁场运动时磁力为零。

Assume uniform magnetic field, perpendicular entry and non-relativistic speed. If velocity is parallel to B, the magnetic force is zero.

磁力提供向心力；非相对论模型中回旋周期与速率无关。

\(F=|q|vB\) for perpendicular motion; \(r=mv/(|q|B)=p/(|q|B)\); \(T=2\pi m/(|q|B)\); \(f=|q|B/(2\pi m)\).
