简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

To maintain the required upward heat flow through the carpet, the heating system must operate at a higher temperature. The rate of heat loss to the ground depends on its temperature difference from the ground, so the higher system temperature increases the wasted heat transfer downwards.
