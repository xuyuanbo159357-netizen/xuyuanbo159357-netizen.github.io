U4/07 常用物理模型

热量收支与相变 / Temperature rise and latent heat

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

heater → temperature rise → latent heat → continuous flow → 热量收支与相变

推理顺序 / Reasoning

分开升温与相变，建立含热损的能量收支，连续流动用质量流率。

Separate temperature change from phase change; form an energy balance including losses; use mass flow rate for continuous flow.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.11.1

3.11.1 内能、热力学第一定律与比热容

流入系统的热量和外界对系统做功取正；升降温用比热容，恒温相变用潜热；有给定热损或容器热容时须计入。

Positive Q is heating into the system; positive work is done on the system. Heating/cooling without phase change uses c; phase change at constant temperature uses latent heat. Include heat loss or container heat capacity if given.

第一定律中的功为外界对系统所做的功；比热容单位 J kg\(^{-1}\) K\(^{-1}\)，比潜热单位 J kg\(^{-1}\)。

\(\Delta U=Q+W_{\mathrm{on}}\); temperature change \(Q=mc\Delta T\); state change \(Q=ml\); electrical input \(E=IVt\); continuous flow \(P=\dot m c\Delta T\).
