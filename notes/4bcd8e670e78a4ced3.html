U2 通用规范与有效数字 / General conventions and significant figures

U2 答题规范 / Exam answering conventions · 返回真题练习 / Past-paper practice

阅读方式 / How to use

本页区分物理原则、推荐书写及具体试题的评分要求；具体容差和扣分范围只用于所引题目。 / Distinguish physical principles, presentation advice and question-specific marking.

1. U2 的有效数字不能统一为两位 / U2 precision is question dependent

按题目指定精度作答；中间计算保留计算器精度，最终结果再取舍。 / Follow the requested precision and retain intermediate digits until the final result.

真题例证 / Evidence 本题具体要求 / Requirement in this question
2026 June Q07.1，质量 / Mass \(0.20\ \mathrm{kg}\)；不接受仅一位有效数字的 \(0.2\ \mathrm{kg}\)。 / 1 sf is not accepted.
2026 June Q10.1，条纹间距 / Fringe spacing \(4.32\ \mathrm{mm}\)；该结果分只接受三位有效数字。 / 3 sf only for the answer mark.
2026 June Q10.2，不确定度 / Uncertainty 该问最终百分比不确定度不接受超过两位。 / No more than 2 sf in the final percentage.
2026 June Q10.4，合成不确定度 / Combined uncertainty 合理处理可得到 \(6.8\%\) 或 \(7\%\)，最多两位。 / At most 2 sf, with values depending on the accepted route.

来源 / Sources：PH02 2026 June Q07.1 · MS PDF p12 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer；PH02 2026 June Q10.1–10.2 · MS PDF p19 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer；PH02 2026 June Q10.4 · MS PDF p20 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer。不能据此声称所有 U2 题都限定这些位数。 / Do not extend these restrictions to every U2 question.

2. 不提前截断电路或光学数据 / Avoid premature rounding

2017 June Q07.2 要看到 \(82\ \Omega\)，只写 \(80\ \Omega\) 不足；用检验法时，评分标准要求看到未取整的计算值。 / The cited potential-divider question requires 82 Ω rather than only 80 Ω, with unrounded checking values. PH02 2017 June Q07.2 · MS PDF p6 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

2026 June Q09.3 接受能取整为 \(710\) 或 \(715\ \mathrm m\) 的结果，不接受 \(720\ \mathrm m\)。因此临界角、折射率和传播速率等中间结果不要过早截位。 / Retain intermediate optical values to avoid an incorrect final rounding. PH02 2026 June Q09.3 · MS PDF p17 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

3. 单位、无量纲量和精确表达 / Units and exact forms

  • 区分 \(\mathrm{mA}\)、\(\mathrm{\mu A}\)、\(\mathrm{k\Omega}\) 与 SI 单位；\(\mathrm{eV}\) 转 \(\mathrm J\) 后再与 SI 形式的 \(h\) 配套。 / Convert current, resistance and energy units consistently.
  • 折射率、应变、效率比值等本身无量纲；百分数应带 \(\%\)。 / Dimensionless ratios need no physical unit; percentages need the percent sign.
  • 科学计数法可清楚显示精度，但不是“超过两位必须使用”。数值求解一般给小数或科学计数法；要求精确式、比值或选择题选项时，分数和根式可以保留。 / Standard form helps communicate precision, but exact fractions and surds remain valid where appropriate.
  • 真实反例：2026 June Q13 的正确选项是 \(3\sqrt6\%\)。因此不能规定“所有答案不得有根号”。 / The cited multiple-choice answer explicitly contains a surd. PH02 2026 June Q13 · MS PDF p23 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer
精度复述 / Precision recall

\(0.20\) 有两位有效数字，\(0.2\) 只有一位；末尾零可表达精度，不能随意删去。 / The trailing zero in 0.20 communicates a second significant figure.

过程保留精度不是把测量当作精确值；最终仍需按题目和数据精度报告。 / Retaining digits does not remove measurement uncertainty.

同一大题的数据沿用 / Carrying answers into later parts

课堂统一答题规范 / Classroom convention

同一大题内，后续小问用到前面小问的答案数据时，直接使用前问已写出的答案数值，不改用计算器内保留的更精确值。前问若为证明题，后续直接使用题目要求证明的数值。

Within the same question, use the numerical answer reported in the earlier part when it is required in a later part, rather than a more precise calculator value. If the earlier part is a show-that question, use the value stated in that question for later parts.

区分两个阶段：同一小问内部保留中间精度；跨小问沿用已报告的答案或题给证明值。 / Within one part, retain intermediate precision; between parts, carry forward the reported answer or the stated show-that value.

这是本知识库采用的课堂统一约定；原评分标准有时还接受其他跟进路线，不将本约定表述成考局所有题目的唯一评分规则。若题目另给指定数据，以该明确指令为准。 / This is the classroom convention used in this vault. Some mark schemes also accept other follow-through routes; it is not presented as the board's sole permitted method. Follow any explicit value separately specified in the question.

2026 June Q10.1 的答案为 \(w=4.32\ \mathrm{mm}\)；Q10.3 用此答案换成 \(4.32\times10^{-3}\ \mathrm m\) 代入波长关系，不另用 \(38.9/9\) 的更多位小数。 / Carry the reported spacing, with the required unit conversion, into the wavelength calculation.
