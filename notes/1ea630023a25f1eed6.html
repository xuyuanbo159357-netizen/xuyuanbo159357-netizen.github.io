简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

Work is done on the gas, so the first law ΔU = Q + W requires an equal heat transfer out to keep ΔU zero. But heat cannot leave for surroundings at exactly the same temperature. The gas must first become slightly warmer, so its temperature cannot remain exactly constant during the work.
