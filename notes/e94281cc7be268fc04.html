考纲 / Specification： 3.12.1　｜　本单元知识谱系

本节讨论原子核半径 / nuclear radius。核半径通常为 \(10^{-15}\)–\(10^{-14}\,\mathrm m\)；原子半径约为 \(10^{-10}\,\mathrm m\)，二者尺度不同。 / This section concerns nuclear radius, typically \(10^{-15}\)–\(10^{-14}\,\mathrm m\). An atomic radius is of order \(10^{-10}\,\mathrm m\).

1. α 粒子最接近距离法 / Alpha-particle closest approach

1.1 公式与物理规律 / Formulae and physical laws

\[\Large\displaystyle E_k=\frac{(2e)(Ze)}{4\pi\varepsilon_0r_{\min}}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(E_{k}\) 动能 / kinetic energy \(\mathrm{J}\)
\(e\) 元电荷大小 / elementary charge \(\mathrm{C}\)
\(Z\) 质子数、原子序数 / proton number, atomic number 无量纲 / dimensionless
\(\varepsilon_{0}\) 真空介电常量 / permittivity of free space \(\mathrm{F\,m^{-1}}\)
\(r_{\min}\) α 粒子到核中心的最接近距离 / closest centre-to-alpha separation \(\mathrm{m}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

正面接近时，α 粒子初动能转为静电势能，在最近距离处瞬时停止。

In a head-on approach, an alpha particle’s initial kinetic energy becomes electrostatic potential energy at its turning point.

适用条件 / Conditions

忽略核反冲及辐射损失，最近点仍适用库仑相互作用。

Neglect nuclear recoil and radiation losses and use Coulomb interaction at the turning point.

1.2 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

Conditions / 条件与范围

最接近模型假设 α 粒子从远处正对原子核入射，并忽略原子核反冲。算出的是核中心与 α 粒子之间的折返距离；是否接近真实核表面需要另行判断。

The model assumes an initially distant alpha particle on a head-on trajectory and neglects nuclear recoil. The result is the centre-to-alpha separation at the turning point; proximity to the actual nuclear surface needs further evidence.

α粒子最接近距离 / Alpha closest approach

核半径通常为数个 \(10^{-15}\) m，原子半径约 \(10^{-10}\) m。头对头入射、重核近似固定且只有静电作用时，\(E_{k,\alpha}=\dfrac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\dfrac{(2e)(Ze)}{r_{\min}}\)；该点径向速率为零。

Nuclear radii are a few femtometres, much smaller than atomic radii. For a head-on alpha particle and a heavy stationary nucleus, initial kinetic energy converts to electrostatic potential energy at closest approach.

若 α 粒子在核外就折返，\(r_{\min}\) 可能比真实核半径大得多，粒子尚未到达核表面。非正碰时仍有横向运动，不能把总动能设为零。

An alpha particle can turn back well outside the nuclear surface, so the closest-approach distance can greatly exceed the actual nuclear radius. In a non-head-on encounter, transverse motion remains and total kinetic energy is not zero.

最接近距离估算的局限 / Limits of the closest-approach estimate

读图 / Reading： 算出的距离从铅核中心量到 α 粒子的折返点，不是图中蓝色原子核的实际半径。 / The calculated distance runs from the lead-nucleus centre to the alpha particle’s turning point, rather than measuring the blue nucleus itself.

评分要点 / Scoring point 为什么影响核半径估算 / Why it matters
折返点可能远离核表面 / The turning point can lie well outside the nucleus. α 粒子尚未到达原子核，算出的距离可能比真实核半径大得多。 / The alpha particle may not reach the nucleus, so the calculated distance may greatly exceed nuclear radius.
结果取决于入射 α 粒子的动能 / The result depends on incident alpha-particle kinetic energy. 对同一个核，入射动能越大，α 粒子能克服静电排斥而接近得越近；真实核半径不会因此改变。 / For the same nucleus, greater incident kinetic energy permits a closer approach, while the nuclear radius is unchanged.
计算忽略原子核反冲 / The calculation neglects nuclear recoil. 将重核近似固定，未计入其反冲动能；若考虑反冲，能量应在整个体系中分配。 / Treating the heavy nucleus as stationary omits its recoil kinetic energy. Including recoil requires an energy balance for the whole system.
库仑估算取决于质子数 Z，而非核子数 A / The Coulomb estimate depends on proton number Z rather than nucleon number A. 静电作用由核电荷决定；经验核半径规律则与核子数的立方根有关，不能把两种依赖关系混为一谈。 / Electrostatic interaction depends on nuclear charge, whereas the empirical radius law depends on the cube root of nucleon number.

答题注意 / Exam wording： 在 PH04 202606 Q03.2 中，评分标准明确不接受仅写 “upper limit”。应说明“α 粒子没有到达原子核，最接近距离可能比真实核半径大得多”等具体物理原因；该题从上述要点中给出两点。 / For PH04 202606 Q03.2, a bare “upper limit” is not accepted. State the physical reason, for example that the alpha particle can turn back before reaching the nucleus and the calculated separation may greatly exceed its radius. The question awards two of the listed points.

真题依据 / Mark-scheme evidence： PH04 202606 Q03.2；对应评分标准 PDF 第 10 页。

2. 电子衍射确定核半径 / Nuclear radius from electron diffraction

2.1 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

电子衍射与强度图 / Electron diffraction and intensity

电子的德布罗意波长需与核尺度可比才能探测核结构；由散射强度—角度图上的衍射极小位置估算半径。第一极小角越大，在相同电子波长下所推得核半径越小。

Electrons need a wavelength comparable with nuclear size. Use the diffraction minima on an intensity–angle graph to estimate radius; at fixed wavelength, a larger first-minimum angle indicates a smaller nucleus.

若采用学习资料给出的圆孔近似，\(R\sin\theta_{\min}\approx0.61\lambda\)，要明确 \(R\) 为半径而非直径，并按题给模型使用。高能电子优先用 \(\lambda=h/p\)；只有在超相对论近似 \(E\approx pc\) 下才可用 \(\lambda\approx hc/E\)，不能与低速 \(p=mv\) 无条件画等号。

In the slide's aperture approximation, distinguish radius from diameter. Always start with \(\lambda=h/p\); \(hc/E\) applies only when the stated high-energy approximation \(E\approx pc\) is valid.

U4 Nuclear electron diffraction

核电子衍射定性示意，并非实测数据或需记忆公式；同电子波长时，大核特征极小角更小。 Qualitative size comparison, not measured data.

电子衍射的核尺寸信息 / Nuclear size from electron diffraction

读图 / Reading： 改变探测角得到强度分布，衍射特征与电子波长及散射体尺寸有关；不能把图中的薄金属靶当成单个放大的原子核。

Angular intensity reveals diffraction related to wavelength and nuclear size; the thin target is a sample, not a magnified individual nucleus.

Applications / 应用与实验

使用题给衍射关系或图像求核半径，并用核子数立方根比比较核尺寸。

Use a given diffraction relation or graph to find radius; compare nuclei through \(R_2/R_1=(A_2/A_1)^{1/3}\).

3. 核半径、核子数与核密度 / Nuclear radius, nucleon number and density

3.1 关键概念与定义 / Key concepts and definitions

nuclear radius — 核半径

Definition / 定义

English: A characteristic size of the region occupied by a nucleus.

中文： 原子核所占区域的特征尺度。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式

定义按考纲与指定教材概括；本页未列出可直接对应的定义题评分条目。 Definitions are summarised from the specification and textbook; no directly matched definition-item citation is claimed here.

\[\Large\displaystyle R=R_0A^{1/3}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(R\) 核半径 / nuclear radius \(\mathrm{m}\)
\(R_{0}\) 核半径常量 / nuclear-radius constant \(\mathrm{m}\)
\(A\) 核子数、质量数 / nucleon number, mass number 无量纲 / dimensionless

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

核半径近似随核子数的立方根增大，反映不同原子核具有近似相同核子数密度。

Nuclear radius scales approximately with the cube root of nucleon number, consistent with approximately constant nucleon density.

核半径的图像检验 / Testing the nuclear-radius law

读图 / Reading： R–A¹ᐟ³ 图斜率为 R₀；lnR–lnA 图斜率为 1/3。先看坐标轴变量，不能把两图斜率含义交换。

The gradient of R against A¹ᐟ³ is R₀; the lnR–lnA gradient is 1/3. Identify axes before interpreting slopes.

3.2 公式与物理规律 / Formulae and physical laws

\[\Large\displaystyle V_{\mathrm{nucleus}}=\frac{4}{3}\pi R^3\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(V_{\mathrm{nucleus}}\) 原子核体积 / nuclear volume \(\mathrm{m^3}\)
\(R\) 核半径 / nuclear radius \(\mathrm{m}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

把原子核近似为球体，可由核半径求核体积。

Approximating the nucleus as a sphere gives its volume from its radius.

\[\Large\displaystyle \rho\approx\frac{Am_u}{\frac{4}{3}\pi R^3}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(\rho\) 质量密度 / mass density \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}\)
\(A\) 核子数、质量数 / nucleon number, mass number 无量纲 / dimensionless
\(m_{u}\) 原子质量常量 / atomic mass constant \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(R\) 核半径 / nuclear radius \(\mathrm{m}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

以核子数乘原子质量单位估算核质量，再除以球形体积求核密度。

Estimate nuclear mass from nucleon number and atomic mass unit, then divide by spherical volume to obtain nuclear density.

适用条件 / Conditions

采用球形核及近似核子质量。

Use a spherical nucleus and approximate nucleon mass.

3.3 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

适用范围与条件 / Scope and conditions

Conditions / 适用条件： 核半径约 10⁻¹⁵ 至 10⁻¹⁴ m；正碰最接近距离假设初动能转为静电势能。R₀ 是核半径常量，A 是核子数。 / Nuclear radii are about 10⁻¹⁵ to 10⁻¹⁴ m; head-on closest approach converts initial kinetic energy to electrostatic potential energy. R₀ is a constant and A is nucleon number.

核子数标度与密度 / Nucleon scaling and density

实验 \(R=R_0A^{1/3}\)，因此 \(R^3\propto A\)；作 \(R\)–\(A^{1/3}\) 为过原点直线，或 \(\ln R\)–\(\ln A\) 斜率为 \(1/3\)。\(R_0\) 用题给值，数量级为 \(10^{-15}\) m。

Radius scales with the cube root of nucleon number. A log–log plot has gradient one third; use the value of the empirical constant supplied.

球形核 \(\rho\approx Au/[(4/3)\pi R^3]=3u/(4\pi R_0^3)\)，与 \(A\) 近似无关，为核物质密度近似恒定的证据；注意立方换算单位。

Substituting the empirical radius into mass over volume makes nucleon number cancel, supporting approximately constant nuclear density.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.12.1

U4 Nuclear size density

核半径立方根规律与近似恒定核密度。 Nuclear volume is proportional to nucleon number.

Common errors / 易错点

核密度不能使用原子半径；最接近距离不自动等于核半径。

Do not use atomic radius in a nuclear-density calculation. A closest-approach distance is not automatically the measured nuclear radius.

考点精讲：最接近距离为何不等于核半径 / Why closest approach is not the nuclear radius

α粒子在最接近点仍可能离核表面很远，估算出的距离可显著大于真实核半径。它还随入射α粒子的动能及靶核质子数改变；简单计算常忽略靶核反冲。讨论方法局限时应给出这些具体原因，不能只写“upper limit”。电子衍射提供的是不同的尺寸证据。

An alpha particle can turn around well outside the nuclear surface, so closest approach may be much greater than the true radius. It depends on incident kinetic energy and target proton number, and simple calculations neglect nuclear recoil. Give these specific limitations rather than only “upper limit”. Electron diffraction provides different size evidence.

考点精讲：核半径直线检验与单位 / Linear test of nuclear radius and units

先按每个核素的核子数计算其三分之一次方，再与核半径配对作图。应注明半径单位及核子数变换后的无量纲坐标，采用充分展开数据的标度并画最佳拟合直线。说明“接近过原点的直线支持所给关系”，并指出斜率对应核半径常量；不能仅因画出了直线就宣称关系已被证明。

Calculate the cube root of nucleon number for each nuclide and plot it against the matching radius. Label the radius unit and dimensionless transformed axis, spread the data and draw a best-fit line. A near-linear trend through the origin supports the proposed relation; its gradient gives the radius constant. A drawn line alone is not proof.
