简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

Each photon transfers all its energy to one electron. An electron emitted from the surface can have the maximum kinetic energy hf − ϕ. Other electrons lose extra energy while reaching the surface, so they emerge with smaller kinetic energies.
