U3/06 真题练习/答题规范

U3 通用规范与有效数字 / General conventions and significant figures

U3 答题规范 / Exam answering conventions · 返回真题练习 / Past-paper practice

阅读方式 / How to use

本页区分物理原则、推荐书写及具体试题的评分要求；具体容差和扣分范围只用于所引题目。 / Distinguish physical principles, presentation advice and question-specific marking.

1. 精度由题目决定 / Precision is question dependent

中间计算保留精度，按题目给定精度报告最终量；两位有效数字不是固定模板。 / Retain intermediate precision and use the final precision appropriate to the question.

2018 June Q02.1 的力接受二或三位有效数字；Q02.3 的功接受 \(1050\) 或 \(1045\ \mathrm J\)，若写 \(1000\ \mathrm J\)，须明确是两位有效数字。用 \(1.0\times10^3\ \mathrm J\) 可清楚表明末尾零的有效性。 / Standard form removes ambiguity about the precision of trailing zeros. PH03 2018 June Q02.1–02.3 · MS PDF p5 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

科学计数法有利于场量、电荷和核计数的书写，但不要求所有超过两位的数一律改写。 / Standard form is useful for extreme values, rather than mandatory beyond 2 sf.

2. 大小、方向与负号 / Magnitudes, directions and signs

  • 问 magnitude / 大小时给非负值；若还问方向，另写清物理方向。 / Give a non-negative magnitude and state direction separately if requested.
  • 问 change / 变化量时，先写终态减初态，再判断正负，不能只报告两数之差的绝对值。 / Calculate final minus initial rather than automatically taking an absolute difference.
  • 2026 June Q03.2 求力的大小时，评分标准容许忽略负号；Q04.2 势能变化则有独立负号分。两问要求不同。 / The sign treatment differs between a force magnitude and a change in potential energy. PH03 2026 June Q03.2 · MS PDF p9 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer；PH03 2026 June Q04.2 · MS PDF p13 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

3. 单位和无量纲量 / Units and dimensionless quantities

量 / Quantity 书写检查 / Reporting check
Gravitational potential / 引力势 \(\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}}\)；不是势能的 J。 / Do not confuse potential with potential energy.
Electric potential / 电势 V 或 \(\mathrm{J\,C^{-1}}\)；不是电荷或电场强度。 / Distinguish potential, charge and field strength.
Relative permittivity / 相对介电常数 无单位；不能随意加 F 或 \(\mathrm{F\,m^{-1}}\)。 / No physical unit.
Oscilloscope settings / 示波器设置 \(\mathrm{V\,div^{-1}}\)、\(\mathrm{ms\,div^{-1}}\)；不是仅 V、ms。 / State the value per division.

2026 June Q06.2 给相对介电常数加单位会失去该结果分；Q06.4 的单位要求包含每格，缺失最多处罚一次。处罚范围仅指这两问。 / These are explicit restrictions in the cited rubric. PH03 2026 June Q06.2 · MS PDF p17 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer；PH03 2026 June Q06.4 · MS PDF p18 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

4. 最终数值与精确表达 / Numerical and exact answers

要求数值时完成数值运算并给单位；推导、比例和精确式可以保留 \(\pi\)、分数或根式。不要把尚未求值的计算器表达式当作所有数值题的最终答案。 / Evaluate numerical questions; exact algebraic forms remain appropriate when requested.

快速复述 / Quick recall

“A negative change in gravitational potential energy means the final potential energy is lower. It does not mean that the magnitude of the change is negative.”

势能变化为负表示末态势能更低；变化量与变化大小不是同一问法。

同一大题的数据沿用 / Carrying answers into later parts

课堂统一答题规范 / Classroom convention

同一大题内，后续小问用到前面小问的答案数据时，直接使用前问已写出的答案数值，不改用计算器内保留的更精确值。前问若为证明题，后续直接使用题目要求证明的数值。

Within the same question, use the numerical answer reported in the earlier part when it is required in a later part, rather than a more precise calculator value. If the earlier part is a show-that question, use the value stated in that question for later parts.

区分两个阶段：同一小问内部保留中间精度；跨小问沿用已报告的答案或题给证明值。 / Within one part, retain intermediate precision; between parts, carry forward the reported answer or the stated show-that value.

这是本知识库采用的课堂统一约定；原评分标准有时还接受其他跟进路线，不将本约定表述成考局所有题目的唯一评分规则。若题目另给指定数据，以该明确指令为准。 / This is the classroom convention used in this vault. Some mark schemes also accept other follow-through routes; it is not presented as the board's sole permitted method. Follow any explicit value separately specified in the question.

U3 例证 / Unit-specific example

2026 June Q04.1 要证明质量约 \(2000\ \mathrm{kg}\)；按本库约定，Q04.2 直接使用 \(2000\ \mathrm{kg}\)，不使用更精确的 \(1562\ \mathrm{kg}\)。原评分标准明确接受 \(2000\ \mathrm{kg}\)，同时也列出其他跟进值。 / Use the stated show-that mass in the later part; the rubric explicitly accepts it.
