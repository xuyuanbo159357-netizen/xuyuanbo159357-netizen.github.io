关系摘要

3.1.2 物理测量的局限 / Limitation of physical measurements

误差与测量质量、不确定度与传播规则、误差棒与图像不确定度、有效数字与结果表达。

知识节点 / Knowledge nodes

  • M04 误差与测量质量 - Errors and measurement quality：准确度看真值接近程度，精密度看数据分散程度。Accuracy and precision describe different properties.
  • M05 不确定度与传播规则 - Uncertainty and propagation：加减加绝对不确定度，乘除加相对不确定度。Use absolute uncertainties for sums and relative uncertainties for products.
  • M06 误差棒与图像不确定度 - Error bars and graph uncertainty：最佳直线给出估计值，允许的极端直线给出梯度和截距范围。Use a best line and acceptable extreme lines.
  • M07 有效数字与结果表达 - Significant figures and reporting：报告精度与数据及不确定度一致。Reported precision must match the evidence.

2 Precision, repeatability, reproducibility, resolution and accuracy. 精密度、重复性、再现性、分辨率和准确度。 M04 误差与测量质量 PDF 10／印刷 11
3 Use of absolute, fractional and percentage uncertainties to represent uncertainty in the final answer for a quantity. 用绝对不确定度、相对不确定度和百分比不确定度表示物理量最终结果的不确定度。 M05 不确定度与传播规则 PDF 10／印刷 11
4 Combination of absolute and percentage uncertainties where measurements are added, subtracted, multiplied, divided, or raised to powers. 测量值相加、相减、相乘、相除或乘方时，合成绝对不确定度和百分比不确定度。 M05 不确定度与传播规则 PDF 10／印刷 11
5 Combinations involving trigonometric or logarithmic functions will not be required. 不要求涉及三角函数或对数函数的不确定度合成。 M04 误差与测量质量；M05 不确定度与传播规则；M06 误差棒与图像不确定度；M07 有效数字与结果表达 PDF 10／印刷 11
6 Representation of uncertainty in a data point on a graph using error bars. 用误差棒表示图上数据点的不确定度。 M06 误差棒与图像不确定度 PDF 10／印刷 11
7 Determine the uncertainties in the gradient and intercept of a straight-line graph for graphs with or without associated error bars. 对于有或无误差棒的直线图，确定斜率和截距的不确定度。 M06 误差棒与图像不确定度 PDF 10／印刷 11
8 In practical work students should understand the link between the number of significant figures in the value of a quantity and its associated uncertainty. 在实验中理解物理量数值的有效数字位数与其不确定度之间的联系。 M07 有效数字与结果表达 PDF 10／印刷 11
