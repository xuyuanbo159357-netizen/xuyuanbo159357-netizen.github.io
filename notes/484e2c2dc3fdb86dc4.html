U4/07 常用物理模型

反应堆中子控制 / Moderator and control rod

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

moderator → control rod → coolant → shutdown → 反应堆中子控制

推理顺序 / Reasoning

分清慢化、吸收和移热作用，说明材料选择与中子收支；停堆后仍需冷却。

Assign slowing, absorption and heat-removal functions separately; explain material choice and neutron balance; retain cooling after shutdown.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.12.3

3.12.3 诱发裂变、链式反应与反应堆

采用热中子反应堆模型；临界性与中子损失、材料、形状和环境有关。

Use a thermal-neutron reactor model. Criticality depends on neutron losses, material, geometry and surrounding conditions, not a universal mass value.

裂变方程满足核子数与电荷守恒；总功率由每次裂变能量与裂变率决定。

Balance total nucleon number and charge in fission equations; reaction energy \(=\Delta mc^2\); thermal power \(=(\text{fissions per second})(\text{energy per fission})\).

3.12.4

3.12.4 核反应堆安全与放射性废物

只需一般安全原则，无需特定堆型细节；废物管理需考虑活度、辐射类型、半衰期和发热。

Describe general safety principles; details of particular reactor designs are not required. Waste handling/storage depends on activity, radiation type, half-life and heat generation.

需要时把活度随时间变化、距离与辐射强度关系用于安全分析。

Relate activity reduction to half-life and source distance to radiation intensity where appropriate.
