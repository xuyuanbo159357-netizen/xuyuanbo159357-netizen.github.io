简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

The electron and positron annihilate and produce two gamma photons travelling in opposite directions. Each photon has an energy of 0.511 + 0.340 = 0.851 MeV. The total photon energy equals the total rest energy plus kinetic energy before the collision.
