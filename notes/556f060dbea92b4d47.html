简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

The initial momentum is zero, so the products have equal momentum magnitudes and opposite directions. Using masses 8u and 4u gives 8u vBe = 4u vα, hence vα = 2vBe. Their energy ratio is Eα/EBe = [½(4u)(2vBe)²]/[½(8u)vBe²] = 2. The alpha particle therefore has twice the kinetic energy of the beryllium nucleus.
