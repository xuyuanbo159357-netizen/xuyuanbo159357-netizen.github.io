Concept / 概念

resistivity — 电阻率

Definition / 定义

English: A material property equal to the resistance of a uniform specimen multiplied by its cross-sectional area and divided by its length.

中文： 均匀试样的电阻与横截面积的乘积除以长度，描述材料本身的性质。
