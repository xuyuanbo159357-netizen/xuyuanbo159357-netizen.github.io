U2/07 常用物理模型

电源内阻与图像截距 / EMF and internal resistance

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

emf → terminal pd → internal resistance → gradient → 电源内阻与图像截距

推理顺序 / Reasoning

用路端电压式；截距求电动势、负斜率求内阻；总电流越大内损越大。

Use \(V=\varepsilon-Ir\); a voltage–current line gives emf at the intercept and resistance from the negative gradient; increasing load current increases internal loss.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.4.6

3.4.6 电动势与内阻

以上符号用于电源放电供电；开路时路端电势差等于电动势；内阻电流为总电流。

These signs refer to a source supplying current. On open circuit, \(I=0\) and \(V=\varepsilon\). Use the total current through the source, not a branch current.

电动势分配为路端电势差和内电势降。

\(\varepsilon=E/Q=I(R+r)\); \(V=\varepsilon-Ir\); \(I=\varepsilon/(R+r)\); lost volts \(=Ir\); internal power loss \(=I^2r\).
