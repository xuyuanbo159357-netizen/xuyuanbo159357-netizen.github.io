关系 1 / Relation 1

\[\Large\displaystyle V=\mathcal E-Ir\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量 / Quantity SI 单位 / SI unit
\(V\) 电势差 / potential difference \(\mathrm{V}\)

物理关系 / Physical relationship： 电源对外供电时，端电压比电动势少内部电压降。 / When supplying current, terminal voltage is emf minus the internal voltage drop.

These signs refer to a source supplying current. On open circuit, \(I=0\) and \(V=\varepsilon\). Use the total current through the source, not a branch current.
