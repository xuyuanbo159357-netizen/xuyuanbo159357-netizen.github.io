3.10.3 磁通量与磁通链 / Magnetic flux and flux linkage

考纲 / Specification： 3.10.3　｜　本单元知识谱系

Definition / 定义

↗ magnetic flux — 磁通量

Definition / 定义

English: The product of magnetic flux density and the area perpendicular to the field, for a uniform field.

中文： 匀强磁场中，磁感应强度与垂直于场方向的面积之积。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

↗ flux linkage — 磁通链

Definition / 定义

English: The sum of the magnetic flux through all the turns of a coil.

中文： 线圈各匝磁通量之和。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

↗ weber — 韦伯

Definition / 定义

English: The magnetic flux through one square metre perpendicular to a uniform field of one tesla.

中文： 一特斯拉匀强磁场垂直穿过一平方米面积时的磁通量。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式与单位

角度为磁场与线圈平面法线之间夹角。

\(\Phi=BA\cos\theta\); \(N\Phi=NBA\cos\theta\) for equal flux per turn.

Conditions / 条件与范围

磁场均匀、线圈平面；乘匝数需每匝磁通相同。

Uniform B and a flat coil; multiplying by N assumes every turn links the same flux.

Explanation / 解释

磁通量、磁通链和角度 / Flux, linkage and angles

均匀磁场中平面面积的磁通量 \(\Phi=BA\cos\theta\)，\(\theta\) 是磁场与面积法线的夹角；\(\Phi\) 的单位 Wb。若给磁场与平面的夹角，应改用正弦。磁场垂直平面时通量最大，平行平面时为零。

Flux through a flat area in a uniform field is \(BA\cos\theta\), where the angle is to the area normal. Use sine if the angle is to the plane. Flux is maximum for field normal to the plane and zero for parallel field.

\(N\) 匝每匝磁通相同时磁通链 \(\Psi=N\Phi\)，单位 Wb turn（turn无量纲）。匝数不改变一匝本身的磁通量，却改变总磁通链。

If each turn has equal flux, linkage is \(N\Phi\). Increasing turns changes linkage, not the flux through a single turn.

匀速转动且 \(t=0\) 法线平行磁场时，\(\Psi=NBA\cos\omega t\)。先定义角度和初始位置，后续感应电动势才可正确确定相位和符号。

For uniform rotation from maximum linkage, use \(NBA\cos\omega t\). State the reference orientation before assigning phases or signs.

U3 Flux linkage emf

匀速转动线圈的磁通链和电动势；电动势为磁通链的负变化率。 Uniformly rotating coil.

Applications / 应用与实验

磁场、面积或方向变化均可改变磁通链；先读磁通链图，再由斜率判断感应电动势。

Changes in B, area or orientation can change flux linkage and lead to induction. Read a flux-linkage–time graph before differentiating it.

Common errors / 易错点

给线圈平面夹角时，需先换算为法线夹角。

If an angle is given to the coil plane rather than its normal, convert the angle before using cosine.

依据本库现有真题与评分标准核对。下列步骤按题型使用，先核对题设条件；综合题需结合其他关联知识点。 / Checked against the papers and mark schemes held in this vault; select the approach that fits the stated conditions.

3.10.3 方法 1 · 磁通、角度与图像面积 / Flux, angles and graph areas

  1. \(\Phi=BA\cos\theta\)，\(\theta\) 是磁场与面积法线的夹角；题给平面夹角时换为正弦。磁通链 \(N\Phi\) 只乘一次匝数。 / Define the angle and distinguish flux from flux linkage.
  2. 变化量取 \(NBA(\cos\theta_f-\cos\theta_i)\)，不能用 \(NBA\cos(\theta_f-\theta_i)\)。 / Subtract the two linkages.
  3. \(\varepsilon=-d(N\Phi)/dt\)：切线斜率给瞬时电动势，端点差除时间给平均电动势；一个区间端点磁通链相同，平均可为零，瞬时值仍不为零。 / Distinguish mean from instantaneous emf.
  4. \(-\int\varepsilon\,dt=\Delta(N\Phi)\)，线圈进入、离开同一场的脉冲有符号面积应相应抵消。 / Use signed pulse areas as a consistency check.
  5. \(1\,\mathrm{Wb}=1\,\mathrm{V\,s}\)，磁通链单位可写Wb（匝无量纲），基本单位 \(\mathrm{kg\,m^2\,s^{-2}\,A^{-1}}\)。 / Keep tesla and weber distinct.
