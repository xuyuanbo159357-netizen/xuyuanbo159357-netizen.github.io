3.5.3 行波、相位与波参数 / Progressive waves

考纲 / Specification： 3.5.3　｜　本单元知识谱系

知识逐项讲解 / Concept-by-concept guide

progressive wave — 行波

Definition / 定义

↗ progressive wave — 行波

Definition / 定义

English: A travelling disturbance that transfers energy from one place to another without a net transfer of matter.

中文： 传播扰动把能量从一处传至另一处，不产生介质物质的净迁移。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

wavelength — 波长

Definition / 定义

↗ wavelength — 波长

Definition / 定义

English: The shortest distance between two points on a wave that oscillate in phase.

中文： 波上相邻同相振动点之间的最短距离。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式

\[\Large\displaystyle \lambda=\frac{c}{f}\]

\(\lambda\) 波长 / wavelength \(\mathrm{m}\)
\(c\) 波在该介质中的传播速率 / wave speed in the medium \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)
\(f\) 频率 / frequency \(\mathrm{Hz}\)

frequency — 频率

Definition / 定义

↗ frequency — 频率

Definition / 定义

English: The number of complete oscillations or cycles per unit time.

中文： 单位时间内完成的振动或周期数。

\(f\) 频率 / frequency \(\mathrm{Hz}\)
\(T\) 周期 / period \(\mathrm{s}\)

phase difference — 相位差

Definition / 定义

↗ phase difference — 相位差

Definition / 定义

English: The difference in the stages of the oscillation cycle of two oscillations.

中文： 两个振动在同一时刻所处周期阶段的差异。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式

\[\Large\displaystyle \Delta\phi=\frac{2\pi\Delta x}{\lambda}\]
\[\Large\displaystyle \Delta\phi=\frac{2\pi\Delta t}{T}\]

Symbols and SI units / 符号与 SI 单位

符号 / Symbol 物理量 / Quantity SI 单位 / SI unit
\(\Delta\phi\) 相位差 / phase difference \(\mathrm{rad}\)
\(\Delta x\) 沿传播方向的间距 / separation along propagation \(\mathrm{m}\)
\(\lambda\) 波长 / wavelength \(\mathrm{m}\)
\(\Delta t\) 对应的时间差 / corresponding time difference \(\mathrm{s}\)
\(T\) 周期 / period \(\mathrm{s}\)

Conditions / 条件与说明： 第一式用于同一行波中两点；相位差可按完整周期取模。 / The first equation applies to two points on the same progressive wave; phase can be compared modulo a full cycle.

定义按考纲与指定教材概括；本页未列出可直接对应的定义题评分条目。 Definitions are summarised from the specification and textbook; no directly matched definition-item citation is claimed here.

Formula / 公式与单位

Conditions / 适用条件： c 为介质中的波速；相位差公式取弧度，空间相位关系用于同一行波。 / c is wave speed in the medium; phase formulae use radians and the spatial relation applies to one progressive wave.

\[\Large\displaystyle c=f\lambda\]
\[\Large\displaystyle f=\frac{1}{T}\]
\[\Large\displaystyle \Delta\phi=\frac{2\pi\Delta x}{\lambda}\]
\[\Large\displaystyle \Delta\phi=\frac{2\pi\Delta t}{T}\]

物理量 / Quantity 符号 / Symbol SI单位 / SI unit 说明 / Notes

位移 / displacement \(s,\ x\) \(\mathrm{m}\) 一维正负号表示方向 / A sign gives direction in one dimension.

Conditions / 条件与范围

空间相位关系用于同一行波的不同位置；时间相位关系比较固定位置的周期阶段。

Spatial phase formula applies to points on the same progressive wave; time separation compares equivalent cycles at a fixed point.

Explanation / 解释

介质振动与能量传播 / Oscillation and propagation

行波传递能量而不造成介质的整体净迁移；介质粒子在各自平衡位置附近振动。机械波需要介质，电磁波是场的振动，不需要物质介质。

A progressive wave transfers energy without bulk transport of the medium. Particles oscillate around their equilibrium positions; electromagnetic waves are field oscillations and need no material medium.

位移—距离图是一瞬间的波形，读出 \(A\) 和 \(\lambda\)；位移—时间图是一粒子的振动，读出 \(A\) 和 \(T\)。\(f=\frac{1}{T}\)、\(c=f\lambda\)；波速不是粒子振动速度。

A displacement–distance graph gives amplitude and wavelength at an instant; a displacement–time graph gives amplitude and period for one point. Wave speed is different from particle velocity.

相位与相位差 / Phase and phase difference

相位表示一个振动在周期中的状态，不仅是位移大小，还包括运动方向。同相要求处于同一周期阶段；位移同为零的两点也可能反相。

Phase identifies the stage of an oscillation, including its direction of motion. Equal displacement alone does not imply equal phase.

同频行波同一时刻两点相距 \(\Delta x\)：\(\Delta\phi=2\pi\Delta x/\lambda\)；同一点相隔 \(\Delta t\)：\(\Delta\phi=2\pi\Delta t/T\)。一周期 \(=360^\circ=2\pi\,\mathrm{rad}\)；半周期为反相，整数周期为同相。

For a single-frequency progressive wave, phase differences follow \(2\pi\Delta x/\lambda\) or \(2\pi\Delta t/T\). Half a cycle is antiphase and whole cycles are in phase.

已知传播方向时，可把波形向前稍移判断某粒子接下来的位移方向；不可用波峰向右移动来断定介质粒子也向右移动。

Translate the waveform slightly in the propagation direction to infer a particle's next displacement; travelling crests do not imply bulk particle motion in that direction.

读图 / Reading： 这是同一时刻不同位置的位移；纵向最大位移是振幅，相邻同相点的水平间距是波长，不能从此图横轴直接读周期。

This is a spatial snapshot: vertical maximum displacement is amplitude and horizontal repeat distance is wavelength; its horizontal axis does not give period.

图示：同相与相位差 / Phase comparison

读图 / Reading： 比较两个振动在相同时间位置的状态；同频率不保证同相。相位差须给定两点、时间或同一参考。

Compare states at the same time; equal frequency does not guarantee phase agreement. Specify the points or a common time reference.

Applications / 应用与实验

从图像读取振幅、波长、相位，并用传播距离和时间求波速。

Read amplitude, wavelength and phase from graphs; use time of flight and distance to find speed.

Common errors / 易错点

振幅为平衡位置到波峰，非峰谷距离；反相点可相隔半波长。

Amplitude is measured from equilibrium to a crest, not crest to trough. Adjacent antiphase points can be half a wavelength apart.

真题检验与答题思路 / Exam coverage and approaches

依据本库现有真题与评分标准核对。下列步骤按题型使用，先核对题设条件；综合题需结合其他关联知识点。 / Checked against the papers and mark schemes held in this vault; select the approach that fits the stated conditions.

3.5.3 方法 1 · 传播图像、相位与脉冲 / Wave profiles, phase and pulses

  1. 区分介质粒子的运动与波形传播；把波形沿传播方向平移一小段，从同一粒子的新旧位移判断其瞬时运动方向。 / Translate the profile to determine particle motion.
  2. \(\Delta\phi=2\pi\Delta x/\lambda\)，有初始相位差时也要加上；相位相同可相差整数个 \(2\pi\)。 / Combine initial and propagation phase differences.
  3. 固定距离 \(L\) 上周期数为 \(fL/v\)；问频率增大后的下一次同相，要找下一个整数。 / Use the next integer cycle count.
  4. 载波周期、脉冲宽度、脉冲重复周期是不同量；\(N=f\Delta t\) 用的是题目对应的频率。 / Separate carrier frequency from pulse repetition frequency.
  5. 滚筒记录先由周长和转动周期求纸速，再把图上距离变为时间差，最后用波速求传播距离。 / Convert the recording scale before applying wave speed.
