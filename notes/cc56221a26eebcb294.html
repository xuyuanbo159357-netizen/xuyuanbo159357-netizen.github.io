简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

Measure h with a vertical metre rule and time the oscillations with a stopwatch. Use at least five values of h and find the period T at each. Time at least five oscillations, or at least 20 s of motion, then divide by the number of oscillations to reduce timing uncertainty.
