3.5.11 波粒二象性与德布罗意波长 / Wave–particle duality

考纲 / Specification： 3.5.11　｜　本单元知识谱系

Definition / 定义

↗ wave–particle duality — 波粒二象性

Definition / 定义

English: The behaviour of matter and radiation showing wave properties in some experiments and particle properties in others.

中文： 物质和辐射在不同实验中分别表现波动性和粒子性的行为。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

↗ de Broglie wavelength — 德布罗意波长

Definition / 定义

English: The wavelength associated with a moving particle.

中文： 与运动粒子相联系的波长。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

定义按考纲与指定教材概括；本页未列出可直接对应的定义题评分条目。 Definitions are summarised from the specification and textbook; no directly matched definition-item citation is claimed here.

Formula / 公式与单位

德布罗意波长与动量成反比；电子加速关系结合 U1 动能与动量公式。

\(\lambda=h/p=h/(mv)\) for non-relativistic particles. If accelerated from rest through \(V\), \(p=\sqrt{2meV}\).

Conditions / 条件与范围

使用非相对论动量和动能模型；不要求具体粒子衍射方法的细节。

Use \(p=mv\) and \(E_k=\tfrac12mv^2\) in the non-relativistic model. Details of particular particle-diffraction methods are not expected.

Explanation / 解释

证据与模型发展 / Evidence and changing models

干涉、衍射支持光的波动描述，光电效应支持能量以光子为单位传递。电子具有质量和电荷，表现出粒子性；电子衍射又表明物质粒子有波动性质。使用哪种描述由实验决定，不是把光子想成沿正弦轨迹运动的小球。

Interference and diffraction support a wave description of light; the photoelectric effect supports photons. Electrons have particle properties and show diffraction. The appropriate model is determined by experimental evidence.

物理模型会随新证据修正：经典波模型不能解释阈频，单纯经典粒子模型不能解释电子衍射。考纲不要求特定粒子衍射装置的细节。

New evidence revises models: a classical wave-only model cannot explain threshold frequency, while a classical particle-only model cannot explain electron diffraction. Specific diffraction apparatus details are excluded.

动量与衍射程度 / Momentum and diffraction

\(\lambda=h/p\)；非相对论粒子 \(p=mv\)，\(p=\sqrt{2mE_k}\)。电子从静止经电压 \(V\) 加速时，可在非相对论近似下用 \(E_k=eV\)，得到 \(\lambda=h/\sqrt{2meV}\)。

Use \(\lambda=h/p\). For nonrelativistic particles, \(p=mv=\sqrt{2mE_k}\); an electron accelerated from rest through a pd gains energy \(eV\).

同一衍射结构下，动量增大→波长减小→衍射角减小、环半径减小。不可把高能核衍射电子一律用 \(p=mv\)；若题目给出相对论关系，应使用题目关系。

For the same diffracting structure, larger momentum gives shorter wavelength and smaller diffraction angles or rings. Do not extend \(p=mv\) to relativistic electrons; use any relation supplied in the question.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.5.11

U2 de Broglie

德布罗意波长与动量成反比；固定衍射间距下，动量越大衍射角越小。 Wavelength is inversely proportional to momentum.

图示：电子衍射证据 / Electron diffraction

读图 / Reading： 薄金属箔后的环状图样说明电子有波动性；改变加速电压会改变动量及德布罗意波长，而不是改变电子电荷。

Rings after a thin foil show electron wave behaviour; changing accelerating voltage changes momentum and de Broglie wavelength, not charge.

Applications / 应用与实验

解释加速电压增加时衍射环变小，并准确表述实验支持的波动性质。

Explain smaller electron diffraction rings when accelerating voltage increases. Distinguish experimental evidence from a claim that particles simply become classical waves.

Common errors / 易错点

波长由动量决定；不同质量粒子即使速率相同，波长也不同。

The relevant quantity is momentum, not speed alone; particles of different masses can have different wavelengths at the same speed.

3.5.11 方法 1 · 加速粒子与衍射环 / Accelerated particles and diffraction rings

非相对论范围：\(|q|V=K=p^2/(2m)\)，因此 \(\lambda=h/\sqrt{2m|q|V}\)。比较不同粒子时先说明固定的是速度、动量还是动能。 / Identify the controlled quantity before taking ratios.

加速电压降低 → 动量减小 → 德布罗意波长增大 → 衍射角或环直径增大。在固定绝对直径读数误差下，较大环的相对误差较小。束流主要影响亮度。 / Give the full causal chain.

探测结构需波长与间距同数量级；若题目提供晶体的光栅模型，按给定模型计算，不把额外的布拉格方程列为本节记忆要求。 / Use the supplied structural model.
