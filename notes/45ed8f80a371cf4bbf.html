3.8.4 电容、介质与储能 / Capacitors

考纲 / Specification： 3.8.4　｜　本单元知识谱系

Definition / 定义

↗ capacitance — 电容

Definition / 定义

English: The charge stored on one plate of a capacitor per unit potential difference between its plates.

中文： 电容器一极板所储电荷与两极板电势差之比。

Formula / 公式与单位

极板电荷指一板电荷大小，非两板相加；法拉为库仑每伏特。

\(C=Q/V\); \(C=\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r A/d\); \(E=\frac12QV=\frac12CV^2=Q^2/(2C)\). Farad: C V\(^{-1}\).

Conditions / 条件与范围

平行板公式忽略边缘效应；线性电容两种坐标排列的三角形面积均为相同储能。

Parallel-plate equation assumes uniform field and negligible edge effects. For a linear capacitor, work/energy is the area under either the \(V\)–\(Q\) graph or the straight \(Q\)–\(V\) graph: the same triangle.

Explanation / 解释

电容、几何与介电常数 / Capacitance and geometry

\(C=Q/V\)，\(Q\) 为一块极板上电荷量的大小，两板等量异号，不能取两板电荷绝对值之和。固定结构的线性电容器 \(Q\propto V\)，\(Q\)–\(V\) 图斜率为 \(C\)。

Capacitance uses the magnitude of charge on one plate, not the sum of both plate magnitudes. A linear fixed capacitor has \(Q\propto V\) and a charge–pd gradient equal to capacitance.

大而平行、间距远小于板尺寸且忽略边缘效应时，\(C=\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r A/d\)。\(A\) 为重叠面积，\(\varepsilon_r=C/C_0\) 无单位；介质需填充所考虑的板间区域。

For large parallel plates with negligible edge effects, capacitance increases with overlap area and relative permittivity and decreases with spacing. Relative permittivity is dimensionless.

极性分子与两种约束 / Polar molecules and constraints

极性分子在外电场中受力矩而部分转向，形成抵消原场的极化场。相同自由电荷下净场和电势差减小，因此 \(C=Q/V\) 增大；不是电介质把两块板导通。

Polar molecules partly align in the field and produce an opposing polarisation field. At fixed free charge, net field and pd fall, increasing capacitance; the dielectric does not conduct between the plates.

保持接电池：\(V\) 固定，插入介质使 \(C,Q\) 增大。断开电池：\(Q\) 固定，\(C\) 增大而 \(V\) 减小。改变极板距离也先判断 \(Q\) 或 \(V\) 哪个固定，再用公式。

Connected to a battery, voltage is fixed and charge increases with capacitance. Disconnected, charge is fixed and voltage falls as capacitance increases. Identify the constraint before analysing any geometry change.

储能与能量来源 / Stored energy

充入微小电荷所需能量为 \(dE=V\,dQ\)，所以储能是 \(V\)–\(Q\) 图面积；对线性电容，\(E=\tfrac12QV=\tfrac12CV^2=Q^2/(2C)\)。\(Q\)–\(V\) 图的三角形面积也等于该数值，不能由此把任意充电过程都写成 \(E=QV\)。

Energy is the integral of pd with respect to charge. A linear capacitor stores half the product of final charge and pd; it does not store the full \(QV\) supplied by an ideal constant-voltage source in a simple resistive charging circuit.

接电池插入介质时储能随 \(C\) 增大；断开时储能随 \(C\) 增大而减小。解释能量变化时计入电源供能、介质受到的机械作用和可能损耗，不能把系统能量凭空增加。

Stored energy changes differently at fixed voltage and fixed charge. Include energy exchanged with the source and mechanical work when explaining the change.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.8.4

图示：储能为何有二分之一 / Why capacitor energy has a half

读图 / Reading： 充电中 V 随 Q 增大，V–Q 图下的三角形面积才是储能，不是末电压乘总电荷形成的矩形。

Voltage increases with charge, so stored energy is the triangular V–Q area, not the rectangle of final voltage times total charge.

U3 Capacitor energy

充电做功为电压—电荷图线下面积，线性电容对应三角形。 Capacitor energy from the V–Q area.

图示：充电的闭合外回路 / External charging circuit

读图 / Reading： 电子在外部导线移动，两板获得等量异号电荷；并不是电子穿过两板间绝缘层。

Electrons move in the external wires, producing equal opposite plate charges; they do not cross the insulating gap.

图示：电介质极化 / Dielectric polarisation

读图 / Reading： 介质内部正、负电荷发生微小相对位移，削弱内部合电场；保持电压还是保持电荷不变，会影响后续量的变化。

Small internal charge separations oppose the applied field; subsequent changes depend on whether voltage or charge is held fixed.

Applications / 应用与实验

比较板间距、介质和储能前，先确定电荷还是电压固定。

Identify whether charge or voltage is fixed before comparing plate separation, dielectric insertion and energy.

Common errors / 易错点

两板等量异号时整体净电荷为零，但储存电荷大小并非零。

The capacitor as a whole is electrically neutral when plate charges are equal and opposite; this does not mean its stored charge \(Q\) is zero.

3.8.4 方法 1 · 电容改变时的约束 / Constraints when capacitance changes

每次先问电源是否连接 / First identify whether the source remains connected.

状态 / State 保持量 / Fixed quantity 推理 / Reasoning
接理想电压源 / Connected \(V\) \(Q=CV\)，\(E=CV^2/2\)
已隔离 / Isolated \(Q\) \(V=Q/C\)，\(E=Q^2/(2C)\)
  1. \(C=\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r A/d\)，面积为实际重叠面积；长度单位换算后再平方。\(\varepsilon_r\) 无单位。 / Use overlap area and distinguish absolute from relative permittivity.
  2. 插入介质使极化产生反向附加场；固定自由电荷时净场和电压降低，所以电容增大。若电源仍连接，则补充电荷以保持电压。 / Distinguish reduced voltage from increased charge.
  3. 拉开隔离电容的极板需要外力做功，储能增加由外界提供；接源时需连同电源一起记能量账目。 / Include mechanical work and the source where relevant.
  4. 微音器固定 \(Q\) 时，板距减小 → \(C\) 增大 → \(V\) 降低；示波器交流振幅用 \((V_{max}-V_{min})/2\)。 / Separate the dc level from the ac amplitude.
