U2/07 常用物理模型

行波相位与回波计时 / Phase and echo

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

phase → wavelength → echo → ultrasound → 行波相位与回波计时

推理顺序 / Reasoning

区分时间图和距离图，把相位换成周期分数；回波往返路程需除二。

Distinguish time and distance graphs; express phase as a cycle fraction; for an echo divide the total travel distance by two.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.5.3

3.5.3 行波、相位与波参数

空间相位关系用于同一行波的不同位置；时间相位关系比较固定位置的周期阶段。

Spatial phase formula applies to points on the same progressive wave; time separation compares equivalent cycles at a fixed point.

相位差可用弧度、角度或周期分数表示。

\(c=f\lambda\); \(f=1/T\); \(\Delta\phi=2\pi\Delta x/\lambda=2\pi\Delta t/T\) in radians, or \(360^\circ\Delta x/\lambda\).

3.5.4

3.5.4 横波、纵波、偏振与超声

不要求马吕斯定律；所有电磁波在真空中速率相同，电场和磁场的振动为横向。

Malus’s law is not required. All electromagnetic waves have the same speed in vacuum; their electric and magnetic fields oscillate transversely.

往返超声时间对应的深度须除以二。

Echo distance \(d=ct/2\) for a pulse travelling to an interface and back.
