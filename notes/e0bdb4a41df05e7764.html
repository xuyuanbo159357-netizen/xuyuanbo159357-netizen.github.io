RP7 变压器效率 / Required practical 7 - Transformer efficiency

3.10.6 变压器、效率与高压输电

物理量与符号 / Quantities and symbols

符号沿用本节公式；若题目另有定义，以题设为准。 / These symbols follow this section; use any notation defined in the question.

物理量 / Quantity 符号 / Symbol SI单位 / SI unit 说明 / Notes
原、副线圈电势差 / primary and secondary voltage \(V_p,\ V_s\) \(\mathrm{V}\)
原、副线圈电流 / primary and secondary current \(I_p,\ I_s\) \(\mathrm{A}\)
线圈匝数 / number of turns \(N,\ N_p,\ N_s\) \(1\)（无量纲 / dimensionless） 计数；下标 p/s 为原/副线圈 / Count; p/s identify primary/secondary.

方法、分析与误差 / Method, analysis and uncertainty

1

初级接合适低压交流电源，次级接可控制负载。

Supply the primary with a suitable low-voltage alternating supply; connect a controlled load to the secondary.

2

用适用仪表测两侧有效电压和电流。

Measure primary and secondary rms voltages and currents using appropriate meters.

3

稳定或同时读数，计算输入输出功率比。

Calculate η=VsIs/(VpIp), keeping simultaneous/stable readings.

4

系统改变负载并重复，监测发热，固定铁芯和线圈。

Vary the load systematically, repeat and observe heating; keep the core/coil arrangement fixed.

5

解释铜损、涡流、磁滞与漏磁；降低损失与改变匝比不同。

Explain copper heating, eddy currents, hysteresis and flux leakage; distinguish reducing losses from changing the turns ratio.

定量分析与条件核对 / Quantitative analysis and controls

↗ 3.10.6 变压器、效率与高压输电 / The operation of a transformer

必做实验7 / Required practical 7

用低电压交流电源，分别测原、副线圈的rms电压和电流，对多个负载计算输入、输出功率与效率；控制频率、匝数和铁芯连接。

Use a low-voltage AC source and measure primary and secondary rms voltages and currents for several loads. Keep frequency, turns and core assembly fixed.

实际变压器有空载损耗，副线圈无输出电流并不意味着原线圈输入功率严格为零；讨论表计精度、接触与发热。

A real transformer can draw power even without a secondary load. Consider meter accuracy, connections and heating.
