3.1 测量及其误差 / MEASUREMENTS AND THEIR ERRORS

考纲章节原文 / Chapter text

考纲依据 / Source： 物理大纲.pdf · OxfordAQA 9630 · Version 4.4；PDF第10页／印刷第11页。

English: Content in this section is a continuing study for a student of physics. A working knowledge of the specified fundamental (base) units of measurement is vital. Likewise, practical work in the subject needs to be underpinned by an awareness of the nature of measurement errors and of their numerical treatment. The ability to carry through reasonable estimations is a skill that is required throughout the course and beyond.

中文： 本节内容贯穿物理学习；熟练掌握指定的测量基本单位十分重要。实验需要以理解测量误差的性质及其数值处理方法为基础；合理估算能力是整个课程及后续学习均需要的技能。
