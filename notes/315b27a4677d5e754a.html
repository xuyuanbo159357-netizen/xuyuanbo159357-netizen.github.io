3.10.2 磁场中的带电粒子与回旋加速器 / Moving charges in a magnetic field

考纲 / Specification： 3.10.2　｜　本单元知识谱系

知识逐项讲解 / Concept-by-concept guide

cyclotron — 回旋加速器

Definition / 定义

↗ cyclotron — 回旋加速器

Definition / 定义

English: A device using a magnetic field to bend charged particles and an alternating electric field to accelerate them repeatedly.

中文： 用磁场使带电粒子偏转、用交变电场反复加速的装置。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式与单位

Conditions / 适用条件： 速度垂直于磁场；取非相对论质量。磁力提供向心力但不做功；方向须考虑电荷正负。 / Velocity is perpendicular to field in the non-relativistic model. Magnetic force supplies centripetal force and does no work; include charge sign for direction.

\[\Large\displaystyle r=\frac{mv}{|q|B}\]
\[\Large\displaystyle r=\frac{p}{|q|B}\]

Conditions / 条件与范围

匀强磁场、垂直入射、非相对论速率；平行磁场运动时磁力为零。

Assume uniform magnetic field, perpendicular entry and non-relativistic speed. If velocity is parallel to B, the magnetic force is zero.

Explanation / 解释

粒子磁力与圆轨道 / Particle force and circular paths

速度垂直磁场时，磁力大小 \(F=B|q|v\)。正电荷用传统电流方向判断，负电荷反向；静止或沿磁场运动的电荷没有磁力。磁力垂直速度，不做功，只改变方向，不改变速率和动能。

For perpendicular motion the magnetic force is \(B|q|v\). Reverse the positive-charge force direction for a negative charge. Magnetic force does no work and does not change kinetic energy.

\(B|q|v=mv^2/r\)，\(r=mv/(B|q|)=p/(B|q|)\)。同一磁场中动量越大轨道越大；由偏转方向可判电荷符号。轨道一周时间 \(T=2\pi m/(B|q|)\) 是非相对论模型下的推论。

Magnetic force supplies the radial resultant. Orbit radius measures momentum per unit charge; curvature gives charge sign. The period expression assumes nonrelativistic motion.

回旋加速器与组合场 / Cyclotron and combined fields

回旋加速器：磁场使粒子在D形盒内作半圆运动，盒间交变电场每次加速，粒子速率和半径逐渐增大；磁场本身不提供动能。非相对论下调节电场频率为 \(f=B|q|/(2\pi m)\)，保证每次跨缝都受加速。

A cyclotron uses a magnetic field for bending and an alternating electric field across the gap for acceleration. Speed and radius increase; the magnetic field supplies no energy. The driving frequency matches the nonrelativistic orbit frequency.

若题目给交叉电磁场且粒子不偏转，应先判电力与磁力相反，再由 \(|q|E=B|q|v\) 得 \(v=E/B\)；它是受力平衡应用，连接已有速度选择模型。

For crossed fields and an undeflected particle, opposing electric and magnetic forces give \(v=E/B\). Check force directions before using the balance.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.10.2

图示：回旋加速器中的路径 / Cyclotron path

读图 / Reading： D 形盒内磁力转弯而不做功；粒子每次跨越间隙由电场加速，速率增加使圆弧半径增大。

Magnetic force bends motion inside the dees without doing work; the electric field accelerates particles across the gap, increasing orbit radius.

Applications / 应用与实验

由轨迹半径求动量或质量，区分电场加速和磁场偏转。

Find momentum or mass from curvature; distinguish electric acceleration from magnetic steering in particle devices.

Common errors / 易错点

磁场反向改变弯曲方向；平行入射不能用垂直受力公式。

Changing magnetic-field direction reverses curvature but does not reverse the particle’s speed. Do not use \(qvB\) when v is parallel to B.

3.10.2 方法 1 · 带电粒子、选择器与回旋加速 / Charged particles, selectors and cyclotrons

  1. \(\mathbf F=q\mathbf v\times\mathbf B\)；非零磁力只需速度有垂直于磁场的分量。磁力垂直速度，不做功，不改变动能。 / A perpendicular component is sufficient; motion need not be entirely perpendicular.
  2. 垂直入射圆轨道 \(r=p/(|q|B)\)；同速度 \(r\propto m/|q|\)，同动能 \(r\propto\sqrt{mK}/|q|\)。探测器半圆轨迹的落点距常是直径，先除二。 / State the controlled quantity and the geometric radius.
  3. 斜入射：\(v_\parallel\) 不变，\(v_\perp\) 决定半径，得到螺旋；非相对论回转周期 \(2\pi m/(|q|B)\) 与速率无关。 / Use the perpendicular speed, not total speed, for the radius.
  4. 速度选择器令电力与磁力等大反向，\(qE=qvB\Rightarrow v=E/B\)。速率改变时电力不变、磁力改变，再判断偏转方向。 / Explain both force changes.
  5. 回旋加速器：间隙电场增动能，dee内磁力改变方向；每半圈电压反相，半径随速率增大、角速率保持不变。每圈过间隙两次，峰值电压与峰峰值还要另行区分。 / Count gap crossings and voltage definitions.
  6. 云室螺旋变紧是电离损能使动量下降，不是磁场做负功。由相反弯曲推异号，由初始相似半径推相似动量；无轨迹的光子不因此“没有能量”。 / Tie each inference to a visible track feature.
