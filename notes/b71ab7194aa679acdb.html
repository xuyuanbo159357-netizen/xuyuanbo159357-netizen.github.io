U2/07 常用物理模型

共振峰与阻尼 / Resonance and damping

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

driving frequency → natural frequency → resonance → damping → 共振峰与阻尼

推理顺序 / Reasoning

比较驱动与固有频率，说明能量传递，再由阻尼判断峰值高低和宽度。

Compare driving and natural frequencies; explain energy transfer; use damping to predict peak height and width.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.5.2

3.5.2 受迫振动与共振

U2 对共振采用定性处理；稳定受迫振动的频率由驱动源决定。

Treat resonance qualitatively in U2. In steady forced vibration, oscillation frequency is set by the driver.

轻阻尼时共振频率接近固有频率；不要求共振曲线定量方程。

For lightly damped systems, the resonance frequency is close to the natural frequency. No quantitative resonance-curve equation is required.
