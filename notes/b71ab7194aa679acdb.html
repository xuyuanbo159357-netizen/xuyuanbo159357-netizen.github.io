3.5.2

3.5.2 受迫振动与共振

U2 对共振采用定性处理；稳定受迫振动的频率由驱动源决定。

Treat resonance qualitatively in U2. In steady forced vibration, oscillation frequency is set by the driver.

轻阻尼时共振频率接近固有频率；不要求共振曲线定量方程。

For lightly damped systems, the resonance frequency is close to the natural frequency. No quantitative resonance-curve equation is required.
