简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

The string's extension decreases, so its tension decreases. The string also becomes more nearly vertical, reducing the horizontal components of tension. The forward resultant force therefore decreases, so the arrow's acceleration decreases by F = ma. It becomes zero when the string is vertical at a length of 1.48 m.
