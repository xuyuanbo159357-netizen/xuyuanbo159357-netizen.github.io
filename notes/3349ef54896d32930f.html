Concept / 概念

threshold frequency — 截止频率

Definition / 定义

English: The minimum frequency of incident radiation required for photoelectric emission from a given metal.

中文： 使指定金属发生光电子发射所需的最低入射频率。

定义评分依据 / Definition marking points： PH02 202506-Q01。原答案在题目下展开；此处按评分要点概括。 Summarised from the marking points; originals are folded below each question.
