3.7.2 引力场强与场线 / Gravitational field strength

考纲 / Specification： 3.7.2　｜　本单元知识谱系

Formula / 公式与单位

星球表面以上需将半径与高度相加；场强为矢量。

\(g=F/m\); radial magnitude \(g=GM/r^2\); \(r=R+h\) above a spherical planet. Units: N kg\(^{-1}\), equivalent to m s\(^{-2}\).

Conditions / 条件与范围

径向公式用于球体外部；表面附近小高度范围可近似匀强。

The radial formula applies outside a spherical body. Near a planet’s surface over small heights, the field can be approximated as uniform.

Explanation / 解释

场线与局部场强 / Field lines and local strength

引力场是质量会受到引力作用的区域，\(\vec g=\vec F/m\)，单位 \(\mathrm{N\,kg^{-1}}\)，等价于 \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\)。试探质量受力越大不表示原场更强；比较时要除以质量。

A gravitational field is a region where a mass experiences gravitational force. Field strength is force per unit mass and also equals free-fall acceleration at that position.

场线切线给 \(\vec g\) 的方向，疏密表示相对强弱，场线不相交。球对称天体外部为指向中心的径向场，\(g=GM/r^2\)（大小）；近地小区域可近似为平行等距的匀强场。

Field-line tangents give direction and line density indicates relative strength. Lines never cross. Outside a spherical source the field is radial and inward; a small region near Earth's surface is approximately uniform.

高度、叠加与引力势 / Height, superposition and potential

随高度增加 \(g\) 减小，计算用 \(r=R+h\)。多个天体的场强是矢量和，沿一直线时先定正方向；两天体之间可能存在合场强为零的点，但该处引力势不必为零。

Field strength decreases with altitude. Superpose fields as vectors; between two bodies the resultant field can be zero while the potential is nonzero.

\(g\)–\(r\) 的外部大小曲线按 \(1/r^2\) 衰减；不能把地表 \(g\) 当作所有高度的常量。

Outside the source, field magnitude falls as \(1/r^2\); surface gravity is not constant at arbitrary altitude.

U3 Gravitational field potential

球体外部场强大小按反平方下降，势为负并趋近零；势的负斜率给出带符号径向场强。 Exterior spherical field.

Applications / 应用与实验

能分辨场强随距离的反平方曲线和势随距离的反比曲线。

Read a \(g\)–\(r\) graph and distinguish its inverse-square shape from the potential’s inverse-distance shape.

Common errors / 易错点

零场强不等于零引力势；自由落体并非没有重力。

A point of zero field does not imply zero gravitational potential. Free fall does not mean gravity is absent.

3.7.2 方法 1 · 引力场叠加与图像 / Gravitational superposition and graphs

  1. 场强是矢量；两天体之间令 \(GM_A/r_A^2=GM_B/r_B^2\) 求零场点，该点更靠近较小质量。势是标量，两者负势相加，不因零场而变零。 / Zero resultant field does not mean zero potential.
  2. 场线与等势线垂直，向势降低方向；等势差相同时，等势线越密场强越大。 / State the spacing condition when comparing equipotentials.
  3. 从 \(V\)–\(r\) 图求 \(g=-dV/dr\)；若图为 \(E_p\)–\(r\)，先求斜率再除物体质量并加负号。不要用 \(V/r\) 代替切线斜率。 / Check whether the vertical axis is potential or potential energy.
  4. \(g=GM/R^2=(4\pi/3)G\rho R\) 用于球面密度比较；高度 \(h\) 要转为 \(r=R+h\)。 / Use the radius from the centre.
  5. 二星对称中垂线上，中心零场，远处又趋零，中间有最大场强；\(\ln g\)–\(\ln x\) 斜率为 \(-2\) 的直线区域才表明反平方关系，读横坐标后还需取指数。 / Interpret logarithmic axes explicitly.
