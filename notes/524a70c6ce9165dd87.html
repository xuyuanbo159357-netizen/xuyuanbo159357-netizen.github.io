3.4.1 电学基础 / Basics of electricity

考纲 / Specification： 3.4.1　｜　本单元知识谱系

↗ resistance — 电阻

Definition / 定义

English: The ratio of the potential difference across a component to the current through it.

中文： 元件两端电势差与通过元件电流的比值。

↗ coulomb — 库仑

Definition / 定义

English: The charge transferred by a current of one ampere in one second.

中文： 一安培电流在一秒内转移的电荷量。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

真题定义依据 / Mark-scheme checks： PH02 202001-Q08、PH02 202401-Q05。定义按评分要点概括；原答案可在对应题目下展开。 Definitions are summarised from the scoring points, with original answers below the linked questions.

Formula / 公式与单位

单位分别为安培、库仑、伏特和欧姆；一伏特为一焦耳每库仑。

\(I=\Delta Q/\Delta t\); \(V=W/Q\); \(R=V/I\). Units: A, C, V and \(\Omega\). One volt is one joule per coulomb.

Conditions / 条件与范围

时间间隔内使用平均电流；瞬时电流为电荷—时间图像的切线斜率。

Use average current over an interval; instantaneous current is the gradient of a charge–time graph.

Explanation / 解释

电荷、电流与方向 / Charge and current

电荷守恒；金属中载流子是电子。传统电流取正电荷流动方向，与电子漂移方向相反。电流是通过横截面的电荷量随时间的变化率，不是“电子个数每秒”。

Charge is conserved. Electrons carry charge in metals; conventional current is opposite to electron drift. Current is charge passing a cross-section per unit time, not a number of electrons per second.

\(I_{\rm av}=\Delta Q/\Delta t\)；恒定电流时 \(Q=It\)。\(Q\)–\(t\) 图的斜率表示电流；\(I\)–\(t\) 图的有符号面积表示净电荷转移量。若求电子数，\(N=|Q|/e\)。

The gradient of a charge–time graph is current; the signed area under a current–time graph is net transferred charge. Use \(N=|Q|/e\) for the number of electrons.

电势差与电阻的含义 / Potential difference and resistance

\(V=W/Q\) 表示每库仑电荷对应的能量转移，\(1\,\mathrm V=1\,\mathrm{J\,C^{-1}}\)。\(R=V/I\) 是元件在指定工作状态下的电阻，\(1\,\Omega=1\,\mathrm{V\,A^{-1}}\)；它本身不保证电阻恒定。

Potential difference is energy transferred per unit charge. Resistance is the ratio of potential difference to current at a specified operating point; its definition does not imply constant resistance.

先将 mA、μC、ms 换为 SI 单位，再代入。区分“电荷通过元件”和“能量在元件中转移”：电荷不会被灯泡消耗。

Convert prefixes to SI units before substitution. Charge passes through a lamp while energy is transferred; charge is not used up.

Applications / 应用与实验

求电荷前将毫安、分钟换算为安培、秒；用 U1 的功与能量框架理解电能转移。

Convert mA to A and minutes to seconds before finding charge; relate electrical energy to the U1 work and energy framework.

Common errors / 易错点

电阻并不必然恒定；电阻定义与有条件的欧姆定律必须区分。

Resistance is not automatically constant; \(R=V/I\) is a definition, whereas Ohm’s law requires constant physical conditions.

3.4.1 方法 1 · 载流子、容量与电荷账目 / Charge carriers and capacity

补充知识 / Knowledge. 正负载流子向相反方向移动时，产生同方向的传统电流；在同一截面上各自的电荷流率按传统电流方向相加。 / Oppositely moving positive and negative carriers can contribute to the same conventional current.

  1. 明确截面、时间和电荷方向；算 \(I=\Delta Q/\Delta t\)，变电流时用 \(I\)–\(t\) 图的有符号面积。 / Identify the section, interval and conventional direction; use the signed area for variable current.
  2. \(1\,\mathrm{A\,h}=3600\,\mathrm C\)；容量是电荷，求能量还需要电压。 / Capacity is charge; energy also requires a voltage.
  3. 粒子数 \(N=|Q|/|q|\)。已给功率和电阻时，可先由 \(I=\sqrt{P/R}\) 求电流。 / Find current first when power and resistance are given.

对应真题 / Examples： PH02 · 202406 · Q14；PH02 · 202501 · Q02；PH02 · 202501 · Q23；PH02 · 202401 · Q13。

3.4.1 方法 2 · 从定义推导单位 / Deriving units

先写物理关系，再用基本单位逐项替换；不要由名称猜测。 / Start with a physical relation, then substitute base units.

\(V=W/Q\Rightarrow\mathrm{kg\,m^2\,s^{-3}\,A^{-1}}\)；\(R=V/I\Rightarrow\mathrm{kg\,m^2\,s^{-3}\,A^{-2}}\)；\(\rho=RA/L\Rightarrow\mathrm{kg\,m^3\,s^{-3}\,A^{-2}}\)。幂次作用于单位整体。 / Apply powers to the entire unit.

对应真题 / Examples： PH02 · 202606 · Q01；PH02 · 202206 · Q11；PH02 · 202401 · Q11。
