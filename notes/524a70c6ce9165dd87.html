1. 电荷、电流与载流粒子 / Charge, current and charge carriers

1.1 关键概念与定义 / Key concepts and definitions

electric current — 电流

Definition / 定义

English: The rate of flow of electric charge.

中文： 电荷流动的速率。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式

\[\Large\displaystyle I=\frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta t}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(I\) 电流 / electric current \(\mathrm{A}\)
\(\Delta Q\) 通过截面的电荷量 / charge flowing through a cross-section \(\mathrm{C}\)
\(\Delta t\) 时间、时间间隔 / time, time interval \(\mathrm{s}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

电流表示电荷通过某截面的速率，有限时间间隔给出平均电流。

Current is the rate of charge flow through a cross-section. A finite interval gives average current.

coulomb — 库仑

Definition / 定义

English: The charge transferred by a current of one ampere in one second.

中文： 一安培电流在一秒内转移的电荷量。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式

真题定义依据 / Mark-scheme checks： PH02 202001-Q08、PH02 202401-Q05。定义按评分要点概括；原答案可在对应题目下展开。 Definitions are summarised from the scoring points, with original answers below the linked questions.

\[\Large\displaystyle 1\,\mathrm{C}=1\,\mathrm{A\,s}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(\mathrm{C}\) 电荷量的 SI 单位 / SI unit of charge \(\mathrm{C}\)
\(\mathrm{A}\) 电流的 SI 单位 / SI unit of current \(\mathrm{A}\)
\(\mathrm{s}\) 时间单位 / time unit \(\mathrm{s}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

一库仑是以一安培电流在一秒内通过的电荷量。

One coulomb is the charge passing in one second at a current of one ampere.

1.2 公式与物理规律 / Formulae and physical laws

\[\Large\displaystyle Q=It\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(Q\) 电荷量 / electric charge \(\mathrm{C}\)
\(I\) 电流 / electric current \(\mathrm{A}\)
\(t\) 时间、时间间隔 / time, time interval \(\mathrm{s}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

恒定电流通过的电荷量等于电流乘时间；变电流时要用电流—时间图面积。

For constant current, transferred charge is current times time. With varying current, use the area under the current–time graph.

\[\Large\displaystyle N=\frac{|Q|}{|q|}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(N\) 载流粒子数 / number of charge carriers 无量纲 / dimensionless
\(Q\) 电荷量 / electric charge \(\mathrm{C}\)
\(q\) 每个载流粒子的电荷量 / charge per carrier \(\mathrm{C}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

总电荷量除以每个载流粒子的电荷量大小给出载流粒子数。

Total charge magnitude divided by the charge magnitude per carrier gives the number of carriers.

1.3 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

适用范围与条件 / Scope and conditions

Conditions / 适用条件： 电流式给出时间间隔内平均电流；电压为单位电荷转移的能量，电阻为该工作点的 V/I。 / Current is averaged over the interval; voltage is energy transferred per unit charge and resistance is V/I at the operating point.

Conditions / 条件与范围

时间间隔内使用平均电流；瞬时电流为电荷—时间图像的切线斜率。

Use average current over an interval; instantaneous current is the gradient of a charge–time graph.

电荷、电流与方向 / Charge and current

电荷守恒；金属中载流子是电子。传统电流取正电荷流动方向，与电子漂移方向相反。电流是通过横截面的电荷量随时间的变化率，不是“电子个数每秒”。

Charge is conserved. Electrons carry charge in metals; conventional current is opposite to electron drift. Current is charge passing a cross-section per unit time, not a number of electrons per second.

\(I_{\rm av}=\Delta Q/\Delta t\)；恒定电流时 \(Q=It\)。\(Q\)–\(t\) 图的斜率表示电流；\(I\)–\(t\) 图的有符号面积表示净电荷转移量。若求电子数，\(N=|Q|/e\)。

The gradient of a charge–time graph is current; the signed area under a current–time graph is net transferred charge. Use \(N=|Q|/e\) for the number of electrons.

电流与电子的方向 / Current and electron flow

读图 / Reading： 在金属导线中，传统电流方向与电子漂移方向相反；电荷绕闭合回路运动，能量由电源转移到元件。

In a metal, conventional current opposes electron drift; charge circulates while the source transfers energy to components.

2. 电势差与能量转移 / Potential difference and energy transfer

2.1 关键概念与定义 / Key concepts and definitions

potential difference — 电势差

Definition / 定义

English: The work done per unit charge transferred between two points.

中文： 两点间转移单位电荷所做的功。

定义评分依据 / Definition marking points： PH02 202206-Q02。原答案在题目下展开；此处按评分要点概括。 Summarised from the marking points; originals are folded below each question.

Formula / 公式

Conditions / 条件与说明： 按势能变化定义带符号电势差；电路能量转移题常用电压大小 V = W/Q。 / This is the signed potential difference from potential-energy change; circuit energy-transfer questions often use the magnitude V = W/Q.

\[\Large\displaystyle \Delta V=\frac{\Delta U}{q}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(\Delta V\) 电势差、电压 / potential difference, voltage \(\mathrm{V}\)
\(\Delta U\) 电势能变化 / change in electric potential energy \(\mathrm{J}\)
\(q\) 电荷量 / electric charge \(\mathrm{C}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

电势差衡量每单位电荷在两点间转移的能量或所做的功。

Potential difference is energy transferred or work done per unit charge between two points.

2.2 公式与物理规律 / Formulae and physical laws

\[\Large\displaystyle V=\frac{W}{Q}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(V\) 电势差、电压 / potential difference, voltage \(\mathrm{V}\)
\(W\) 功 / work done \(\mathrm{J}\)
\(Q\) 电荷量 / electric charge \(\mathrm{C}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

电势差衡量每单位电荷在两点间转移的能量或所做的功。

Potential difference is energy transferred or work done per unit charge between two points.

2.3 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

电势差与电阻的含义 / Potential difference and resistance

\(V=\frac{W}{Q}\) 表示每库仑电荷对应的能量转移，\(1\,\mathrm V=1\,\mathrm{J\,C^{-1}}\)。\(R=\frac{V}{I}\) 是元件在指定工作状态下的电阻，\(1\,\Omega=1\,\mathrm{V\,A^{-1}}\)；它本身不保证电阻恒定。

Potential difference is energy transferred per unit charge. Resistance is the ratio of potential difference to current at a specified operating point; its definition does not imply constant resistance.

先将 mA、μC、ms 换为 SI 单位，再代入。区分“电荷通过元件”和“能量在元件中转移”：电荷不会被灯泡消耗。

Convert prefixes to SI units before substitution. Charge passes through a lamp while energy is transferred; charge is not used up.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.4.1

Applications / 应用与实验

求电荷前将毫安、分钟换算为安培、秒；用 U1 的功与能量框架理解电能转移。

Convert mA to A and minutes to seconds before finding charge; relate electrical energy to the U1 work and energy framework.

3. 电阻的含义与单位 / Resistance and its unit

3.1 关键概念与定义 / Key concepts and definitions

resistance — 电阻

Definition / 定义

English: The ratio of the potential difference across a component to the current through it.

中文： 元件两端电势差与通过元件电流的比值。

定义评分依据 / Definition marking points： PH02 202401-Q05、PH02 202001-Q08。原答案在题目下展开；此处按评分要点概括。 Summarised from the marking points; originals are folded below each question.

Formula / 公式

\[\Large\displaystyle R=\frac{V}{I}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(R\) 电阻 / resistance \(\mathrm{\Omega}\)
\(V\) 电势差、电压 / potential difference, voltage \(\mathrm{V}\)
\(I\) 电流 / electric current \(\mathrm{A}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

电阻由元件两端电势差与通过它的电流之比定义。

Resistance is defined as potential difference across a component divided by its current.

适用条件 / Conditions

各值对应同一工作状态。

Use values for the same operating state.

3.3 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

Common errors / 易错点

电阻并不必然恒定；电阻定义与有条件的欧姆定律必须区分。

Resistance is not automatically constant; \(R=\frac{V}{I}\) is a definition, whereas Ohm’s law requires constant physical conditions.

考点精讲：电荷、能量、电流与电势差 / Charge, energy, current and potential difference

3.4.1 方法 1 · 载流子、容量与电荷账目 / Charge carriers and capacity

补充知识 / Knowledge. 正负载流子向相反方向移动时，产生同方向的传统电流；在同一截面上各自的电荷流率按传统电流方向相加。 / Oppositely moving positive and negative carriers can contribute to the same conventional current.

  1. 明确截面、时间和电荷方向；算 \(I=\frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta t}\)，变电流时用 \(I\)–\(t\) 图的有符号面积。 / Identify the section, interval and conventional direction; use the signed area for variable current.
  2. \(1\,\mathrm{A\,h}=3600\,\mathrm C\)；容量是电荷，求能量还需要电压。 / Capacity is charge; energy also requires a voltage.
  3. 粒子数 \(N=|Q|/|q|\)。已给功率和电阻时，可先由 \(I=\sqrt{P/R}\) 求电流。 / Find current first when power and resistance are given.

对应真题 / Examples： PH02 · 202406 · Q14；PH02 · 202501 · Q02；PH02 · 202501 · Q23；PH02 · 202401 · Q13。

3.4.1 方法 2 · 从定义推导单位 / Deriving units

先写物理关系，再用基本单位逐项替换；不要由名称猜测。 / Start with a physical relation, then substitute base units.

\(V=W/Q\Rightarrow\mathrm{kg\,m^2\,s^{-3}\,A^{-1}}\)；\(R=V/I\Rightarrow\mathrm{kg\,m^2\,s^{-3}\,A^{-2}}\)；\(\rho=RA/L\Rightarrow\mathrm{kg\,m^3\,s^{-3}\,A^{-2}}\)。幂次作用于单位整体。 / Apply powers to the entire unit.
