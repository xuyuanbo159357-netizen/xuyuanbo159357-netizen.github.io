简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

Fusion joins light nuclei in the low-A region of the graph to form a heavier nucleus closer to the binding-energy peak. The binding energy per nucleon increases, so the total nuclear mass decreases. The corresponding mass-energy is released.
