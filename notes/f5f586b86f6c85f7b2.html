简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

The cross-sectional area is π(5.2 × 10⁻³)²/4 = 2.12 × 10⁻⁵ m². Stress equals Young modulus multiplied by strain. The tension is therefore F = EεA ≈ 7.96 × 10³ N. This exceeds the allowed 7.5 × 10³ N, so the cable is not suitable for this load.
