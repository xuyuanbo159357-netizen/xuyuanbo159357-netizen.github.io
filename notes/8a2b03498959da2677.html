1. 通电导线受力与磁感应强度 / Wire force and magnetic flux density

1.1 关键概念与定义 / Key concepts and definitions

magnetic flux density — 磁感应强度

Definition / 定义

English: The force per unit current per unit length on a straight conductor placed perpendicular to a magnetic field.

中文： 垂直于磁场的直导线，每单位电流、每单位长度所受的力。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式

Conditions / 条件与说明： 直导线与均匀磁场垂直；1 T = 1 N A⁻¹ m⁻¹。 / A straight wire perpendicular to a uniform field; 1 T = 1 N A⁻¹ m⁻¹.

\[\Large\displaystyle B=\frac{F}{IL}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(B\) 磁感应强度 / magnetic flux density \(\mathrm{T}\)
\(F\) 力 / force \(\mathrm{N}\)
\(I\) 电流 / electric current \(\mathrm{A}\)
\(L\) 长度 / length \(\mathrm{m}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

磁感应强度可由垂直磁场中的单位电流、单位导线长度受到的力定义。

Magnetic flux density is force per unit current per unit wire length when the wire is perpendicular to the field.

适用条件 / Conditions

导线与均匀磁场垂直。

The wire is perpendicular to a uniform field.

tesla — 特斯拉

Definition / 定义

English: The magnetic flux density that produces a force of one newton on a one-metre wire carrying one ampere perpendicular to the field.

中文： 使垂直于磁场的一米导线在一安培电流下受一牛顿力的磁感应强度。

定义评分依据 / Definition marking points： PH03 202601-Q02、PH03 202501-Q06。原答案在题目下展开；此处按评分要点概括。 Summarised from the marking points; originals are folded below each question.

\[\Large\displaystyle 1\,\mathrm{T}=1\,\mathrm{N\,A^{-1}\,m^{-1}}\]

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(\mathrm{T}\) 磁感应强度单位 / magnetic-flux-density unit \(\mathrm{T}\)

\(\mathrm{A}\) 电流单位 / current unit \(\mathrm{A}\)
\(\mathrm{m}\) 长度单位 / length unit \(\mathrm{m}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

特斯拉是单位电流、单位导线长度对应的磁力所定义的磁感应强度单位。

The tesla is the magnetic-flux-density unit defined by magnetic force per current per wire length.

1.2 公式与物理规律 / Formulae and physical laws

\[\Large\displaystyle F=BIL\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(F\) 力 / force \(\mathrm{N}\)
\(B\) 磁感应强度 / magnetic flux density \(\mathrm{T}\)
\(I\) 电流 / electric current \(\mathrm{A}\)
\(L\) 长度 / length \(\mathrm{m}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

垂直磁场中的通电导线受到磁力，大小随磁感应强度、电流和有效长度增大。

A current-carrying wire perpendicular to a magnetic field experiences force proportional to flux density, current and effective length.

适用条件 / Conditions

均匀磁场，导线与磁场垂直。

Use a uniform field with the wire perpendicular to it.

\[\Large\displaystyle F=BIL\sin\theta\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(F\) 力 / force \(\mathrm{N}\)
\(B\) 磁感应强度 / magnetic flux density \(\mathrm{T}\)
\(I\) 电流 / electric current \(\mathrm{A}\)
\(L\) 长度 / length \(\mathrm{m}\)
\(\theta\) 角、转角 / angle \(\mathrm{rad}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

磁力由电流方向垂直于磁场的分量决定，平行时该磁力为零。

Magnetic force depends on the current component perpendicular to the field and is zero for a parallel wire.

适用条件 / Conditions

夹角是导线电流方向与磁场方向之间的角。

The angle is between conventional current and the magnetic field.

\[\Large\displaystyle F=|\Delta m|g\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(F\) 力 / force \(\mathrm{N}\)
\(\Delta m\) 通电前后的质量示值变化 / change in indicated mass when current is switched on \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(g\) 重力场强 / gravitational field strength \(\mathrm{N\,kg^{-1}}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

电流天平中，读数变化对应支持力变化，由质量读数变化的大小求磁力。

In a current balance, a mass-reading change corresponds to a support-force change, allowing magnetic force to be found.

1.3 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

适用范围与条件 / Scope and conditions

Conditions / 适用条件： F=BIL 要求有效导线与均匀磁场垂直；斜向时使用垂直分量。B 的单位为 T。 / F=BIL requires a field-exposed wire perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field; use the perpendicular component for angled cases. B is in teslas.

Conditions / 条件与范围

垂直公式要求电流与磁场垂直；其他角度先找垂直分量。

Use the perpendicular formula under the stated orientation. With other angles, identify the component perpendicular to the field if required by the given situation.

通电导线受力与特斯拉 / Wire force and tesla

均匀磁场中，直导线有效长度 \(L\) 与场垂直时 \(F=BIL\)，故 \(B=\frac{F}{IL}\)。一特斯拉使垂直于磁场的1 m导线在电流1 A时受力1 N，\(1\,\mathrm T=1\,\mathrm{N\,A^{-1}\,m^{-1}}\)。

For a wire perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field, force is \(BIL\). One tesla produces one newton on one metre of perpendicular wire carrying one ampere.

导线受磁力的方向 / Force on a wire

读图 / Reading： 图中 I 与 B 垂直，力同时垂直二者；L 只计处于磁场内的有效导线长度。

For perpendicular I and B, force is normal to both; L is only the effective field-exposed length.

2. 弗莱明左手定则与实验 / Fleming’s left-hand rule and experiments

2.1 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

弗莱明左手定则 / Fleming's left-hand rule

适用情境 / Use： 已知磁场和电流（或带电粒子运动），判断磁力方向。电动机情境常为电能 → 机械能；单个粒子只受磁力时，磁力本身不做功。 / Use the magnetic field and conventional current to find magnetic-force direction. A motor converts electrical to mechanical energy; magnetic force alone does no work on a moving particle.

左手三指互相垂直。 / Hold the three fingers of the left hand mutually perpendicular.

左手手指 / Left-hand finger 对应方向 / Direction
食指 / First finger 磁场 \(B\)；磁体外从 N 指向 S / magnetic field, from N to S outside the magnet
中指 / Second finger 传统电流 \(I\)，即正电荷运动方向 / conventional current, the direction of positive-charge motion
拇指 / Thumb 导线或粒子所受磁力 \(F\) / magnetic force on the conductor or particle

带电粒子 / Charged particles： 正电荷用速度方向代替传统电流方向；负电荷用与其速度相反的方向作为传统电流方向，再判受力。 / For a positive charge use its velocity as the current direction; for a negative charge use the direction opposite to its velocity before finding the force.

检查 / Check： 磁力垂直于磁场和电流方向；若运动或电流与磁场平行，磁力为零。 / Magnetic force is perpendicular to both directions; parallel motion or current gives zero magnetic force.

易混点 / Distinction： 右手握拳定则判断电流周围的磁场：拇指指传统电流，弯曲四指指环绕导线的磁场。它不是弗莱明右手发电机定则。 / The right-hand grip rule finds the field around a current: thumb for conventional current and curled fingers for field. It is different from Fleming's right-hand generator rule.

右手发电机定则：运动 → 感应电流 / Generator rule: motion → induced current

斜向情况用 \(F=BIL\sin\theta\)，\(\theta\) 为导线与磁场夹角；平行时为零。只计处于磁场中的导线长度，不是整个回路长度。

For an angled case use the perpendicular component. Only the wire length inside the field contributes.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.10.1

左手：场、电流、力 / Left hand: field, current, force

读图 / Reading： 左手食指指磁场 B，中指指传统电流 I，拇指指磁力 F。图中 Thrust 指推力，不是负电荷本身的速度方向。

Left first finger: B; second finger: conventional I; thumb: magnetic F. “Thrust” means force, not the velocity of a negative charge.

Applications / 应用与实验

利用不同电流或有效长度的受力测磁感应强度，分析电动机受力。

Use force measurements with different currents or wire lengths to determine B; explain motor-force situations.

Common errors / 易错点

定则需用磁场与传统电流方向，不能直接用电子运动方向。

Field-line direction and conventional-current direction are required; electron motion is opposite to conventional current.

3.10.1 方法 1 · 磁力、反作用与导线长度 / Wire force, reaction and effective length

  1. \(F=BIL\sin\theta\)，\(L\) 是磁场中有效导线长度，\(\theta\) 为电流与磁场夹角；平行时为零。左手定则使用传统电流。 / Use the field-exposed length and conventional current.
  2. 天平测的是磁体受到的反作用；导线力与磁体力相反。由质量读数变化得 \(F=|\Delta m|g\) 后求 \(B\)，并保留正负读数的方向解释。 / Apply Newton's third law before interpreting the balance.
  3. 磁体边缘漏场也可能作用于延长的导线；改成竖直引线可使其力无竖直分量，从而不影响竖直天平读数，并非一定“场外无力”。 / Explain the measurement improvement by force components.
  4. 线圈力偶先求一边的力，再乘两作用线垂距和匝数；不能再无条件乘二。 / A couple uses one force times the separation.
  5. 轨道发射器 \(W=Fs\)，再减去上升势能求动能。若题给 \(B\propto I\)，则 \(F\propto I^2\)，不能只考虑公式中的一个 \(I\)。 / Include every current-dependent quantity.
