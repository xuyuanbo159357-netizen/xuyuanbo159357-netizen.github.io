U3PM19 绳摆下落与最低点张力 / Swing and lowest-point tension

所属知识 / Knowledge： 3.6.1 Circular motion / 圆周运动 · 模型目录 / Model index

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

swing / released from rest / cable / lowest point / drop → 绳摆下落与最低点张力 / Swing and lowest-point tension

关键词只是识别线索；必须同时核对受力方向、几何和约束。 Keywords are cues, not sufficient conditions; check the actual forces and constraints.

建模、方程与适用条件 / Model, equations and conditions

两步计算 / Two-step calculation

从静止下降竖直高度 \(h\)，忽略阻力，先用 \(mgh=mv^2/2\) 得最低点速率；再用 \(T_s-mg=mv^2/l\) 得 \(T_s=mg+mv^2/l\)。绳长 \(l\) 为曲率半径。

Use energy to obtain the speed after a vertical drop, then use the radial force equation at the bottom to find tension.

若从绳与竖直夹角 \(\theta_0\) 处释放，\(h=l(1-\cos\theta_0)\)。不可把圆弧路程代替竖直高度，也不可把 \(T_s=mg\) 用于最低点运动状态。

For release at a stated angle, calculate the vertical height difference rather than arc length. Bottom tension exceeds weight while moving.

与简谐摆区别 / Relation to SHM

本模型的能量和最低点受力可用于较大摆角；只有把切向恢复力近似成正比于位移时，才需要小角度简谐近似。

The energy and bottom-force analysis can apply at larger amplitudes. The small-angle condition is needed for the SHM approximation, not for this general energy calculation.
