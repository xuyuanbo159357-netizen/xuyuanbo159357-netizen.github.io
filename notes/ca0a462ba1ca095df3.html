简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

The alpha-particle distance of closest approach depends on the alpha's initial energy and gives only an upper limit to nuclear radius. Electron diffraction gives a measured radius from the diffraction pattern rather than assuming the closest approach equals the nuclear surface.
