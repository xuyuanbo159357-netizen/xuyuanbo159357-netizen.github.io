简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

Xenon has the greater binding energy per nucleon. The decay releases energy, leaving a more tightly bound product nucleus with less nuclear rest energy and a greater mass defect. The number of nucleons stays the same, so the increase in total binding energy is also an increase per nucleon.
