简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

At zero current the open-circuit voltage 0.63 V is the cell's emf. At zero terminal voltage the short-circuit current is 3.5 A, and all the emf is dropped across the internal resistance. Therefore r = 0.63/3.5.
