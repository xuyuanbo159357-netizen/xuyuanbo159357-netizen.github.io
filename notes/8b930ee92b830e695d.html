方法、分析与误差 / Method, analysis and uncertainty

1

使用单色激光，保持缝或光栅稳定对准、图样清晰。

Use monochromatic laser light, stable slit/grating alignment and a clearly visible pattern.

2

双缝测多条纹间隔、屏距与缝距，求波长。

For double slits, measure many fringe intervals, screen distance and slit separation; calculate λ=ws/D.

3

光栅由刻线数求缝距，测已知级次角度，用光栅方程并比较两侧。

For a grating, convert line density to d, measure θ for a known order, use d sinθ=nλ and compare both sides.

4

重复测量并按测量量合成相对不确定度。

Use repeat measurements and percentage uncertainty combinations appropriate to the measured quantities.

5

避免直视光束及镜面反射，光束高度避开眼睛。

Avoid direct laser viewing and specular reflections; keep beam height away from eye level.

必做实验5及安全 / Required practical 5 and safety

双缝实验测 \(D\)、已知 \(s\)，跨多个条纹测总宽后除以间隔数，用 \(\lambda=ws/D\)；区分“条纹条数”和“间隔数”。光栅实验参见3.5.7，以两侧同级亮斑求角度。

Measure several fringe spacings and divide by the number of intervals, then calculate wavelength. The grating part is developed in 3.5.7 using matching orders on both sides.

低功率激光固定在眼睛高度以下，避免直视及镜面反射，用屏接收；不要求激光器内部工作原理。

Secure a low-power laser below eye level, avoid direct and specularly reflected beams, and use a screen. Laser operating details are not required.
