3.5.5 叠加原理、驻波与谐波 / Superposition and stationary waves

Definition / 定义

↗ superposition — 叠加

Definition / 定义

English: When waves overlap, the resultant displacement is the algebraic sum of their individual displacements at each point.

中文： 波相遇时，各点合位移等于各波位移的代数和。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

↗ stationary wave — 驻波

Definition / 定义

English: A wave pattern with fixed nodes and antinodes, formed by the superposition of two waves of the same frequency and amplitude travelling in opposite directions.

中文： 两列频率相同、振幅相同且反向传播的波叠加，形成波节与波腹位置固定的波形。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

↗ node — 波节

Definition / 定义

English: A point on a stationary wave at which the displacement is always zero.

中文： 驻波上位移始终为零的位置。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

↗ antinode — 波腹

Definition / 定义

English: A point on a stationary wave at which the amplitude is maximum.

中文： 驻波上振幅最大的位置。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

↗ mass per unit length — 线密度

Definition / 定义

English: The mass of a string or wire divided by its length.

中文： 弦或导线的质量与长度之比。

Formula / 公式与单位

弦张力用 \(F\) 表示，避免与周期 \(T\) 混淆；两端固定弦的第 \(n\) 谐波含 \(n\) 个波腹。

\(f_1=\frac{1}{2L}\sqrt{F/\mu}\); \(\mu=m/L\); \(\lambda_n=2L/n\) and \(f_n=nf_1\) for a string fixed at both ends.

Conditions / 条件与范围

标准完整波节图要求反向波同频、等振幅；弦需均匀且两端固定。采用“第一谐波”和“谐波”术语。

For the standard complete-node pattern, the counter-propagating waves have equal frequency and equal amplitude. String relationships assume uniform linear density and both ends fixed. Use “first harmonic” and “harmonics”.

Explanation / 解释

叠加形成驻波 / Superposition and formation

重叠时合位移是各列波位移的代数和。相同频率、同一介质中沿相反方向传播的波叠加，可形成固定波节和波腹；完全波节要求两波振幅相等。常见情形是入射波与反射波叠加。

Resultant displacement is the algebraic sum of component displacements. Counter-propagating waves of the same frequency can form fixed nodes and antinodes; complete nodes require equal amplitudes.

画图时选几个相隔四分之一周期的时刻，将两波逐点相加：波节始终为零，波腹在最大正位移、零、最大负位移间变化。驻波无沿波传播方向的净能量传递，但各点仍可振动。

Add the two waveforms point by point at successive quarter-cycle intervals. Nodes remain at zero while antinodes oscillate. An ideal stationary wave has no net energy transfer along the wave.

间距、相位与谐波 / Spacing, phase and harmonics

相邻波节或相邻波腹相距 \(\lambda/2\)，相邻波节与波腹相距 \(\lambda/4\)。同一对相邻波节内的振动同相，跨过一个波节反相；波节处振幅为零，不给它指定振动相位。

Adjacent nodes or antinodes are separated by half a wavelength; a node and its adjacent antinode by a quarter wavelength. Points within one loop are in phase; adjacent loops are in antiphase. Phase is undefined at a node.

两端固定弦的第 \(n\) 谐波：\(L=n\lambda_n/2\)，\(f_n=nf_1\)，\(f_1=(1/2L)\sqrt{\mathcal T/\mu}\)，\(\mu=m/L\)。这里 \(\mathcal T\) 表示张力，避免与周期 \(T\) 混淆。采用 first harmonic、second harmonic 等术语。

For a string fixed at both ends, \(L=n\lambda_n/2\) and \(f_n=nf_1\). Use harmonic terminology; distinguish tension from period.

三种装置与变量关系 / Apparatus and variable relationships

弦：振动发生器驱动并调频观察波腹。微波：发射器、反射板和探头测相邻极小值间距。声波：声源、反射端和麦克风测声强变化；由相邻波节间距求 \(\lambda\)，再用 \(c=f\lambda\)。

Use a vibrator and string, a microwave transmitter with reflector and probe, or a sound source with reflector and microphone. Adjacent minima give half the wavelength, allowing wave speed to be found.

研究弦频率时一次改变一个量：\(f\propto1/L\)、\(f\propto\sqrt{\mathcal T}\)、\(f\propto1/\sqrt\mu\)；可作 \(f\)–\(1/L\)、\(f^2\)–\(\mathcal T\)、\(f^2\)–\(1/\mu\) 图，保持同一谐波。

Change one variable at a time and keep the harmonic fixed. Linearise the predicted relationships using reciprocal length or squared frequency.

U2 Stationary wave formation

同频、等振幅、反向传播波的逐点叠加；波节位置固定。 Superposition at three instants; fixed nodes.

Applications / 应用与实验

实验研究频率与弦长、张力、线密度关系；声波或微波驻波可由连续极大、极小位置识别。

Investigate frequency against string length, tension and mass per unit length. Sound or microwave standing waves can be detected by successive maxima/minima.

Common errors / 易错点

不能将两端固定弦公式直接套用于所有空气柱，应先看边界条件。

Do not apply the two-fixed-end string formula unchanged to every air column; infer boundary conditions from the question.

3.5.5 方法 1 · 驻波合成、谐波与不等振幅 / Standing waves and unequal amplitudes

  1. 两列波逐点相加：\(y=y_1+y_2\)；求缺失波用 \(y_2=y-y_1\)。过 \(T/4\) 后，两行波各沿相反方向移动 \(\lambda/4\)，不能平移整个驻波图。 / Superpose locally and propagate the component waves separately.
  2. 波腹处两波同相、波节处反相；不能说两列反向行波处处同相。振幅不等时极大 \(A_1+A_2\)，极小 \(|A_1-A_2|\)，极小未必为零。 / A minimum need not be a true node when amplitudes differ.
  3. 固定两端的弦先数半波段，\(L=n\lambda/2\)；相邻波节间同相、跨一个波节反相。选谐波的拨弦点应在相应波腹。 / Count loops and identify phase regions.
  4. \(f_n=n\sqrt{T/\mu}/(2L)\)，\(\mu=m/L\)；先统一质量和长度单位，再从实际坐标推导斜率。 / Derive the graph relation, including unit conversions.

对应真题 / Examples： PH02 · 202201 · Q07；PH02 · 202306 · Q06；PH02 · 202606 · Q19；PH02 · 202601 · Q11。

3.5.5 方法 2 · 闭端空气柱与端部修正 / Closed air columns and end correction

水面为位移波节，开口附近为位移波腹。基频时 \(\lambda=4(L+x)\)，\(x>0\) 为端部修正。 / The closed end is a displacement node and the open end is near an antinode.

\(1/f=4L/v+4x/v\)：画 \(1/f\) 对 \(L\)，斜率 \(m=4/v\)，纵截距 \(b=4x/v\)，因此 \(v=4/m\)、\(x=b/m\)。横截距为 \(-x\)；正的端部修正取该负横截距的绝对值。 / Distinguish the signed intercept from the positive correction.

对应真题 / Examples： PH02 · 202406 · Q07。
