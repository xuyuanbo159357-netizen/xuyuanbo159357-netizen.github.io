太阳能阵列最大功率 / Solar cell and maximum power

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

solar cell → array → maximum power → inverse square → 太阳能阵列最大功率

推理顺序 / Reasoning

求板面入射功率，从伏安曲线找乘积最大点，按串联电压并联电流组成阵列并计效率。

Use the incident intensity and panel area; maximise VI from the characteristic; combine series voltage and parallel current; include conversion efficiency.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.13.3

3.13.3 太阳辐射、太阳能电池与阵列

反平方规律假设各向同性点源、无显著吸收；地面太阳辐射受大气、天气、纬度、季节、时间与朝向影响，按题给数据计算。

Inverse-square law assumes a point/isotropic source and negligible intervening absorption. At Earth’s surface solar intensity depends on atmosphere, cloud, latitude, season, time and panel orientation; use supplied data.

光强单位 W m\(^{-2}\)；注意 I 可代表光强，也可代表电流，按语境区分。

\(I=P_{\mathrm{source}}/(4\pi r^2)\) for an isotropic point source; \(P_{\mathrm{incident}}=IA\) for a perpendicular surface; cell power \(P=VI\); efficiency \(\eta=P_{\mathrm{electrical}}/P_{\mathrm{incident}}\).
