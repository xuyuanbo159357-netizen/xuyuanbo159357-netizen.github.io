3.6.2 简谐运动方程、图像与能量 / Simple harmonic motion

考纲 / Specification： 3.6.2　｜　本单元知识谱系

知识逐项讲解 / Concept-by-concept guide

1. 简谐运动的定义 / Definition of simple harmonic motion

simple harmonic motion — 简谐运动

↗ simple harmonic motion — 简谐运动

Definition / 定义

English: Motion in which acceleration is directly proportional to displacement from equilibrium and is always directed towards equilibrium.

中文： 加速度与偏离平衡位置的位移成正比，方向始终指向平衡位置的运动。

定义评分依据 / Definition marking points： PH03 201806-Q01。原答案在题目下展开；此处按评分要点概括。 Summarised from the marking points; originals are folded below each question.

以平衡位置为 \(x=0\)，任选一侧为正方向。简谐运动有两个判定点：加速度大小与离开平衡位置的距离成正比，加速度方向始终指向平衡位置。例如物体在平衡位置右侧时，\(x>0\)，回复加速度向左。 / Measure signed displacement from equilibrium. Acceleration is proportional in magnitude to displacement from equilibrium and is directed towards equilibrium. For a displacement to the right, the restoring acceleration points left.

\[\Large\displaystyle a=-\omega^2x\]

\(x\) 是有符号位移（\(\mathrm{m}\)），\(a\) 是同方向坐标下的加速度（\(\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\)），\(\omega\) 是恒定的角频率（\(\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\)）。负号表示 \(a\) 与 \(x\) 方向相反。 / The minus sign gives the restoring direction. The constant of proportionality in magnitude is \(\omega^2\).

真题定义依据 / Mark-scheme check PH03 201806-Q01。

Explanation / 解释

2. 两种基本模型 / Two basic models

弹簧模型 / Mass–spring system. 水平面上的物块向右拉开后释放，弹簧拉它回到平衡位置。物块凭惯性越过平衡位置，压缩弹簧后受到向右的回复力。以平衡位置量 \(x\)，在胡克定律适用范围内，回复合力为 \(-kx\)。 / Pull a mass to the right and release it. The spring pulls it back, and the mass passes through equilibrium. A compressed spring then pushes it back towards equilibrium. In the Hooke’s law range, the restoring resultant is \(-kx\).

钟摆模型 / Simple pendulum. 把钟摆简化为轻质、不可伸长摆线末端的摆球。摆球偏离最低点时，重力的切向分量使它沿圆弧返回。摆角较小时，切向回复加速度近似与弧长位移成正比。 / Model a clock pendulum as a bob on a light, inextensible string. The tangential component of its weight pulls it towards the lowest point. At small angles, tangential acceleration is approximately proportional to arc displacement.

3. 从弹簧模型到单位圆 / From the mass–spring system to the unit circle

3.1 弹簧的回复力与角频率 / Spring restoring force and angular frequency

取物块质量 \(m\)（\(\mathrm{kg}\)）、弹簧劲度系数 \(k\)（\(\mathrm{N\,m^{-1}}\)）。对水平弹簧，按牛顿第二定律把回复合力写成 \(ma\)。与定义式比较得到 \(\omega^2=k/m\)。 / Apply Newton’s second law to the spring’s restoring force and compare the resulting acceleration with the SHM condition.

\[\Large\displaystyle ma=-kx\]
\[\Large\displaystyle \omega=\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}\]

竖直弹簧以静态平衡位置为原点，重力与平衡时的弹簧力相消，额外位移 \(x\) 仍对应回复合力 \(-kx\)。 / For a vertical spring, weight balances the spring force at equilibrium, leaving a restoring resultant \(-kx\) for displacement from that position.

3.2 转一圈对应振动一次 / One turn represents one oscillation

设想点 \(P\) 在半径为 1 的圆上逆时针匀速转动，取它在水平轴上的投影。横坐标是归一化位移 \(x/A\)，乘振幅 \(A\)（\(\mathrm{m}\)）得到弹簧物块的实际位移。图中物块的起点为 \(x=+A\)，且从静止释放。 / Let a reference point \(P\) turn anticlockwise on a unit circle. Its horizontal coordinate is the normalised displacement \(x/A\). Multiplying by the amplitude \(A\) gives the displacement of the mass. The example begins at rest at \(x=+A\).

angular frequency — 角频率 对应参考点的角速度大小，即每秒转过多少弧度。圆上的点转过 \(2\pi\) 弧度，物块回到相同的位置和运动状态，经历一个周期 \(T\)（\(\mathrm{s}\)）。频率 \(f\)（\(\mathrm{Hz}\)）是一秒内的完整振动次数。 / The angular speed of the reference point is the oscillator’s angular frequency. One revolution of \(2\pi\) radians returns the mass to the same position and state of motion after one period \(T\). Frequency \(f\) counts oscillations per second.

↗ angular frequency — 角频率

Definition / 定义

English: The rate of change of the phase angle of an oscillation, equal to two pi times its frequency.

中文： 振动相位角的变化率，等于频率的二π倍。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

\[\Large\displaystyle f=\frac{1}{T}\]
\[\Large\displaystyle \omega=\frac{2\pi}{T}=2\pi f\]

代入弹簧振子的角频率，得到它的周期。同一根弹簧上，质量越大，周期越长。同一质量接上更硬的弹簧，周期越短。 / Substitute the angular frequency of the mass–spring system to obtain its period. Increasing mass lengthens the period. Increasing stiffness shortens it.

\[\Large\displaystyle T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}}\]

3.3 从投影读出位移、速度和加速度 / Reading displacement, velocity and acceleration

\(t\) 是经过时间（\(\mathrm{s}\)），\(\theta\) 是参考点的相位角（\(\mathrm{rad}\)），\(\phi\) 是 \(t=0\) 的初相位（\(\mathrm{rad}\)）。参考点从正横轴量角，从 \(+A\) 静止释放时 \(\phi=0\)。 / Measure the reference angle from the positive horizontal axis. The initial angle \(\phi\) is zero for release from the positive extreme.

\[\Large\displaystyle \theta=\omega t+\phi\]

位移 / Displacement. 圆上点的水平投影为 \(\cos\theta\)，所以实际位移为 / Multiply the horizontal projection by \(A\).

\[\Large\displaystyle x=A\cos(\omega t+\phi)\]

速度 / Velocity. 把参考圆的半径由 1 缩放到 \(A\)，切向速率为 \(\omega A\)。取切向速度的水平分量，得到物块速度 \(v\)（\(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)）。当 \(0<\theta<\pi\) 时投影向左，\(v<0\)。 / On the circle scaled to radius \(A\), the tangential speed is \(\omega A\). Its horizontal component gives the mass’s signed velocity.

\[\Large\displaystyle v=-\omega A\sin(\omega t+\phi)\]

加速度 / Acceleration. 缩放后圆周运动的向心加速度大小为 \(\omega^2 A\)，始终指向圆心。它的水平分量指向平衡位置。 / Project the inward acceleration of magnitude \(\omega^2 A\) onto the horizontal axis.

\[\Large\displaystyle a=-\omega^2 A\cos(\omega t+\phi)\]

比较位移式与加速度式，立即得到定义中的 \(a=-\omega^2x\)。消去相位即可由位置求速度，正负号根据物体运动方向选择。 / Comparing the displacement and acceleration equations recovers the SHM condition. Eliminating phase gives velocity at a specified displacement. Choose the sign from the direction of motion.

\[\Large\displaystyle v=\pm\omega\sqrt{A^2-x^2}\]

4. 读懂 x、v、a 的时序图与相位 / Time graphs and phase

三图采用同一个时钟，纵轴分别用各自的最大量值归一化。沿同一条竖线读三个量。\(v\) 是 \(x\)–\(t\) 图的斜率，\(a\) 是 \(v\)–\(t\) 图的斜率。 / The graphs share the same time axis. Each vertical axis is normalised by its own amplitude. Read all three quantities at the same time. Velocity is the gradient of the displacement graph, and acceleration is the gradient of the velocity graph.

时刻 / Time 位移 / Displacement 速度 / Velocity 加速度 / Acceleration
\(0\) \(+A\) \(0\) \(-\omega^2 A\)
\(T/4\) \(0\) \(-\omega A\) \(0\)
\(T/2\) \(-A\) \(0\) \(+\omega^2 A\)
\(3T/4\) \(0\) \(+\omega A\) \(0\)
\(T\) \(+A\) \(0\) \(-\omega^2 A\)

在平衡位置，速率达到最大，加速度为零。在两端，速度为零，加速度大小达到最大。 / Speed is greatest at equilibrium. Acceleration magnitude is greatest at the extremes.

\[\Large\displaystyle v_{\max}=\omega A\]
\[\Large\displaystyle |a|_{\max}=\omega^2 A\]

相位 / Phase \(\theta\) 表示振动走到了一个周期中的哪个状态。初相位 \(\phi\) 决定从哪一状态开始。例如同为 \(x=0\)，向左通过对应 \(\theta=\pi/2\)，向右通过对应 \(\theta=3\pi/2\)。 / Phase specifies where the oscillator is in its cycle. The initial phase fixes the starting state. The two equilibrium crossings have opposite velocities and different phases.

相位差 / Phase difference. 比较同频图像上的对应事件，例如两个正峰。若它们相隔 \(\Delta t\)（\(\mathrm{s}\)），相位差的大小以弧度表示为 / For equal-frequency graphs, use the time offset between equivalent points, such as positive peaks.

\[\Large\displaystyle |\Delta\theta|=\frac{2\pi|\Delta t|}{T}\]

在本页余弦约定中，\(v\) 的正峰比下一次 \(x\) 的正峰早 \(T/4\)，所以 \(v\) 超前 \(x\) 四分之一周期（\(\pi/2\)）。\(a\) 与 \(x\) 相差半周期（\(\pi\)），两者反相。 / In this cosine convention, velocity leads displacement by a quarter cycle. Acceleration and displacement are in antiphase.

同一个振子的能量 / Energy of the same oscillator

以平衡位置为振动势能零点。无阻尼时，平衡位置动能最大，两端势能最大，总能量恒定。能量只依赖位移或速率的平方，因此经过半个振动周期就重复。 / Take oscillator potential energy to be zero at equilibrium. Without damping, kinetic energy peaks at equilibrium and potential energy peaks at the extremes. The energy pattern repeats every half period.

\[\Large\displaystyle E_p=\frac12m\omega^2x^2\]
\[\Large\displaystyle E_k=\frac12m\omega^2(A^2-x^2)\]
\[\Large\displaystyle E_{\rm total}=\frac12m\omega^2A^2\]

\(E_p\)、\(E_k\)、\(E_{\rm total}\) 分别为振动势能、动能与总能量，单位均为 \(\mathrm{J}\)。 / Potential, kinetic and total energies are measured in joules.

5. 钟摆模型的特点 / Features of the simple pendulum

回看两种模型图右侧。设摆长 \(L\)（\(\mathrm{m}\)）从支点量到摆球质心，摆角 \(\alpha\)（\(\mathrm{rad}\)）从竖直向下方向量起，弧长位移 \(s\)（\(\mathrm{m}\)）从最低点量起。重力的切向分量指向最低点，切向加速度用 \(a_t\)（\(\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\)）表示。 / In the pendulum diagram, measure length from the pivot to the bob’s centre of mass. Measure the angle from the downward vertical and the arc displacement from the lowest point. The tangential component of weight provides the restoring acceleration.

\[\Large\displaystyle s=L\alpha\]
\[\Large\displaystyle a_t=-g\sin\alpha\]

小摆角用弧度表示时，\(\sin\alpha\approx\alpha\)。代入弧长关系后，切向加速度具有简谐运动的形式。 / For small angles in radians, substitute the small-angle approximation and the arc-length relation into the tangential acceleration.

\[\Large\displaystyle a_t\approx-\frac{g}{L}s\]
\[\Large\displaystyle \omega=\sqrt{\frac{g}{L}}\]
\[\Large\displaystyle T=2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}\]

\(g\) 是重力场强（\(\mathrm{N\,kg^{-1}}\)），数值等于当地自由落体加速度（\(\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\)）。同一地点，摆长越长，周期越长。小角度理想模型的周期与摆球质量无关。 摆角较大时，应保留 \(\sin\alpha\)，小角度周期公式的精度随之降低。摆球在圆弧上运动，用于简谐判定的是沿圆弧的切向加速度。 / A longer pendulum has a longer period at the same location. The bob’s mass cancels. The approximation is accurate for small swings, and it is the tangential acceleration along the arc that enters the SHM condition.

Conditions / 条件与范围

理想简谐运动要求线性回复力且阻尼可忽略；单摆采用小角度近似。

For ideal SHM use a linear restoring force and negligible damping. For a simple pendulum, use small angles so \(\sin\theta\approx\theta\).

Applications / 应用与实验

由初始位置与速度选相位；掌握周期推导。

Choose phase from the initial position and velocity; derive period expressions rather than memorising unexplained equations.

Common errors / 易错点

端点加速度最大但速度零；平衡处加速度零而速率最大。

Maximum acceleration occurs at the extreme position, where velocity is zero. At equilibrium, acceleration is zero and speed is maximum.

示例 / Worked example. 弹簧振子从 \(+A\) 静止释放，\(A=0.060\,\mathrm{m}\)，\(T=2.0\,\mathrm{s}\)。求它首次到达 \(x=+0.030\,\mathrm{m}\) 时的速度、加速度和经过时间。 / A mass starts at rest at the positive extreme. Find its velocity, acceleration and elapsed time at its first arrival at the stated displacement.

  1. 由周期得 \(\omega=\pi\,\mathrm{rad\,s^{-1}}\)。首次从 \(+A\) 向平衡位置运动，速度取负，\(v\approx-0.163\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)。 / Calculate angular frequency and choose the negative velocity branch.
  2. \(x\) 为正，回复加速度为负，\(a\approx-0.296\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\)。 / The acceleration points towards equilibrium.
  3. 单位圆上 \(x/A=1/2\)，首次到达时相位为 \(\pi/3\)，故 \(t=T/6\approx0.333\,\mathrm{s}\)。同一位置在一周期内还会再次经过，此时速度方向不同。 / The first matching phase is \(\pi/3\). The later passage through the same position has the opposite velocity.

3.6.2 方法 1 · 简谐条件与失效判断 / Testing and rejecting SHM

规范表述 / Standard statement: Acceleration is directly proportional to displacement from equilibrium and is directed towards equilibrium. / 加速度与相对平衡位置的位移成正比，方向指向平衡位置。

  1. 先写合力，不把单独的弹簧力误作合力。竖直弹簧以静态平衡为原点，可得 \(F_{net}=-kx\)，\(\omega^2=k/m\)。 / Use the resultant force and the equilibrium origin.
  2. \(a\)–\(x\) 图须为过原点的负斜率直线；斜率为 \(-\omega^2\)，故 \(f=\sqrt{-m_{graph}}/(2\pi)\)。 / Use both proportionality and direction.
  3. 弹性绳松弛后，张力为零而合力为重力，加速度恒定为 \(g\)，不再满足 \(a\propto-x\)。库仑力始终指向中心也不保证其大小与位移成正比。 / A restoring direction alone is insufficient.
  4. 外界阻力问题根据是否显著破坏上述比例作判断，并给出题境中的理由；不要只说“有阻力所以一定不是”。 / Explain how the actual extra force affects the SHM condition.

对应真题 / Examples： PH03 · 202001 · Q01；PH03 · 202301 · Q02；PH03 · 202201 · Q01；PH03 · 202206 · Q04；PH03 · 202606 · Q03。

3.6.2 方法 2 · 位移、时间与能量图像 / Displacement, timing and energy graphs

  1. \(x=A\cos(\omega t+\phi)\)，用初始位置和运动方向确定相位；求首次到达或返回某位置，画一周期并选满足方向的最小正时间差，不能只取反余弦的主值。 / Select the physical branch, not merely the inverse-trigonometric value.
  2. \(v=\pm\omega\sqrt{A^2-x^2}\) 的正负由运动方向决定；\(a=-\omega^2x\) 的方向由位移决定。\(v\) 与 \(x\) 相差 \(\pi/2\)，\(a\) 与 \(x\) 相差 \(\pi\)。 / Motion direction and acceleration direction are different decisions.
  3. \(E=\frac12m\omega^2A^2\)，\(E_p=\frac12m\omega^2x^2\)，\(E_k=\frac12m\omega^2(A^2-x^2)\)。能量图不取负值；能量随时间的重复周期为 \(T/2\)。 / Distinguish displacement cycles from energy cycles.
  4. 竖直振子“振动能量”以平衡位置为参考，包括重力与弹性势能的合成变化，不能把振幅当作弹簧总伸长。衰减平均功率用 \((E_i-E_f)/\Delta t\)，能量与振幅平方成正比。 / Use the oscillatory energy rather than total spring extension.
  5. 碰钉摆分段使用有效摆长；高度图峰谷间的时间不一定是完整位移周期。周期实验误把半振动当一周期，会使由 \(T^2\) 求出的 \(g\) 偏大四倍。 / Identify what event is being timed.
