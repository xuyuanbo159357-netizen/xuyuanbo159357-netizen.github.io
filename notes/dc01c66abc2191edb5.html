3.11.1 内能、热力学第一定律与比热容 / Energy transfer by heating and doing work

考纲 / Specification： 3.11.1　｜　本单元知识谱系

知识逐项讲解 / Concept-by-concept guide

internal energy — 内能

Definition / 定义

↗ internal energy — 内能

Definition / 定义

English: The sum of the randomly distributed kinetic energies and potential energies of the particles in a system.

中文： 系统内粒子随机运动的动能与粒子间相互作用势能的总和。

定义评分依据 / Definition marking points： PH04 202401-Q01。原答案在题目下展开；此处按评分要点概括。 Summarised from the marking points; originals are folded below each question.

评分依据 / Mark-scheme basis： PH04 · 202401 · Q01.1

Formula / 公式

First law of thermodynamics / 热力学第一定律

Law statement / 定律表述

English: The increase in the internal energy of a system equals the energy transferred to it by heating plus the work done on the system.

中文： 系统内能的增加量，等于通过加热传入系统的能量与外界对系统所做的功之和。

\[\Large\displaystyle \Delta U=Q+W_{\mathrm{on}}\]

Symbols and SI units / 符号与 SI 单位

符号 / Symbol 物理量 / Quantity SI 单位 / SI unit
\(\Delta U\) 内能变化 / change in internal energy \(\mathrm{J}\)
\(Q\) 传入系统的热量 / energy transferred into system by heating \(\mathrm{J}\)
\(W_{\mathrm{on}}\) 外界对系统做功 / work done on system \(\mathrm{J}\)

Conditions / 条件与说明： 这是能量收支关系，不是内能的微观定义；Q 向系统传热为正，W_on 对系统做功为正。 / This is an energy-balance equation, not the microscopic definition; heating into and work on the system are positive.

specific heat capacity — 比热容

Definition / 定义

↗ specific heat capacity — 比热容

Definition / 定义

English: The energy required to raise the temperature of unit mass of a substance by one kelvin without a change of state.

中文： 无物态变化时，使单位质量物质升温一开尔文所需能量。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式

\[\Large\displaystyle c=\frac{Q}{m\Delta T}\]

Symbols and SI units / 符号与 SI 单位

符号 / Symbol 物理量 / Quantity SI 单位 / SI unit
\(c\) 比热容 / specific heat capacity \(\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}\)
\(Q\) 用于升温的热量 / energy transferred by heating \(\mathrm{J}\)
\(m\) 质量 / mass \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(\Delta T\) 温度变化 / temperature change \(\mathrm{K}\)

Conditions / 条件与说明： 无相变；实验须考虑散热等能量转移。 / No change of state; consider heat loss in experiments.

specific latent heat — 比潜热

Definition / 定义

↗ specific latent heat — 比潜热

Definition / 定义

English: The energy required to change the state of unit mass of a substance without changing its temperature.

中文： 温度不变时，使单位质量物质改变物态所需能量。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式

\[\Large\displaystyle l=\frac{Q}{m}\]

Symbols and SI units / 符号与 SI 单位

符号 / Symbol 物理量 / Quantity SI 单位 / SI unit
\(l\) 比潜热 / specific latent heat \(\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}}\)
\(Q\) 用于相变的热量 / energy for change of state \(\mathrm{J}\)
\(m\) 发生相变的质量 / mass changing state \(\mathrm{kg}\)

Conditions / 条件与说明： 相变过程中温度不变。 / Temperature remains constant during the change of state.

Conditions / 条件与范围

流入系统的热量和外界对系统做功取正；升降温用比热容，恒温相变用潜热；有给定热损或容器热容时须计入。

Positive Q is heating into the system; positive work is done on the system. Heating/cooling without phase change uses c; phase change at constant temperature uses latent heat. Include heat loss or container heat capacity if given.

Explanation / 解释

内能、温度与第一定律 / Internal energy and the first law

内能为系统粒子随机运动的动能和相互作用势能之和，不包括系统整体的平动动能或相对于地面的整体重力势能。温度反映粒子平均动能，不能单凭温度比较不同质量、物态系统的总内能。

Internal energy is the sum of randomly distributed particle kinetic and interaction potential energies. It excludes the body's bulk kinetic and external gravitational energies. Temperature alone does not determine total internal energy.

统一采用考纲约定 \(\Delta U=Q+W_{\rm on}\)：加热输入为正，外界对系统做功为正；气体膨胀对外做功时 \(W_{\rm on}<0\)。绝热过程 \(Q=0\)，不等于温度不变；理想气体等温才有 \(\Delta U=0\)，继而 \(Q=-W_{\rm on}\)。

Use the specification sign convention: heating into the system and work done on it are positive. Expansion gives negative work on the gas. Adiabatic means no heating, not constant temperature. An isothermal ideal gas has zero internal-energy change.

图示：固态粒子的运动 / Particle motion in a solid

读图 / Reading： 粒子围绕平衡位置振动；内能含随机运动动能与相互作用势能，不能只说“分子运动速度”。

Particles vibrate about equilibrium; internal energy includes random kinetic and interaction potential energy.

升温、物态变化与加热曲线 / Heating and phase changes

单一物态内 \(Q=mc\Delta\theta\)，\(c\) 单位 \(\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}\)。物态变化 \(Q=ml\)，\(l\) 单位 \(\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}}\)；熔化和汽化要用相应潜热。跨越多个阶段时分段求和。

Within a phase use specific heat capacity; during a phase change use the appropriate specific latent heat. Add contributions when several stages occur.

纯物质在固定压强下相变的温度平台：平均随机动能不变，输入能量改变粒子相互作用势能。不能把“温度不变”推成“没有吸热或内能不变”。

At a constant-pressure phase-change plateau, mean random kinetic energy is unchanged while interaction potential energy changes. Constant temperature does not imply zero energy transfer or unchanged internal energy.

恒定净加热功率下，单相区温度—时间斜率 \(P_{\rm net}/(mc)\)；平台时间 \(ml/P_{\rm net}\)。冷却或凝固时能量流出，符号按所选系统处理。

At constant net heating power, the temperature gradient depends inversely on heat capacity, and plateau duration on latent heat. Apply signs consistently during cooling.

连续流动与必做实验8 / Continuous flow and practical 8

连续流加热稳态下 \(P_{\rm net}=\dot m c(\theta_{\rm out}-\theta_{\rm in})\)，若有物态变化增加 \(\dot m l\) 项。质量流率 \(\dot m\) 单位 kg/s；不能把一段时间流过的质量误当流率。

At steady continuous flow, net heating power supplies the enthalpy change per second, using mass flow rate rather than total mass. Include latent heat when a phase change occurs in the stated model.

电学测比热容：测 \(V,I,t,m,\Delta\theta\)，理想 \(VIt=mc\Delta\theta\)。绝热包覆、良好热接触和搅拌可改善测量；容器或加热器吸热、向环境散热会使直接算得 \(c\) 偏大，需考虑修正。

Measure electrical input and temperature rise. Insulation, thermal contact and mixing improve the method. Heat absorbed by apparatus or lost to surroundings makes an uncorrected value of specific heat capacity too large.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.11.1

图示：电加热量热装置 / Electrical calorimetry setup

读图 / Reading： 电流表串联，电压表跨加热器；加热器与温度计须接触良好，样品外保温。只有进入样品的能量才用于 mcΔT。

Measure heater current in series and voltage across it; ensure good thermal contact and insulation. Only energy entering the specimen contributes to mcΔT.

纯物质定压升温示意：斜段温升，平台物态变化；区分动能和势能变化。 Qualitative heating curve.

图示：升温与相变平台 / Heating and phase-change plateaux

读图 / Reading： 平台处温度不变，但输入能量仍改变内能；理想恒功率且忽略散热时，平台时长与该相变所需能量有关。

During a plateau temperature is constant while internal energy changes; with constant power and negligible loss, its duration indicates transition energy.

Applications / 应用与实验

必做实验 8：电加热已知质量，测电流、电压、时间与温升，保温并保持热接触，液体搅拌，估计热损；温度—时间斜率给出升温率。

Required practical 8: heat a measured mass using an electrical heater, record I,V,t and temperature change, insulate and ensure thermal contact, stir liquids, allow thermal equilibrium, estimate losses. A temperature–time gradient gives heating rate.

Common errors / 易错点

热是温差导致的能量传递，不是储存量；内能不包含物体整体平移动能。

Heat is energy transferred because of a temperature difference, not energy stored. Internal energy excludes bulk motion of the whole object.

真题检验与答题思路 / Exam coverage and approaches

依据本库现有真题与评分标准核对。下列步骤按题型使用，先核对题设条件；综合题需结合其他关联知识点。 / Checked against the papers and mark schemes held in this vault; select the approach that fits the stated conditions.

3.11.1 方法 1 · 分段热量、混合与流量 / Staged heating, mixtures and flow

  1. 按物态和过程分段：升温 \(mc\Delta T\)、相变 \(mL\)、再升温；每段使用该物态的比热。沸腾后剩余能量再用于汽化，不能整段都用 \(mc\Delta T\)。 / Split the energy budget at each phase change.
  2. 蒸汽凝结进入量热器时，放热为 \(m_sL+m_sc_w(100-T_f)\)；吸热方要同时计入原水和容器。 / Include cooling of the condensate and heating of the container.
  3. 连续流动用 \(P_{useful}=\dot m c\Delta T\)，\(\dot m=\rho\dot V\)。\(\mathrm{kW\,h}\) 是能量，\(1\,\mathrm{kW\,h}=3.6\times10^6\,J\)；Mg是兆克，不是mg。 / Distinguish mass flow, volume flow and energy units.
  4. 冰融化实验用相同时间内开、关加热器的融水质量差扣背景；先将不同记录时长归一。 / Subtract background melting rates, not unmatched masses.
  5. 冷却曲线的瞬时热功率 \(P=-mc\,dT/dt\)，使用该时刻切线，不用整段平均冷却率。 / Use a local gradient for instantaneous power.
  6. 同一大题后问引用前问结果时，按本库课堂规范使用前问的报告答案；证明题使用题目给出的目标值。仅在同一小问内部保留计算精度。 / Use the previously reported answer in a later part, including a stated show-that value.

3.11.1 方法 2 · 热力学第一定律的符号链 / First-law signs and explanations

本库统一用 \(\Delta U=Q+W_{on}\)：入热 \(Q>0\)，外界对系统做功 \(W_{on}>0\)；气体膨胀对外做功则 \(W_{on}<0\)。 / Use work done on the system in this sign convention.

  1. 明确系统 → 判热传递方向 → 判谁对谁做功 → 写符号 → 得 \(\Delta U\) → 连接平均分子动能与温度。 / Make the causal chain explicit.
  2. 快速压缩通常因来不及明显换热可近似 \(Q\simeq0\)，\(W_{on}>0\)，故 \(U,T\) 升高；不是摩擦必然升温。 / Explain the adiabatic approximation by the timescale.
  3. 理想气体定温且粒子数固定时 \(\Delta U=0\)；压缩功必须以热传出。若与环境严格同温，有限速率热传递缺少温差；缓慢、薄壁良导体可使过程接近等温。 / Distinguish an ideal limit from a practical approximation.
  4. 内能是微观动能与相互作用势能总和；单原子理想气体 \(U=3NkT/2\)。增添粒子可在温度不变时增加总内能。 / Constant temperature does not imply constant total energy when the amount changes.
  5. 热泵给室内的热量可为电功加外界吸收热量；比电功大不违反守恒，不能把供热性能比直接当作热机效率。 / Draw the complete energy budget.

对应真题 / Examples： PH04 · 201906 · Q01；PH04 · 202201 · Q01；PH04 · 202501 · Q04；PH04 · 202306 · Q02；PH04 · 202001 · Q02。

3.11.1 方法 3 · 量热实验的误差方向 / Calorimetry and error direction

  • 每条建议写“具体改变 → 减少的能量转移或测量问题 → 结果如何改善”。重复只减少随机影响，不能消除固定热损失。 / Match the improvement to the mechanism.
  • 忽略散热而用全部电能求比热或汽化潜热，通常使结果偏大；漏计已融化水或液滴造成的质量过大，可使潜热结果偏小。 / Trace the error through the actual equation.
  • 加热器停止后勿立即移出，可让其余热传给样品；搅拌、保温、加盖分别针对温度不均、向外散热和蒸发。 / Give a reason for each procedural change.
  • 使初末温度分布在室温两侧可近似抵消部分进、出热量；须说明该近似条件。 / State why the temperature range is chosen.
