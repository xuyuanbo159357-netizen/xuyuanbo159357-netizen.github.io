简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

With the switch open there is no current, so there is no voltage drop inside the cell and the reading is its emf, 1.53 V. Closing the switch produces current through the cell's internal resistance. Some emf is then lost internally as Ir, so the terminal voltmeter reading decreases.
