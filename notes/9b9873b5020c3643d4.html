3.4.4 直流电路与电功率 / Circuits

考纲 / Specification： 3.4.4　｜　本单元知识谱系

Definition / 定义

Definition / 定义

English: A circuit arrangement in which components share a single current path.

中文： 元件共用一条电流路径的连接方式。

Formula / 公式与单位

掌握串并联等效电阻、电能和功率。

\(R_{\mathrm{s}}=R_1+R_2+\cdots\); \(1/R_{\mathrm{p}}=1/R_1+1/R_2+\cdots\); \(E=IVt\); \(P=IV=I^2R=V^2/R\).

Conditions / 条件与范围

恒定电压和电流时使用电能公式；功率公式中的电流、电压和电阻必须对应同一元件。

Apply \(E=IVt\) when voltage and current are constant. \(I^2R\) and \(V^2/R\) refer to the voltage/current of the same resistor.

Explanation / 解释

串并联和守恒 / Connections and conservation

串联：同一电流依次通过元件，电势降相加，\(R_T=\sum R\)。并联：支路接同一对节点，电势差相同，电流相加，\(1/R_T=\sum1/R\)。并联总电阻小于任一支路电阻。判断连接看节点，不能只看图形位置。

Series components share current and their potential drops add. Parallel branches share potential difference and their currents add. Parallel equivalent resistance is smaller than any branch resistance; identify connections from nodes.

节点：\(\sum I_{\rm in}=\sum I_{\rm out}\)，来自电荷守恒。回路：按统一绕行方向列电动势和电势降的代数和为零，来自能量守恒。先化简等效电阻，再逐级还原支路量。

The junction rule expresses charge conservation; the loop rule expresses energy conservation. Use consistent signs around a loop, reduce the network, then recover individual branch quantities.

电池组合 / Combinations of cells

电池串联时电动势按极性代数相加，内阻相加；反向的一节会抵消部分电动势。\(n\) 节相同电池同向并联时 \(\varepsilon_{\rm eq}=\varepsilon\)，\(r_{\rm eq}=r/n\)；供电电压不会变为 \(n\varepsilon\)。

Series emfs add algebraically and internal resistances add. For \(n\) identical cells in parallel with the same polarity, emf is unchanged and internal resistance is \(r/n\).

功率与能量比较 / Power and energy comparisons

\(P=IV=I^2R=V^2/R\) 中各量必须属于同一元件；\(E=IVt\) 要求该时间内电压、电流恒定。恒流比较用 \(I^2R\)，恒压比较用 \(V^2/R\)，结论方向不同。

Use the voltage, current and resistance of the same component. \(E=IVt\) requires constant voltage and current. At fixed current power increases with resistance; at fixed voltage it decreases with resistance.

效率 \(\eta=E_{\rm useful}/E_{\rm input}=P_{\rm useful}/P_{\rm input}\)。包含电源内阻时区分供电功率 \(\varepsilon I\)、负载功率 \(VI\) 和内耗 \(I^2r\)。

Distinguish total supplied power, useful load power and internal dissipation when source resistance matters.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.4.4

图示：串联电路 / Series circuit

读图 / Reading： 同一串联回路电流相同，各电阻电压相加；不能把每个电阻都当作独占全部电源电压。

Series components share current; their potential differences add to the supply value.

图示：并联电路 / Parallel circuit

读图 / Reading： 支路两端连接同一对节点，所以电势差相同；总电流为各支路电流之和。

Branches connect across the same two nodes and share potential difference; branch currents add to the total.

Applications / 应用与实验

逐级化简混联电路，再求支路电压、电流；功率比较须看电源条件。

Reduce mixed circuits stage by stage, then recover branch voltages and currents. Compare power under constant supply voltage or constant current as stated.

Common errors / 易错点

固定电压时电阻越大功率越小，固定电流时相反；支路电流不同于总电流。

A larger resistance produces less power at fixed voltage but more at fixed current. Do not treat every branch current as the total supply current.

真题检验与答题思路 / Exam coverage and approaches

依据本库现有真题与评分标准核对。下列步骤按题型使用，先核对题设条件；综合题需结合其他关联知识点。 / Checked against the papers and mark schemes held in this vault; select the approach that fits the stated conditions.

3.4.4 方法 1 · 节点、开关与电源负载 / Nodes, switches and source loading

  1. 用理想导线连接的点合并为同一节点；按开关状态重画串并联结构。 / Merge equipotential nodes and redraw the circuit.
  2. 先判断电源是否有内阻。理想电源并联新支路时原支路电压不变；若 \(r>0\)，总电流增加会增加内部损失，使端电压下降，原支路电流可能减小。 / Distinguish an ideal source from a source with internal resistance.
  3. 每支路 \(p\) 个相同电池串联、共 \(b\) 支路并联：\(\varepsilon_{eq}=p\varepsilon\)，\(r_{eq}=pr/b\)。移去一个电池若使该支路断路，应去掉整条支路再计算。 / A removed cell may open an entire branch.
  4. 用节点电流守恒和回路能量守恒建立方程；\(\varepsilon=IR+Ir\) 中各项都是单位电荷的能量。 / Interpret emf and voltage as energy per unit charge.

对应真题 / Examples： PH02 · 202606 · Q08；PH02 · 202401 · Q08；PH02 · 202406 · Q05；PH02 · 202406 · Q03。

3.4.4 方法 2 · 功率、百分变化与效率链 / Power and efficiency chains

先说明保持不变的是 \(I\)、\(V\) 还是电源参数，再选 \(P=IV=I^2R=V^2/R\)。 / Choose the power form after identifying what remains constant.

  • 电阻变为 \(1.15R\) 且电压恒定，功率比为 \(1/1.15\)，不可直接说减少15%。 / Calculate the ratio before the percentage change.
  • 连续能量转换的总效率为各阶段效率的乘积；每一级都要明确输入和有用输出。 / Multiply stage efficiencies with consistent input/output definitions.
  • 串联电阻相加时加绝对不确定度；乘除及幂次才按相应百分不确定度规则。 / Match the uncertainty rule to the operation.

对应真题 / Examples： PH02 · 202506 · Q15；PH02 · 202506 · Q16；PH02 · 201806 · Q11；PH02 · 202606 · Q13。
