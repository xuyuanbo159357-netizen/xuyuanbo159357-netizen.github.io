Concept / 概念

potential divider — 分压器

Definition / 定义

English: An arrangement of series resistors used to provide a fraction of a supply potential difference.

中文： 利用串联电阻提供电源电势差一部分的电路。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.
