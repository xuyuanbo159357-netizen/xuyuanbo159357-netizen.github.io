U2/07 常用物理模型

弹簧振子与单摆实验 / Pendulum and mass-spring

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

pendulum → mass-spring → period → timing → 弹簧振子与单摆实验

推理顺序 / Reasoning

选周期关系，多周期计时，作周期平方图，用斜率和控制变量求参数。

Choose the correct oscillator period relation; measure many cycles; plot \(T^2\) against mass or length; use the gradient and controlled variables.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.5.1

3.5.1 振动系统、能量与阻尼

弹簧近似轻且满足胡克定律、阻尼可忽略；单摆需小角度、轻绳和近似质点摆球；摆长为支点至球心距离。

Use a light Hookean spring and negligible damping for the spring formula; use a small-angle pendulum, light string and concentrated bob for the pendulum formula. Length is pivot to the centre of the bob.

掌握弹簧振子、单摆周期与频率关系。

\(T=2\pi\sqrt{m/k}\) for a mass–spring system; \(T=2\pi\sqrt{l/g}\) for a simple pendulum; \(f=1/T\).
