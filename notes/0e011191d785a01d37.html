Concept / 概念

stationary wave — 驻波

Definition / 定义

English: A wave pattern with fixed nodes and antinodes, formed by the superposition of two waves of the same frequency and amplitude travelling in opposite directions.

中文： 两列频率相同、振幅相同且反向传播的波叠加，形成波节与波腹位置固定的波形。

图示：波节与波腹 / Nodes and antinodes

读图 / Reading： 两条曲线表示同一驻波的不同瞬间。波节始终位移为零，波腹振幅最大；相邻波节相距 λ/2。

The curves are different instants of one stationary wave; nodes remain at zero displacement and adjacent nodes are λ/2 apart.
