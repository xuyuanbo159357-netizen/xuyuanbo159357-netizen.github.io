核衰变活度与背景修正 / Activity and half-life

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

activity → decay constant → half-life → background → 核衰变活度与背景修正

推理顺序 / Reasoning

先扣背景并保持探测几何，再用活度与核数关系以及一致时间单位计算。

Subtract background; keep detection geometry fixed; use activity proportional to nuclei number; apply exponential or logarithmic ratios with consistent time units.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.9.2

3.9.2 放射性指数衰变

采用同一种核素且概率恒定；固定探测效率与几何、扣除背景后，计数率才与活度成正比。

Use a single radionuclide with constant decay probability. Detected count rate is proportional to activity only with fixed detection efficiency/geometry and corrected background.

活度单位 Bq，衰变常量单位 s\(^{-1}\)；质量与摩尔质量使用一致单位。

\(dN/dt=-\lambda N\); \(N=N_0e^{-\lambda t}\); \(A=\lambda N=A_0e^{-\lambda t}\); \(T_{1/2}=\ln2/\lambda\); \(N=(m/M)N_A\).
