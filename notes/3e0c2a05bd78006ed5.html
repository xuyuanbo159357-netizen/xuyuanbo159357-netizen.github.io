“Ohm’s law”（欧姆定律） 伏安特性与工作点 / Characteristic and operating point 08.1；相关题干 / plus context：先看坐标和工作点，再求 V/I 或 VI；非欧姆曲线的切线斜率不直接等于静态电阻。 / Check axes and operating point before calculating resistance or power.
“parallel”（并联）；“resistor”（电阻器）；“circuit”（电路） 混联电路与功率分配 / Series and parallel 题干与相关小问 / Stem and related parts：辨认串并联及支路，按电荷与能量守恒求各电流、电压和功率。 / Resolve the actual network and distinguish total quantities from branch quantities.
“thermistor”（热敏电阻） 热敏光敏分压器 / Potential divider 题干与相关小问 / Stem and related parts：先判断温度或光照怎样改变元件电阻；若题给分压电路，再确定输出端及负载。 / Identify the sensor resistance change; for a divider, then analyse the output and any load.
“internal resistance”（内阻）；“emf”（电动势） 电源内阻与图像截距 / EMF and internal resistance 题干与相关小问 / Stem and related parts：区分电动势、路端电势差和内阻压降；V-I 直线的截距与负斜率分别对应电动势和内阻。 / Distinguish emf from terminal pd and interpret the voltage–current graph.
