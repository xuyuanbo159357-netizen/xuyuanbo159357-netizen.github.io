简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

The waste emits penetrating gamma radiation, so it needs thick shielding such as lead or concrete. With a 30-year half-life, it remains hazardous for many decades and needs secure storage for many half-lives, for example at least 150 years in the cited comparison.
