简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

The gradient of the velocity-time graph is acceleration. Initially, drag is small, so the resultant downward force and acceleration are large. As speed increases, drag increases. The resultant force and acceleration therefore decrease. When drag equals weight, the resultant force and gradient become zero and the speed is constant.
