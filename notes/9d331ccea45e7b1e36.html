U4/07 常用物理模型

风水来流功率 / Wind and flow rate

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

wind → flow → density → swept area → turbine → 风水来流功率

推理顺序 / Reasoning

由密度、面积、速率求质量流率，再乘单位质量能量；区分来流动能和水头，避免重复计能。

Find mass per second from density, area and speed; multiply by energy per kilogram; choose incoming kinetic energy or gravitational head without double counting.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.13.2

3.13.2 风能与风电机组

假设入射风近似均匀；使用题给效率，不自行添加需记忆的贝茨极限定量值。

Assume uniform incoming wind, air density and swept area. Use the stated conversion efficiency; a numerical Betz limit is not required unless supplied.

这是可用功率，非总能量；叶片扫掠面积为半径平方乘π。

\(\dot m=\rho Av\); \(P_{\mathrm{available}}=\frac12\rho Av^3=\frac12\rho\pi r^2v^3\); \(P_{\mathrm{output}}=\eta P_{\mathrm{available}}\).

3.13.4

3.13.4 水电与抽水蓄能

水头电站用高度差和质量流率；来流动能用密度、面积、速率；重力势能转为动能后不可重复相加。

For a head-driven plant, use the height drop and mass flow; for incoming-stream kinetic power, use density, area and speed. Do not double-count the same gravitational energy after it has become kinetic energy.

体积流率 \(Q_v\) 与电荷 Q 区分；水头模型与水流动能模型按题目条件选择。

\(P_{\mathrm{grav}}=\dot m gh=\rho Q_vgh\); stream kinetic power \(P=\frac12\rho Av^3\); \(E=mgh\); electrical output \(=\eta E\) or \(\eta P\).
