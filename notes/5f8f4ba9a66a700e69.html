推理顺序 / Reasoning

看端点条件、数波腹，求波长，再用张力与线密度关系，说明反射与叠加。

Inspect end conditions and count loops; find wavelength; use the tension and linear-density relation; explain reflection and superposition.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.5.5

3.5.5 叠加原理、驻波与谐波

标准完整波节图要求反向波同频、等振幅；弦需均匀且两端固定。采用“第一谐波”和“谐波”术语。

For the standard complete-node pattern, the counter-propagating waves have equal frequency and equal amplitude. String relationships assume uniform linear density and both ends fixed. Use “first harmonic” and “harmonics”.

弦张力用 \(F\) 表示，避免与周期 \(T\) 混淆；两端固定弦的第 \(n\) 谐波含 \(n\) 个波腹。

\(f_1=\frac{1}{2L}\sqrt{F/\mu}\); \(\mu=m/L\); \(\lambda_n=2L/n\) and \(f_n=nf_1\) for a string fixed at both ends.
