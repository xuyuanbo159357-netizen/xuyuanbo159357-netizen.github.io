关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

parallel plates → deflection → trajectory → perpendicular → 带电粒子平行板偏转

推理顺序 / Reasoning

求匀强场与加速度，先求水平飞行时间，再求偏转和出口速度。

Find uniform field and acceleration; use horizontal flight time; solve the transverse motion and exit velocity; motion becomes tangential after leaving.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.8.2

3.8.2 电场强度与带电粒子偏转

平行板匀强近似忽略边缘效应；粒子电荷、质量及水平速度近似恒定。

Uniform parallel-plate approximation neglects edge effects. Treat the deflected particle’s charge, mass and horizontal speed as constant.

单位为 N C\(^{-1}\) 或 V m\(^{-1}\)；负电荷受力与场方向相反。

\(E=F/Q\); uniform \(E=\Delta V/d\) in magnitude; radial magnitude \(E=|Q|/(4\pi\varepsilon_0r^2)\); \(a=qE/m\).
