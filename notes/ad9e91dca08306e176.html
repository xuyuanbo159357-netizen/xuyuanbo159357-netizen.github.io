RP8 电加热法测比热容 / Required practical 8 - Specific heat capacity

3.11.1 内能、热力学第一定律与比热容

物理量与符号 / Quantities and symbols

符号沿用本节公式；若题目另有定义，以题设为准。 / These symbols follow this section; use any notation defined in the question.

物理量 / Quantity 符号 / Symbol SI单位 / SI unit 说明 / Notes
比热容 / specific heat capacity \(c\) \(\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}\) 不是波速 c / Not wave speed.
质量 / mass \(m\) \(\mathrm{kg}\) 与重力不同 / Not weight.
电流 / electric current \(I\) \(\mathrm{A}\) 与光强、转动惯量的 I 区分 / Not intensity or moment of inertia.
电势差、电压 / potential difference, voltage \(V,\ \Delta V\) \(\mathrm{V}\) 斜体 V 是物理量，正体 V 是伏特 / Italic V is the quantity; upright V is the volt.
时间、时间间隔 / time, time interval \(t,\ \Delta t\) \(\mathrm{s}\)
温度变化 / temperature change \(\Delta T,\ \Delta\theta\) \(\mathrm{K}\) 温差用 K 或 °C 数值相同 / Same numerical change in K or °C.
能量 / energy \(E\) \(\mathrm{J}\) 与杨氏模量、电场强度等 E 区分 / Not Young modulus or electric field strength here.
加热传递的能量、热量 / energy transferred by heating \(Q\) \(\mathrm{J}\) 不是电荷 Q；本节输入为正 / Not charge; input is positive here.

方法、分析与误差 / Method, analysis and uncertainty

1

测样品质量，使加热器和温度计充分接触并保温。

Measure specimen mass; place heater and thermometer in good thermal contact and insulate the specimen.

2

测电功率、加热时间与温升，并等待热平衡。

Measure electrical power and heating time, record temperature rise and allow thermal equilibration.

3

可忽略损耗时用电能除以质量与温升；有估计损耗和容器贡献时修正。

Use c=IVt/(mΔT) in the negligible-loss model; include losses/container contributions if estimated.

4

由温度图斜率求升温率，通过冷却或匹配条件重复估计损失。

A temperature–time gradient gives ΔT/Δt; compare heating/cooling behaviour or repeat at matched conditions to estimate losses.

5

避免烫伤与过热，测温位置应代表整体。

Avoid burns and overheating; measure temperature where it represents the whole specimen.

图示：电加热量热装置 / Electrical calorimetry setup

读图 / Reading： 电流表串联，电压表跨加热器；加热器与温度计须接触良好，样品外保温。只有进入样品的能量才用于 mcΔT。

Measure heater current in series and voltage across it; ensure good thermal contact and insulation. Only energy entering the specimen contributes to mcΔT.

定量分析与条件核对 / Quantitative analysis and controls

连续流动与必做实验8 / Continuous flow and practical 8

连续流加热稳态下 \(P_{\rm net}=\dot m c(\theta_{\rm out}-\theta_{\rm in})\)，若有物态变化增加 \(\dot m l\) 项。质量流率 \(\dot m\) 单位 kg/s；不能把一段时间流过的质量误当流率。

At steady continuous flow, net heating power supplies the enthalpy change per second, using mass flow rate rather than total mass. Include latent heat when a phase change occurs in the stated model.

电学测比热容：测 \(V,I,t,m,\Delta\theta\)，理想 \(VIt=mc\Delta\theta\)。绝热包覆、良好热接触和搅拌可改善测量；容器或加热器吸热、向环境散热会使直接算得 \(c\) 偏大，需考虑修正。

Measure electrical input and temperature rise. Insulation, thermal contact and mixing improve the method. Heat absorbed by apparatus or lost to surroundings makes an uncorrected value of specific heat capacity too large.
