Concept / 概念

modal dispersion — 模式色散

Definition / 定义

English: Pulse broadening because rays or modes travel along paths of different lengths in an optical fibre.

中文： 光纤内不同光路或传播模式路程不同而导致的脉冲展宽。

定义评分依据 / Definition marking points： PH02 202406-Q08。原答案在题目下展开；此处按评分要点概括。 Summarised from the marking points; originals are folded below each question.

图示讲解 / Diagram explained

图示：模式色散 / Modal dispersion

读图 / Reading： 不同传播路径用时不同，同一个输入脉冲变宽；这与不同波长传播速度不同所致的材料色散要区分。

Different path lengths give different arrival times and broaden a pulse; distinguish this from wavelength-dependent material dispersion.
