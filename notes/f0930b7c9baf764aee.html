U3/07 常用物理模型

磁力与粒子圆轨道 / Magnetic field and curvature

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

magnetic field → charge → cyclotron → curvature → 磁力与粒子圆轨道

推理顺序 / Reasoning

按电荷符号定受力，用磁力提供向心合力，区分磁场偏转与电场加速。

Find force direction using charge sign; use magnetic force as radial resultant; separate steering by B from acceleration by E.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.10.1

3.10.1 磁感应强度与通电导线受力

垂直公式要求电流与磁场垂直；其他角度先找垂直分量。

Use the perpendicular formula under the stated orientation. With other angles, identify the component perpendicular to the field if required by the given situation.

长度取处于磁场中的有效导线长度。

\(F=BIL\) for perpendicular current and field; \(B=F/(IL)\). Unit T = N A\(^{-1}\) m\(^{-1}\).

3.10.2

3.10.2 磁场中的带电粒子与回旋加速器

匀强磁场、垂直入射、非相对论速率；平行磁场运动时磁力为零。

Assume uniform magnetic field, perpendicular entry and non-relativistic speed. If velocity is parallel to B, the magnetic force is zero.

磁力提供向心力；非相对论模型中回旋周期与速率无关。

\(F=|q|vB\) for perpendicular motion; \(r=mv/(|q|B)=p/(|q|B)\); \(T=2\pi m/(|q|B)\); \(f=|q|B/(2\pi m)\).
