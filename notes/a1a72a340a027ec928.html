1 Students should know that electron diffraction suggests that particles possess wave properties and the photoelectric effect suggests that electromagnetic waves have a particulate nature. 应知道电子衍射表明粒子具有波动性质，而光电效应表明电磁波具有粒子性质。 3.5.11 波粒二象性与德布罗意波长 · Explanation / 解释 PDF 20／印刷 21
2 Details of particular methods of particle diffraction are not expected. 不要求具体粒子衍射方法的细节。 3.5.11 波粒二象性与德布罗意波长 · Conditions / 条件与范围 PDF 20／印刷 21
3 de Broglie equation \(\lambda=\frac{h}{mv}\) where mv is the momentum. 德布罗意方程\(\lambda=\frac{h}{mv}\)，其中mv是动量。 3.5.11 波粒二象性与德布罗意波长 · Formula / 公式与单位 PDF 20／印刷 21
4 Students should be able to explain how and why the amount of diffraction changes when the momentum of the particle is changed. 应能解释粒子动量改变时衍射程度如何变化，以及变化的原因。 3.5.11 波粒二象性与德布罗意波长 · Explanation / 解释 PDF 20／印刷 21
