简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

The target rises more than 15 cm. Rebounding gives the arrow a larger change in momentum. The target receives an equal and opposite impulse, so it gains more momentum and has a higher speed after impact. Its greater kinetic energy can become more gravitational potential energy.
