3.9.1 电容充放电与时间常量 / Capacitor charge and discharge

考纲 / Specification： 3.9.1　｜　本单元知识谱系

物理量与符号 / Quantities and symbols

符号沿用本节公式；若题目另有定义，以题设为准。 / These symbols follow this section; use any notation defined in the question.

Concept / 概念

  • time constant — 时间常量

Definition / 定义

↗ time constant — 时间常量

Definition / 定义

English: The time for a discharging capacitor’s charge or voltage to fall to one over e of its initial value.

中文： 放电时电容器电荷或电压降至初值的 e 分之一所需时间。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

定义按考纲与指定教材概括；本页未列出可直接对应的定义题评分条目。 Definitions are summarised from the specification and textbook; no directly matched definition-item citation is claimed here.

Formula / 公式与单位

\(\tau=RC\)；\(t_{1/2}=RC\ln2\)。

放电 / Discharge：\(Q=Q_0e^{-t/RC}\)，\(V_C=V_0e^{-t/RC}\)，\(|I|=(V_0/R)e^{-t/RC}\)，\(\ln V_C=\ln V_0-t/(RC)\)。

从零开始充电 / Charging from zero：\(Q=C\varepsilon(1-e^{-t/RC})\)，\(V_C=\varepsilon(1-e^{-t/RC})\)，\(I=(\varepsilon/R)e^{-t/RC}\)。

充电时电荷和电压上升、电流衰减；放电时三者大小均衰减。电流符号须先规定正方向。

Charge and voltage rise during charging while current falls; all three magnitudes fall during discharge. State a current reference direction.

Conditions / 条件与范围

电阻应恒定，电容近似理想；有效串联电阻包含相关内阻；有符号电流需先定正方向。

Use a constant resistor and ideal capacitor; include any relevant source/internal resistance in the effective series resistance. State the reference direction before giving a signed current.

Explanation / 解释

初末状态和指数方程 / Initial and final states

初始未充电电容经恒定电源 \(\varepsilon\) 和串联电阻 \(R\) 充电：\(Q=C\varepsilon(1-e^{-t/RC})\)、\(V_C=\varepsilon(1-e^{-t/RC})\)、\(I=(\varepsilon/R)e^{-t/RC}\)。初始电流最大，最终电流趋零，电容电压趋于电源电压。

During charging from zero, charge and capacitor pd rise towards their final values while current falls exponentially. State the assumed initial condition and constant source voltage.

断开电源经 \(R\) 放电：\(Q=Q_0e^{-t/RC}\)、\(V_C=V_0e^{-t/RC}\)、\(|I|=(V_0/R)e^{-t/RC}\)。若把充电方向取正，放电电流为负；符号取决于选定方向，不能与电流大小混用。

During discharge, charge, capacitor voltage and current magnitude decay exponentially. Discharge current is negative if charging current defines the positive direction.

时间常量和图像 / Time constant and graphs

\(\tau=RC\)；放电一时间常量后剩 \(e^{-1}\approx36.8\%\)，充电电荷和电压达到最终值的 \(63.2\%\)，但充电电流降到初值的 \(36.8\%\)。减半时间 \(t_{1/2}=RC\ln2\) 也等于充电初始达到一半最终值的时间，之后每个相同时间只把“未充满差值”减半。

After one time constant, discharge values and charging current are at 36.8% of initial values; charging voltage and charge reach 63.2% of final values. Equal half-times halve the remaining charging deficit, not repeatedly double accumulated charge.

\(I=dQ/dt\)（方向一致时），\(I\)–\(t\) 有符号面积为电荷转移量。放电 \(\ln V=\ln V_0-t/RC\)，斜率 \(-1/RC\)；充电应作 \(\ln(\varepsilon-V_C)\)–\(t\)，不能直接把上升的 \(\ln V_C\) 当直线。

Current relates to the charge gradient and its area gives transferred charge. Discharge voltage is linear on a natural-log plot; charging requires the remaining voltage deficit, not \(\ln V_C\).

必做实验6 / Required practical 6

电压表并联电容，充电后切换到已知电阻放电，记录多组 \((t,V_C)\)；选择可可靠采样的 \(RC\)。由对数图斜率求 \(RC=-1/\mathrm{gradient}\)，还可用 \(1/e\) 点和初始切线交点交叉检查。

Record capacitor pd against time through a known resistance. Choose a resolvable time constant and determine it from a log-linear gradient, cross-checking with the \(1/e\) point or initial tangent.

高阻表仍可能与放电电阻并联，使实际电阻变小；检查漏电、开关与导线电阻、实际电容值和采样时间。无需用积分推导作为额外记忆负担。

Meter loading and leakage can change the effective resistance and fitted time constant. Check actual component values and timing resolution.

Applications / 应用与实验

必做实验 6：定时测电压，选择足够大的 RC 和高阻电压表，利用对数直线求时间常量并重复。

Required practical 6: measure voltage at timed intervals; use a large RC for readable changes and a high-resistance voltmeter; plot \(\ln V\) against time to determine RC, and repeat.

Common errors / 易错点

充电不能直接套放电式；欧姆乘法拉的单位为秒。

Do not use the discharge equation unchanged for charging. Units \(\Omega\times\mathrm{F}\) simplify to seconds.

3.9.1 方法 1 · 充放电路径、指数与图像 / RC paths, exponentials and graphs

  1. 按开关状态重新找充电或放电回路的等效 \(R\)，不要把只在另一状态接通的电阻算入 \(RC\)。 / Find the resistance of the active path.
  2. 放电 \(Q,V,I\) 的幅值按 \(e^{-t/RC}\) 衰减；充电 \(V_C=\varepsilon(1-e^{-t/RC})\)，而电流和电阻电压衰减。切换后电流可反号，电容电压不能无原因突跳。 / Distinguish capacitor voltage, resistor voltage and current direction.
  3. \(\tau=RC\)、\(t_{1/2}=RC\ln2\)；经过一时间常数放电剩36.8%，充电达到63.2%。问“失去75%”应以剩25%代入。 / Use the remaining fraction.
  4. \(\ln Q\)–\(t\) 斜率为 \(-1/(RC)\)，截距需取指数得到 \(Q_0\)。任意两放电时刻 \(\tau=-\Delta t/\ln(Q_2/Q_1)\)；充电图使用距离最终值的差，不能对 \(V_C\) 直接取衰减比。 / Take ratios of the exponentially decaying quantity.
  5. 恒流充电 \(Q=Q_0+It\)，\(dV/dt=I/C\)，不是固定电阻下的指数充电；要随电容电压升高而减小串联电阻。 / Identify the charging regime before choosing an equation.
  6. 等时间采样的 \(V_C\)–\(I\) 图虽是直线，点会随电流下降变密；\(V_C=\varepsilon-IR\)，放电幅值关系则为 \(V_C=|I|R\)。 / Do not confuse a straight locus with uniform motion along it.

对应真题 / Examples： PH03 · 201901 · Q02；PH03 · 202001 · Q02；PH03 · 202301 · Q04；PH03 · 202501 · Q04；PH03 · 202506 · Q06。

3.9.1 方法 2 · 电容能量、闪光与指数检验 / Capacitor energy, flashes and exponential tests

  • 电容从 \(V_i\) 降到 \(V_f\)，放出能量 \(\frac12C(V_i^2-V_f^2)\)，不是 \(\frac12C(V_i-V_f)^2\)。电压减半只剩四分之一能量。 / Subtract energies, not voltages inside one square.
  • 理想恒压源经电阻把空电容充满，电源提供 \(CV^2\)，电容储存 \(CV^2/2\)，另一半在电阻耗散。 / Track energy supplied as well as energy stored.
  • 两隔离电容并联重分配时守恒的是总电荷，最终同电压；初末储能一般不同，差额经回路耗散或辐射。 / Charge conservation alone does not imply stored-energy conservation.
  • 增大 \(C\) 在同初电压下增加持续时间和总能量，初始电流或亮度未必增大。灯泡电阻随温度变，光强还有热响应，不应自动视为指数。 / Distinguish initial power, duration and thermal lag.
  • 用至少三个读数检验两个相同时间间隔的比值，或比较两个半衰时间；只见曲线下降不能证明指数。 / Test a constant fractional change quantitatively.
  • 实验选取 \(RC\) 与记录分辨率匹配；毫秒过程应用数据记录，不能靠手动秒表追踪。 / Justify instruments from the timescale.

对应真题 / Examples： PH03 · 201806 · Q05；PH03 · 202101 · Q06；PH03 · 201806 · Q20；PH03 · 202601 · Q04；PH03 · 202501 · Q04。
