U4/07 常用物理模型

墙窗并列传热 / U-value and heat loss

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

U-value → wall → window → heat loss → 墙窗并列传热

推理顺序 / Reasoning

分别求墙窗传热功率，并列相加，再乘时间求能量。

Calculate each surface’s \(UA\Delta T\) separately; add parallel surface losses; multiply power by time for energy.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.11.2

3.11.2 热传导与U值

采用平行平面间稳态传导；温差取该层两侧；不同墙、窗等并列表面损失分别求和。

Use steady conduction through plane parallel faces; temperature difference is across the layer. Sum losses for separate parallel surfaces such as wall and window areas.

导热系数单位 W m\(^{-1}\) K\(^{-1}\)；U值单位 W m\(^{-2}\) K\(^{-1}\)。

\(P=kA\Delta T/L\); \(P=UA\Delta T\); \(U=k/L\) for a single homogeneous layer; \(P_{\mathrm{total}}=\sum U_iA_i\Delta T\).
