简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

Halving the wavelength doubles the energy of each photon, so the emitted electrons have a greater maximum kinetic energy. The incident power is unchanged, so only half as many photons arrive per second. Hence half as many photoelectrons are emitted per second.
