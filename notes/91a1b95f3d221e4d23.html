简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

Angular acceleration decreases as the generator's opposing torque increases. The resultant torque becomes smaller, so α = τ/I decreases. By about 50–55 s, the generator's opposing torque equals the wind's driving torque, so the resultant torque and angular acceleration are zero.
