3.5.7 单缝衍射与光栅 / Diffraction

考纲 / Specification： 3.5.7　｜　本单元知识谱系

Formula / 公式与单位

缝距为每米刻线数的倒数；最大级次受正弦不超过一限制。

\(d\sin\theta=n\lambda\); \(d=1/(\text{lines per metre})\); permitted orders satisfy \(n\lambda/d\leq1\).

Conditions / 条件与范围

光栅方程用于平面透射光栅、正入射；不要求分光计操作或单缝强度—角度曲线。

Use the grating equation for a plane transmission grating at normal incidence. A spectrometer’s operation and a single-slit intensity-versus-angle graph are not required.

Explanation / 解释

单缝图样与改变条件 / Single-slit pattern

单色光单缝图样有明亮、较宽的中央极大，两侧有较弱、较窄的亮纹并由暗区分隔；图样关于中央对称。增大波长或减小缝宽会增大中央极大宽度。这里要求定性变化，不把单缝强度—角度函数列为必学内容。

A single slit produces a broad bright central maximum and weaker, narrower side maxima separated by minima. Longer wavelength or a narrower slit broadens the central maximum. Only qualitative width changes are required.

白光中央为白色，两侧有彩色边缘，较长波长的红光偏离中心更多。需区分单缝中央极大与双缝等间距干涉条纹。

White light gives a white centre and coloured side regions, with red extending farther out. Distinguish the broad single-slit maximum from double-slit fringe spacing.

光栅方程的几何推导 / Deriving the grating equation

平面透射光栅正入射：相邻缝到同一出射方向的程差为 \(d\sin\theta\)；第 \(n\) 级主极大要求程差 \(n\lambda\)，故 \(d\sin\theta=n\lambda\)。\(\theta\) 从光栅法线量起，\(d\) 是相邻缝中心距。

At normal incidence, adjacent slits have path difference \(d\sin\theta\). Constructive interference gives \(d\sin\theta=n\lambda\), with angle measured from the grating normal.

若线密度为每毫米 \(N\) 条，\(d=10^{-3}/N\) 米。最高级次满足 \(n\lambda\le d\)；左右的 \(+n\)、\(-n\) 对称，对应同一级而不是两个不同级次。相同级次下长波长的衍射角更大。

Convert line density to spacing in metres. Allowed orders satisfy \(n\lambda\le d\). Equal orders occur symmetrically on both sides; longer wavelengths have larger angles at a fixed order.

测量与光谱应用 / Measurement and applications

可从屏上位移求 \(\theta=\tan^{-1}(x/D)\)，再代光栅方程；\(x\) 是中央到一侧亮斑的距离，不能把两侧间距直接代入。使用多级次或双侧读数比较，可减少定位误差。

Calculate the angle from screen geometry before using the grating equation. Use centre-to-order distance, or halve the separation of symmetric orders. Compare several orders or both sides to reduce alignment error.

光栅把不同波长分开，用于测波长、分析元素线谱。考纲不考分光计操作细节；光栅观察仍遵守激光安全。

Gratings separate wavelengths for wavelength measurement and spectral identification. Spectrometer operation is excluded; laser precautions still apply.

Applications / 应用与实验

光栅用于分辨波长、分析光谱；实验中测左右角度取平均，注意每毫米到每米的刻线数换算。

Use diffraction gratings to distinguish wavelengths and analyse spectra. For practical 5, measure angles on both sides and average; convert lines per mm to lines per m.

Common errors / 易错点

中央为零级；计算总主极大数时通常包括左右两侧和中央。

The central maximum is order zero. Maxima occur symmetrically on both sides; a count of total maxima usually includes both sides and the central one.

3.5.7 方法 1 · 单缝与光栅的判断步骤 / Slits and gratings

  1. 单缝变窄：衍射更明显、中央极大变宽；同一入射光束下透过光减少，中央强度降低。中央极大宽度约为邻近次级极大的两倍。 / Describe width and intensity separately.
  2. 光栅常数 \(d=1/N\)（先把线密度统一为每米）；对称两侧亮纹夹角的一半才是 \(\theta\)。 / Convert line density and identify the measured angle.
  3. 用 \(d\sin\theta=n\lambda\) 逐级计算，\(\theta_n\) 不是 \(n\theta_1\)。\(n_{max}=\lfloor d/\lambda\rfloor\)，总亮点数通常 \(2n_{max}+1\)，另检查屏幕范围及是否存在掠射阶。 / Apply the sine condition and visibility limits.
  4. 级次重叠满足 \(n_1\lambda_1=n_2\lambda_2\)。增加线密度使给定级次角度较大，可降低固定读数误差的相对影响，但应仍能观测该级。 / Separate order overlap from resolution of a measurement.
  5. 推导按“相邻缝路程差 \(d\sin\theta\) → 整数波长时相长”；衍射不是偏振。 / State the physical interference condition.
