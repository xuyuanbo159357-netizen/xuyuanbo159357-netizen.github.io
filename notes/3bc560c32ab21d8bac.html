简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

Put the thermistor and a thermometer in a water bath. Connect the thermistor to a multimeter on its resistance setting. Start near 0 °C using ice, then warm the water and stir it. At at least six temperatures covering 0–100 °C, wait for a steady temperature and record temperature and resistance. Repeat readings and take means.
