关系 1 / Relation 1

\[\Large\displaystyle C=\frac{\varepsilon_0\varepsilon_r A}{d}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量 / Quantity SI 单位 / SI unit
\(C\) 电容 / capacitance \(\mathrm{F}\)
\(\varepsilon_0\) 真空介电常量 / vacuum permittivity \(\mathrm{F\,m^{-1}}\)
\(\varepsilon_r\) 相对介电常量 / relative permittivity \(1\)（无量纲 / dimensionless）
\(A\) 面积 / area \(\mathrm{m^2}\)
\(d\) 间距或厚度 / spacing or thickness \(\mathrm{m}\)

物理关系 / Physical relationship： 平行板电容随介电常量和面积增加，随板距增加而减小。 / Parallel-plate capacitance grows with permittivity and area and falls with gap.

关系 2 / Relation 2

\[\Large\displaystyle V=\frac{Q}{C}\]

\(Q\) 电荷量 / charge \(\mathrm{C}\)

物理关系 / Physical relationship： 固定电荷时电容增大使电压减小；固定电压时电荷随电容增大。 / Larger capacitance lowers voltage at fixed charge or raises charge at fixed voltage.

3.8.4 电容、介质与储能

Parallel-plate equation assumes uniform field and negligible edge effects. For a linear capacitor, work/energy is the area under either the \(V\)–\(Q\) graph or the straight \(Q\)–\(V\) graph: the same triangle.
