3.5.6 干涉、相干与杨氏双缝 / Interference

考纲 / Specification： 3.5.6　｜　本单元知识谱系

Definition / 定义

↗ interference — 干涉

Definition / 定义

English: The superposition of waves producing a resultant whose amplitude depends on their phase difference.

中文： 波叠加后，合振幅随相位差而变化的现象。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

↗ coherent sources — 相干源

Definition / 定义

English: Sources emitting waves of the same frequency with a constant phase difference.

中文： 发出同频且保持恒定相位差的波的源。

↗ path difference — 程差

Definition / 定义

English: The difference between the distances travelled by two waves to the same point.

中文： 两列波到同一点所走路程的差。

Formula / 公式与单位

同相源增强与相消分别对应整波长和奇数半波长程差；双缝条纹间距与波长、屏距成正比，与缝距成反比。

For in-phase sources: constructive \(\Delta x=n\lambda\), destructive \(\Delta x=(n+\tfrac12)\lambda\). Double-slit fringe spacing \(w=\lambda D/s\).

Conditions / 条件与范围

条纹间距近似需屏距远大于缝距且角度小；完全相消还需振幅相等；相干不要求相位差为零。

The fringe-spacing approximation requires \(D\gg s\) and small angles. Complete cancellation additionally requires equal amplitudes. Coherence does not require zero phase difference.

Explanation / 解释

相干、程差与明暗 / Coherence and interference conditions

相干源具有相同频率和恒定相位差。来自同一源的双缝可保持相干；两个独立灯泡的相位差随机变化，不能形成稳定条纹。

Coherent sources have the same frequency and a constant phase difference. Two slits illuminated by one source are coherent; independent lamps do not maintain a stable relative phase.

两源同相时，程差 \(\delta=n\lambda\) 为相长，\(\delta=(n+1/2)\lambda\) 为相消；完全相消还需两波在该处振幅相等。若两源初相不同，应先加上初相差，不能直接套程差判据。

For in-phase sources, whole-wavelength path differences give constructive interference and odd half-wavelengths give destructive interference. Complete cancellation requires equal amplitudes; include any initial source phase difference.

声波可用同一信号发生器驱动双扬声器，以麦克风检测强弱交替；微波用接收器检测极大和极小。

Drive two speakers from one generator and detect alternating loud and quiet positions; use a receiver for analogous microwave maxima and minima.

双缝间距与白光 / Double-slit spacing and white light

\(w=\lambda D/s\) 适用于远屏、小角度且两缝间距 \(s\) 已知的情形。\(w\) 是相邻亮条纹之间或相邻暗条纹之间的距离；亮到相邻暗才是 \(w/2\)。改变 \(\lambda,D,s\) 的影响应逐一判断。

The double-slit spacing equation uses the small-angle, distant-screen approximation. Adjacent bright fringes and adjacent dark fringes have the same spacing \(w\); bright to neighbouring dark is \(w/2\).

白光中央程差为零，各色相长形成白色中央条纹；两侧形成彩色条纹，红光离中心更远、紫光更近，远处不同级次重叠后难以辨认。

With white light the central zero-path-difference fringe is white. Coloured side fringes have red farther from the centre and violet nearer it; overlap eventually washes out the pattern.

必做实验5及安全 / Required practical 5 and safety

双缝实验测 \(D\)、已知 \(s\)，跨多个条纹测总宽后除以间隔数，用 \(\lambda=ws/D\)；区分“条纹条数”和“间隔数”。光栅实验参见3.5.7，以两侧同级亮斑求角度。

Measure several fringe spacings and divide by the number of intervals, then calculate wavelength. The grating part is developed in 3.5.7 using matching orders on both sides.

低功率激光固定在眼睛高度以下，避免直视及镜面反射，用屏接收；不要求激光器内部工作原理。

Secure a low-power laser below eye level, avoid direct and specularly reflected beams, and use a screen. Laser operating details are not required.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.5.6

图示：双缝几何量 / Double-slit geometry

读图 / Reading： s 为两缝间距、D 为缝到屏距离、w 为相邻亮纹或暗纹中心间距；不要把跨多条纹的长度直接当 w。

s is slit separation, D screen distance and w adjacent-fringe spacing; divide a multi-fringe length by the number of intervals.

图示：激光双缝装置 / Laser double-slit setup

读图 / Reading： 让激光、双缝与屏对准，量取 D；跨多个条纹间隔测量以减小 w 的相对误差，激光不照向眼睛。

Align laser, slits and screen; measure D and several fringe intervals to reduce fractional uncertainty in w. Keep the beam away from eyes.

图示：两缝为何可以相干 / Coherence of the two slits

读图 / Reading： 同一单色光先照亮两缝，使两缝成为同频率、相位差恒定的波源；不是任意两只独立灯泡都能产生稳定干涉。

Illumination from one monochromatic source makes the slits coherent: same frequency and constant phase difference. Two independent lamps need not do this.

Applications / 应用与实验

必做实验 5：跨多个条纹间隔测量，求平均间距，测屏距和缝距，求波长与不确定度；注意声波、微波干涉；避免直视激光及镜面反射。

Required practical 5: measure several fringe spacings, divide by the number of intervals, measure \(D\) and \(s\), calculate wavelength and uncertainty. Explain sound and microwave interference. Never look into a laser beam or its specular reflection.

Common errors / 易错点

数条纹间隔而非亮纹个数；两个独立灯通常不相干。

Count the number of intervals, not the number of bright fringes. Two independent lamps are not normally coherent.

真题检验与答题思路 / Exam coverage and approaches

依据本库现有真题与评分标准核对。下列步骤按题型使用，先核对题设条件；综合题需结合其他关联知识点。 / Checked against the papers and mark schemes held in this vault; select the approach that fits the stated conditions.

3.5.6 方法 1 · 干涉的路径与相位账目 / Path and phase accounting

  1. 明确两条实际路径并求 \(\Delta s\)；距离不满足远场条件时用几何长度，不能盲用 \(w=\lambda D/s\)。 / Calculate the actual paths before using a far-field approximation.
  2. 总相位差 \(\Delta\phi_{total}=\Delta\phi_{initial}+2\pi\Delta s/\lambda\)；相干要求同频和恒定相位差，完全相消还需要相等振幅。 / Coherence alone does not guarantee zero intensity.
  3. 接收器在相向两声源间移动 \(x\) 时，路程差改变 \(2x\)；只移动一个声源时通常改变 \(x\)。 / Identify which paths change.
  4. 镜面移动 \(d\) 使往返光程改变 \(2d\)，相邻亮暗对应 \(d=\lambda/4\)，相邻亮纹为 \(\lambda/2\)。 / Include the return path in an interferometer.
  5. 房间反射可引入额外波并使极小不清晰。 / Relate imperfect minima to additional waves or unequal amplitudes.

对应真题 / Examples： PH02 · 202206 · Q08；PH02 · 202501 · Q08；PH02 · 202606 · Q22；PH02 · 202101 · Q08。

3.5.6 方法 2 · 条纹测量与误差 / Fringe measurements and uncertainty

量跨多条条纹的总距离后除以间隔数，不是亮线数；先处理有证据的异常值，再求平均。 / Count intervals, not lines.

\(\lambda=ws/D\) 的百分不确定度按乘除相加；\(w\) 的百分不确定度与所测总跨度相同。小角近似下 \(\theta_n\simeq nw/D\simeq n\lambda/s\)，依题给屏幕或角标尺求量。双缝的条纹近似式不可与光栅方程不加条件地互换。 / Match the method to the geometry and the stated approximation.

对应真题 / Examples： PH02 · 202606 · Q10；PH02 · 202001 · Q10；PH02 · 202406 · Q09。
