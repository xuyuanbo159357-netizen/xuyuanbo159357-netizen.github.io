Concept / 概念

total internal reflection — 全反射

Definition / 定义

English: Complete reflection at a boundary when light in the higher-index medium is incident at an angle greater than the critical angle.

中文： 光在高折射率介质中以大于临界角入射边界时发生的完全反射。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

图示讲解 / Diagram explained

图示：超过临界角 / Beyond the critical angle

读图 / Reading： 光从高折射率一侧射向低折射率一侧，且入射角大于临界角时发生全反射；反射角仍等于入射角。

Total internal reflection requires travel from higher to lower refractive index and incidence above the critical angle; reflection angle still equals incidence angle.
