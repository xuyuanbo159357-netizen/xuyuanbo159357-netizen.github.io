Concept / 概念

gravitational field strength — 引力场强

Definition / 定义

English: The gravitational force per unit mass on a small test mass at a point.

中文： 某点小检验质量所受的引力与其质量之比。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

图示讲解 / Diagram explained

图示：径向场与近似匀强场 / Radial and approximately uniform fields

读图 / Reading： 大范围看引力场线向中心汇聚；在很小的近地面区域，可近似平行且等间距，使用恒定 g。

Globally field lines converge towards the centre; a small near-surface region can be approximated by parallel lines and constant g.
