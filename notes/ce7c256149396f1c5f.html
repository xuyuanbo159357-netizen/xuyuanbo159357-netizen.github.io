Concept / 概念

internal resistance — 内阻

Definition / 定义

English: The resistance within a source that causes energy dissipation when current flows.

中文： 电源内部在电流通过时产生能量耗散的电阻。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

图示讲解 / Diagram explained

图示：V–I 图求 ε 与 r / Finding emf and internal resistance

读图 / Reading： V=ε−Ir，所以纵截距为 ε、斜率为 −r；r 是负斜率的大小，不是负电阻。

For V=ε−Ir the vertical intercept is ε and gradient −r; resistance is the positive magnitude of that slope.
