U4/06 真题练习/答题规范

U4 通用规范与有效数字 / General conventions and significant figures

U4 答题规范 / Exam answering conventions · 返回真题练习 / Past-paper practice

阅读方式 / How to use

本页区分物理原则、推荐书写及具体试题的评分要求；具体容差和扣分范围只用于所引题目。 / Distinguish physical principles, presentation advice and question-specific marking.

1. 有效数字先看本题要求 / Check the question-specific precision

中间计算保留精度，最终结果按题目与数据精度取舍，不统一限定两位有效数字。 / Retain intermediate digits and report the final result at the appropriate precision.

2026 June Q01.1 的燃料质量只接受 \(2.1\ \mathrm{kg}\)，两位有效数字；Q04.2 的质量亏损则接受两位或更多位。不能把第一问的限制推广到整张卷。 / The fuel-mass and mass-defect questions have different precision requirements. PH04 2026 June Q01.1 · MS PDF p4 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer；PH04 2026 June Q04.2 · MS PDF p12 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

科学计数法适合核质量、能量和微小温差等量，也能明确末尾零的精度；不是只要多于两位就强制使用。 / Standard form is useful for extreme values and clear precision, rather than compulsory beyond 2 sf.

2. 微小温差最忌提前取整 / Protect small temperature differences

2026 June Q01.3–01.4 中，薄壁温差约 \(0.0108\ \mathrm K\)，过早取成一位有效数字 \(0.01\ \mathrm K\) 会影响后续导热计算。该题 Q01.4 明确不接受相应步骤使用一位有效数字的温差。若采用前问的近似答案导致薄壁温差过粗，可改用绝热层的相应温差列等价导热关系，避免把“近似为零”的温差当精确值。 / A small difference can be badly affected by premature rounding. When carrying a coarse earlier approximation, use an equivalent conduction relationship with the insulating layer rather than treating a negligible wall drop as an exact zero. PH04 2026 June Q01.3 · MS PDF p5 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer；PH04 2026 June Q01.4 · MS PDF p6 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

区分“中间计算需要保留多少位”与“最后答案需要报告多少位”；不要逐行把计算器值全部截成两位。 / Intermediate precision and final reporting precision serve different purposes.

3. 温标、能量和时间 / Temperature, energy and time units

  • 气体定律用 K，如 \(18^\circ\mathrm C=291\ \mathrm K\)（按所用换算常数）；不能把 18 直接代 \(pV=nRT\)。 / Use absolute temperature in the gas law. PH04 2026 June Q02.1 · MS PDF p7 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer
  • 温差 \(1^\circ\mathrm C\) 与 \(1\ \mathrm K\) 数值相同；\(mc\Delta T\) 与导热式使用温差，而不是某端绝对温度。 / Use a temperature difference for heating and conduction. PH04 2026 June Q01.4 · MS PDF p6 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer
  • \(1\ \mathrm{kWh}=3.6\times10^6\ \mathrm J\)；功率与能量不能只因都来自电表而混同。 / Distinguish energy from power. PH04 2026 June Q01.1 · MS PDF p4 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer
  • 摩尔质量用 \(\mathrm{kg\,mol^{-1}}\) 配 SI 关系；单分子质量不能直接用一摩尔质量代替。 / Distinguish molar mass from molecular mass. PH04 2026 June Q02.1 · MS PDF p7 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer；PH04 2026 June Q02.3 · MS PDF p8 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

4. 结果形式、符号和单位 / Form, signs and units

数值题给求值后的数值和单位；代数推导、精确比值可保留分数或根式。转动惯量为 \(\mathrm{kg\,m^2}\)，转矩为 \(\mathrm{N\,m}\)，角动量为 \(\mathrm{kg\,m^2\,s^{-1}}\)；不要把转矩单位含义当作能量变化。 / Evaluate numerical answers and distinguish the rotational quantities and their units.

精度复述 / Precision recall

末步“2 sf only”和“2 or more sf”是不同要求；极小温差必须在计算过程中保留足够精度。 / “2 sf only” and “2 or more sf” are different requirements; retain sufficient precision for small temperature differences.

同一大题的数据沿用 / Carrying answers into later parts

课堂统一答题规范 / Classroom convention

同一大题内，后续小问用到前面小问的答案数据时，直接使用前问已写出的答案数值，不改用计算器内保留的更精确值。前问若为证明题，后续直接使用题目要求证明的数值。

Within the same question, use the numerical answer reported in the earlier part when it is required in a later part, rather than a more precise calculator value. If the earlier part is a show-that question, use the value stated in that question for later parts.

区分两个阶段：同一小问内部保留中间精度；跨小问沿用已报告的答案或题给证明值。 / Within one part, retain intermediate precision; between parts, carry forward the reported answer or the stated show-that value.

这是本知识库采用的课堂统一约定；原评分标准有时还接受其他跟进路线，不将本约定表述成考局所有题目的唯一评分规则。若题目另给指定数据，以该明确指令为准。 / This is the classroom convention used in this vault. Some mark schemes also accept other follow-through routes; it is not presented as the board's sole permitted method. Follow any explicit value separately specified in the question.

U4 例证 / Unit-specific example

2018 June Q04.3 要证明路程约 \(3.9\ \mathrm m\)；Q04.4 直接用 \(3.9\ \mathrm m\) 求高度和势能变化，不改用 \(3.9375\ \mathrm m\)。评分标准明确接受由 \(3.9\ \mathrm m\) 得到的约 \(34.4\ \mathrm J\) 路线。 / Carry the stated show-that distance into the next part; this route is explicitly accepted.
