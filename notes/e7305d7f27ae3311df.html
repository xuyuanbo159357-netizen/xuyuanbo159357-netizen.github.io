简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

Fission releases fast neutrons, which have a low probability of inducing further U-235 fission. Slow thermal neutrons are more likely to be absorbed by U-235, forming unstable U-236 that can split. Fast neutrons enter the moderator and scatter from its nuclei. Through many collisions they transfer kinetic energy and slow to thermal speeds, reaching thermal equilibrium with it. Water is a suitable moderator: its hydrogen nuclei have masses close to a neutron's mass, giving efficient energy transfer. A moderator should absorb few neutrons so they remain available for fission, and should be chemically and thermally stable under operating conditions.
