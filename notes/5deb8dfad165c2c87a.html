U2/07 常用物理模型

电荷流与电流 / Charge and current

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

electrons → charge → current → time → 电荷流与电流

推理顺序 / Reasoning

先由电子数求电荷，再除以时间；传统电流与电子运动方向相反。

Count charge using \(Q=ne\); divide by the time interval; state conventional current opposite to electron motion.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.4.1

3.4.1 电学基础

时间间隔内使用平均电流；瞬时电流为电荷—时间图像的切线斜率。

Use average current over an interval; instantaneous current is the gradient of a charge–time graph.

单位分别为安培、库仑、伏特和欧姆；一伏特为一焦耳每库仑。

\(I=\Delta Q/\Delta t\); \(V=W/Q\); \(R=V/I\). Units: A, C, V and \(\Omega\). One volt is one joule per coulomb.
