3.8.1 库仑定律与静电力 / Coulomb’s law

考纲 / Specification： 3.8.1　｜　本单元知识谱系

知识逐项讲解 / Concept-by-concept guide

electrostatic force — 静电力

Definition / 定义

↗ electrostatic force — 静电力

Definition / 定义

English: The interaction between electric charges at rest.

中文： 静止电荷之间的相互作用。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式

Coulomb's law / 库仑定律

Law statement / 定律表述

English: The magnitude of the force between two point charges in a vacuum is directly proportional to the product of their charge magnitudes and inversely proportional to the square of their separation.

中文： 真空中两个点电荷之间的静电力大小，与两者电荷量绝对值的乘积成正比，与它们间距离的平方成反比。

\[\Large\displaystyle F=\frac{|Q_1Q_2|}{4\pi\varepsilon_0r^2}\]

Symbols and SI units / 符号与 SI 单位

符号 / Symbol 物理量 / Quantity SI 单位 / SI unit
\(F\) 静电力大小 / electrostatic force magnitude \(\mathrm{N}\)
\(Q_1,Q_2\) 两电荷量 / charges \(\mathrm{C}\)
\(\varepsilon_0\) 真空介电常量 / permittivity of free space \(\mathrm{F\,m^{-1}}\)
\(r\) 两电荷间距 / charge separation \(\mathrm{m}\)

Conditions / 条件与说明： 真空中的点电荷；同号相斥，异号相吸。 / Point charges in vacuum; like charges repel and unlike charges attract.

permittivity of free space — 真空介电常量

Definition / 定义

↗ permittivity of free space — 真空介电常量

Definition / 定义

English: The constant describing the electric response of vacuum in Coulomb’s law and capacitor equations.

中文： 在库仑定律和电容公式中表征真空电学响应的常量。

符号 / Symbol 物理量 / Quantity SI 单位 / SI unit
\(\varepsilon_0\) 真空介电常量 / permittivity of free space \(\mathrm{F\,m^{-1}}\)

Formula / 公式与单位

Conditions / 适用条件： 此式求静电力大小，方向由电荷符号决定；r 为电荷间距，介质取真空。 / The equation gives force magnitude in vacuum; charge signs determine direction and r is separation.

\[\Large\displaystyle F=\frac{|Q_1Q_2|}{4\pi\varepsilon_0r^2}\]

Conditions / 条件与范围

用于真空点电荷，空气可近似真空；球对称电荷分布的外部计算可把电荷集中在球心。

Apply to point charges in vacuum; air can be treated as vacuum. For spherical charge distributions outside the sphere, take charge at the centre.

Explanation / 解释

力的大小、方向和对象 / Magnitude, direction and objects

真空中点电荷作用力大小 \(F=\frac{|Q_1Q_2|}{4\pi\varepsilon_0r^2}\)；同号相斥、异号相吸。空气通常近似真空。先用电荷符号判方向，再求大小、作矢量和。

Use the magnitude of the charge product for force magnitude; like charges repel and unlike charges attract. Air can usually be treated as vacuum. Determine directions before vector addition.

对球对称带电球体外部，可把电荷视为集中在球心；距离取球心距。不要把此近似无条件推广到任意非均匀分布或球内位置。

Outside a spherically symmetric charged sphere, use its charge at the centre. Do not apply this external model to arbitrary distributions or internal points.

与引力的比较 / Comparison with gravitation

引力和静电力都为平方反比、沿源与粒子连线；引力只吸引，静电力可吸引或排斥。两亚原子粒子的大小比为 \(F_e/F_g=|q_1q_2|/(4\pi\varepsilon_0Gm_1m_2)\)，同一距离下 \(r^2\) 抵消。

Both forces are inverse-square central forces, but gravity is always attractive. For a given particle pair their force ratio is independent of separation; electrostatic interactions are much stronger at the subatomic scale.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.8.1

图示：库仑力的方向 / Coulomb-force directions

读图 / Reading： 同号相斥、异号相吸；两电荷受力等大反向，但由于质量可能不同，加速度不一定等大。

Like charges repel and unlike charges attract; equal opposite forces do not require equal accelerations for unequal masses.

Applications / 应用与实验

多电荷力需矢量叠加；比较静电力和引力时统一距离。

Combine forces vectorially when several charges act on one test charge; compare \(F_e/F_g\) with consistent centre separation.

Common errors / 易错点

注意距离平方和微、纳库仑换算；力大小为正不代表必然吸引。

Use squared distance and convert micro/nanocoulombs. Do not infer attraction merely because the force magnitude is positive.

3.8.1 方法 1 · 库仑力与非匀强场中的功 / Coulomb forces and work

  1. 画力的方向，大小用 \(|Q_1Q_2|/(4\pi\varepsilon_0r^2)\)；带电球采用题设点电荷或球对称近似，\(r\) 为中心距。 / Check the point-charge approximation and distance.
  2. 两滴接触时中心距为 \(r_1+r_2\)。接近过程力随距离改变，外力功用 \(\Delta E_p=Q_1Q_2(1/r_f-1/r_i)/(4\pi\varepsilon_0)\)，不能用初始力乘全程距离。 / Use a potential-energy difference for a nonuniform force.
  3. 正面入射最近接近时可令初动能等于电势能增量；非正面偏转的最近点速度一般不为零。 / Distinguish head-on stopping from general scattering.
  4. 作比值题，先明确改变的是一个电荷还是两个电荷，并将距离变化平方。 / Write the full ratio before evaluating.
