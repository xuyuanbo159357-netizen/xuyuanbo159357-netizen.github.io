1. 电源能量与电动势 / Source energy and emf

1.1 关键概念与定义 / Key concepts and definitions

electromotive force — 电动势

Definition / 定义

English: The energy supplied by a source per unit charge to the whole circuit, including its internal resistance.

中文： 电源向包括其内阻在内的整个电路提供的能量，与通过电源的电荷量之比。

定义评分依据 / Definition marking points： PH02 201801-Q07、PH02 202606-Q08。原答案在题目下展开；此处按评分要点概括。 Summarised from the marking points; originals are folded below each question.

1.3 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

适用范围与条件 / Scope and conditions

Conditions / 适用条件： 电源向外供电时，电动势 = 外电压 + 内电压降；单回路总电阻为 R+r。 / For a source delivering current, emf equals terminal voltage plus lost volts; the simple circuit resistance is R+r.

电动势、路端电压与能量 / EMF, terminal pd and energy

电动势是电源每单位电荷提供的能量，\(\varepsilon=E/Q\)，不是机械力。电源向外供电时 \(\varepsilon=I(R+r)\)，\(V=IR=\varepsilon-Ir\)；\(Ir\) 是内阻上的电势降。

EMF is energy supplied per unit charge, not a mechanical force. For a discharging source, \(\varepsilon=I(R+r)\) and terminal pd is \(V=\varepsilon-Ir\).

开路时 \(I\approx0\)，高阻电压表读数近似为电动势；外电阻减小→电流增大→内部电势降增大→路端电压降低。短路极限 \(I\approx\varepsilon/r\) 只作模型分析，不作为实验操作。

Open-circuit terminal pd approximates emf. Reducing load resistance increases current and lost volts, reducing terminal pd. The short-circuit limit is a calculation model, not an experimental procedure.

\(\varepsilon I=VI+I^2r\)；负载效率 \(V/\varepsilon=R/(R+r)\)。学习资料的最大负载功率结论 \(R=r\) 可由 \(P=\varepsilon^2R/(R+r)^2\) 理解；这是电路公式的应用，不新增独立定律。

The source supplies both load power and internal heating. Maximum load power at \(R=r\) is an application of the circuit equations, rather than an additional law to memorise.

Common errors / 易错点

电动势是每单位电荷的能量而不是力；路端电压变化不一定说明电动势变化。

Electromotive force is energy per charge, not a mechanical force. Emf can remain constant while terminal pd changes.

2. 内阻、路端电压与功率 / Internal resistance, terminal pd and power

2.1 关键概念与定义 / Key concepts and definitions

internal resistance — 内阻

Definition / 定义

English: The resistance within a source that causes energy dissipation when current flows.

中文： 电源内部在电流通过时产生能量耗散的电阻。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式

\[\Large\displaystyle V_{\mathrm{lost}}=Ir\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(V_{\mathrm{lost}}\) 内电压降 / lost volts \(\mathrm{V}\)
\(I\) 电流 / electric current \(\mathrm{A}\)
\(r\) 内阻 / internal resistance \(\mathrm{\Omega}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

电源内阻上的电势降等于电流乘内阻，解释电动势与端电压之差。

The voltage drop across internal resistance is current times internal resistance, accounting for the difference between emf and terminal voltage.

\(I\) 电流 / electric current \(\mathrm{A}\)
\(r\) 内阻 / internal resistance \(\mathrm{\Omega}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

电源内阻以热的形式耗散功率，大小与电流平方成正比。

Internal resistance dissipates thermal power proportional to squared current.

terminal potential difference — 路端电势差

Definition / 定义

English: The energy transferred to the external circuit per unit charge leaving a source.

中文： 电源向外电路每单位电荷提供的能量。

2.3 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

Conditions / 条件与范围

以上符号用于电源放电供电；开路时路端电势差等于电动势；内阻电流为总电流。

These signs refer to a source supplying current. On open circuit, \(I=0\) and \(V=\varepsilon\). Use the total current through the source, not a branch current.

3. 必做实验 3：电动势与内阻 / Required practical 3: emf and internal resistance

3.1 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

必做实验3与线性图 / Required practical 3

电压表接电池两端，电流表、可变电阻和开关串联；改变负载取得多组 \((I,V)\)。作纵轴 \(V\)、横轴 \(I\) 的最佳拟合线：截距 \(\varepsilon\)，斜率 \(-r\)。

Measure terminal pd and current for several loads. On a graph of \(V\) against \(I\), the intercept gives emf and the gradient gives negative internal resistance.

间歇接通，避免电池温度和化学状态明显改变；不只取两个相邻点求斜率，检查表量程和零点。

Open the switch between readings to limit changes in cell temperature and state. Use a wide interval on the best-fit line and suitable meter ranges.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.4.6

电动势与内阻测量 / EMF and internal-resistance measurement

读图 / Reading： 电压表跨电源两端，电流表串联；改变外电阻，保持电池状态尽量稳定并避免持续大电流发热。

Measure terminal voltage across the source and current in series; vary external resistance while limiting changes in cell condition and heating.

V–I 图求 ε 与 r / Finding emf and internal resistance

读图 / Reading： V=ε−Ir，所以纵截距为 ε、斜率为 −r；r 是负斜率的大小，不是负电阻。

For V=ε−Ir the vertical intercept is ε and gradient −r; resistance is the positive magnitude of that slope.

Applications / 应用与实验

必做实验 3：改变外阻，串联测电流、并联测路端电压，重复读数并拟合，避免发热和过大电流。

Required practical 3: vary the external resistance, measure current in series and terminal pd in parallel, repeat readings, use a best-fit graph, avoid heating and large currents.

考点精讲：并联支路合闸的三表因果链 / Three-meter chain when a parallel branch closes

新增并联支路使外电路等效电阻减小，总电流增大。对内阻不变的实际电源，内电压降随之增大，所以整个外电路的端电压下降。原有定值电阻支路分得的电压下降，支路电流反而减小。应分别解释总电流表、电压表和原支路电流表；还要核对各表实际接在哪里。

Adding a parallel branch lowers external resistance and raises total current. With fixed internal resistance, lost volts increase and terminal voltage falls. The original fixed-resistance branch then has a lower voltage and a smaller current. Explain each meter separately and check its actual connections.

3.4.6 方法 2 · 短路、最大功率与电池适用性 / Short circuits and battery suitability

  1. \(I=\frac{\varepsilon}{R+r}\)；短路时最大电流 \(\varepsilon/r\)，外电阻功率为零，电池内部仍发热。 / Zero external power does not mean zero heating.
