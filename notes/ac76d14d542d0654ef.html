3.4.6 电动势与内阻 / Electromotive force and internal resistance

考纲 / Specification： 3.4.6　｜　本单元知识谱系

符号沿用本节公式；若题目另有定义，以题设为准。 / These symbols follow this section; use any notation defined in the question.

物理量 / Quantity 符号 / Symbol SI单位 / SI unit 说明 / Notes

Concept / 概念

  • electromotive force — 电动势
  • internal resistance — 内阻
  • terminal potential difference — 路端电势差

Definition / 定义

↗ electromotive force — 电动势

Definition / 定义

English: The energy transferred from other forms into electrical energy by a source per unit charge passing through it.

中文： 电源中每单位电荷从其他形式获得的电能。

定义评分依据 / Definition marking points： PH02 201801-Q07、PH02 202606-Q08。原答案在题目下展开；此处按评分要点概括。 Summarised from the marking points; originals are folded below each question.

↗ internal resistance — 内阻

Definition / 定义

English: The resistance within a source that causes energy dissipation when current flows.

中文： 电源内部在电流通过时产生能量耗散的电阻。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

↗ terminal potential difference — 路端电势差

Definition / 定义

English: The energy transferred to the external circuit per unit charge leaving a source.

中文： 电源向外电路每单位电荷提供的能量。

定义评分依据 / Definition marking points： PH02 202206-Q02。原答案在题目下展开；此处按评分要点概括。 Summarised from the marking points; originals are folded below each question.

真题定义依据 / Mark-scheme checks： PH02 201801-Q07、PH02 202206-Q02、PH02 202606-Q08。定义按评分要点概括；原答案可在对应题目下展开。 Definitions are summarised from the scoring points, with original answers below the linked questions.

Formula / 公式与单位

电动势分配为路端电势差和内电势降。

\(\varepsilon=E/Q=I(R+r)\); \(V=\varepsilon-Ir\); \(I=\varepsilon/(R+r)\); lost volts \(=Ir\); internal power loss \(=I^2r\).

Conditions / 条件与范围

以上符号用于电源放电供电；开路时路端电势差等于电动势；内阻电流为总电流。

These signs refer to a source supplying current. On open circuit, \(I=0\) and \(V=\varepsilon\). Use the total current through the source, not a branch current.

Explanation / 解释

电动势、路端电压与能量 / EMF, terminal pd and energy

电动势是电源每单位电荷提供的能量，\(\varepsilon=E/Q\)，不是机械力。电源向外供电时 \(\varepsilon=I(R+r)\)，\(V=IR=\varepsilon-Ir\)；\(Ir\) 是内阻上的电势降。

EMF is energy supplied per unit charge, not a mechanical force. For a discharging source, \(\varepsilon=I(R+r)\) and terminal pd is \(V=\varepsilon-Ir\).

开路时 \(I\approx0\)，高阻电压表读数近似为电动势；外电阻减小→电流增大→内部电势降增大→路端电压降低。短路极限 \(I\approx\varepsilon/r\) 只作模型分析，不作为实验操作。

Open-circuit terminal pd approximates emf. Reducing load resistance increases current and lost volts, reducing terminal pd. The short-circuit limit is a calculation model, not an experimental procedure.

\(\varepsilon I=VI+I^2r\)；负载效率 \(V/\varepsilon=R/(R+r)\)。课件的最大负载功率结论 \(R=r\) 可由 \(P=\varepsilon^2R/(R+r)^2\) 理解；这是电路公式的应用，不新增独立定律。

The source supplies both load power and internal heating. Maximum load power at \(R=r\) is an application of the circuit equations, rather than an additional law to memorise.

必做实验3与线性图 / Required practical 3

电压表接电池两端，电流表、可变电阻和开关串联；改变负载取得多组 \((I,V)\)。作纵轴 \(V\)、横轴 \(I\) 的最佳拟合线：截距 \(\varepsilon\)，斜率 \(-r\)。

Measure terminal pd and current for several loads. On a graph of \(V\) against \(I\), the intercept gives emf and the gradient gives negative internal resistance.

间歇接通，避免电池温度和化学状态明显改变；不只取两个相邻点求斜率，检查表量程和零点。

Open the switch between readings to limit changes in cell temperature and state. Use a wide interval on the best-fit line and suitable meter ranges.

Applications / 应用与实验

必做实验 3：改变外阻，串联测电流、并联测路端电压，重复读数并拟合，避免发热和过大电流。

Required practical 3: vary the external resistance, measure current in series and terminal pd in parallel, repeat readings, use a best-fit graph, avoid heating and large currents.

Common errors / 易错点

电动势是每单位电荷的能量而不是力；路端电压变化不一定说明电动势变化。

Electromotive force is energy per charge, not a mechanical force. Emf can remain constant while terminal pd changes.

3.4.6 方法 1 · 内阻图像与电压表负载 / Internal-resistance graphs and loading

先重排到实际坐标 / Rearrange for the actual axes.

坐标 / Axes 线性式 / Linear form 斜率；截距 / Gradient; intercept
\(V\) 对 \(I\) \(V=\varepsilon-rI\) \(-r\)；\(\varepsilon\)
\(1/I\) 对 \(R\) \(1/I=R/\varepsilon+r/\varepsilon\) \(1/\varepsilon\)；\(r/\varepsilon\)
\(R\) 对 \(1/I\) \(R=\varepsilon(1/I)-r\) \(\varepsilon\)；\(-r\)

电压表内阻 \(R_V\) 有限时仍有电流，读数 \(\varepsilon R_V/(r+R_V)<\varepsilon\)；测电动势应尽量接近开路。 / A finite-resistance voltmeter loads the cell.

对应真题 / Examples： PH02 · 202301 · Q08；PH02 · 202101 · Q14；PH02 · 202601 · Q10；PH02 · 202606 · Q08。

3.4.6 方法 2 · 短路、最大功率与电池适用性 / Short circuits and battery suitability

  1. \(I=\varepsilon/(R+r)\)；短路时最大电流 \(\varepsilon/r\)，外电阻功率为零，电池内部仍发热。 / Zero external power does not mean zero heating.
  2. \(P_{load}=\varepsilon^2R/(R+r)^2\) 在 \(R=r\) 时最大；这不等于效率最大。效率 \(R/(R+r)\) 在该点仅为 \(1/2\)。 / Separate maximum power from maximum efficiency.
  3. 电池是否合用：同时核对所需端电压、电流、容量与运行时间，区分 \(\varepsilon I\)、\(VI\) 和 \(I^2r\)。 / Check voltage, current, charge capacity and runtime.

对应真题 / Examples： PH02 · 201801 · Q07；PH02 · 202001 · Q11；PH02 · 202001 · Q16。
