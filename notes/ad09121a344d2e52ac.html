3.11.3 气体定律与理想气体

物理量与符号 / Quantities and symbols

符号沿用本节公式；若题目另有定义，以题设为准。 / These symbols follow this section; use any notation defined in the question.

物理量 / Quantity 符号 / Symbol SI单位 / SI unit 说明 / Notes
压强 / pressure \(p\) \(\mathrm{Pa}\) 不是动量 p / Not momentum.
体积 / volume \(V\) \(\mathrm{m^3}\) 与电势、电势差 V 区分 / Not potential or voltage here.
热力学温度 / thermodynamic temperature \(T\) \(\mathrm{K}\) 气体定律用 K；与周期 T 区分 / Gas laws use kelvin; not period.
摄氏温度 / Celsius temperature \(\theta\) \(\mathrm{{}^\circ C}\) 与转角 θ 按语境区分 / Not angular displacement here.
物质的量 / amount of substance \(n\) \(\mathrm{mol}\) 不是分子数 N / Not number of molecules.

方法、分析与误差 / Method, analysis and uncertainty

1

波义耳实验固定气体量，缓慢改体积，热平衡后测绝对压强。

For Boyle’s law, trap a fixed quantity of gas, vary volume slowly and measure absolute pressure after thermal equilibrium.

2

保持温度，作压强—体积倒数图检验正比。

Keep temperature constant; plot p against 1/V and test proportionality.

3

查理实验用控温浴改变温度，保持压强并测体积。

For Charles’s law, vary temperature in a controlled bath while maintaining pressure and measuring gas volume.

4

使用开尔文，检验体积温度关系并解释摄氏外推零点。

Use kelvin; check the V–T line and distinguish true zero from a Celsius extrapolation.

5

等待热平衡、防漏气，考虑连接管气体体积和压强偏移。

Allow equilibrium, prevent leaks, and account for unmeasured connecting-tube gas volume and pressure offset.

定量分析与条件核对 / Quantitative analysis and controls

↗ 3.11.3 气体定律与理想气体 / Ideal gases

必做实验9 / Required practical 9

玻意耳：缓慢改变密闭气体体积，待温度恢复，测绝对压强；作 \(p\)–\(1/V\) 或检验 \(pV\)。查理：保持压强与气体量不变，水浴改变温度，待平衡测体积；作 \(V\)–\(T\)（K）。

For Boyle's law change volume slowly and allow thermal recovery. For Charles's law vary bath temperature at constant pressure and gas amount. Linearise using reciprocal volume or kelvin temperature.

检查管路死体积、气密性、压强零点和温度均匀性；不能把快速压缩导致升温的数据当作等温关系。

Consider dead volume, leakage, pressure zero and uniform temperature. Rapid compression is not an isothermal measurement.
