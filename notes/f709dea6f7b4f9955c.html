3.7.1 万有引力定律 / Newton’s gravitational law

考纲 / Specification： 3.7.1　｜　本单元知识谱系

物理量与符号 / Quantities and symbols

符号沿用本节公式；若题目另有定义，以题设为准。 / These symbols follow this section; use any notation defined in the question.

物理量 / Quantity 符号 / Symbol SI单位 / SI unit 说明 / Notes
力 / force \(F\) \(\mathrm{N}\) 矢量；合力常记 ΣF 或 F_resultant / Vector; resultant force is often ΣF or F_resultant.
万有引力常量 / gravitational constant \(G\) \(\mathrm{N\,m^2\,kg^{-2}}\)

定义按考纲与指定教材概括；本页未列出可直接对应的定义题评分条目。 Definitions are summarised from the specification and textbook; no directly matched definition-item citation is claimed here.

Formula / 公式与单位

Conditions / 适用条件： 点质量，或球对称物体外部；r 为两质量中心间距，力为吸引力。 / Point masses or outside spherical masses; r is centre-to-centre distance and the force is attractive.

\[\Large\displaystyle F=\frac{Gm_1m_2}{r^2}\]

Conditions / 条件与范围

采用质点或球对称物体外部模型；距离为中心间距而非距表面高度。

Treat masses as point masses, or use spherically symmetric bodies viewed from outside. Use centre-to-centre separation, not height above the surface.

Explanation / 解释

万有引力的方向与适用对象 / Direction and source masses

\(F=Gm_1m_2/r^2\) 是两个点质量的引力大小，总为相互吸引，沿两者连线；两力大小相等、方向相反，分别作用在不同物体上。\(G\) 是普适常量，\(g\) 是随位置变化的场强。

Gravitation between point masses is attractive along their line of centres. The action–reaction pair acts on different bodies. Distinguish universal constant \(G\) from local field strength \(g\).

球对称天体外部可把其质量视为集中在球心；\(r\) 是中心距，卫星高度 \(h\) 应先转为 \(r=R+h\)。不能将外部平方反比公式直接用于天体内部。

Outside a spherically symmetric body, use its mass at its centre. Radius is centre-to-centre distance, not height above the surface; the external inverse-square expression cannot automatically be extended inside.

比例、叠加与联系 / Scaling and superposition

质量不变时，距离加倍使引力为四分之一；多个天体的作用力须作矢量和。引力不需要接触或介质；引力提供卫星圆轨道所需的向心合力。

Doubling separation quarters the force. Add forces from several bodies as vectors. Gravity acts without contact or a medium and can supply the centripetal force for an orbit.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.7.1

图示：万有引力的一对相互作用 / Gravitational interaction pair

读图 / Reading： 两力等大反向但分别作用在两质量上；r 从质心到质心量取，不是表面间空隙。

Forces are equal and opposite on different masses; r is centre-to-centre separation, not the gap between surfaces.

Applications / 应用与实验

比较粒子间引力与静电力，分别代入质量、电荷与距离。

Compare gravitational and electrostatic forces between particles, keeping masses, charges and distance distinct.

Common errors / 易错点

不要把万有引力常量与当地重力场强混淆。

Do not confuse the universal constant \(G\) with the local field strength \(g\).

3.7.1 方法 1 · 引力的距离、质量与比值 / Gravitational distances and ratios

  1. \(F=GMm/r^2\) 中距离是中心间距，不是表面间隙；相接球体中心距为半径之和。由密度先求 \(M=\rho(4\pi R^3/3)\)。 / Establish the geometric distance before substituting.
  2. 比较用 \(F_2/F_1=(M_2m_2/M_1m_1)(r_1/r_2)^2\)。相互作用两物体的力大小相等，不能因受力物质量不同而改变作用反作用关系。 / Combine inverse-square scaling with Newton's third law.
  3. 同一对粒子的电力与引力比，\(r^2\) 可消去；保留核电荷 \(Ze\) 与核质量约 \(Am_n\) 的区别。 / Cancel a common distance only when both forces use that distance.
  4. \([G]=\mathrm{m^3\,kg^{-1}\,s^{-2}}\)，可由 \(G=Fr^2/(Mm)\) 推导。 / Derive the units from the law.
