Concept / 概念

equipotential surface — 等势面

Definition / 定义

English: A surface on which every point has the same potential.

中文： 各点势相同的面。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

图示讲解 / Diagram explained

图示：等势面与场线 / Equipotentials and field lines

读图 / Reading： 沿同一等势面移动，势能不变；场线与等势面垂直，相邻等势面间距也帮助判断场强大小。

Motion on one equipotential does not change potential energy; field lines are normal to it and equipotential spacing indicates field strength.
