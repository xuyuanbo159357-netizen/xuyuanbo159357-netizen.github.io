阅读方式 / How to use

本页区分物理原则、推荐书写及具体试题的评分要求；具体容差和扣分范围只用于所引题目。 / Distinguish physical principles, presentation advice and question-specific marking.

1. 力箭头的位置和方向 / Place and direct force arrows correctly

2026 June Q02.1：箭头从球上起画，沿水平直线指向圆心，并标为 \(F\)；箭头与球之间不能留空隙。此为该题明确的图示要求。 / The cited force arrow starts on the ball, points horizontally towards the centre and is labelled F. PH03 2026 June Q02.1 · MS PDF p7 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

受力图只含对象实际受到的力；把张力分量相加得到向心合力，不再额外加一个“centripetal force”。 / Obtain the radial resultant from real forces.

2. 势能曲线切线 / Tangents to potential-energy curves

曲线某点的变化率要画该点切线；不是取整个曲线任意两点的平均斜率，也不是用 \(E_p/r\) 代替斜率。 / Draw a tangent at the specified point rather than using an arbitrary secant or the ordinate divided by the coordinate.

2024 June Q03.3 要求在 \(d=1.0\times10^7\ \mathrm m\) 处作切线，求其斜率，再除以 \(37\ \mathrm{kg}\) 求场强大小。一般关系为 \(F_r=-dE_p/dr\)、\(g_r=-dV_g/dr\)；若问大小取相应绝对值。 / Distinguish a potential-energy gradient from a potential gradient and account for mass and direction. PH03 2024 June Q03.3 · MS PDF p10 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

3. 指数图像的面积与衰减 / Areas and decay on exponential graphs

  • 电流–时间图面积是通过的电荷；电容剩余电荷为初始电荷减去已流过的电荷。 / Area under an I–t graph gives transferred charge, not automatically charge remaining.
  • 2024 June Q05.1 明确阴影面积表示 6 s 内移走的电荷，不能把“总面积”含混当初始电荷。 / Identify the interval and charge represented by the area. PH03 2024 June Q05.1 · MS PDF p14 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer
  • 放电曲线通常平滑趋近时间轴，负斜率绝对值减小；不能在有限时间突然截断到零。 / An exponential discharge approaches the axis smoothly. PH03 2024 June Q05.5 · MS PDF p15 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer
  • 已衰变数量增加与剩余数量减少应分清，先看纵轴标签。 / Read whether the ordinate is decayed or remaining quantity.

4. 示波器、相位与磁场图 / Oscilloscope, phase and magnetic diagrams

2026 June Q06.4：振幅约 3.2–3.3 格，峰峰值约 6.4–6.6 格，周期 10 格；正确设置为 \(0.5\ \mathrm{V\,div^{-1}}\) 和 \(1\ \mathrm{ms\,div^{-1}}\)。不能用峰峰值除以振幅格数。 / Match the voltage difference and time interval to the corresponding number of divisions. PH03 2026 June Q06.4 · MS PDF p18 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

磁场“进入纸面”不能写成含混的“向下”。用 \(\otimes\)、\(\odot\) 时说明方向；确定电荷符号后再用规则判力向。2026 June Q07.2 可用正确且有标签的左手定则图示表达规则。 / Distinguish into the page from down the page and label the directional rule. PH03 2026 June Q07.2 · MS PDF p19 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

图后自检 / Diagram check

力箭头从何处起？对象是什么？方向相对哪里？纵轴量是势还是势能、剩余还是移走？读的是振幅还是峰峰值？ / Arrow origin? Chosen object? Reference direction? Potential or energy, remaining or transferred quantity? Amplitude or peak-to-peak value?
