离散能级与光谱 / Excitation and transition

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

excitation → ionisation → transition → spectrum → 离散能级与光谱

推理顺序 / Reasoning

区分激发与移除电子，求能级差、换单位，判断发射吸收与X射线机制。

Distinguish excitation from removal; subtract level energies; convert eV to J; identify photon emission or absorption and X-ray mechanisms.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.5.9

3.5.9 原子能级、线谱与X射线

光子吸收需能量匹配允许能级差；入射电子可只转移激发所需部分能量；使用 SI 制普朗克常量前把电子伏特换成焦耳。

For photon absorption, photon energy must match the allowed energy gap. An incident electron can transfer the required excitation energy and retain the remainder. Convert eV to J before using SI Planck constant.

电子伏特是能量单位；发射光子的能量为上下能级差，X射线最高光子能量受加速电压限制。

\(1\,\mathrm{eV}=1.602\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm{J}\); emitted photon \(hf=E_{\mathrm{upper}}-E_{\mathrm{lower}}\); electron accelerated through \(V\) gains \(eV\); X-ray maximum photon energy \(=eV\).
