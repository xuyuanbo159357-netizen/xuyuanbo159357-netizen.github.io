简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

The incident and reflected waves superpose, so their displacements add at each point. At nodes they have equal and opposite displacements and cancel, so P has zero displacement. At antinodes they reinforce, giving an amplitude of 2A. The resulting shape has fixed nodes and antinodes.
