简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

The grille acts as a polariser. In one orientation it blocks the microwaves and the reading is zero; after a 90° rotation the microwaves pass and the reading is greatest. This is polarisation, which occurs only for transverse waves, showing that microwaves are transverse.
