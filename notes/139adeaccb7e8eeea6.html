3.11.2 热传导与U值 / Energy transfer by conduction

考纲 / Specification： 3.11.2　｜　本单元知识谱系

知识逐项讲解 / Concept-by-concept guide

thermal conductivity — 导热系数

Definition / 定义

↗ thermal conductivity — 导热系数

Definition / 定义

English: The rate of energy transfer by conduction per unit cross-sectional area per unit temperature gradient.

中文： 单位横截面积、单位温度梯度对应的热传导能量传递速率。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式

\[\Large\displaystyle P=\frac{kA\Delta T}{L}\]

Symbols and SI units / 符号与 SI 单位

符号 / Symbol 物理量 / Quantity SI 单位 / SI unit
\(P\) 稳态导热功率大小 / steady conduction power magnitude \(\mathrm{W}\)
\(k\) 导热系数 / thermal conductivity \(\mathrm{W\,m^{-1}\,K^{-1}}\)
\(A\) 导热截面积 / conduction cross-sectional area \(\mathrm{m^2}\)
\(\Delta T\) 两侧温差大小 / temperature difference magnitude \(\mathrm{K}\)
\(L\) 厚度 / thickness \(\mathrm{m}\)

Conditions / 条件与说明： 均匀平板、稳态传热。 / Steady heat flow through a uniform slab.

U-value — 传热系数U值

Definition / 定义

↗ U-value — 传热系数U值

Definition / 定义

English: The rate of energy transfer per unit area per unit temperature difference across a structure.

中文： 结构单位面积、单位温差对应的能量传递速率。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式

\[\Large\displaystyle U=\frac{P}{A\Delta T}\]

Symbols and SI units / 符号与 SI 单位

符号 / Symbol 物理量 / Quantity SI 单位 / SI unit
\(U\) 传热系数 / U-value \(\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}\,K^{-1}}\)
\(P\) 传热功率 / heat-transfer power \(\mathrm{W}\)
\(A\) 面积 / area \(\mathrm{m^2}\)
\(\Delta T\) 两侧温差 / temperature difference \(\mathrm{K}\)

定义按考纲与指定教材概括；本页未列出可直接对应的定义题评分条目。 Definitions are summarised from the specification and textbook; no directly matched definition-item citation is claimed here.

Formula / 公式与单位

Conditions / 适用条件： 稳态传热；U=k/L 仅为单层均匀材料、忽略表面附加热阻时的关系。 / Steady conduction; U=k/L assumes one homogeneous layer and negligible additional surface resistance.

\[\Large\displaystyle P=\frac{kA\Delta T}{L}\]
\[\Large\displaystyle P=UA\Delta T\]
\[\Large\displaystyle U=\frac{k}{L}\]
\[\Large\displaystyle P_{\mathrm{total}}=\sum U_iA_i\Delta T\]

物理量与符号 / Quantities and symbols

符号沿用本节公式；若题目另有定义，以题设为准。 / These symbols follow this section; use any notation defined in the question.

物理量 / Quantity 符号 / Symbol SI单位 / SI unit 说明 / Notes
导热系数 / thermal conductivity \(k\) \(\mathrm{W\,m^{-1}\,K^{-1}}\) 不是弹簧劲度系数 k / Not spring stiffness.
传热系数、U值 / thermal transmittance, U-value \(U\) \(\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}\,K^{-1}}\) 不是内能 U / Not internal energy.
功率 / power \(P\) \(\mathrm{W}\) 斜体 P 为功率，正体 W 为瓦特 / P is the quantity; upright W is the watt.
面积、横截面积 / area, cross-sectional area \(A,\ A_0\) \(\mathrm{m^2}\) A₀ 表初始横截面积；与活度 A 区分 / A₀ is initial area; not activity A.
长度 / length \(L,\ l\) \(\mathrm{m}\) 按题目给出的记号 / Use the question's notation.
温度变化 / temperature change \(\Delta T,\ \Delta\theta\) \(\mathrm{K}\) 温差用 K 或 °C 数值相同 / Same numerical change in K or °C.

Conditions / 条件与范围

采用平行平面间稳态传导；温差取该层两侧；不同墙、窗等并列表面损失分别求和。

Use steady conduction through plane parallel faces; temperature difference is across the layer. Sum losses for separate parallel surfaces such as wall and window areas.

Explanation / 解释

导热关系与微观解释 / Conduction and its model

稳态、一维、均匀平板近似下，导热功率 \(P=kA\Delta\theta/L\)。\(A\) 是垂直热流的面积，\(L\) 是热流经过厚度；\(k\) 的单位 \(\mathrm{W\,m^{-1}\,K^{-1}}\)。两端温差与厚度比给温度梯度大小。

For steady one-dimensional conduction through a uniform slab, power is proportional to area and temperature gradient. Use thickness along the heat-flow direction and area perpendicular to it.

非金属主要通过相邻粒子振动传递能量；金属还有自由电子传能，通常导热较好。热从高温向低温传递，稳态表示各处温度不再随时间改变，并非温度处处相同。

Vibrations transfer energy in nonmetals; free electrons provide an additional pathway in metals. Steady state means temperatures do not change with time, not that all temperatures are equal.

U值、能量损失与范围 / U-values and energy losses

平行表面模型 \(P=UA\Delta\theta\)；均匀单层 \(U=\frac{k}{L}\)，单位 \(\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}\,K^{-1}}\)。U值越小，在同样面积和温差下损失越小；能量损失还需乘时间 \(E=Pt\)。

A lower U-value reduces heat loss for fixed area and temperature difference. A U-value is a power-per-area-per-temperature quantity; multiply power by time for energy.

多面墙、窗可分别求功率后相加，面积不得重复计算。考纲限平行表面；课件黑体辐射内容可作导热背景，不新增斯忒藩—玻尔兹曼定律等考纲外必背公式。

Calculate losses through different surfaces separately and add them without duplicating areas. The assessed U-value model is limited to parallel surfaces; radiation background does not add extra required radiation laws.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.11.2

图示：温度梯度与传导路径 / Temperature gradient and conduction

读图 / Reading： 稳态时沿均匀杆近似线性降温；传热面积垂直于热流，长度沿热流方向量取。

In steady conduction a uniform bar has an approximately linear temperature profile; area is normal to heat flow and length follows it.

图示：U 值中的单位面积 / Unit area in a U-value

读图 / Reading： U 值是每平方米、每开尔文温差的传热功率；比较实际墙面散热仍须乘面积与温差。

A U-value is heat-transfer power per square metre per kelvin; actual heat loss also depends on area and temperature difference.

Applications / 应用与实验

使用实际面积、U值比较保温，并由功率乘时间求损失能量；整体 U值不能再重复乘厚度。

Compare insulation using actual U-values and areas; compute energy loss as Pt. When using a quoted overall U-value, do not multiply it by thickness again.

Common errors / 易错点

并列表面不同于叠层材料；开尔文与摄氏温差数值相同。

Do not treat parallel surfaces as a stack of layers. Temperature differences in kelvin and degrees Celsius have equal numerical sizes.

真题检验与答题思路 / Exam coverage and approaches

依据本库现有真题与评分标准核对。下列步骤按题型使用，先核对题设条件；综合题需结合其他关联知识点。 / Checked against the papers and mark schemes held in this vault; select the approach that fits the stated conditions.

3.11.2 方法 1 · 复合导热、界面温度与保温比较 / Composite conduction and insulation

  1. 单层 \(P=kA\Delta T/L=UA\Delta T\)；\(U\) 已含厚度影响，给出 \(U\) 后不应再除一次 \(L\)。 / Do not count thickness twice.
  2. 串联层稳态热流相等，热阻 \(R_{th}=L/(kA)\)，\(P=\Delta T_{total}/\sum R_{th}\)。温降按热阻分配，界面通常不是平均温度。 / Equal heat flow does not imply equal temperature drops.
  3. 同温差的墙、窗、门等并列路径功率相加；保温收益比较 \(A\Delta U\)，不能只比 \(U\) 或百分变化。 / Compare actual reductions in heat flow.
  4. 封闭立方盒常计六面，薄球壳面积约 \(4\pi R^2\)、体积约 \(4\pi R^2t\)；先检查题目是否只指定底面或部分面积。 / Match the area to the stated heat path.
  5. 加地毯却维持同一室内供热功率，需要提高地板下温度，可能增加向地面散热；不要机械断言“加保温所有损失都减少”。 / Apply the operating constraint.
  6. 单位：\(k\) 为 \(\mathrm{W\,m^{-1}\,K^{-1}}\)，\(U\) 为 \(\mathrm{W\,m^{-2}\,K^{-1}}\)。温差用K或°C数值相同，不加273。 / Separate temperature from temperature difference.
