质心与稳定性 / Centre of mass and stability

Concept / 概念

Centre of mass / 质心；Centre of gravity / 重心

Definition / 定义

Centre of mass / 质心

EN: The centre of mass is the point through which a force can act on an object without producing a turning effect on it.

中: 质心是力通过该点作用于物体而不产生转动效应的点。

表达要点 / Wording: point; force; no turning effect

依据 / Evidence: PH01 202206 Q05.1 · MS p8（定义/陈述评分点）

评分标准接受的另一种定义 / Accepted alternative: The centre of mass is the point at which all of an object’s mass can be considered to be concentrated. / 质心是物体全部质量可视为集中于的点。

Centre of gravity / 重心

EN: The centre of gravity is the point at which the resultant weight of an object can be considered to act.

中: 重心是物体整体重力可视为作用于的点。

Explanation / 讲解

知识辨析 / Interpretation

质量可等效集中在质心；在均匀重力场中重心与质心重合。The mass can be considered concentrated at the centre of mass; in a uniform gravitational field it coincides with the centre of gravity.

均匀、规则固体的质心位于几何中心。空心或弯曲形状的质心可以位于物体材料之外。A uniform regular solid has its centre of mass at its centre.

可通过悬挂不规则薄板，沿铅垂线画出重力作用线，换悬挂点后求交点定位质心。Suspension gives intersecting vertical lines through the centre of mass.

物理图像与图示 / Physics graphs and diagrams

读图 / Reading: 悬挂薄板：质心位于每次悬挂形成的铅垂线上，多次画线的交点确定位置。 / Repeated suspension locates the centre of mass at the intersection of vertical lines.

读图 / Reading: 重力绕支点的力矩取决于重心位置；均匀场中重心与质心重合。 / The weight moment depends on the centre of gravity, which coincides with the centre of mass in a uniform field.

Application / 应用

力矩应用 / Moment application

梁或搁板自重应按其质心位置计算力矩。Uniform beams have weight acting at their midpoint, not automatically at an end or pivot.

支撑稳定性取决于重力作用线相对支撑底面的关系。重力作用线越出支撑范围时，重力可产生翻倒力矩。A lower centre of mass and wider base usually improve resistance to tipping.

本节用重力与力矩解释稳定性，不引入转动惯量或转动动力学。The U1 explanation uses moments rather than rotational dynamics.

D06 方法 1 · 质心、稳定性与分布质量 / Centre of mass and stability

标准定义可表述为：作用线通过该点的力对物体不产生绕质心的转动效应。均匀重力场中可视重力作用于该点。 / A force acting through the centre of mass has no turning effect about it.

  1. 点质量组合 \(x_{cm}=\sum m_ix_i/\sum m_i\)，质心靠近较大质量；均匀杆在中点，非均匀部件要分别计入。 / Use the actual mass distribution.
  2. 临界倾倒绕接触边取矩，重力作用线恰到支承边界；降低质心、增大支承宽度一般提高稳定性。 / Identify the limiting pivot.
  3. 绳或长物体提升时看每段质心位移；竖直绳从悬垂状态收上去，质心只上升初始长度的一半。 / Use the centre-of-mass height for distributed material.
