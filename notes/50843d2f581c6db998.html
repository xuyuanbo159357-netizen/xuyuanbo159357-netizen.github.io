3.10.6 变压器、效率与高压输电 / The operation of a transformer

考纲 / Specification： 3.10.6　｜　本单元知识谱系

物理量与符号 / Quantities and symbols

符号沿用本节公式；若题目另有定义，以题设为准。 / These symbols follow this section; use any notation defined in the question.

物理量 / Quantity 符号 / Symbol SI单位 / SI unit 说明 / Notes

原、副线圈电流 / primary and secondary current \(I_p,\ I_s\) \(\mathrm{A}\)

Concept / 概念

  • transformer — 变压器
  • transformer efficiency — 变压器效率

Definition / 定义

↗ transformer — 变压器

Definition / 定义

English: A device transferring electrical energy between coils by electromagnetic induction, usually changing the alternating voltage.

中文： 通过电磁感应在线圈间传递电能、通常改变交流电压的装置。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

↗ transformer efficiency — 变压器效率

Definition / 定义

English: The ratio of useful output electrical power to input electrical power.

中文： 有用输出电功率与输入电功率之比。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

定义按考纲与指定教材概括；本页未列出可直接对应的定义题评分条目。 Definitions are summarised from the specification and textbook; no directly matched definition-item citation is claimed here.

Formula / 公式与单位

正弦交流功率计算使用有效值；百分比效率乘一百。

\(V_s/V_p=N_s/N_p\); \(\eta=V_sI_s/(V_pI_p)\); ideal \(V_pI_p=V_sI_s\); line loss \(P_{\mathrm{loss}}=I^2R\); line voltage drop \(=IR\).

Conditions / 条件与范围

匝比假设两线圈链接相同变化磁通；理想模型无损耗，实际效率小于一；恒定直流不能维持变压作用。

The turns ratio assumes both coils link the same changing flux. Ideal power conservation excludes losses; real efficiency is below unity. Steady dc does not give continuous transformer operation.

Explanation / 解释

互感过程与匝数关系 / Induction and turns ratio

原线圈交流→铁芯磁通随时间变化→穿过副线圈的磁通链改变→副线圈产生交流电动势。稳定直流不能维持这一过程。理想耦合时 \(V_s/V_p=N_s/N_p\)，电压须用同一类型的值（例如均用rms）。

Alternating primary current changes core flux and secondary linkage, inducing secondary emf. Steady DC cannot sustain transformer action. Compare voltages consistently, such as rms with rms.

升压 \(N_s>N_p\)，降压 \(N_s<N_p\)。理想变压器 \(V_pI_p=V_sI_s\)，所以升压伴随电流减小；不能同时无代价增大电压和电流。

An ideal transformer conserves power, so increasing voltage reduces current. It cannot increase both output voltage and current without an increased input.

损耗与输电 / Losses and transmission

效率 \(\eta=V_sI_s/(V_pI_p)\)。线圈电阻产生 \(I^2R\) 热损耗，可用低电阻导线减少；绝缘叠片减少涡流；软磁芯减小反复磁化损耗；良好耦合减少漏磁。冷却移走热量，不等于消除了产生损耗的电阻。

Efficiency is output power divided by input power. Low-resistance windings, laminated cores, soft magnetic material and good coupling reduce different losses. Cooling removes heat rather than eliminating its electrical cause.

同一输送功率下，升高输电电压使电流降低，线路损耗 \(P_{\rm loss}=I^2R_{\rm line}\)、电压降 \(\Delta V=IR_{\rm line}\) 均减小。明确题目给的线路电阻是否包含往返两条线。

At fixed transmitted power, higher voltage lowers current, line heating and voltage drop. Check whether quoted line resistance includes both conductors.

必做实验7 / Required practical 7

用低电压交流电源，分别测原、副线圈的rms电压和电流，对多个负载计算输入、输出功率与效率；控制频率、匝数和铁芯连接。

Use a low-voltage AC source and measure primary and secondary rms voltages and currents for several loads. Keep frequency, turns and core assembly fixed.

实际变压器有空载损耗，副线圈无输出电流并不意味着原线圈输入功率严格为零；讨论表计精度、接触与发热。

A real transformer can draw power even without a secondary load. Consider meter accuracy, connections and heating.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.10.6

图示：变压器的共同磁通 / Shared transformer flux

读图 / Reading： 交变原电流改变铁芯磁通，副线圈因磁通链变化产生电动势；两线圈不需要导线直接连通。

Alternating primary current changes core flux and induces secondary emf; the coils need no direct conducting connection.

图示：效率测量的仪表位置 / Meter positions for efficiency

读图 / Reading： Aₚ、Aₛ 分别串联，Vₚ、Vₛ 分别跨对应线圈；用有效值算两侧功率。改变次级负载时保持原电源与铁芯装配条件。

Put each ammeter in series and each voltmeter across its coil; use rms values for powers and keep primary supply and core conditions fixed while varying secondary load.

Applications / 应用与实验

相同输送功率下升压减小电流及线损，用电端降压；必做实验 7 测不同负载的输入、输出电压电流，计算效率并控制发热。

For fixed transmitted power, raising voltage lowers current and \(I^2R\) line losses; step down near users. Required practical 7: measure input/output voltage and current for different loads; calculate efficiency and control heating.

Common errors / 易错点

电流反匝比关系需理想条件；功率损失不同于电压降。

Secondary current is not always found from the inverse turns ratio unless the ideal model applies. Do not confuse power loss with voltage drop.

3.10.6 方法 1 · 变压器损失与输电 / Transformer losses and transmission

  1. 在题目给定磁通完全耦合或理想电压比条件下 \(V_s/V_p=N_s/N_p\)；非100%效率用 \(\eta=V_sI_s/(V_pI_p)\)，不可同时强加理想电流反比。 / Use the stated voltage-ratio assumption separately from power efficiency.
  2. 解释损失采用“机制 → 何处耗能 → 对应设计”：线圈 \(I^2R\) → 粗、低电阻率导线；涡流 → 薄且互相绝缘的叠片；磁滞 → 易磁化反磁化的软磁芯；漏磁 → 线圈耦合与闭合磁路。 / Match each remedy to its own loss mechanism.
  3. 叠片减少而不消除涡流；不能笼统说“涡流总与磁场反向”，其方向取决于场的变化。 / Avoid absolute or incorrect Lenz-law statements.
  4. 固定传输功率时升压使 \(I=P/V\) 减小，线路损失 \(I^2R\) 降低；由效率先求损失功率，再求线路电阻和 \(IR\) 压降。 / Distinguish voltage delivered from voltage lost in the cable.
  5. 变压器不改变交流频率；接二极管后按极性判半波，并考虑题设二极管压降，不能把整流当作变压。 / Separate transformation from rectification.
