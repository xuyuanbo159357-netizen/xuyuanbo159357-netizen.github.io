3.9.1 电容充放电与时间常量

物理量与符号 / Quantities and symbols

符号沿用本节公式；若题目另有定义，以题设为准。 / These symbols follow this section; use any notation defined in the question.

物理量 / Quantity 符号 / Symbol SI单位 / SI unit 说明 / Notes

方法、分析与误差 / Method, analysis and uncertainty

图示：充放电的回路切换 / Switching charge and discharge paths

读图 / Reading： 先跟踪开关位置对应的闭合路径；放电时电容经电阻形成回路，电源应从该放电回路中断开。

Trace the closed path for each switch position; discharge is through the resistor with the source excluded from that path.

图示：放电的三条指数曲线 / Discharge curves

读图 / Reading： 电流大小、板电荷和电压以相同时间常量衰减；图中电流画的是所选方向下的大小，要和正文符号约定核对。

Current magnitude, charge and voltage decay with the same time constant; check the chosen sign convention for current.

1

把电容充至已知电压，再经已知电阻放电。

Charge a capacitor to a known supply voltage, then discharge it through a known resistor.

2

用高阻电压表或记录器，选择足够大的时间常量。

Use a high-resistance voltmeter/data logger; choose RC large enough to sample the change reliably.

3

定时记录电压，重复并比较充放电图形。

Record voltage at known times, repeat and compare charging and discharging shapes.

4

放电作对数图由斜率求时间常量，也可用初值的 e分之一点或初始切线。

Plot ln V against t for discharge; slope is −1/RC. Alternatively use the 1/e point or the initial tangent.

5

考虑电压表负载、计时分辨率、漏电与真实电阻电容偏差。

Account for voltmeter loading, timing resolution, capacitor leakage and variation of actual R or C.

定量分析与条件核对 / Quantitative analysis and controls

↗ 3.9.1 电容充放电与时间常量 / Capacitor charge and discharge

必做实验6 / Required practical 6

电压表并联电容，充电后切换到已知电阻放电，记录多组 \((t,V_C)\)；选择可可靠采样的 \(RC\)。由对数图斜率求 \(RC=-1/\mathrm{gradient}\)，还可用 \(1/e\) 点和初始切线交点交叉检查。

Record capacitor pd against time through a known resistance. Choose a resolvable time constant and determine it from a log-linear gradient, cross-checking with the \(1/e\) point or initial tangent.

高阻表仍可能与放电电阻并联，使实际电阻变小；检查漏电、开关与导线电阻、实际电容值和采样时间。无需用积分推导作为额外记忆负担。

Meter loading and leakage can change the effective resistance and fitted time constant. Check actual component values and timing resolution.
