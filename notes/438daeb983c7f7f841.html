简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

For two widely separated plotted points, p/n is approximately the same, about 4.0 × 10⁴ Pa mol⁻¹. The straight line also passes through the origin. From pV = nRT, its gradient is RT/V. R and V are constant, so the constant gradient shows that T is constant.
