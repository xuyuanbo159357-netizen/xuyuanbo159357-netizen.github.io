简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

The required current is 7.5 A. In 30 minutes it needs Q = It = 7.5 × 30 × 60 = 1.35 × 10⁴ C. The battery can supply only 1.15 × 10⁴ C, corresponding to about 25.6 minutes, so it is unsuitable.
