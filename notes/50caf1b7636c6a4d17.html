Conditions / 条件与范围

采用中心质量占主导的圆轨道并忽略阻力；同步周期为地球一自转周期，通常计算近似 24 小时。

Use circular orbits around a dominant central mass and neglect resistive forces. Earth-synchronous period is one sidereal rotation, approximately 24 h for usual calculations.

Explanation / 解释

圆轨道速度与周期 / Circular orbit speed and period

仅引力提供向心力时 \(\frac{GMm}{r^2}=\frac{mv^2}{r}\)，故 \(v=\sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}}\)，\(T=2\pi\sqrt{r^3/(GM)}\)。同一天体周围，轨道越高速度越小、周期越长；理想轨道参数与卫星自身质量无关。

Equate gravitation to the radial resultant. For the same central body, larger circular orbits have lower speeds and longer periods; the satellite's mass cancels.

先用 \(r=R+h\)。卫星和宇航员共同自由落体，失重不等于轨道处没有重力。

Use orbital radius, not altitude. Apparent weightlessness occurs because craft and occupants are in free fall, not because gravity is absent.

轨道能量与逃逸应用 / Orbital energy and escape

圆轨道 \(E_k=\frac{GMm}{2r}\)、\(E_p=-GMm/r\)、\(E_{\rm total}=-GMm/(2r)\)。升入更高圆轨道时总能量增大、势能增大，但最终动能和速率减小；需要输入能量，不能仅比较末态速率。

For a circular orbit, total energy is negative. Moving to a higher circular orbit requires an increase in total energy despite the lower final speed.

课件逃逸应用：不计大气且最终在无穷远恰好静止时，总机械能为零，得 \(v_{\rm esc}=\sqrt{2GM/r}\)。它来自能量分析，不等于该处圆轨道速度。

For the slide's escape application, neglecting drag and reaching infinity at rest gives zero total energy and \(v_{\rm esc}=\sqrt{2GM/r}\). This differs from circular orbital speed.

同步与静止轨道 / Synchronous and stationary orbits

地球同步轨道周期与地球自转周期相同；地球静止轨道还须圆形、赤道平面内、与地球同向绕行，才在地面看来位置固定。计算按题给自转周期；近似一天时约24 h。

A geosynchronous orbit matches Earth's rotation period. A geostationary orbit must additionally be circular, equatorial and prograde so that it remains above one ground location. Use the rotation period given in the question.

通信天线可固定指向静止卫星，但远距离带来传输延迟，极区覆盖受限。不要把所有同步轨道都说成地面上空静止。

Geostationary communication permits fixed aerials, with propagation delay and limited polar coverage. Not every synchronous orbit is stationary relative to the ground.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.7.4

图示：圆轨道能量 / Circular-orbit energy

读图 / Reading： 势能为负、动能为正、总能量仍为负。升高圆轨道后动能减小但总能量增加，不能只比较速率。

Potential energy is negative, kinetic positive and total negative; a higher circular orbit has lower kinetic but higher total energy.

Applications / 应用与实验

解释静止卫星通信覆盖与固定天线，并区分同步与静止轨道。

Explain communications coverage and fixed ground dishes for geostationary satellites; distinguish general synchronous orbits from stationary ones.

Common errors / 易错点

轨道半径从星球中心量起；失重来自共同自由落体而非引力场零。

Orbit radius is measured from the planet centre. Weightlessness in orbit arises from shared free fall, not zero gravitational field.

3.7.4 方法 1 · 圆轨道、变轨与同步条件 / Circular orbits and orbital changes

  1. 推导题从 \(GMm/r^2=mv^2/r=mr\omega^2\) 起步，结合 \(v=2\pi r/T\)，再得到 \(v^2=GM/r\) 和 \(T^2=\frac{4\pi^2r^3}{GM}\)。 / Show the force equation before quoting derived results.
  2. 圆轨道 \(E_k=\frac{GMm}{2r}\)、\(E_p=-GMm/r\)、\(E=-GMm/(2r)\)。降轨后动能增加、势能下降更多、总能量变得更负；制动推力移除的是总机械能。 / Distinguish a brief manoeuvre from the final circular-orbit comparison.
  3. 同步需周期等于地球自转周期；静止还需赤道平面、同向且圆轨道。向东从赤道发射可利用最大地面自转速度并减少轨道平面调整。 / List the extra geostationary conditions.
  4. 非圆轨道是否可能用给定 \(F\) 与 \(mv^2/r\) 比较；不能把所有引力运动当匀速圆周。总能量非负表示在孤立吸引场中不受束缚。 / State the assumed isolated-system model.
  5. 附近第二天体的引力可能提供额外向心分量，使轨道偏离单一天体的开普勒圆轨道关系。 / Include the fields specified in the question.
