1. 圆轨道的速度与周期 / Circular-orbit speed and period

1.1 关键概念与定义 / Key concepts and definitions

orbital period — 公转周期

Definition / 定义

English: The time taken for one complete orbit.

中文： 完成一周轨道运动所需时间。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式

\[\Large\displaystyle T=\frac{1}{f}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(T\) 周期 / period \(\mathrm{s}\)
\(f\) 频率 / frequency \(\mathrm{Hz}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

周期与频率互为倒数，振动次数越多则单次振动时间越短。

Period and frequency are reciprocals: more oscillations per second means a shorter time per oscillation.

1.2 公式与物理规律 / Formulae and physical laws

\[\Large\displaystyle \frac{GMm}{r^2}=\frac{mv^2}{r}\]

\(r\) 半径、距源距离 / radius, distance from source \(\mathrm{m}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

圆轨道中引力正好提供所需向心力，由此可求轨道速度。

For a circular gravitational orbit, gravity supplies the required centripetal force.

\[\Large\displaystyle v=\sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(v\) 速率 / speed \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)

\(M\) 源天体质量 / source mass \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(r\) 半径、距源距离 / radius, distance from source \(\mathrm{m}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

圆轨道速度由中心天体质量和轨道半径决定。

Circular orbital speed is determined by central mass and orbital radius.

适用条件 / Conditions

中心质量占主导、圆轨道、阻力可忽略。

Use a dominant central mass, a circular orbit and negligible drag.

\[\Large\displaystyle T^2=\frac{4\pi^2r^3}{GM}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(T\) 周期 / period \(\mathrm{s}\)
\(r\) 半径、距源距离 / radius, distance from source \(\mathrm{m}\)
\(G\) 万有引力常量 / gravitational constant \(\mathrm{N\,m^2\,kg^{-2}}\)
\(M\) 源天体质量 / source mass \(\mathrm{kg}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

开普勒第三定律的圆轨道形式说明周期平方与轨道半径立方成正比。

The circular form of Kepler’s third law makes period squared proportional to orbital radius cubed.

适用条件 / Conditions

比较绕同一中心质量的轨道。

Compare orbits about the same central mass.

1.3 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

适用范围与条件 / Scope and conditions

Conditions / 适用条件： 圆轨道，中心质量近似固定；r 为中心到卫星距离。较高轨道速率更小、周期更长，总能量较高但仍为负。 / Circular orbit around a fixed central mass; higher orbits are slower, take longer, and have higher but still negative total energy.

Conditions / 条件与范围

采用中心质量占主导的圆轨道并忽略阻力；同步周期为地球一自转周期，通常计算近似 24 小时。

Use circular orbits around a dominant central mass and neglect resistive forces. Earth-synchronous period is one sidereal rotation, approximately 24 h for usual calculations.

圆轨道速度与周期 / Circular orbit speed and period

仅引力提供向心力时 \(\frac{GMm}{r^2}=\frac{mv^2}{r}\)，故 \(v=\sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}}\)，\(T=2\pi\sqrt{r^3/(GM)}\)。同一天体周围，轨道越高速度越小、周期越长；理想轨道参数与卫星自身质量无关。

Equate gravitation to the radial resultant. For the same central body, larger circular orbits have lower speeds and longer periods; the satellite's mass cancels.

先用 \(r=R+h\)。卫星和宇航员共同自由落体，失重不等于轨道处没有重力。

Use orbital radius, not altitude. Apparent weightlessness occurs because craft and occupants are in free fall, not because gravity is absent.

Common errors / 易错点

轨道半径从星球中心量起；失重来自共同自由落体而非引力场零。

Orbit radius is measured from the planet centre. Weightlessness in orbit arises from shared free fall, not zero gravitational field.

2. 轨道能量与逃逸 / Orbital energy and escape

2.1 公式与物理规律 / Formulae and physical laws

\[\Large\displaystyle E_k=\frac{GMm}{2r}\]

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(E_{k}\) 动能 / kinetic energy \(\mathrm{J}\)

\(m\) 质量 / mass \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(r\) 半径、距源距离 / radius, distance from source \(\mathrm{m}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

圆轨道的动能由引力提供的向心力关系得到，是引力势能大小的一半。

Circular-orbit kinetic energy follows from gravitational centripetal force and is half the magnitude of gravitational potential energy.

\[\Large\displaystyle U=-\frac{GMm}{r}\]

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit

\(m\) 质量 / mass \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(r\) 半径、距源距离 / radius, distance from source \(\mathrm{m}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

物体在引力场中的势能由其质量与该位置的引力势决定。

An object’s gravitational potential energy is its mass times the gravitational potential at its position.

适用条件 / Conditions

点源或球对称源之外，无穷远处势能为零。

For the inverse-distance form, use a point or spherical source with zero energy at infinity.

\[\Large\displaystyle E_{\mathrm{total}}=-\frac{GMm}{2r}\]

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit

\(m\) 质量 / mass \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(r\) 半径、距源距离 / radius, distance from source \(\mathrm{m}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

圆轨道总能量为负，表示物体处于束缚状态。

Circular-orbit total energy is negative, indicating a gravitationally bound state.

适用条件 / Conditions

势能零点在无穷远。

Set potential energy to zero at infinity.

\[\Large\displaystyle E_{k}=\frac{GMm}{2r}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(E_{k}\) 动能 / kinetic energy \(\mathrm{J}\)
\(G\) 万有引力常量 / gravitational constant \(\mathrm{N\,m^2\,kg^{-2}}\)
\(M\) 源天体质量 / source mass \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(m\) 质量 / mass \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(r\) 半径、距源距离 / radius, distance from source \(\mathrm{m}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

圆轨道中把引力提供向心力的条件代入动能表达式，得到轨道动能。

For a circular orbit, substituting the gravitational centripetal condition into kinetic energy gives orbital kinetic energy.

适用条件 / Conditions

理想圆轨道，源天体质量远大于卫星质量。

Use an ideal circular orbit around a much more massive source.

\[\Large\displaystyle v_{\mathrm{esc}}=\sqrt{\frac{2GM}{r}}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量或记号 / Quantity or notation SI 单位 / SI unit
\(v_{\mathrm{esc}}\) 逃逸速率 / escape speed \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)
\(G\) 万有引力常量 / gravitational constant \(\mathrm{N\,m^2\,kg^{-2}}\)
\(M\) 源天体质量 / source mass \(\mathrm{kg}\)
\(r\) 半径、距源距离 / radius, distance from source \(\mathrm{m}\)

物理规律与含义 / Law and meaning

最小逃逸速率使物体到无穷远处时动能趋于零、总机械能为零。

Minimum escape speed gives zero final kinetic energy at infinity and zero total mechanical energy.

适用条件 / Conditions

只考虑源天体引力，忽略空气阻力及继续推进。

Include only source gravity; neglect drag and further propulsion.

2.2 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

轨道能量与逃逸应用 / Orbital energy and escape

圆轨道 \(E_k=\frac{GMm}{2r}\)、\(E_p=-GMm/r\)、\(E_{\rm total}=-GMm/(2r)\)。升入更高圆轨道时总能量增大、势能增大，但最终动能和速率减小；需要输入能量，不能仅比较末态速率。

For a circular orbit, total energy is negative. Moving to a higher circular orbit requires an increase in total energy despite the lower final speed.

学习资料逃逸应用：不计大气且最终在无穷远恰好静止时，总机械能为零，得 \(v_{\rm esc}=\sqrt{2GM/r}\)。它来自能量分析，不等于该处圆轨道速度。

For the slide's escape application, neglecting drag and reaching infinity at rest gives zero total energy and \(v_{\rm esc}=\sqrt{2GM/r}\). This differs from circular orbital speed.

圆轨道能量 / Circular-orbit energy

读图 / Reading： 势能为负、动能为正、总能量仍为负。升高圆轨道后动能减小但总能量增加，不能只比较速率。

Potential energy is negative, kinetic positive and total negative; a higher circular orbit has lower kinetic but higher total energy.

3. 同步与地球静止轨道 / Synchronous and geostationary orbits

geosynchronous orbit — 地球同步轨道

Definition / 定义

English: An orbit whose period equals the Earth’s rotation period.

中文： 轨道周期等于地球自转周期的轨道。

Definition / 定义

English: A circular equatorial geosynchronous orbit in the same direction as Earth’s rotation, so the satellite remains above one point on Earth.

中文： 与地球同向、位于赤道平面的圆形同步轨道，卫星相对地面位置不变。

3.2 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

同步与静止轨道 / Synchronous and stationary orbits

地球同步轨道周期与地球自转周期相同；地球静止轨道还须圆形、赤道平面内、与地球同向绕行，才在地面看来位置固定。计算按题给自转周期；近似一天时约24 h。

A geosynchronous orbit matches Earth's rotation period. A geostationary orbit must additionally be circular, equatorial and prograde so that it remains above one ground location. Use the rotation period given in the question.

通信天线可固定指向静止卫星，但远距离带来传输延迟，极区覆盖受限。不要把所有同步轨道都说成地面上空静止。

Geostationary communication permits fixed aerials, with propagation delay and limited polar coverage. Not every synchronous orbit is stationary relative to the ground.

As the radius decreases, kinetic energy increases, but gravitational potential energy falls by a larger amount. The total energy therefore decreases and becomes more negative, consistent with E = −GMm/(2r).

3.7.4 方法 1 · 圆轨道、变轨与同步条件 / Circular orbits and orbital changes

  1. 推导题从 \(GMm/r^2=mv^2/r=mr\omega^2\) 起步，结合 \(v=2\pi r/T\)，再得到 \(v^2=GM/r\) 和 \(T^2=\frac{4\pi^2r^3}{GM}\)。 / Show the force equation before quoting derived results.
  2. 圆轨道 \(E_k=\frac{GMm}{2r}\)、\(E_p=-GMm/r\)、\(E=-GMm/(2r)\)。降轨后动能增加、势能下降更多、总能量变得更负；制动推力移除的是总机械能。 / Distinguish a brief manoeuvre from the final circular-orbit comparison.
  3. 同步需周期等于地球自转周期；静止还需赤道平面、同向且圆轨道。向东从赤道发射可利用最大地面自转速度并减少轨道平面调整。 / List the extra geostationary conditions.
