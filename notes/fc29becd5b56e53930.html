3.10.5 交流有效值与示波器 / Alternating currents

考纲 / Specification： 3.10.5　｜　本单元知识谱系

知识逐项讲解 / Concept-by-concept guide

root mean square current — 电流有效值

Definition / 定义

↗ root mean square current — 电流有效值

Definition / 定义

English: The steady direct current that gives the same average power in a resistor as the alternating current.

中文： 在同一电阻上产生相同平均功率的恒定直流电流。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

Formula / 公式

\[\Large\displaystyle I_{\mathrm{rms}}=\frac{I_0}{\sqrt2}\]

Formula / 公式与单位

Conditions / 适用条件： 根号二和两倍关系用于正弦、关于零对称的交流；纯电阻平均功率使用有效值。 / Square-root-of-two and doubling relations assume a sinusoid symmetric about zero; use rms values for mean resistor power.

Conditions / 条件与范围

只要求正弦电压、电流；有效值功率比较采用电阻负载。

Only sinusoidal voltages and currents are required. Power equality applies to a resistive load.

Explanation / 解释

正弦量、峰值与有效值 / Sinusoidal and rms quantities

本节仅讨论正弦交流：\(V=V_0\sin\omega t\)，峰峰值 \(V_{pp}=2V_0\)，\(V_{\rm rms}=V_0/\sqrt2\)；电流同理。有效值是产生相同平均电阻发热功率的直流等效值，不是一个周期电流的代数平均（后者为零）。

For sinusoidal AC, rms is peak divided by \(\sqrt2\) and peak-to-peak is twice peak. RMS is the DC-equivalent heating value, not the signed cycle average.

纯电阻负载 \(\bar P=I_{\rm rms}V_{\rm rms}=I_{\rm rms}^2R=V_{\rm rms}^2/R=V_0I_0/2\)。使用 rms 后不再额外乘 \(1/2\)；有相位差的其他负载不能直接推广此等式。

For a resistor, average power is the product of rms voltage and current. Do not insert a second factor of one half after using rms. The equality is not general for phase-shifted loads.

市电标示电压通常是有效值；求峰值乘 \(\sqrt2\)，求峰峰值再乘2。

A quoted mains voltage is normally rms; distinguish conversion to peak from conversion to peak-to-peak.

示波器读数与控制 / Oscilloscope measurements

先用地电位基线确定零电压，再以格数乘纵向灵敏度（V/div）求电压；峰到峰格数得到峰峰值，不能误当峰值。直流使轨迹相对零线偏移，交流显示随时间变化的波形。

Establish the zero-voltage reference, then multiply vertical divisions by V/div. Peak-to-peak divisions give peak-to-peak voltage. DC offsets the trace; AC gives a time-varying waveform.

一个完整周期的横向格数乘 time-base（s/div）得 \(T\)，\(f=\frac{1}{T}\)；跨多周期测量后除以周期数可减小误差。熟悉Y增益、时基、位置和触发控制；不要求电子枪、偏转板结构或李萨如图测频方法。

Multiply horizontal divisions per complete cycle by the time-base setting. Averaging several cycles improves timing. Know the display controls; internal construction and Lissajous methods are not required.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.10.5

U3 AC peak rms

正弦电压峰值、峰峰值和有效值；有效值非时间平均电压。 Sinusoidal rms level is peak / sqrt(2).

图示：示波器显示的含义 / Oscilloscope displays

读图 / Reading： 先区分直流偏移、振幅和频率：竖直偏移看电压，水平周期乘时基读周期；有效值不是屏幕高度的直接读数。

Distinguish DC offset, amplitude and frequency; vertical scale gives voltage and time-base scale gives period. RMS is not read directly as screen height.

Applications / 应用与实验

由市电有效值求峰值、峰峰值；使用控制项显示直流、交流并测时间，不要求内部结构。

Calculate mains peak and peak-to-peak values from rms values; use controls to display dc and ac signals and measure time intervals. Internal oscilloscope structure is not required.

Common errors / 易错点

峰峰值为峰值两倍而非有效值两倍；对称正弦电流平均值零但仍发热。

Peak-to-peak voltage is twice the peak, not twice the rms. A symmetrical sine wave has zero mean current but non-zero heating.

依据本库现有真题与评分标准核对。下列步骤按题型使用，先核对题设条件；综合题需结合其他关联知识点。 / Checked against the papers and mark schemes held in this vault; select the approach that fits the stated conditions.

3.10.5 方法 1 · 示波器、有效值与功率 / Oscilloscopes, rms and power

  1. 一周期横格数乘时间基准得 \(T\)，再 \(f=\frac{1}{T}\)；峰到中线格数乘电压灵敏度得 \(V_0\)，峰峰值为 \(2V_0\)。含直流偏置时先找中线。 / Separate amplitude, peak-to-peak voltage and dc offset.
  2. 正弦交流 \(V_{rms}=V_0/\sqrt2\)、\(I_{rms}=I_0/\sqrt2\)；纯电阻平均功率 \(P=V_{rms}I_{rms}=V_0I_0/2\)。不可将一个峰值直接与另一个有效值相乘。 / Convert both quantities to compatible forms.
  3. 有效值定义为使同一电阻产生相同平均热功率的直流值。正弦电阻负载峰值功率为平均功率两倍；非正弦波不能直接套 \(\sqrt2\)。 / State the waveform and load assumptions.
  4. “V/div”数值增大，显示高度反而减小；时间基准关闭，交流形成竖直线段，不能读频率。选择完整且尽量占屏幕的波形降低相对读数误差。 / Distinguish a dial value from the displayed size.
  5. 发电线圈 \(\varepsilon_0=NBA\omega\)；转速减半时幅值减半、周期加倍，磁通与电动势相差四分之一周期。 / Change amplitude and period together.
