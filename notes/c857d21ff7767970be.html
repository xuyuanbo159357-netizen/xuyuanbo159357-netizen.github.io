3.11.3 气体定律与理想气体 / Ideal gases

↗ absolute zero — 绝对零度

Definition / 定义

English: The lowest thermodynamic temperature, zero kelvin; the ideal-gas extrapolation corresponds to zero average random kinetic energy.

Formula / 公式与单位

区分摩尔数与分子数；理想气体温度必须使用开尔文。

\(pV=nRT=Nk_BT\); \(N=nN_A\); \(n=m/M\); \(R=N_Ak_B\); \(T/\mathrm K=\theta/{}^\circ\mathrm C+273.15\).

三条气体关系各有约束；恒压膨胀时气体对外做功为正，对气体做功为负。

Fixed n: Boyle \(pV=\mathrm{constant}\) at fixed T; Charles \(V/T=\mathrm{constant}\) at fixed p; pressure law \(p/T=\mathrm{constant}\) at fixed V. Work by gas at constant pressure \(W_{\mathrm{by}}=p\Delta V\), work on gas \(=-p\Delta V\).

Conditions / 条件与范围

使用绝对压强、开尔文温度和立方米；简单压强乘体积变化式要求恒压，变压时用压强—体积图面积。

Use absolute pressure, absolute temperature and SI volume. The simple \(p\Delta V\) expression assumes constant pressure; generally work by gas is the area under a pressure–volume curve.

Explanation / 解释

气体定律及控制条件 / Gas laws and controls

固定气体量：等温 \(pV=\text{constant}\)（玻意耳），恒压 \(V/T=\text{constant}\)（查理），恒容 \(p/T=\text{constant}\)。\(p\) 用绝对压强，\(T\) 用K；表压要加环境压强。若泄漏或加入气体，固定气体量的比例关系不能直接使用。

For a fixed amount of gas, apply Boyle's, Charles's and pressure laws with their stated controls. Use absolute pressure and kelvin temperature. Leaks or added gas invalidate fixed-amount comparisons.

\(T/\mathrm K=\theta/{}^\circ\mathrm C+273.15\)；温差的K与°C数值相同，温度的比值必须用K。绝对零度是热力学温度零点，在经典理想气体模型中平均平动动能趋零；不能推广为所有物质总内能为零。

Temperature intervals have equal numerical values in kelvin and Celsius, but temperature ratios require kelvin. Zero kelvin does not imply zero total internal energy for every material.

气体量、粒子数与做功 / Amount, particles and work

\(pV=nRT=NkT\)，\(N=nN_A\)，\(R=N_Ak\)；\(n=m_{\rm gas}/M\)，单分子质量 \(m_0=M/N_A\)。摩尔质量若用 g/mol，应先与质量单位统一；\(N\) 是粒子数，\(n\) 是摩尔数，不能互换。

Distinguish amount in moles from particle number. Keep mass and molar-mass units consistent and distinguish sample mass from individual molecular mass.

恒定外压下气体对外做功 \(W_{\rm by}=p\Delta V\)，对气体做功 \(W_{\rm on}=-p\Delta V\)；一般准静态过程为 \(p\)–\(V\) 图有符号面积。膨胀对应 \(W_{\rm by}>0\)，压缩相反。

At constant pressure, expansion work by the gas is \(p\Delta V\). Work on the gas has the opposite sign. For a quasistatic varying-pressure path, use the signed area under the pressure–volume graph.

理想气体近似在较低密度、远离液化条件时较好；方程中的温度是气体达到热平衡后的温度。

The ideal-gas approximation is most reliable at low density and away from condensation; use the equilibrium gas temperature.

必做实验9 / Required practical 9

玻意耳：缓慢改变密闭气体体积，待温度恢复，测绝对压强；作 \(p\)–\(1/V\) 或检验 \(pV\)。查理：保持压强与气体量不变，水浴改变温度，待平衡测体积；作 \(V\)–\(T\)（K）。

For Boyle's law change volume slowly and allow thermal recovery. For Charles's law vary bath temperature at constant pressure and gas amount. Linearise using reciprocal volume or kelvin temperature.

检查管路死体积、气密性、压强零点和温度均匀性；不能把快速压缩导致升温的数据当作等温关系。

Consider dead volume, leakage, pressure zero and uniform temperature. Rapid compression is not an isothermal measurement.

U4 Gas laws

固定气体量的三条经验气体定律；温度坐标为开尔文。 Empirical gas laws with specified fixed variables.

Applications / 应用与实验

必做实验 9：波义耳定律缓慢改变体积、保持温度，作压强与体积倒数图；查理定律恒压改变温度，作体积—温度图，等待热平衡并考虑残留体积。

Required practical 9: Boyle’s law—vary volume slowly while controlling temperature, plot p against 1/V. Charles’s law—vary temperature while maintaining pressure, plot V against T; allow thermal equilibrium and account for trapped dead volume.

Common errors / 易错点

不能代入摄氏温度或表压；体积变化不代表分子数改变。

Do not substitute Celsius temperature or gauge pressure. A gas can change volume without changing particle number.

3.11.3 方法 1 · 理想气体状态、泄放与绝对零度 / Gas states, release and absolute zero

  1. \(pV=nRT=NkT\) 中 \(p\) 是绝对压强，\(T\) 用K；表压先加大气压。\(M\)、\(m\)、单粒子质量与 \(N\)、\(n\) 分别标清。 / Label the amount and mass quantities explicitly.
  2. 固定气体量才可令 \(pV/T\) 不变；放气、加气或连接容器用粒子数守恒。等温连通容器可用 \(p_f(V_1+V_2)=p_1V_1+p_2V_2\)，连接管体积不一定可忽略。 / Include all accessible gas volume.
  3. 容器内有固体时，气体体积是剩余空间；由压强下降求放气量可用 \(\Delta n=V\Delta p/(RT)\)。 / Use free volume, not the outer container volume.
  4. \(p=\rho RT/M\)，固定成分时比较 \(p/(\rho T)\)；不同分子质量、相同总质量并不等于相同摩尔数。 / Check what is conserved in a ratio.
  5. 定压毛细管 \(V=Al\)，\(l\)–\(\theta(^\circ C)\) 线性外推横截距估绝对零度；管截面积减半而气量不变时斜率和纵截距加倍、横截距不变。 / Interpret all intercepts, not just the slope.
  6. 验证比例需多组数据、原点及常量条件；较长空气柱、较宽温度范围减小相对读数及外推不确定度，并等待热平衡。 / Distinguish a straight line from proportionality.

对应真题 / Examples： PH04 · 202406 · Q11；PH04 · 201806 · Q20；PH04 · 202601 · Q01；PH04 · 202606 · Q02；PH04 · 202206 · Q02；PH04 · 202201 · Q05。

3.11.3 方法 2 · 压强—体积图与循环 / Pressure-volume work and cycles

  1. 图下 \(\int p\,dV\) 为气体对外功；压缩时外界对气体功为正。曲线不能用一个端点 \(p\Delta V\)，除非压强恒定。 / Use an area for variable pressure.
  2. 检查坐标倍率、体积单位和截断原点；图底以下到 \(p=0\) 的矩形面积也属于功。 / Include the area hidden by a false origin.
  3. 循环 \(\Delta U=0\)，顺时针净对外功为正，数值为闭合面积，净入热等于净对外功。 / State the direction as well as the magnitude.
  4. 定量比较温度看各状态的 \(pV/n\)，不能仅由压缩认定温度升高。给定 \(pV^b=C\) 时，\(\ln p=\ln C-b\ln V\)，按坐标确定斜率符号。 / Derive the logarithmic linearisation.
  5. 恒温恒压排出相同粒子数时，\(p\Delta V=\Delta NkT\)；若改为相同体积，功随外压改变。 / Distinguish fixed volume from fixed number of particles.
