3.8.3 电势、电势能与等势面 / Electric potential

考纲 / Specification： 3.8.3　｜　本单元知识谱系

Concept / 概念

  • electric potential — 电势
  • electric potential difference — 电势差

Definition / 定义

↗ electric potential — 电势

Definition / 定义

English: The work done per unit positive charge by an external agent in moving a small test charge from infinity to a point without changing its kinetic energy.

中文： 外力使单位正检验电荷从无穷远移至该点而不改变动能时所做的功。

定义评分依据 / Definition marking points： PH03 201901-Q04。原答案在题目下展开；此处按评分要点概括。 Summarised from the marking points; originals are folded below each question.

↗ electric potential difference — 电势差

Definition / 定义

English: The change in electric potential energy per unit charge between two points.

中文： 两点间电势能变化与电荷量之比。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

真题定义依据 / Mark-scheme checks： PH03 201901-Q04。定义按评分要点概括；原答案可在对应题目下展开。 Definitions are summarised from the scoring points, with original answers below the linked questions.

Formula / 公式与单位

电势由源电荷决定；电势能还取决于检验电荷，带符号径向关系含负号。

\(V=Q/(4\pi\varepsilon_0r)\); \(U=qV\); \(\Delta U=q\Delta V\); signed radial \(E_r=-dV/dr\); \(\Delta V=-\int E_r\,dr\).

Conditions / 条件与范围

无穷远为零势；图若使用场强大小，应取斜率或面积大小并单独判断方向与符号。

Take zero potential at infinity. In graphs described by field magnitudes, use gradient/area magnitudes and determine direction/sign separately.

Explanation / 解释

带符号电势与做功 / Signed potential and work

电势是将单位正电荷从无穷远缓慢移到该点时外力做功；\(V=Q/(4\pi\varepsilon_0r)\)，保留源电荷符号。电势是标量，多源电势作代数和；场强为矢量，须另行叠加。

Electric potential is external work per unit positive charge in a slow transfer from infinity. Potential is signed and scalar; add potentials algebraically and fields vectorially.

\(\Delta E_p=q\Delta V\)，电场做功 \(W_{\rm field}=-q\Delta V\)；只有电场做功时 \(\Delta E_k=-q\Delta V\)。正电荷自由加速趋向低电势，负电荷趋向高电势，但二者均趋向较低电势能。

Electric field work is the negative potential-energy change. Positive and negative charges accelerate towards lower and higher potential respectively, while both can lose potential energy.

等势面上 \(\Delta V=0\)，沿面移动电荷电场不做功；电场线与等势面垂直。\(V=0\) 不推出 \(E=0\)，反之亦然。

Moving along an equipotential involves no electrical work. Equipotentials are perpendicular to field lines. Zero potential does not imply zero field, or conversely.

图像的方向约定 / Signed graphical relationships

正点电荷：\(V>0\) 随 \(r\) 按 \(1/r\) 下降，径向场向外、大小按 \(1/r^2\) 下降；负点电荷：\(V<0\) 趋向零，场向内。

For a positive source, potential is positive and decreases with radius; for a negative source it is negative and rises towards zero. Field magnitude falls as \(1/r^2\).

有方向分量满足 \(E_r=-dV/dr\)，\(\Delta V=-\int E_r\,dr\)。考纲中省略负号的写法应按大小关系理解，不能据此把电场方向画成电势升高方向。\(V\)–\(r\) 切线、\(E_r\)–\(r\) 有符号面积都要交代正方向。

For signed radial quantities, field is the negative potential gradient and potential change is the negative field area. Interpret any unsigned specification shorthand as a magnitude relation; fields point towards decreasing potential.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.8.3

U3 Electric field potential

正负点电荷的带符号电场和电势；径向向外定义为正，E_r=-dV/dr。 Signed point-charge field and potential.

图示：电势面与电场线 / Electric equipotentials

读图 / Reading： 径向场线穿过同心等势面且与其垂直；沿同一等势面移动不改变电势能，是否有其他力做功需另行判断。

Radial field lines are normal to concentric equipotentials; motion along one equipotential leaves electric potential energy unchanged.

图示：E–r 与 V–r 对照 / Comparing field and potential graphs

读图 / Reading： 电场强度大小按 1/r² 衰减，电势按 1/r 衰减；带方向的关系为 Eᵣ=−dV/dr，不可把两条曲线的纵坐标当成同一种物理量。

Field magnitude decays as 1/r² and potential as 1/r; the signed relation is Eᵣ=−dV/dr. The vertical quantities are different.

Applications / 应用与实验

仅电场力做功时，动能变化为电势能变化的负值；由场强—距离面积解释做功。

Use \(\Delta E_k=-q\Delta V\) when only electric force does work; interpret work from areas on field–distance graphs.

Common errors / 易错点

电势较高不一定电势能较高，还需看运动电荷正负。

Do not assume higher potential always means higher potential energy; the sign of the moving charge matters.

3.8.3 方法 1 · 电势、势差与能量符号 / Electric potential and energy signs

  1. \(V=\sum Q_i/(4\pi\varepsilon_0r_i)\) 为代数和，保留源电荷正负；方形中心到角的距离用半对角线。 / Add signed scalar potentials.
  2. \(\Delta E_p=q\Delta V\) 为外力缓慢移动电荷所需功；电场做功为 \(-q\Delta V\)。负电荷向较高电势移动时势能仍可降低。 / Distinguish external work from work by the field.
  3. 沿等势面移动电荷，电场功为零；不能据此说该处电场为零。 / Zero potential difference does not imply zero field.
  4. \(E_r=-dV/dr\)，\(\Delta V=-\int E_r\,dr\)。非匀强场的几何中点通常不是电势中点；用图下有符号面积比较。 / Do not assume a linear potential profile in a radial field.
  5. 电子绕质子的给定模型中，总能量包括正动能和负电势能；电离所需能量为最终零能量减初始总能量。 / Respect whether the question includes or excludes initial kinetic energy.
