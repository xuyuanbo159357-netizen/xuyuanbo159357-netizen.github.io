建模、方程与适用条件 / Model, equations and conditions

顶端和底端 / Top and bottom

质点由轻、不可伸长绳约束在竖直面转动。顶端朝下为内向：\(T_{\rm top}+mg=mv_{\rm top}^2/r\)；底端朝上为内向：\(T_{\rm bottom}-mg=mv_{\rm bottom}^2/r\)。两处速率通常不同。

For a light string in a vertical circle, weight assists the inward force at the top and opposes it at the bottom. Local speeds are normally different.

能量与临界 / Energy and critical case

绳始终拉紧、无阻力且半径固定时，\(v_{\rm bottom}^2=v_{\rm top}^2+4gr\)。恰好保持顶端绳不松的边界是 \(T_{\rm top}=0\)，故 \(v_{\rm top,min}=\sqrt{gr}\)；相应底端 \(v_{\rm bottom,min}=\sqrt{5gr}\)。这些结果必须连同条件使用。

With no losses and a taut fixed-length string, energy relates top and bottom speeds. Zero top tension gives the limiting complete-circle speeds; these are conditional results.

若绳松，约束圆轨迹失效；若改成可推可拉的刚杆，顶端临界条件不同，不能照搬 \(T\ge0\)。任意位置先投影重力径向分量，再列式。

A slack string invalidates the circular constraint. A rigid rod can push as well as pull and has different limiting conditions. Resolve weight radially at intermediate positions.
