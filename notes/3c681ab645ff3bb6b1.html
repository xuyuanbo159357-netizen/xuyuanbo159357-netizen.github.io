知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.7.1

3.7.1 万有引力定律

采用质点或球对称物体外部模型；距离为中心间距而非距表面高度。

Treat masses as point masses, or use spherically symmetric bodies viewed from outside. Use centre-to-centre separation, not height above the surface.

距离为两质量中心间距。

3.7.2

3.7.2 引力场强与场线

径向公式用于球体外部；表面附近小高度范围可近似匀强。

The radial formula applies outside a spherical body. Near a planet’s surface over small heights, the field can be approximated as uniform.

星球表面以上需将半径与高度相加；场强为矢量。

3.7.3

3.7.3 引力势与引力势能

缓慢移动或动能不变时，外力功等于势能变化；引力功符号相反。

An external-work value equals potential energy change for a slow transfer or unchanged kinetic energy; work done by gravity has the opposite sign.

无穷远规定为零势，吸引质量的引力势为负。

3.7.4

3.7.4 卫星轨道、周期与能量

采用中心质量占主导的圆轨道并忽略阻力；同步周期为地球一自转周期，通常计算近似 24 小时。

Use circular orbits around a dominant central mass and neglect resistive forces. Earth-synchronous period is one sidereal rotation, approximately 24 h for usual calculations.

较高圆轨道速率更小、周期更长，总能量较高但仍为负。
