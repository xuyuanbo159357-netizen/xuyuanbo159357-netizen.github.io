Concept / 概念

Couple / 力偶；Moment of a couple / 力偶矩

Definition / 定义

Couple / 力偶

EN: A couple consists of two forces of equal magnitude acting in opposite directions on the same body, along parallel lines of action separated by a perpendicular distance.

中: 力偶由作用于同一物体、大小相等、方向相反，且平行作用线之间存在垂直间距的两个力组成。

表达要点 / Wording: equal magnitude; opposite directions; separated lines of action

依据 / Evidence: PH01 202506 Q02 · MS p5（定义/陈述评分点）；PH01 202101 Q04.1 · MS p7（定义/陈述评分点）

Moment of a couple / 力偶矩

EN: The moment of a couple is the product of one of the forces and the perpendicular separation of their lines of action.

中: 力偶矩等于其中一个力乘以两个力作用线之间的垂直间距。

依据 / Evidence: PH01 201806 Q06.2 · MS p9（相关计算或应用）

Formula / 公式

\[M_{\rm couple}=Fd\]

Explanation / 讲解

含义与关系 / Meaning and relationships

力偶由一对大小相等、方向相反，且两者的作用线之间存在一段垂直距离的力组成。 A couple consists of two forces that are equal in magnitude, opposite in direction, and separated by a perpendicular distance.

\[M_{\text{couple}}=Fd\]

\(d\) 是两条作用线之间的垂直距离。The separation is between the two force lines, not from a chosen pivot to one force.

特征 / Properties

合力为零，因此不会单独产生质心的平移加速度；总力矩不为零，可使物体发生转动效应。The two forces cancel in translation but reinforce in rotation.

力偶矩与支点选择无关。计算两力各自对任一点的力矩并相加，可得到 \(F(d_1+d_2)=Fd\)。The net moment of the couple is independent of the point chosen.

易错：若 \(d\) 已经是两作用线的总间距，不能再写成 \(2Fd\)。Example: two 10 N forces separated by0.20 m give2.0 N m.

读图 / Reading: 等大反向力沿两条不同作用线作用，间距d是垂直距离。 / Equal opposite forces act along separated lines; d is their perpendicular separation.

D05 方法 1 · 力偶的完整判据 / Complete conditions for a couple

力偶由作用于同一物体的两等大、反向、平行但不共线的力组成，合力为零而合力矩非零。 / A couple consists of equal, opposite, parallel forces with separated lines of action.

力偶矩 \(\tau=Fd\) 的 \(d\) 为两作用线之间垂距，不能再乘二。两个同向支持力、两个大小不同的力都不构成纯力偶。只有一个力且作用线不经过质心时也可转动，但同时通常使质心加速。 / Distinguish a couple from a single off-centre force.

对应真题 / Examples： PH01 · 201806 · Q06；PH01 · 202201 · Q06；PH01 · 202506 · Q02；PH01 · 202601 · Q23。
