Concept / 概念

relative permittivity — 相对介电常量

Definition / 定义

English: The ratio of a material’s permittivity to the permittivity of free space.

中文： 材料介电常量与真空介电常量之比。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

\[\Large\displaystyle \varepsilon_r=\frac{C}{C_0}\]
