简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

Find the steepest and shallowest acceptable gradients. Take half their difference as the absolute gradient uncertainty and divide by the mean or best-fit gradient, then multiply by 100. Since h is proportional to the gradient and e has negligible uncertainty, h has the same percentage uncertainty.
