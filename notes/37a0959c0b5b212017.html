U4/07 常用物理模型

核尺寸与反平方势垒 / Closest approach

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

closest approach → radius → electron diffraction → 核尺寸与反平方势垒

推理顺序 / Reasoning

正碰用动能转静电势能；衍射用题给波长角度关系；核半径立方联系核子数。

For head-on approach convert kinetic energy to electric potential energy; for diffraction use the given wavelength/angle relation; relate radius cubed to nucleon number.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.12.1

3.12.1 核半径、最接近距离与电子衍射

最接近模型假设初始距离远、核反冲可忽略且正碰；α 未进入核时最接近距离给出核半径上限。

Closest approach assumes an initially distant alpha particle, negligible nuclear recoil and a head-on trajectory. It gives an upper limit to radius when the alpha does not enter the nucleus.

核半径约 \(10^{-15}\) 至 \(10^{-14}\) 米；正碰最接近距离由初动能转为静电势能估算。

\(R=R_0A^{1/3}\); nuclear volume \(=4\pi R^3/3\); nuclear density \(\rho\approx Am_u/(4\pi R^3/3)\). Head-on alpha closest approach: \(E_k=(2e)(Ze)/(4\pi\varepsilon_0r_{\min})\).
