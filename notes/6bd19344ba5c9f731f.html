简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

The lower-density carbon fibre gives a smaller mass and moment of inertia for the same shape. To store the same energy ½Iω², it must rotate faster. This increases the required centripetal forces and stresses, so the breaking stress must be high enough for safe operation.
