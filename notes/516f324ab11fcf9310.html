3.13.4 水电与抽水蓄能 / Hydroelectric power and pumped storage

考纲 / Specification： 3.13.4　｜　本单元知识谱系

符号沿用本节公式；若题目另有定义，以题设为准。 / These symbols follow this section; use any notation defined in the question.

重力加速度、引力场强 / gravitational acceleration, field strength \(g\) \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\) 等效单位 N kg⁻¹；按所述物理量解释 / N kg⁻¹ is equivalent; interpret the quantity in context.

↗ base-load power station — 基础负荷电站

Formula / 公式与单位

体积流率 \(Q_v\) 与电荷 Q 区分；水头模型与水流动能模型按题目条件选择。

\(P_{\mathrm{grav}}=\dot m gh=\rho Q_vgh\); stream kinetic power \(P=\frac12\rho Av^3\); \(E=mgh\); electrical output \(=\eta E\) or \(\eta P\).

Conditions / 条件与范围

水头电站用高度差和质量流率；来流动能用密度、面积、速率；重力势能转为动能后不可重复相加。

For a head-driven plant, use the height drop and mass flow; for incoming-stream kinetic power, use density, area and speed. Do not double-count the same gravitational energy after it has become kinetic energy.

Explanation / 解释

能量链与两种流动模型 / Energy chain and flow models

水库水的重力势能经水流动能传给水轮机转动，再由发电机电磁感应转为电能。给水头 \(h\) 和质量流率时 \(P_{\rm available}=\dot m gh=\rho\dot Vgh\)；\(\dot V\) 为体积流率。

Gravitational energy drives flowing water, turbine rotation and generator output. A specified head and flow rate give available gravitational power.

若题目给流速 \(v\) 和有效横截面积 \(A=\pi r^2\)，可用动能流模型 \(P=\tfrac12\rho Av^3\)。理想从水头加速有 \(v^2=2gh\)，两种表达描述同一能量转移时不能相加，避免重复计能。电输出再乘效率。

For a stated stream speed and area use kinetic power flow. When speed comes from the same gravitational head, the two formulae describe the same energy and must not be added.

基荷、备用与抽水蓄能 / Base load, backup and storage

基荷电站持续满足较稳定的最低需求；备用或调峰电站快速响应额外需求。水电启停较快，但持续输出取决于水库、水量和水头条件。

Base-load generation supplies relatively steady minimum demand; backup or peak-load stations respond to additional demand. Hydroelectric output can respond quickly but is constrained by water availability and head.

低需求时用电将水从低库抽至高库储存重力势能，高需求时放水经水轮机发电。往返效率小于100%，抽水蓄能是能量储存与调度，不是净能量来源；可用 \(E=\eta mgh\) 和放电功率求持续时间。

Pumped storage uses off-peak electricity to raise water and releases it for peak generation. Round-trip losses make it storage rather than a net energy source. Stored useful energy and output power determine duration.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.13.4

Applications / 应用与实验

评价落差、流量、库容、降水、淹没、生态、移民及调峰；分别计算抽水能量与往返效率。

Evaluate available height, flow, reservoir capacity, rainfall, flooding, habitat, population relocation and demand response. Calculate pumping energy and round-trip efficiency separately.

Common errors / 易错点

抽水蓄能用于储能，完整循环不产生净能量；功率与储能单位不同。

Pumped storage stores energy; it is not a net energy source over a complete cycle. Power and stored energy use different units.

3.13.4 方法 1 · 水电流量、储能与电网调峰 / Hydro flow, storage and grid balancing

  1. 水头 \(h\) 由水面到涡轮的垂直落差求，不能只取出水口高度；\(P_{grav}=\dot mgh=\rho\dot Vgh\)。给电输出时先除发电效率。 / Identify the energy drop and the power stage.
  2. 水位改变需用平均水头或积分求总能量；总储水可受上下水库可用容积共同限制。 / Match available mass to the limiting reservoir.
  3. 反复抽水发电的总效率为抽水效率乘发电效率，小于任一阶段效率；系统储能调峰而不净创造能量。 / Distinguish round-trip efficiency from generation efficiency.
  4. 有符号功率—时间图，正面积减负面积得净电能；容量除供需缺口功率得支撑时间，不是除全镇总需求。 / Use the deficit and consistent energy/power units.
  5. 论述按“工作原理 → 用途 → 功率限制”：低谷抽水、高峰发电，短启动时间补需求；功率受水头、流量、涡轮与发电机能力和损耗影响。 / Link operation to the grid need.
  6. 基荷需持续可用，备用需快速响应；径流或水库水电能否供基荷取决于水流可靠性、库容和补水，不能仅凭能源名称判断。 / Apply reliability conditions to the actual scheme.
  7. 若题给流速与转子面积模型，可用 \(\dot m=\rho Av\)、\(P=\dot mv^2/2\)；它与以水头估能量是不同的已给条件。 / Use the model specified for the turbine flow.
