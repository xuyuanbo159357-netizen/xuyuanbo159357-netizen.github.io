1. 水流的势能、动能与功率 / Potential energy, kinetic energy and power in water flow

1.1 过程、现象与问题 / Process, observations and questions

能量链与两种流动模型 / Energy chain and flow models

水库水的重力势能经水流动能传给水轮机转动，再由发电机电磁感应转为电能。给水头 \(h\) 和质量流率时 \(P_{\rm available}=\dot m gh=\rho\dot Vgh\)；\(\dot V\) 为体积流率。

Gravitational energy drives flowing water, turbine rotation and generator output. A specified head and flow rate give available gravitational power.

若题目给流速 \(v\) 和有效横截面积 \(A=\pi r^2\)，可用动能流模型 \(P=\frac12\rho Av^3\)。理想从水头加速有 \(v^2=2gh\)，两种表达描述同一能量转移时不能相加，避免重复计能。电输出再乘效率。

For a stated stream speed and area use kinetic power flow. When speed comes from the same gravitational head, the two formulae describe the same energy and must not be added.

水电的能量链 / Hydroelectric energy chain

读图 / Reading： 水头高度由有效上、下水位决定；重力势能经水流、涡轮再到发电机，输出需考虑效率及剩余水流能量。

Effective head is set by upstream/downstream levels; gravitational energy passes through flow, turbine and generator, with losses and remaining flow energy.

1.4 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

适用范围与条件 / Scope and conditions

Conditions / 适用条件： Q_v 为体积流率，不能当作电荷；水头势能模型与水流动能模型按题意选择，输出须考虑效率。 / Q_v is volume flow rate, not charge; choose the head-energy or stream-kinetic model as stated and include efficiency for output.

Conditions / 条件与范围

水头电站用高度差和质量流率；来流动能用密度、面积、速率；重力势能转为动能后不可重复相加。

For a head-driven plant, use the height drop and mass flow; for incoming-stream kinetic power, use density, area and speed. Do not double-count the same gravitational energy after it has become kinetic energy.

2. 基荷、备用与抽水蓄能 / Base load, backup and pumped storage

2.1 过程、现象与问题 / Process, observations and questions

基荷、备用与抽水蓄能 / Base load, backup and storage

基荷电站持续满足较稳定的最低需求；备用或调峰电站快速响应额外需求。水电启停较快，但持续输出取决于水库、水量和水头条件。

Base-load generation supplies relatively steady minimum demand; backup or peak-load stations respond to additional demand. Hydroelectric output can respond quickly but is constrained by water availability and head.

低需求时用电将水从低库抽至高库储存重力势能，高需求时放水经水轮机发电。往返效率小于100%，抽水蓄能是能量储存与调度，不是净能量来源；可用 \(E=\eta mgh\) 和放电功率求持续时间。

Pumped storage uses off-peak electricity to raise water and releases it for peak generation. Round-trip losses make it storage rather than a net energy source. Stored useful energy and output power determine duration.

Common errors / 易错点

抽水蓄能用于储能，完整循环不产生净能量；功率与储能单位不同。

Pumped storage stores energy; it is not a net energy source over a complete cycle. Power and stored energy use different units.

考点精讲：抽水蓄能按两个工况讲解 / Explain both pumped-storage operating modes

供电超过需求时，用富余电能驱动水泵把水提升到高水库；高需求时放水，经水轮机和发电机补充电力。它储存并调配能量，不是额外创造能量。快速启动可应对需求变化，并让基础负荷电站保持稳定运行。输出还受水头、流量、设备尺寸与效率限制。

When supply exceeds demand, surplus electricity pumps water uphill. At high demand, water returns through turbines and generators. The scheme stores and shifts energy rather than creating extra energy. Rapid start-up meets changing demand and lets base-load stations run steadily. Head, flow, equipment size and efficiency limit output.

PH04-202601-Q07 · Pumped-storage power system / 抽水蓄能电力系统

PH04-202601-Q07 评分点与讲解 / Marking points and explanation

考查内容 / Focus： Pumped-storage power system / 抽水蓄能电力系统

物理解释与方法 / Explanation and method： 返回本考点的规律、条件与解释 / Physical explanation

简明英文表述 / Simple English response

Water is stored in high and low reservoirs. When supply exceeds demand, a motor drives pumps to move water uphill. When demand is high, water flows down through turbines and generators to provide electricity. This lets base-load stations run continuously and stores their surplus energy; rapid start-up meets sudden demand and can reduce operating costs. The available power depends on the water flow and speed. For a water jet, P = ½ρπr²v³, where ρ is water density, r is the jet's effective radius and v is water speed. Greater reservoir height gives more gravitational energy per kilogram and can produce a higher speed. Turbine size limits the flow, and friction and resistive heating reduce useful electrical power.

3.13.4 方法 1 · 水电流量、储能与电网调峰 / Hydro flow, storage and grid balancing

  1. 水头 \(h\) 由水面到涡轮的垂直落差求，不能只取出水口高度；\(P_{grav}=\dot mgh=\rho\dot Vgh\)。给电输出时先除发电效率。 / Identify the energy drop and the power stage.
  2. 水位改变需用平均水头或积分求总能量；总储水可受上下水库可用容积共同限制。 / Match available mass to the limiting reservoir.
  3. 反复抽水发电的总效率为抽水效率乘发电效率，小于任一阶段效率；系统储能调峰而不净创造能量。 / Distinguish round-trip efficiency from generation efficiency.
  4. 有符号功率—时间图，正面积减负面积得净电能；容量除供需缺口功率得支撑时间，不是除全镇总需求。 / Use the deficit and consistent energy/power units.
  5. 论述按“工作原理 → 用途 → 功率限制”：低谷抽水、高峰发电，短启动时间补需求；功率受水头、流量、涡轮与发电机能力和损耗影响。 / Link operation to the grid need.
  6. 基荷需持续可用，备用需快速响应；径流或水库水电能否供基荷取决于水流可靠性、库容和补水，不能仅凭能源名称判断。 / Apply reliability conditions to the actual scheme.
  7. 若题给流速与转子面积模型，可用 \(\dot m=\rho Av\)、\(P=\dot mv^2/2\)；它与以水头估能量是不同的已给条件。 / Use the model specified for the turbine flow.
