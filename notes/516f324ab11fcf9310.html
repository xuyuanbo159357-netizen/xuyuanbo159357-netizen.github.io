1. 水流的势能、动能与功率 / Potential energy, kinetic energy and power in water flow

1.1 过程、现象与问题 / Process, observations and questions

能量链与两种流动模型 / Energy chain and flow models

水库水的重力势能经水流动能传给水轮机转动，再由发电机电磁感应转为电能。给水头 \(h\) 和质量流率时 \(P_{\rm available}=\dot m gh=\rho\dot Vgh\)；\(\dot V\) 为体积流率。

Gravitational energy drives flowing water, turbine rotation and generator output. A specified head and flow rate give available gravitational power.

若题目给流速 \(v\) 和有效横截面积 \(A=\pi r^2\)，可用动能流模型 \(P=\frac12\rho Av^3\)。理想从水头加速有 \(v^2=2gh\)，两种表达描述同一能量转移时不能相加，避免重复计能。电输出再乘效率。

For a stated stream speed and area use kinetic power flow. When speed comes from the same gravitational head, the two formulae describe the same energy and must not be added.

水电的能量链 / Hydroelectric energy chain

读图 / Reading： 水头高度由有效上、下水位决定；重力势能经水流、涡轮再到发电机，输出需考虑效率及剩余水流能量。

Effective head is set by upstream/downstream levels; gravitational energy passes through flow, turbine and generator, with losses and remaining flow energy.

1.4 内容解析与细节 / Explanation and details

适用范围与条件 / Scope and conditions

Conditions / 适用条件： Q_v 为体积流率，不能当作电荷；水头势能模型与水流动能模型按题意选择，输出须考虑效率。 / Q_v is volume flow rate, not charge; choose the head-energy or stream-kinetic model as stated and include efficiency for output.

Conditions / 条件与范围

水头电站用高度差和质量流率；来流动能用密度、面积、速率；重力势能转为动能后不可重复相加。

For a head-driven plant, use the height drop and mass flow; for incoming-stream kinetic power, use density, area and speed. Do not double-count the same gravitational energy after it has become kinetic energy.

2. 基荷、备用与抽水蓄能 / Base load, backup and pumped storage

2.1 过程、现象与问题 / Process, observations and questions

基荷、备用与抽水蓄能 / Base load, backup and storage

以下按 PH04 202601 Q07 评分标准的 A 运行原理 → B 使用原因 → C 最大功率 展开。 / The explanation follows the three areas in the PH04 202601 Q07 mark scheme: A operation → B purpose → C maximum power.

A. 运行原理 / Principles of operation

装置 / Arrangement： 系统有一个高水库 / high reservoir和一个低水库 / low reservoir，由管道及水轮机—发电机组连接。上下水库的水位差提供可储存和释放的重力势能。

The system has a high reservoir and a low reservoir, connected through pipes and a turbine–generator unit. Their vertical separation allows gravitational potential energy to be stored and released.

结合图像 / Reading the diagram： 沿水流方向区分“低库 → 高库”的抽水阶段与“高库 → 低库”的发电阶段；两阶段的能量转移方向相反。 / Trace the uphill pumping stage and the downhill generating stage separately; the direction of energy transfer is reversed.

低需求：向上抽水 / Low demand: pumping uphill。 电力需求较低、供电超过需求时，用富余电能让发电机作为电动机 / generator acts as a motor，驱动可逆水轮机作为水泵工作，把水从低水库抽到高水库。电能转为水的重力势能并储存。

When demand is low and supply exceeds demand, surplus electricity makes the generator act as a motor. It drives the reversible turbine as a pump, moving water from the low reservoir to the high reservoir. Electrical energy is transferred to stored gravitational potential energy.

高需求：向下放水发电 / High demand: generating downhill。 需求较高时，水从高水库向下流入低水库，驱动水轮机和发电机 / turbine and generator向电网供电。水的重力势能经水流动能、转动机械能转为电能。

At high demand, water flows down from the high reservoir to the low reservoir. It drives the turbine and generator to supply electricity. Gravitational potential energy is transferred through water motion and turbine rotation to electrical energy.

B. 为什么使用抽水蓄能 / Why pumped storage is used

  1. 让基础负荷电站持续运行 / Keep base-load stations running。 基础负荷发电机持续运行 / base-load generators run continuously，不必随短时间的需求变化反复启停；低需求时的富余电能可用于抽水储能。

    Base-load generators can run continuously rather than repeatedly starting and stopping as demand changes. Surplus electricity at low demand is used for pumping.

  2. 供电过剩时储能 / Store energy when supply exceeds demand。 当supply exceeds demand / 供电超过需求，系统消耗电能驱动电动机和水泵，把多余供能转为高水库中水的重力势能。

    When supply exceeds demand, electrical energy drives the motor and pump, storing the surplus as gravitational potential energy.

  3. 供电不足时补充电力 / Supply the shortfall when demand exceeds supply。 当demand exceeds supply / 需求超过供电，系统放水发电，补足其他电站不能满足的功率缺口。

    When demand exceeds supply, the system releases water and generates electricity to supply the shortfall.

  4. 提高电力系统的运行效率、降低成本 / Improve system operation and reduce cost。 储存低需求时的富余电能、减少基础负荷电站低效变负荷或反复启停，可以提高整体运行效率或降低成本 / improve overall operating efficiency or reduce cost。这里说的是电力系统的运行安排，不是抽水—发电循环能够增加能量。

    Storing surplus off-peak energy and reducing inefficient changes or repeated start-ups at base-load stations can improve overall operating efficiency or reduce cost. This refers to operation of the electricity system, not an increase in energy during storage.

  5. 快速应对需求突增 / Respond rapidly to demand surges。 抽水蓄能的启动时间短 / quick start-up time，可在需求突然上升时迅速增加发电功率，作为调峰或备用电源。

    Its quick start-up time allows a rapid increase in output when demand suddenly rises, providing peak-load or backup power.

重要区分 / Important distinction： 抽水蓄能是能量储存与调配 / energy storage and redistribution，不是净能量来源。实际发电回收的电能小于此前抽水消耗的电能；“发电”不能解释为“创造能量”。

Pumped storage provides energy storage and redistribution, not a net energy source. Electrical energy recovered during generation is less than that used for pumping; generation does not mean creating energy.

C. 最大功率取决于什么 / What determines maximum power

C1. 水流动能通量 / Kinetic power of the water flow

按本题采用的均匀水流模型，水以速率 \(v\) 流过有效圆形截面。每秒通过的水量随截面积和流速增大，每千克水的动能随速率平方增大，因此：

In the uniform-flow model used in this question, water passes through an effective circular area at speed \(v\). Mass passing per second increases with area and speed, and kinetic energy per kilogram increases with speed squared. Therefore:

\[\Large\displaystyle P_{\mathrm{flow}}=\frac{1}{2}\rho\pi r^{2}v^{3}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量 / Quantity SI 单位 / SI unit
\(P_{\mathrm{flow}}\) 水流携带的动能功率 / kinetic power carried by the water \(\mathrm{W}\)
\(\rho\) 水的密度 / water density \(\mathrm{kg\,m^{-3}}\)
\(r\) 有效圆形水流截面的半径 / radius of the effective circular flow area \(\mathrm{m}\)
\(v\) 该截面处的水流速率 / water speed at this cross-section \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)
\(\pi\) 圆周率 / pi 无量纲 / dimensionless

规律与限制 / Meaning and limits： 在其他量不变时，水流可用功率与半径平方 / radius squared和流速立方 / speed cubed成正比。本题把水轮机尺寸与有效水流面积联系起来；实际系统还受管道、导流装置、流量及发电机容量限制，不能仅凭半径断定实际电输出。

With other quantities fixed, available flow power is proportional to radius squared and speed cubed. In this model, turbine size is linked to the effective flow area. Pipes, flow control, flow rate and generator capacity also limit real electrical output.

原答案记号注意 / Notation in the original scheme： 评分表把右侧这一式写成 \(E\)，但其量纲为功率，单位是瓦特；此处规范记作 \(P_{\mathrm{flow}}\)。它表示每秒传递的动能，不是总能量。

The original scheme labels this expression \(E\), but its dimensions are power and its unit is the watt. It is written here as \(P_{\mathrm{flow}}\): kinetic energy transferred per second, not total energy.

C2. 上下水库的垂直高度差 / Vertical separation of the reservoirs

垂直高度差 / vertical separation越大，每千克水下降时可释放的重力势能越大。若忽略损耗，并把这部分势能全部转为所考虑水流的动能，则能量守恒给出：

A greater vertical separation gives more gravitational potential energy per kilogram of water. If losses are negligible and this energy is fully converted into the kinetic energy of the flow being considered, energy conservation gives:

\[\Large\displaystyle v=\sqrt{2gh}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量 / Quantity SI 单位 / SI unit
\(v\) 理想情况下由水头获得的水流速率 / ideal water speed produced by the head \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\)
\(g\) 重力加速度 / gravitational acceleration \(\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\)
\(h\) 有效水头，即有关水位的垂直高度差 / effective head, the relevant vertical water-level difference \(\mathrm{m}\)

规律与解释 / Meaning and explanation： 在上述条件下，水头越大，水流可达到的速率越大；由 C1 的流速立方关系，可用功率也增大。实际水电站不能不加条件地把此理想速率关系用于任意管内位置；应按题给水头、流量、压力及损耗条件选模型。

Under these assumptions, a greater head gives a greater possible speed and hence greater available power through the speed-cubed relationship in C1. Do not apply this ideal speed relation at an arbitrary point in a real pipe without checking the stated head, flow, pressure and losses.

C3. 水轮机半径与通过的水量 / Turbine radius and water throughput

较大的水轮机半径 / turbine radius通常允许更大的有效水流截面积。在流速相同、有效截面随水轮机半径变化的简化模型中，半径越大，每秒通过的水质量越大，因此可传递的功率越大。答题时要把“尺寸较大”与“面积、流率、功率”的因果关系说清楚。

A greater turbine radius can allow a larger effective flow area. In the simplified model, at the same speed, this allows more water mass to pass each second and therefore greater power transfer. Link size to area, flow rate and power rather than just stating that the turbine is larger.

C4. 实际效率与能量损耗 / Real efficiency and energy losses

水流和管道摩擦、水轮机机械摩擦，以及发电机和导线中的电阻发热 / resistive heating等，使部分能量转为内能，所以实际电输出小于水流提供的功率。

Water–pipe friction, mechanical friction in the turbine and resistive heating in the generator and conductors transfer some energy to internal energy. Useful electrical output is therefore less than the available water-flow power.

\[\Large\displaystyle P_{\mathrm{electric}}=\eta P_{\mathrm{flow}}\]

符号对应表 / Symbols and SI units

符号 / Symbol 物理量 / Quantity SI 单位 / SI unit
\(P_{\mathrm{electric}}\) 有用电输出功率 / useful electrical output power \(\mathrm{W}\)
\(\eta\) 从所定义水流输入到电输出的效率 / efficiency from the defined water-flow input to electrical output 无量纲 / dimensionless
\(P_{\mathrm{flow}}\) 同一阶段定义的水流输入功率 / water-flow input power for the same stage \(\mathrm{W}\)

规律与条件 / Meaning and conditions： 有用输出等于该阶段输入乘效率，实际效率小于 1。效率必须与公式采用的输入阶段对应；已计入流动损失的输入不要再次扣除同一损失。

Useful output equals input power multiplied by the efficiency for that stage; real efficiency is less than one. Match efficiency to the chosen input stage and do not count the same loss twice.

Common errors / 易错点

抽水蓄能用于储能，完整循环不产生净能量；功率与储能单位不同。

Pumped storage stores energy; it is not a net energy source over a complete cycle. Power and stored energy use different units.

物理解释与方法 / Explanation and method： 返回本考点的规律、条件与解释 / Physical explanation

简明英文表述 / Simple English response

Water is stored in high and low reservoirs. When supply exceeds demand, a motor drives pumps to move water uphill. When demand is high, water flows down through turbines and generators to provide electricity. This lets base-load stations run continuously and stores their surplus energy; rapid start-up meets sudden demand and can reduce operating costs. The available power depends on the water flow and speed. For a water jet, P = ½ρπr²v³, where ρ is water density, r is the jet's effective radius and v is water speed. Greater reservoir height gives more gravitational energy per kilogram and can produce a higher speed. Turbine size limits the flow, and friction and resistive heating reduce useful electrical power.

3.13.4 方法 1 · 水电流量、储能与电网调峰 / Hydro flow, storage and grid balancing

  1. 水头 \(h\) 由水面到涡轮的垂直落差求，不能只取出水口高度；\(P_{grav}=\dot mgh=\rho\dot Vgh\)。给电输出时先除发电效率。 / Identify the energy drop and the power stage.
  2. 水位改变需用平均水头或积分求总能量；总储水可受上下水库可用容积共同限制。 / Match available mass to the limiting reservoir.
  3. 反复抽水发电的总效率为抽水效率乘发电效率，小于任一阶段效率；系统储能调峰而不净创造能量。 / Distinguish round-trip efficiency from generation efficiency.
  4. 有符号功率—时间图，正面积减负面积得净电能；容量除供需缺口功率得支撑时间，不是除全镇总需求。 / Use the deficit and consistent energy/power units.
  5. 论述按“工作原理 → 用途 → 功率限制”：低谷抽水、高峰发电，短启动时间补需求；功率受水头、流量、涡轮与发电机能力和损耗影响。 / Link operation to the grid need.
  6. 基荷需持续可用，备用需快速响应；径流或水库水电能否供基荷取决于水流可靠性、库容和补水，不能仅凭能源名称判断。 / Apply reliability conditions to the actual scheme.
  7. 若题给流速与转子面积模型，可用 \(\dot m=\rho Av\)、\(P=\dot mv^2/2\)；它与以水头估能量是不同的已给条件。 / Use the model specified for the turbine flow.
