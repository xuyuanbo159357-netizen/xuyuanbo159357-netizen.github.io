简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

Water is stored in high and low reservoirs. When supply exceeds demand, a motor drives pumps to move water uphill. When demand is high, water flows down through turbines and generators to provide electricity. This lets base-load stations run continuously and stores their surplus energy; rapid start-up meets sudden demand and can reduce operating costs. The available power depends on the water flow and speed. For a water jet, P = ½ρπr²v³, where ρ is water density, r is the jet's effective radius and v is water speed. Greater reservoir height gives more gravitational energy per kilogram and can produce a higher speed. Turbine size limits the flow, and friction and resistive heating reduce useful electrical power.
