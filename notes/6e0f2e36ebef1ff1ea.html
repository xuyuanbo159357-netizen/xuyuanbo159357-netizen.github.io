Concept / 概念

critical angle — 临界角

Definition / 定义

English: The angle of incidence in the higher-index medium for which the refracted ray travels along the boundary.

中文： 光从高折射率介质射向边界时，使折射光沿界面传播的入射角。

定义评分依据 / Definition marking points： PH02 202001-Q02。原答案在题目下展开；此处按评分要点概括。 Summarised from the marking points; originals are folded below each question.

Formula / 公式与单位

Formula / 公式

\[\Large\displaystyle \sin\theta_c=\frac{n_2}{n_1}\]

Symbols and SI units / 符号与 SI 单位

符号 / Symbol 物理量 / Quantity SI 单位 / SI unit

\(n_1,n_2\) 入射侧与折射侧折射率 / incident and transmitted refractive indices 无量纲 / dimensionless

Conditions / 条件与说明： 要求 n₁ > n₂；临界状态折射角为 90°。 / Requires n₁ > n₂; the refracted angle is 90° at the critical angle.

图示讲解 / Diagram explained

图示：临界状态 / Critical incidence

读图 / Reading： 折射光沿界面传播时，折射角为 90°、入射角才是临界角；同时仍可有部分反射光。

At the critical angle the refracted ray travels along the boundary at 90° to the normal; a reflected ray can also remain.
