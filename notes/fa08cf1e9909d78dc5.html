简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

Put five cells in series to give 5 × 0.72 = 3.6 V. One such string has internal resistance 5 × 2.2 = 11 Ω. Connect two identical strings in parallel to halve this to 5.5 Ω. Ten cells are needed in total.
