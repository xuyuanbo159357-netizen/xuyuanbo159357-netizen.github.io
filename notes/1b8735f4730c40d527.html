建模、方程与适用条件 / Model, equations and conditions

无摩擦设计模型 / Frictionless design model

路面向圆心一侧降低、倾角 \(\theta\) 从水平量起，支持力与竖直夹角同为 \(\theta\)。忽略侧向摩擦时，\(N\cos\theta=mg\)、\(N\sin\theta=mv^2/r\)，得 \(v=\sqrt{rg\tan\theta}\)。

For a banked surface, normal reaction has a vertical component balancing weight and a horizontal inward component providing circular acceleration. The design-speed formula assumes negligible lateral friction.

这里“设计速度”是无需侧向摩擦的速度，不是任何有摩擦道路的绝对最高速度。若题目要求其他速度，摩擦可能沿斜面向上或向下，需重新按趋势画图并分解实际力。

Design speed is the speed needing no lateral friction, not the absolute maximum on a rough road. At other speeds determine friction direction from the tendency to slip and resolve forces again.

与圆锥摆联系 / Link to the conical pendulum

二者都由一个斜力的竖直分量平衡重力、水平分量提供向心力，所以同样出现 \(\tan\theta=v^2/(rg)\)，但实际力分别为支持力和张力。

The same component ratio arises in a conical pendulum, although the real force is tension rather than normal reaction.
