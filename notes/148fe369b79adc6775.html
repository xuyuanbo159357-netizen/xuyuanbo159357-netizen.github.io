3.5.8 折射、全反射与光纤 / Refraction at a plane surface

Definition / 定义

↗ refractive index — 折射率

Definition / 定义

English: The ratio of the speed of light in vacuum to its speed in the medium.

中文： 真空光速与介质中光速的比值。

↗ critical angle — 临界角

Definition / 定义

English: The angle of incidence in the higher-index medium for which the refracted ray travels along the boundary.

中文： 光从高折射率介质射向边界时，使折射光沿界面传播的入射角。

↗ total internal reflection — 全反射

Definition / 定义

English: Complete reflection at a boundary when light in the higher-index medium is incident at an angle greater than the critical angle.

中文： 光在高折射率介质中以大于临界角入射边界时发生的完全反射。

↗ material dispersion — 材料色散

Definition / 定义

English: Pulse broadening because different wavelengths travel at different speeds in a material.

中文： 不同波长在材料中速率不同而导致的脉冲展宽。

↗ modal dispersion — 模式色散

Definition / 定义

English: Pulse broadening because rays or modes travel along paths of different lengths in an optical fibre.

中文： 光纤内不同光路或传播模式路程不同而导致的脉冲展宽。

Formula / 公式与单位

空气折射率近似为一；角度均从法线量起。

\(n=c/c_{\mathrm{s}}\); \(n_1\sin\theta_1=n_2\sin\theta_2\); \(\sin\theta_c=n_2/n_1\) for \(n_1>n_2\).

Conditions / 条件与范围

全反射需从高折射率向低折射率传播且入射角大于临界角；光纤限阶跃型。

Total internal reflection needs both high-to-low refractive index propagation and incidence above the critical angle. Fibres are limited to step-index types.

Explanation / 解释

折射率与法线 / Refractive index and normals

\(n=c/c_s\)，空气 \(n\approx1\)。光过静止界面时频率不变；在更大折射率介质中速率和波长更小。斯涅尔定律 \(n_1\sin\theta_1=n_2\sin\theta_2\) 的两个角均从法线量起。

Frequency is unchanged across a stationary interface; speed and wavelength decrease in a higher-index medium. Both angles in Snell's law are measured from the normal.

从低折射率进入高折射率介质时向法线偏折，反之远离法线；正入射时方向不变，但速率和波长仍可改变。

Light bends towards the normal on entering a higher-index medium and away on leaving it. At normal incidence direction is unchanged, although speed and wavelength may change.

临界角与阶跃光纤 / Critical angle and step-index fibres

全反射同时需要 \(n_1>n_2\) 和 \(\theta_i>\theta_c\)。临界角处折射角为 \(90^\circ\)，由斯涅尔定律得到 \(\sin\theta_c=n_2/n_1\)；不是反正切关系。

Total internal reflection requires travel from higher to lower index and incidence above the critical angle. Snell's law gives \(\sin\theta_c=n_2/n_1\), not an inverse-tangent expression.

阶跃光纤的纤芯折射率高于包层；合适光线在芯—包层边界多次全反射。包层维持可控边界并保护芯、减少相邻光纤串扰；弯曲过紧会破坏全反射条件。

A step-index fibre has a higher-index core than cladding. Suitable rays undergo repeated total internal reflection. Cladding maintains the boundary, protects the core and reduces cross-talk; excessive bending can permit leakage.

两类色散、吸收及后果 / Dispersion and absorption

模式色散：不同传播路径长度不同，抵达时间不同；细纤芯、限制入射角范围可减小。材料色散：折射率和波速随波长变化，多波长脉冲抵达时间不同；窄光谱光源可减小。

Modal dispersion comes from different path lengths; a narrow core or restricted ray angles reduces it. Material dispersion comes from wavelength-dependent refractive index and speed; a narrow spectral source reduces it.

两种色散都导致脉冲展宽，相邻脉冲可重叠而限制数据率。吸收把光能转移到材料，使幅度和强度减小、信噪比变差；吸收不是脉冲展宽的同义词。

Both forms of dispersion broaden pulses and can limit data rate through overlap. Absorption transfers light energy to the material and reduces amplitude and intensity; it is different from dispersion.

Applications / 应用与实验

先画法线并标角，再判断全反射条件，解释光纤中的脉冲失真。

Draw the normal, label incidence/refraction angles, decide whether total internal reflection is possible, and explain fibre pulse distortion.

Common errors / 易错点

临界角处仍有沿界面折射；超过才全反射；色散与吸收影响不同。

At the critical angle refraction is along the surface; total internal reflection occurs above it. Dispersion and absorption have different effects.

3.5.8 方法 1 · 逐界面折射与临界条件 / Refraction at successive interfaces

  1. 每个界面重新画法线；与表面的夹角先取余角。 / Use angles to the local normal.
  2. 写 \(n_1\sin i=n_2\sin r\)；全反射需要 \(n_1>n_2\) 且 \(i>c\)，\(i=c\) 为折射角90°的临界状态。 / State both conditions for total internal reflection.
  3. 平行界面可连续使用 \(n\sin\theta\) 不变，弯曲界面则重新确定局部法线。水波速度形式是 \(v_1/v_2=\sin i/\sin r\)。 / Keep the speed ratio consistent with the angle ratio.

对应真题 / Examples： PH02 · 201901 · Q08；PH02 · 202206 · Q03；PH02 · 202501 · Q11；PH02 · 202506 · Q03。

3.5.8 方法 2 · 光纤光程、时延与脉冲 / Fibre paths, delay and pulses

  1. 光线与轴夹角为 \(\alpha\)，长度 \(L\) 的光纤中路径 \(L/\cos\alpha\)；与直达光的时延 \(\Delta t=(L/v)(\sec\alpha-1)\)。临界时 \(\cos\alpha=\sin c\)。 / Distinguish the axial angle from the incidence angle to the normal.
  2. 输出脉宽约为输入脉宽加路径时延展宽；重复周期不变，脉冲重叠限制传输速率。 / Broadening is not a change in pulse repetition period.
  3. 与空气包围相比，包层提高临界角并筛掉较大轴向角的光路，可减小模态展宽，也可能使脉冲幅值降低。比较不同芯包组合时先保证 \(n_c>n_{cl}\)，再比较比值 \(n_{cl}/n_c\)。 / Do not infer reduced dispersion from the absolute index gap alone.
  4. 液面或V槽传感器逐段追踪折射、反射后是否到达探测器，不能只写“全反射越多越亮”。 / Trace the actual detected ray.

对应真题 / Examples： PH02 · 202406 · Q08；PH02 · 202606 · Q09；PH02 · 202401 · Q17；PH02 · 202306 · Q11。
