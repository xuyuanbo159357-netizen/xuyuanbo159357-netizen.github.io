U4 计算与证明 / Calculations and show-that questions

U4 答题规范 / Exam answering conventions · 返回真题练习 / Past-paper practice

阅读方式 / How to use

本页区分物理原则、推荐书写及具体试题的评分要求；具体容差和扣分范围只用于所引题目。 / Distinguish physical principles, presentation advice and question-specific marking.

1. 热量和功使用一致符号 / Use a consistent first-law convention

本课程常用 \(\Delta U=Q+W\)：\(Q\) 为传入系统的热量，\(W\) 为外界对系统所做的功。若采用“气体对外做功”的符号，必须改写相应减号并保持一致。 / With \(\Delta U=Q+W\), take heat entering the system and work done on it as positive.

2020 January Q01.4：氦气膨胀对外做功，同时向环境放热；该约定下 \(Q<0\)、\(W<0\)，内能降低。不能因看见“does work”就自动写 \(W>0\)。 / Both terms are negative for this expanding, cooling gas. PH04 2020 January Q01.4 · MS PDF p4 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

2. 热传导和气体 / Conduction and gases

  • 稳态串联层各层热流率相同，温差不一定相同；使用 \(P=kA\Delta T/L\)，不是把 \(22^\circ\mathrm C\) 或 \(95^\circ\mathrm C\) 本身当温差。 / Equal steady heat flow does not imply equal temperature drops. PH04 2026 June Q01.3 · MS PDF p5 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer；PH04 2026 June Q01.4 · MS PDF p6 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer
  • 明确系统是否密闭以及物质的量是否不变；漏气题要分别求初末 \(n\)，不可直接套恒定 \(n\) 的比例式。 / Check whether the amount of gas changes. PH04 2026 June Q02.1 · MS PDF p7 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer
  • 用 \(\tfrac12m\langle c^2\rangle=\tfrac32kT\) 时，\(m\) 是一个分子的质量；求方均根速率要开平方。 / Use molecular mass and distinguish mean-square speed from rms speed. PH04 2026 June Q02.3 · MS PDF p8 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

3. 结合能和质量亏损 / Binding energy and mass defect

先辨认题给的是总结合能还是每核子结合能，后者需乘核子数 \(A\)；能量释放按产物总结合能减反应物总结合能，并统一 MeV、J 和质量单位。 / Convert binding energy per nucleon to total binding energy before calculating the release or mass equivalent.

2026 June Q04.2 先计算各核总结合能，再算能量差与质量亏损；不能直接用两项“每核子数值”相减就当一次反应的总能量。 / The per-nucleon values alone are not the total reaction energies. PH04 2026 June Q04.2 · MS PDF p12 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

4. 转动与滚动 / Rotation and rolling

角运动量用弧度；不滑动滚动才可用 \(v=\omega r\)、\(s=r\theta\)。能量收支同时包含 \(\tfrac12mv^2\) 与 \(\tfrac12I\omega^2\)，不能把全部重力势能损失都当平动动能。 / Apply rolling constraints only when justified and include both kinetic-energy terms. PH04 2026 June Q05.4 · MS PDF p15 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer；PH04 2026 June Q05.6 · MS PDF p16 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

外力矩合量可忽略时才用角动量守恒；质量相同不能推出转动惯量相同，还要看质量距轴的分布。 / Check the external torque and the mass distribution about the axis.

5. 证明题和能源量纲 / Show-that and energy-flow calculations

2018 June Q04.3 要证明路程约 \(3.9\ \mathrm m\)。一种计算链为 \(\theta=\tfrac12\alpha t^2=28.125\ \mathrm{rad}\)，\(s=r\theta=3.9375\ \mathrm m\approx3.94\ \mathrm m\)，从而支持 \(3.9\ \mathrm m\)。该题末分要求看到更精确结果，不能只重抄目标值。 / Show a sufficiently precise calculated value before confirming the stated approximation. PH04 2018 June Q04.3 · MS PDF p9 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

风能证明先写体积流率 \(Av\)、质量流率 \(\rho Av\)，再用动能每秒得到 \(P=\tfrac12\rho Av^3\)。区分速率 \(v\)、体积 \(V\) 和流率；新符号必须定义。2026 June Q06.1 明确要求 \(v\) 与 \(V\) 可辨认，若以 \(V\) 表示体积流率必须定义。 / Define the flow quantities and keep velocity and volume symbols distinct. PH04 2026 June Q06.1 · MS PDF p17 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

计算后自检 / Calculation check

热量与功方向？温度还是温差？密闭还是漏气？每核子还是总结合能？半径还是直径？平动与转动是否齐全？功率还是能量或流率？ / Signs? Temperature or temperature difference? Fixed gas amount? Per-nucleon or total energy? Radius or diameter? Both kinetic-energy terms? Power, energy or flow rate?

同一大题的数据沿用 / Carrying answers into later parts

课堂统一答题规范 / Classroom convention

同一大题内，后续小问用到前面小问的答案数据时，直接使用前问已写出的答案数值，不改用计算器内保留的更精确值。前问若为证明题，后续直接使用题目要求证明的数值。

Within the same question, use the numerical answer reported in the earlier part when it is required in a later part, rather than a more precise calculator value. If the earlier part is a show-that question, use the value stated in that question for later parts.

区分两个阶段：同一小问内部保留中间精度；跨小问沿用已报告的答案或题给证明值。 / Within one part, retain intermediate precision; between parts, carry forward the reported answer or the stated show-that value.

这是本知识库采用的课堂统一约定；原评分标准有时还接受其他跟进路线，不将本约定表述成考局所有题目的唯一评分规则。若题目另给指定数据，以该明确指令为准。 / This is the classroom convention used in this vault. Some mark schemes also accept other follow-through routes; it is not presented as the board's sole permitted method. Follow any explicit value separately specified in the question.

U4 例证 / Unit-specific example

2018 June Q04.3 要证明路程约 \(3.9\ \mathrm m\)；Q04.4 直接用 \(3.9\ \mathrm m\) 求高度和势能变化，不改用 \(3.9375\ \mathrm m\)。评分标准明确接受由 \(3.9\ \mathrm m\) 得到的约 \(34.4\ \mathrm J\) 路线。 / Carry the stated show-that distance into the next part; this route is explicitly accepted.
