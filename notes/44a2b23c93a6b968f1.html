关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

threshold → work function → intensity → stopping potential → 光电效应强度与频率

推理顺序 / Reasoning

比较光子能量与逸出功，区分单光子能量和光子到达率，再求最大动能。

Compare photon energy with work function; separate energy per photon from photon arrival rate; use the maximum kinetic energy relation.

3.5.10 光子与光电效应

不要求遏止电势的实验测定；比较强度作用时需固定高于截止频率的频率。

The experimental determination of stopping potential is not required. Compare intensity effects at fixed frequency above threshold.

光子能量用于克服逸出功和提供最大光电子动能。

\(E=hf=hc/\lambda\); \(hf=\phi+E_{k,\max}\); \(\phi=hf_0\); \(E_{k,\max}=eV_{\mathrm{stop}}\).
