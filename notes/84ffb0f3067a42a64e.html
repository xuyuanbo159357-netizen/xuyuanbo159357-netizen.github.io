知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.4.2

3.4.2 伏安特性与欧姆定律

欧姆定律要求温度等物理条件不变。未另作说明时，电流表电阻为零，电压表电阻无限大。

Ohm’s law: current is directly proportional to potential difference provided temperature and other physical conditions remain constant. Ideal ammeters have zero resistance; ideal voltmeters have infinite resistance unless stated otherwise.

工作点的电阻使用电压与电流之比；直线图像的斜率含义取决于坐标轴。

\(R=V/I\) at an operating point. On a straight \(V\)–\(I\) graph, gradient \(=R\); on a straight \(I\)–\(V\) graph, gradient \(=1/R\).

3.4.4

3.4.4 直流电路与电功率

恒定电压和电流时使用电能公式；功率公式中的电流、电压和电阻必须对应同一元件。

Apply \(E=IVt\) when voltage and current are constant. \(I^2R\) and \(V^2/R\) refer to the voltage/current of the same resistor.

掌握串并联等效电阻、电能和功率。

\(R_{\mathrm{s}}=R_1+R_2+\cdots\); \(1/R_{\mathrm{p}}=1/R_1+1/R_2+\cdots\); \(E=IVt\); \(P=IV=I^2R=V^2/R\).
