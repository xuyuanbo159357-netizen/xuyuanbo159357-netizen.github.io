U2/07 常用物理模型

电子衍射与德布罗意波长 / Electron diffraction

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

electron diffraction → accelerating voltage → momentum → 电子衍射与德布罗意波长

推理顺序 / Reasoning

由加速电压求动能、动量和波长，再判断衍射角变化。

Obtain kinetic energy from accelerating pd; find non-relativistic momentum; use wavelength inversely proportional to momentum; infer diffraction change.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.5.11

3.5.11 波粒二象性与德布罗意波长

使用非相对论动量和动能模型；不要求具体粒子衍射方法的细节。

Use \(p=mv\) and \(E_k=\tfrac12mv^2\) in the non-relativistic model. Details of particular particle-diffraction methods are not expected.

德布罗意波长与动量成反比；电子加速关系结合 U1 动能与动量公式。

\(\lambda=h/p=h/(mv)\) for non-relativistic particles. If accelerated from rest through \(V\), \(p=\sqrt{2meV}\).
