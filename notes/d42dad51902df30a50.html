3.5.9 原子能级、线谱与X射线 / Collisions of electrons with atoms

考纲 / Specification： 3.5.9　｜　本单元知识谱系

Formula / 公式与单位

电子伏特是能量单位；发射光子的能量为上下能级差，X射线最高光子能量受加速电压限制。

\(1\,\mathrm{eV}=1.602\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm{J}\); emitted photon \(hf=E_{\mathrm{upper}}-E_{\mathrm{lower}}\); electron accelerated through \(V\) gains \(eV\); X-ray maximum photon energy \(=eV\).

Conditions / 条件与范围

束缚能级间的单光子吸收要求光子能量匹配允许能级差；碰撞电子可转移激发所需能量并保留余能。电离需达到移走电子所需能量；用SI普朗克常量计算前将eV换算为J。

Single-photon absorption between bound levels requires a matching energy gap. Colliding electrons can retain surplus kinetic energy. Ionisation requires sufficient removal energy; convert eV to J before using SI Planck constant.

Explanation / 解释

激发、电离与荧光灯 / Excitation, ionisation and lamps

激发使束缚电子升到更高允许能级；电离使电子脱离原子。碰撞电子只需具备足够能量，可在碰撞后带走余能；吸收一个光子造成束缚能级跃迁时，光子能量需匹配能级差。

Excitation raises a bound electron to a higher allowed level; ionisation removes it. A colliding electron can retain surplus kinetic energy, whereas absorption for a bound-to-bound transition requires a matching photon energy.

荧光灯：电场加速电子→与低压汞蒸气原子碰撞、使其激发或电离→原子退激发发紫外光→荧光涂层吸收紫外并发可见光。能量从高能光子转为较低能可见光，不能把涂层说成简单反射紫外光。

Accelerated electrons excite or ionise mercury atoms. De-excitation produces UV photons; the phosphor absorbs UV and emits visible light rather than merely reflecting UV.

能级、谱线与单位 / Energy levels, spectra and units

向低能级跃迁时 \(hf=E_{\rm upper}-E_{\rm lower}=hc/\lambda>0\)。离散能级只允许离散光子能量，形成特征线谱；能级差越大，频率越高、波长越短。吸收谱对应能级向上跃迁。

Downward transitions emit photons whose energies equal the positive level difference. Discrete levels produce discrete spectral lines; a larger gap gives higher frequency and shorter wavelength.

\(1\,\mathrm{eV}=1.602\times10^{-19}\,\mathrm J\)；eV 是能量单位，不是电势单位。能级若以电离极限为零，束缚能级为负值；计算仍取末、初状态之差。

An electron volt is an energy unit, not a voltage. Bound levels are negative when zero energy is at the ionisation limit; use state differences consistently.

X射线管与特征辐射 / X-ray tube and characteristic radiation

加热阴极释放电子，真空中高电压将电子加速到金属靶阳极。大部分电子能量成为热，部分成为X射线。连续谱来自电子减速；特征线来自内层空位被更高能级电子填充，其能量取决于靶元素。

A heated cathode emits electrons, accelerated through a vacuum towards a metal target. Most energy becomes heat. Braking produces a continuous spectrum; inner-shell transitions produce characteristic lines determined by the target element.

单个电子最大能量 \(eV\) 给出 \(hf_{\max}=eV\)、\(\lambda_{\min}=hc/(eV)\)；提高管电压减小最短波长，但不改变同一靶元素的特征能级差。医学成像利用不同组织吸收不同，骨骼等高吸收部位形成对比。

The maximum photon energy is \(eV\), giving a minimum wavelength. Increasing tube voltage changes the cutoff, not the target's characteristic level gaps. Medical imaging uses differential absorption by tissues.

Applications / 应用与实验

医学成像利用组织吸收差异；电子流改变影响强度，加速电压改变影响最高光子能量。

Medical X-ray images exploit different absorption by tissues. Explain changes to intensity when electron current changes and maximum photon energy when accelerating voltage changes.

Common errors / 易错点

电离不同于升到另一束缚能级；光子能量来自能级差而非单个能级数值。

Ionisation is removal, not simply promotion to another bound level. A line photon energy is a difference between energy levels, not one level’s absolute value.

3.5.9 方法 1 · 能级、光谱与激发 / Energy levels and excitation

  1. 先按能量高低排列；发射箭头向低能级，\(E_\gamma=E_i-E_f=hf\)。在允许跃迁中比较能隙，能隙越小波长越长。 / Compare only transitions allowed by the stated initial and final states.
  2. 若所有两级跃迁均可发生且能隙不同，\(n\) 个能级最多有 \(n(n-1)/2\) 条谱线；不是每种情形都达到该上限。 / State the accessibility assumptions.
  3. 束缚态吸收需光子能量等于能级差；电离可吸收高于电离能的光子，剩余能量成为电子动能。 / Distinguish discrete excitation from ionisation.
  4. 解释线谱：离散波长 → 离散光子能量 → 离散能级差。荧光灯：电子碰撞激发汞 → 汞发紫外 → 荧光粉吸收后多种可见跃迁。 / Give the causal sequence rather than listing terms.

对应真题 / Examples： PH02 · 202601 · Q07；PH02 · 201801 · Q05；PH02 · 202201 · Q04；PH02 · 202506 · Q09。

3.5.9 方法 2 · X射线管的控制变量 / Controls of an X-ray tube

加速电压规定电子最大动能 \(eV\)，因此决定截止光子能量和最短波长；靶材原子能级决定特征峰位置；管电流影响每秒电子数及光子产生率。 / Voltage sets the cutoff, target material sets characteristic energies, and tube current affects photon rate.

求热量时用输入能量 \(VIt\) 乘未转化为X射线的比例；求粒子数先用 \(It/e\)，求光子率用 \(P_\gamma/(hf)\)。 / Keep electrical input, radiation output and heat separate.

对应真题 / Examples： PH02 · 202301 · Q06；PH02 · 201806 · Q09；PH02 · 202201 · Q06。
