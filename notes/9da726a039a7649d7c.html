3.4.6 电动势与内阻

物理量与符号 / Quantities and symbols

符号沿用本节公式；若题目另有定义，以题设为准。 / These symbols follow this section; use any notation defined in the question.

物理量 / Quantity 符号 / Symbol SI单位 / SI unit 说明 / Notes
电动势 / electromotive force \(\varepsilon\) \(\mathrm{V}\) 是能量/电荷，不是力 / Energy per charge, not a force.
内阻 / internal resistance \(r\) \(\mathrm{\Omega}\) 与半径 r 按语境区分 / Not a radius in this context.
路端电势差 / terminal potential difference \(V\) \(\mathrm{V}\)
电流 / electric current \(I\) \(\mathrm{A}\) 与光强、转动惯量的 I 区分 / Not intensity or moment of inertia.
电阻 / resistance \(R\) \(\mathrm{\Omega}\)

方法、分析与误差 / Method, analysis and uncertainty

1

电池、变阻器和电流表串联，电压表并接电池。

Connect an ammeter in series with a cell and variable external resistance, and a voltmeter across the cell.

2

改变外阻取得一组电流，记录对应路端电压。

Change load resistance to obtain a range of currents; record the paired current and terminal voltage values.

3

控制电流，读数间断开，减少发热和电池变化。

Use modest currents, disconnect between readings and avoid cell heating or discharge drift.

4

作电压—电流图，截距求电动势、负斜率求内阻。

Plot V against I; intercept gives emf and the negative gradient gives internal resistance.

5

重复测量，并用合理极限线估计斜率和截距不确定度。

Repeat readings and assess gradient/intercept uncertainty using suitable limiting lines.

定量分析与条件核对 / Quantitative analysis and controls

↗ 3.4.6 电动势与内阻 / Electromotive force and internal resistance

必做实验3与线性图 / Required practical 3

电压表接电池两端，电流表、可变电阻和开关串联；改变负载取得多组 \((I,V)\)。作纵轴 \(V\)、横轴 \(I\) 的最佳拟合线：截距 \(\varepsilon\)，斜率 \(-r\)。

Measure terminal pd and current for several loads. On a graph of \(V\) against \(I\), the intercept gives emf and the gradient gives negative internal resistance.

间歇接通，避免电池温度和化学状态明显改变；不只取两个相邻点求斜率，检查表量程和零点。

Open the switch between readings to limit changes in cell temperature and state. Use a wide interval on the best-fit line and suitable meter ranges.
