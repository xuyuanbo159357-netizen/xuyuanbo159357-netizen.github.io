本卷复盘重点 / Paper-specific review

  • “随温度增加”弱于“与绝对温度成正比”，应按物理关系准确表述。 Distinguish an increase from direct proportionality to absolute temperature. 报告第2页 / Report p2

  • 抽水蓄能储存能量而非功率；泵水投入大于发电输出。 Pumped storage stores energy, not power, and pumping requires more energy than generation returns. 报告第3页 / Report p3

  • 理想气体 / Ideal Gases：14分，17.50%。 回看 / Review：3.11.3 气体定律与理想气体。结合下表原题核对定义、模型条件与评分步骤。 Check definitions, model constraints and credited steps against the linked questions.

  • 转动运动 / Rotational Motion：10分，12.50%。 回看 / Review：3.13.1 转动运动学、转动惯量与角动量。结合下表原题核对定义、模型条件与评分步骤。 Check definitions, model constraints and credited steps against the linked questions.

  • 水力发电与抽水蓄能 / Hydroelectric Power And Pumped Storage：10分，12.50%。 回看 / Review：3.13.4 水电与抽水蓄能。结合下表原题核对定义、模型条件与评分步骤。 Check definitions, model constraints and credited steps against the linked questions.
