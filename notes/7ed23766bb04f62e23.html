U3/07 常用物理模型

磁通变化与感应方向 / Flux linkage and induced EMF

关键词 → 模型 / Keywords → model

flux linkage → induced emf → Lenz → gradient → 磁通变化与感应方向

推理顺序 / Reasoning

判断场、面积、角度哪个变化，求磁通链变化率；楞次定律针对变化，闭路才有电流。

Determine which of field, area or angle changes; use the flux-linkage rate; apply Lenz’s law to the change; a closed path is needed for induced current.

知识与条件 / Knowledge and conditions

3.10.3

3.10.3 磁通量与磁通链

磁场均匀、线圈平面；乘匝数需每匝磁通相同。

Uniform B and a flat coil; multiplying by N assumes every turn links the same flux.

角度为磁场与线圈平面法线之间夹角。

\(\Phi=BA\cos\theta\); \(N\Phi=NBA\cos\theta\) for equal flux per turn.

3.10.4

3.10.4 电磁感应、楞次定律与发电

法拉第定律描述变化率，楞次定律描述反抗引起感应的变化；导体棒公式需正确垂直几何关系。

Faraday’s law: induced emf is proportional to the rate of change of flux linkage. Lenz’s law: induced effects oppose the change producing them. The rod formula uses the velocity component perpendicular to the rod and field.

电动势由磁通链变化率决定；平均变化率不同于瞬时值。

\(\varepsilon=-d(N\Phi)/dt\); magnitude \(=|\Delta(N\Phi)/\Delta t|\) for average rate; moving rod \(\varepsilon=BLv\) for mutually perpendicular geometry; rotating coil \(\varepsilon=NBA\omega\sin\omega t\).
