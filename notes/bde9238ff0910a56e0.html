3.7.3 引力势与引力势能 / Gravitational potential

考纲 / Specification： 3.7.3　｜　本单元知识谱系

Concept / 概念

  • gravitational potential — 引力势
  • gravitational potential difference — 引力势差
  • equipotential surface — 等势面

Definition / 定义

↗ gravitational potential — 引力势

Definition / 定义

English: The work done per unit mass by an external agent in moving a small test mass from infinity to a point, without changing its kinetic energy.

中文： 外力使单位小检验质量从无穷远移至该点、且不改变动能时所做的功。

定义评分依据 / Definition marking points： PH03 202506-Q04、PH03 202101-Q01。原答案在题目下展开；此处按评分要点概括。 Summarised from the marking points; originals are folded below each question.

↗ gravitational potential difference — 引力势差

Definition / 定义

English: The change in gravitational potential energy per unit mass between two points.

中文： 两点间引力势能变化与质量之比。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

↗ equipotential surface — 等势面

Definition / 定义

English: A surface on which every point has the same potential.

中文： 各点势相同的面。

定义按指定教材与 9630 考纲概括；教材页码见对应小节。 Summarised from the specified textbook and specification; page references are in the linked section.

真题定义依据 / Mark-scheme checks： PH03 202101-Q01、PH03 202506-Q04。定义按评分要点概括；原答案可在对应题目下展开。 Definitions are summarised from the scoring points, with original answers below the linked questions.

Formula / 公式与单位

无穷远规定为零势，吸引质量的引力势为负。

\(V=-GM/r\); \(U=mV=-GMm/r\); \(\Delta U=m\Delta V\); radial signed \(g_r=-dV/dr\).

Conditions / 条件与范围

缓慢移动或动能不变时，外力功等于势能变化；引力功符号相反。

An external-work value equals potential energy change for a slow transfer or unchanged kinetic energy; work done by gravity has the opposite sign.

Explanation / 解释

势、势能和正负号 / Potential, energy and signs

引力势是将单位质量从无穷远缓慢移到该点时外力所做的功，无穷远势为零。\(V=-GM/r\)，\(E_p=mV=-GMm/r\)；引力吸引质量，因此有限距离的势为负，向远处移动时势增大并趋近零。

Gravitational potential is external work per unit mass in bringing a mass slowly from infinity. With zero at infinity, \(V=-GM/r\) and potential energy is \(mV\); both rise towards zero on moving outward.

\(\Delta E_p=m(V_2-V_1)\)；缓慢移动时外力做功等于该变化，引力做功则为 \(-\Delta E_p\)。仅在近地小高度且 \(g\) 可视为恒定时使用 \(\Delta E_p=mg\Delta h\)。

External work in a slow transfer equals the increase in potential energy; gravitational work has the opposite sign. Use \(mg\Delta h\) only where gravity is approximately uniform.

等势面上 \(\Delta V=0\)，沿面移动时引力不做功；径向场等势面是同心球面，与场线垂直。

No gravitational work is done along an equipotential surface. Radial-field equipotentials are concentric spheres perpendicular to field lines.

图像斜率与面积 / Gradients and areas

取径向向外为正，\(g_r=-dV/dr=-GM/r^2\)；场强大小 \(g=GM/r^2\)。\(V\)–\(r\) 在负值区随 \(r\) 增大趋近零，切线斜率为正，其负值是有符号场分量。

With outward radius positive, the radial field is the negative potential gradient. The potential curve rises from negative values towards zero; its positive slope gives an inward field after the minus sign.

\(V_2-V_1=-\int_{r_1}^{r_2}g_r\,dr\)；已知图上画的是“场强大小”还是“有符号分量”后再处理面积。考纲原文个别大小写不改变 \(\Delta E_p=m\Delta V\) 的物理含义。

Potential difference is the negative signed field area. Check whether a graph shows field magnitude or signed component before interpreting its area.

逐项覆盖 / Requirement-by-requirement coverage： 3.7.3

U3 Gravitational field potential

球体外部场强大小按反平方下降，势为负并趋近零；势的负斜率给出带符号径向场强。 Exterior spherical field.

图示：负引力势的图像 / Negative gravitational potential

读图 / Reading： 无穷远为零势时，V 从负值趋近零；半径增大时 V 增大，不能把曲线在零线下方误读为场强方向。

With zero at infinity, potential approaches zero from below as radius grows; negative potential is not itself a field-direction statement.

图示：等势面与场线 / Equipotentials and field lines

读图 / Reading： 沿同一等势面移动，势能不变；场线与等势面垂直，相邻等势面间距也帮助判断场强大小。

Motion on one equipotential does not change potential energy; field lines are normal to it and equipotential spacing indicates field strength.

Applications / 应用与实验

大范围提升卫星时用引力势差，近似匀强场才使用重力势能变化公式。

Calculate the work needed to raise a satellite over a large distance; use \(mg\Delta h\) only for an approximately uniform field.

Common errors / 易错点

绝对势须说明无穷远零势；不可删去引力势负号。

“Zero at infinity” is essential to the absolute potential convention; do not drop the negative sign from gravitational potential.

3.7.3 方法 1 · 引力势、能量账目与势垒 / Potential, energy budgets and barriers

定义细节 / Definition detail. 势是把小测试质量从无穷远移至该点的外力功除以质量；以无穷远为零，引力吸引使有限距离处的势为负。 / Gravitational potential is external work per unit mass from infinity to the point, with zero at infinity.

  1. 用 \(E_{p,f}-E_{p,i}=m(V_f-V_i)\)；沿长距离不能默认 \(g\) 恒定而用 \(mgh\)。由能量求出中心距后再减天体半径。 / Keep radius and altitude separate.
  2. 地月间最小发射能量由起点到沿途最大势能的增量决定，不一定是到目的地的净势能差，也不一定要达到无穷远。 / Check the energy barrier along the path.
  3. 非圆轨道用 \(E_{total}=\frac12mv^2-GMm/r\)；不要套仅适用于圆轨道的 \(E=-GMm/(2r)\)。 / Retain the general energy equation for an elliptical trajectory.
  4. 若无耗散，初速度相同的不同质量岩石达到同一高度，因动能、势能都与物体质量成正比。 / Demonstrate cancellation rather than merely asserting equal height.
