简明英文样例答案 / Simple English model answer

Shine monochromatic light through a double slit onto a screen. Measure the slit separation s with a travelling microscope and the slit-screen distance D with a metre rule. Measure the distance across several fringe spacings with a ruler or travelling microscope and divide by the number of spacings to find w. Use λ = ws/D. Measuring several spacings reduces the percentage uncertainty in w.
