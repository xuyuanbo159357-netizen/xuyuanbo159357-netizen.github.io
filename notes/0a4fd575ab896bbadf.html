阅读方式 / How to use

本页区分物理原则、推荐书写及具体试题的评分要求；具体容差和扣分范围只用于所引题目。 / Distinguish physical principles, presentation advice and question-specific marking.

1. 定义要保留方向和参照 / Retain direction and reference

简谐运动不能只写“周期性往返运动”：加速度与相对平衡位置的位移成正比且方向相反。引力势需说明单位质量及参照零点；电势与引力势不能用同一单位。 / SHM requires proportional restoring acceleration; potential definitions require the correct quantity per charge or mass and a reference.

2018 June Q02.2 对负引力势的解释：无限远取零势；将单位质量从该点移到无限远要克服引力做功，因此该点势低于零。 / Explain the negative potential using the chosen zero and work against attraction. PH03 2018 June Q02.2 · MS PDF p5 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

2. 解释先写保持不变的量 / Identify what remains fixed

电容题需辨认是否与电源连接。隔离电容器电荷近似不变；接理想恒压电源时电势差不变，不能同时无条件固定 \(Q\) 和 \(V\)。 / Distinguish fixed charge from fixed voltage before using \(Q=CV\).

2026 June Q06.1–06.3：介质极化降低同电荷下的净场和电势差；板距变小时电容增大，并由固定 \(Q\) 推出 \(V\) 减小。只写“capacitance increases”可能不足以解释所问的电压变化。 / Link the material or geometry change to the fixed quantity and then to voltage. PH03 2026 June Q06.1–06.3 · MS PDF p17 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

3. 方向题要有物理依据 / Support directions physically

  • 简谐回复力与位移方向相反，不总与速度反向。 / The restoring force opposes displacement, not necessarily velocity. PH03 2026 June Q03.3 · MS PDF p9 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer
  • 磁力垂直速度，理想情况下改变方向而不做功改变速率。 / A perpendicular magnetic force changes direction without doing work. PH03 2026 June Q07.1 · MS PDF p19 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer
  • 速度选择器需说明电场力和磁场力大小相等、方向相反；2026 June Q07.6 即使写合力为零，仍要明确反方向。 / Explicitly state the opposed directions as well as cancellation. PH03 2026 June Q07.6 · MS PDF p21 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

4. 比较结论应与推理一致 / Keep comparisons consistent

2026 June Q07.3：同 \(B,q,v\) 下，轨道半径小对应质量小；要点名 P、Q 并用 \(r=mv/(Bq)\) 支持。该问若给出错误质量结论，即使某些中间陈述正确仍不给分。 / Compare the named ions consistently; the cited rubric rejects a wrong final comparison. PH03 2026 June Q07.3 · MS PDF p20 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

评价题不一定只有一种可接受结论。Q03.5 对阻力是否破坏简谐条件容许不同结论，但理由必须与回复力是否正比于位移等关系一致。 / Some evaluations allow different conclusions supported by consistent physics. PH03 2026 June Q03.5 · MS PDF p12 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

5. 电磁感应解释 / Explain electromagnetic induction

变化情境 → 磁通链变化 → 变化率与感应电动势 → 若问方向，再用楞次定律。 / Changing situation → changing flux linkage → rate and induced emf → Lenz's law if direction is asked.

2024 June Q06.2 要把转角变化与磁通链变化联系；仅无说明地写方程不能获得该解释分。若采用 \(\mathcal E=-d(N\Phi)/dt\)，明确 \(\Phi\) 是单匝磁通量，不能重复或遗漏匝数。 / State what the flux symbol represents and connect the equation to the rotating coil. PH03 2024 June Q06.2 · MS PDF p16 · 原题与答案 / Question and answer

英文复述示例 / English recall example

“As the coil rotates, its flux linkage changes. The induced emf depends on the rate of change of flux linkage. The induced current acts to oppose the change that produces it.”

线圈转动使磁通链变化；感应电动势取决于变化率。若回路闭合，感应电流的作用反抗产生它的磁通变化。 / The final sentence concerns a closed circuit.
